 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Purchase Order Creation

Performed By: Purchasing Team, HR Employee

Activity:
- Purchasing team members create new purchase orders by specifying vendor, shipping method, and order details (PurchaseOrderID, VendorID, ShipMethodID, OrderDate).
- They assign the employee responsible for the purchase order (EmployeeID).
- The HR employee enters or updates employee information related to the purchase order (BusinessEntityID, ModifiedDate).

## 2. Purchase Order Approval

Performed By: Purchasing Manager

Activity:
- Purchasing managers review and approve pending purchase orders by updating their status (Status).

## 3. Purchase Order Detail Registration

Performed By: Purchasing Team, Production Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing team members register product details for each line item in a purchase order (ProductID, LineTotal).
- Production staff update the quantity accepted into inventory and any rejected during inspection (StockedQty, RejectedQty).

## 4. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Manager, Finance Team

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal information, job title, hire date, and employment status (Name, GroupName, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag).
- The finance team records the employee's initial salary and pay frequency (Rate, PayFrequency).

## 5. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Manager

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to departments and record their start and end dates (DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate).

## 6. Employee Address Management

Performed By: Employee, HR Assistant

Activity:
- Employees update their personal addresses by providing address details (AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode).
- HR assistants manage employee addresses and record any changes (AddressTypeID, ModifiedDate).

## 7. Purchase Order Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Finance Team

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the inventory levels for each product (Quantity).
- The finance team records any changes to the purchase order subtotal, tax amount, shipping cost, and total due to vendor (SubTotal, TaxAmt, Freight, TotalDue).

## 8. Purchase Order Modification

Performed By: Purchasing Team, Finance Team

Activity:
- Purchasing team members update purchase order details such as order status, shipping method, or vendor (Status, ShipMethodID, VendorID).
- The finance team updates the purchase order subtotal, tax amount, shipping cost, and total due to vendor accordingly (SubTotal, TaxAmt, Freight, TotalDue).

## 9. Employee Payroll Management

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team records employee salary changes and pay history (RateChangeDate, Rate).

## 10. Employee Departure Processing

Performed By: HR Manager, Finance Team

Activity:
- HR managers record an employee's departure by updating their current flag (CurrentFlag).
- The finance team processes any final payroll adjustments and updates the employee's pay history (SickLeaveHours, VacationHours).
