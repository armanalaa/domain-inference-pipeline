 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- Assign employees to departments by selecting a DepartmentID and recording the OrganizationNode, OrganizationLevel, and ModifiedDate.
- Record employee addresses by specifying AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation.

## 2. Employee Departure Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Document employee departures by recording the EndDate for each employee-department association in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 3. Employee Salary Adjustments

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Update employee salaries by modifying the Rate column in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 4. Shift Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Record shift details such as ShiftID, Name, StartTime, and EndTime.

## 5. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Register or update addresses by specifying AddressTypeID, AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation.

## 6. Sales Territory Assignment

Performed By: Sales Managers

Activity:
- Assign sales territories to employees by selecting a TerritoryID and recording the BusinessEntityID, StartDate, and EndDate in the SalesTerritoryHistory table.

## 7. Cost Tracking for Sales Territories

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Record business costs in the territory year to date (CostYTD) and the previous year (CostLastYear).
