 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Sales Personnel Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Sales Managers

Activity:
- Human Resources managers assign territories to sales representatives based on the TerritoryID and SalesTerritory.SalesTerritoryID.
- Sales managers set sales quotas for each sales representative by recording the SalesQuota and SalesLastYear in the SalesPerson table.
- Sales managers update salesperson's commissions and bonuses based on their performance, modifying the CommissionPct and Bonus columns accordingly.

## 2. Sales Performance Tracking

Performed By: Sales Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Sales managers record salesperson's year-to-date and previous year's sales in the SalesYTD and SalesLastYear columns respectively.
- Data analysts track sales performance over time by monitoring changes to the SalesPerson table's ModifiedDate column.
- Data analysts generate reports on sales performance using data from the SalesPersonQuotaHistory table, including quota dates (QuotaDate) and actual sales (SalesQuota).

## 3. Sales Territory Management

Performed By: Sales Managers, Geographic Information Specialists

Activity:
- Sales managers define and assign territories to sales representatives using the SalesTerritory.SalesTerritoryID column.
- Geographic information specialists maintain up-to-date spatial data for each territory by updating the SpatialLocation column in the Person Address table.

## 4. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, HR Assistants

Activity:
- Human resources managers hire new employees and record their information (BusinessEntityID, NationalIDNumber, LoginID, etc.) in the HumanResources Employee table.
- HR assistants assign new hires to departments and update their employment history using the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 5. Employee Information Updates

Performed By: Employees, HR Managers

Activity:
- Employees update their personal information (e.g., address, marital status) by modifying relevant columns in the Person Address and HumanResources Employee tables.
- HR managers review and approve employee updates as needed.

## 6. Payroll Processing

Performed By: Finance Managers, Payroll Specialists

Activity:
- Finance managers set salaries for employees based on their job titles (JobTitle) and salaried status (SalariedFlag).
- Payroll specialists calculate and record employee vacation and sick leave hours (VacationHours, SickLeaveHours) in the HumanResources Employee table.

## 7. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Employees update their home addresses by modifying the Person Address table's columns (AddressLine1, City, StateProvinceID, etc.).
- Data entry clerks maintain accurate address information for all employees in the Person BusinessEntityAddress table.
