 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Record new employee details such as NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- Assign employees to departments by updating the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table with DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate.
- Record employee pay history by adding entries to HumanResources EmployeePayHistory with RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate.

## 2. Employee Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Register employee addresses in the Person Address table using AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation.
- Assign address types to employee addresses through the Person BusinessEntityAddress table with AddressTypeID.

## 3. Employee Contact Information Update

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- Update employee contact information in the Person BusinessEntityContact table with relevant details like phone numbers, emails, etc.

## 4. Sales Territory Assignment

Performed By: Sales Managers

Activity:
- Assign sales territories to employees based on their geographic area of operation using TerritoryID and Group from the Sales.SalesTerritory table.

## 5. Customer Purchase Reason Tracking

Performed By: Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Record customer purchase reasons in the Sales.SalesReason table with relevant details like Name, ReasonType, and ModifiedDate.

## 6. Sales Tax Rate Management

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Manage sales tax rates by updating the Sales.SalesTaxRate table with StateProvinceID, TaxType, TaxRate, Name, and ModifiedDate.

## 7. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounting Team

Activity:
- Generate invoices based on sales orders and tax rates using data from the SalesOrderHeader and SalesTaxRate tables.

## 8. Sales Order Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Register new sales orders with details like product information, customer information, shipping details, etc., in the SalesOrderHeader table.
- Update SalesPersonQuotaHistory with BusinessEntityID and QuotaDate to track sales quotas.

## 9. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Track inventory levels by updating the inventory tables with relevant details like product code, quantity, location, etc.
- Issue purchase orders for replenishing inventory based on current stock levels and demand forecasts.
