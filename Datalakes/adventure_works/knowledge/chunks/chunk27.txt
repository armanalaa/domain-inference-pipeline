 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Vendor Management

Performed By: Purchasing Team, HR Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams research and evaluate potential vendors based on credit rating and account number.
- HR managers hire new employees to join the purchasing team, recording their personal details and employment history.
- Purchasing teams add newly evaluated vendors to the vendor database, including company name, URL, and active status.
- HR managers update employee records when a vendor employee changes roles or leaves the company.
- Purchasing teams maintain vendor relationships, updating vendor records with any changes in account number, company name, or URL.

## 2. Vendor Contact Management

Performed By: Purchasing Team, HR Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams identify key contacts at each vendor for communication purposes.
- HR managers record contact information for employees within the purchasing team.
- Purchasing teams update vendor contact information as needed.

## 3. Vendor Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Purchasing Team

Activity:
- HR managers maintain employee addresses in the business entity address table.
- Purchasing teams record and update vendor addresses in the vendor table.

## 4. Shipping Method Configuration

Performed By: IT Administrators, Purchasing Team

Activity:
- IT administrators create and manage shipping methods, including setting default charges and rates.
- Purchasing team members assign shipping methods to purchase orders as needed.

## 5. Purchase Order Creation

Performed By: Purchasing Team

Activity:
- Purchasing team members create new purchase orders, specifying vendor, shipping method, and order details.

## 6. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounts Payable Team

Activity:
- Accounts payable team members review and approve incoming invoices from vendors.
- Upon approval, the team records the invoice details in the system.

## 7. Payment Processing

Performed By: Accounts Payable Team

Activity:
- Accounts payable team members make payments to vendors based on approved invoices and payment terms.
