 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- Update employee information as needed, modifying the relevant columns.
- Assign employees to departments by recording their BusinessEntityID, DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Record changes in employee pay history, including RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate.

## 2. Address Management

Performed By: Person Administrators

Activity:
- Register new addresses by recording their AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, SpatialLocation, rowguid, and ModifiedDate.
- Assign address types to each address by recording the AddressTypeID.
- Link addresses to business entities by recording the BusinessEntityID, AddressID, AddressTypeID, rowguid, and ModifiedDate.

## 3. Employee Department History Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Record employee department history changes by updating the StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 4. Employee Pay History Management

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Update employee pay history by recording the RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 5. Shift Management

Performed By: Operations Managers

Activity:
- Define shifts by recording their ShiftID, Name, StartTime, EndTime, and ModifiedDate.

## 6. Sales Order Creation

Performed By: Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Create sales orders by recording the customer details, billing and shipping addresses, credit card information, currency rate, salesperson ID, territory ID, ship method ID, and sales order number.

## 7. Sales Reason Coding

Performed By: Sales Managers

Activity:
- Code sales orders with appropriate reason codes by recording the sales order ID and sales reason ID in the SalesOrderHeaderSalesReason table.

## 8. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounts Receivable Clerks

Activity:
- Generate invoices based on sales orders, using the relevant information from the SalesOrderHeader table.

## 9. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Retrieve products for shipment by looking up their product codes in the HumanResources Employee catalog.
- Track inventory levels and update them as needed.
