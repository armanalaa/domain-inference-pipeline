 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, Recruiters

Activity:
- HR managers review job applications and select suitable candidates.
- Recruiters contact selected candidates to schedule interviews.
- HR managers conduct interviews and assess candidate qualifications.
- If successful, HR managers offer the job to the candidate and discuss employment terms.
- Once accepted, HR managers enter new employee details into the HumanResources Employee table.

## 2. Employee Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- Employees update their personal information such as address, phone number, and email in the Person BusinessEntityContact and Person Address tables.
- HR managers verify and approve employee personal information updates.

## 3. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers assign new employees to specific departments based on job roles and department availability.
- HR managers record the assignment details in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 4. Employee Salary Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Payroll Department

Activity:
- HR managers determine employee salaries and update them in the HumanResources EmployeePayHistory table.
- Payroll department calculates paychecks based on salary information and pays employees accordingly.

## 5. Employee Performance Evaluation

Performed By: Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Managers evaluate employee performance and provide feedback.
- HR managers compile performance evaluations and determine any necessary adjustments to salaries or job roles.

## 6. Employee Offboarding

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers terminate employees' contracts and update their status in the HumanResources Employee table.
- HR managers coordinate exit procedures such as returning company property and processing final paychecks.

## 7. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Employees and customers add, update, or delete their addresses in the Person Address table.

## 8. State/Province Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Data Administrators

Activity:
- HR managers and data administrators manage state/province information for employees and customers in the Person StateProvince table.
