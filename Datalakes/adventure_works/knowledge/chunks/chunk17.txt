 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each product and update them accordingly when new shipments arrive or products are sold.

## 2. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, New Hires

Activity:
- HR managers assign a unique employee ID to each new hire during the onboarding process.
- New hires provide their personal information such as birthdate, marital status, and gender for record keeping.

## 3. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff (if needed)

Activity:
- HR managers assign new employees to specific departments based on job titles and organizational structure.
- If necessary, warehouse staff update the employee's department history with the start date of their assignment.

## 4. Employee Salary Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers determine the salary for each employee based on job title and experience level.
- Finance department records the salaries in the employee pay history table along with other relevant details like pay frequency and rate changes.

## 5. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, HR Managers (if needed)

Activity:
- Employees provide their home addresses for company records.
- If an employee moves, they update their address in the system, and HR managers verify and confirm the change if necessary.

## 6. Shift Scheduling

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, HR Managers (if needed)

Activity:
- Warehouse managers create shift schedules for warehouse staff based on workload and operational requirements.
- If required, HR managers update the employee's shift information in the system.

## 7. Inventory Replenishment Requests

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- When inventory levels reach a predefined threshold, warehouse staff submit requests for reordering products to the purchasing team.

## 8. Purchase Order Processing

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Purchasing teams review and approve purchase orders based on inventory needs and vendor offers.
- Once approved, finance department records the purchase order details in the system.

## 9. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounts Payable Team, Finance Department

Activity:
- Accounts payable team receives invoices from vendors and verifies their accuracy.
- If validated, finance department records the invoice details and updates the corresponding purchase orders in the system.

## 10. Sales Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff (if needed)

Activity:
- Sales representatives receive customer orders and process them into sales orders.
- If necessary, warehouse staff update the product inventory levels to reflect sold items and prepare shipments accordingly.
