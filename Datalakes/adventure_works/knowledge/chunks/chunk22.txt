 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for raw materials based on the required quantity for each item.

## 2. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, job titles, and salaries.
- Warehouse staff track employee shifts to ensure proper workforce distribution.

## 3. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to departments based on their roles and organizational structure.

## 4. Employee Department History Tracking

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers record employee department transitions, including start and end dates.
- Warehouse staff update employee shift assignments accordingly when departments change.

## 5. Employee Pay Management

Performed By: Finance, HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers update employee pay history with rate changes, pay frequencies, and vacation/sick leave hours.
- Finance records the updated pay information for each employee.

## 6. Address Management

Performed By: Customers, Employees

Activity:
- Customers provide their addresses during order placement.
- Employees update their personal contact information in the address book.

## 7. Product Line Maintenance

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers maintain product line descriptions, including maintenance details and specifications.

## 8. Manufacturing Instructions Management

Performed By: Production Engineers

Activity:
- Production engineers document manufacturing instructions for each product model.

## 9. Bill of Materials Generation

Performed By: Production Engineers

Activity:
- Production engineers generate a multilevel bill of materials for each assembly, identifying components and their relationships.

## 10. Product Usage Tracking

Performed By: Production Engineers

Activity:
- Production engineers track which products are used in other assemblies to ensure efficient production planning.
