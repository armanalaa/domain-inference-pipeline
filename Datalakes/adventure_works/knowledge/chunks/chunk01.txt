 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, HR Assistants

Activity:
- Human Resources managers review job applications and select candidates for interviews.
- HR assistants schedule interviews with potential employees.
- HR managers conduct interviews and make hiring decisions based on candidate qualifications.
- Once hired, HR managers enter new employee information into the HumanResources Employee table.
- HR assistants assign departments to new hires by updating the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 2. Employee Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, HR Assistants, Employees

Activity:
- Employees update their personal information, such as address or marital status, in the HumanResources Employee table.
- HR managers verify and approve employee updates to ensure accuracy.
- HR assistants update employee records in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table when employees change departments.

## 3. Employee Salary Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Payroll Specialists

Activity:
- HR managers determine employee salaries based on job titles and performance evaluations.
- Payroll specialists update the HumanResources EmployeePayHistory table with new salary rates and pay frequencies.

## 4. Department Management

Performed By: Department Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Department managers request changes to their department structure or composition.
- HR managers approve or deny department requests and update the HumanResources Department table accordingly.

## 5. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, HR Assistants

Activity:
- Employees update their personal addresses in the Person Address table.
- HR assistants verify and approve address updates to ensure accuracy.
