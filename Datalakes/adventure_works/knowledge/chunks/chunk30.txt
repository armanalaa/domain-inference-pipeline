 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- Assign employees to departments by selecting a DepartmentID and recording the StartDate and EndDate of their employment in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Record employee pay history by specifying RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency for each change in compensation.

## 2. Employee Information Update

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Modify employee information by updating their MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, or rowguid.
- Update employee department history by modifying the EndDate of an existing employment record or adding a new one.
- Record changes in employee pay history by updating RateChangeDate, Rate, and PayFrequency.

## 3. Employee Address Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register employee addresses by recording the AddressID, AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, SpatialLocation, rowguid, and ModifiedDate.
- Associate an address with an employee by linking the BusinessEntityID, AddressID, and AddressTypeID.

## 4. Employee Contact Information Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Record employee credit card information by associating the CreditCardID and BusinessEntityID.

## 5. Sales Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Place a sales order by specifying the SalesOrderID, CarrierTrackingNumber, OrderQty, ProductID, SpecialOfferID, and UnitPrice for each product sold.

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Track inventory levels by recording the quantity of products available in stock.

## 7. Customer Information Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Register new customers by recording their AccountNumber, PersonID, StoreID, and TerritoryID.
- Update customer information by modifying the AccountNumber or rowguid.
