 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Team

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal details, job title, and department. (HumanResources Employee)
- HR updates employee information when necessary, such as changes in address or job title. (HumanResources Employee)
- HR tracks employee history across departments and shifts. (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory, HumanResources Shift)
- HR records employee pay history, including rate changes and pay frequencies. (HumanResources EmployeePayHistory)

## 2. Employee Contact Management

Performed By: HR Team, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers maintain employee contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, and mailing addresses. (Person Address, Person BusinessEntityContact)
- Warehouse staff use contact details to communicate with employees about shipments or deliveries.

## 3. Employee Address Management

Performed By: HR Team

Activity:
- HR managers record employee addresses, specifying their types and locations. (Person AddressType, Person BusinessEntityAddress)

## 4. Job Candidate Management

Performed By: HR Team, Recruiters

Activity:
- HR managers store job candidate resumes, including personal details, skills, education history, and employment history. (HumanResources.JobCandidate)

## 5. Employee Department Reporting

Performed By: HR Managers, Management

Activity:
- HR generates reports on employee department assignments for management review. (HumanResources.vEmployeeDepartment)

## 6. Job Candidate Education Reporting

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR generates reports on job candidate education history from the xml column in the HumanResources.JobCandidate table. (HumanResources.vJobCandidateEducation)

## 7. Job Candidate Employment History Reporting

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR generates reports on job candidate employment history from the xml column in the HumanResources.JobCandidate table. (HumanResources.vJobCandidateEmployment)
