 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, Recruiters

Activity:
- HR managers review job applications and select suitable candidates.
- Recruiters contact shortlisted candidates to schedule interviews.
- HR managers conduct interviews and make hiring decisions based on candidate qualifications and fit.
- Successful candidates receive job offers with details like job title, salary, benefits, and start date.
- New hires provide necessary personal information such as national ID number, birthdate, marital status, gender, and address for employment records.

## 2. Employee Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- Employees update their personal information (e.g., address, phone number) as needed.
- HR managers maintain employee records, including job titles, hire dates, salaries, and benefits.
- HR managers assign employees to specific departments and shifts based on department needs and employee preferences.
- Shift assignments are recorded in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 3. Payroll Processing

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers update employee pay rates and pay frequencies when necessary.
- Finance department calculates employees' salaries based on their pay rates and hours worked.
- Employee pay history is recorded in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 4. Error Logging

Performed By: Database Administrators, Developers

Activity:
- Database administrators and developers log errors that occur during database operations using the uspLogError stored procedure.
- The error details are saved in the ErrorLog table for future reference and analysis.

## 5. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, HR Managers

Activity:
- Employees provide their addresses when they join the company or update them as needed.
- HR managers maintain employee address records, including address lines, city, state, postal code, and country.

## 6. Invoice Processing (Future Workflow)

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices for customers based on sales orders.
- The finance department reviews and approves the invoices before they are sent to customers.

## 7. Inventory Management (Future Workflow)

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update them as items are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams order new inventory when stock levels fall below a certain threshold.
