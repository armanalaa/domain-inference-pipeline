 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Location Management

Performed By: Facilities Manager, Inventory Planner

Activity:
- Facilities managers register new manufacturing locations by recording the location description, cost rate, and availability. (HumanResources Department)
- Inventory planners assign inventory locations to specific products based on storage requirements. (Production.Location)

## 2. Product Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Planner

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update product inventory levels by recording the quantity of products in each inventory location. (Production.ProductInventory)
- Inventory planners adjust inventory levels when products are sold or returned. (Production.ProductInventory)

## 3. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Manager, Employee

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal information, job title, and hire date. (HumanResources Employee)
- Employees update their personal information as needed throughout their employment. (HumanResources Employee)

## 4. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Manager, Employee

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to specific departments and record the start date. (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory)
- Employees update their department assignments when they transfer or are promoted. (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory)

## 5. Employee Pay History Management

Performed By: HR Manager, Payroll Officer

Activity:
- HR managers record employee salary changes and pay frequencies. (HumanResources EmployeePayHistory)
- Payroll officers calculate and distribute employee salaries based on the recorded pay history.

## 6. Address Management

Performed By: Employee, Data Administrator

Activity:
- Employees update their personal addresses by recording the address line, city, state, postal code, and address type. (Person Address)
- Data administrators manage employee addresses for accuracy and completeness.

## 7. Sales Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representative, Customer Service Representative

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by recording the product ID, inventory location, and shipping address. (Sales.SalesOrderHeader)
- Customer service representatives handle order inquiries and updates. (Sales.SalesOrderDetail)

## 8. Currency Mapping

Performed By: Data Administrator

Activity:
- Data administrators map ISO currency codes to specific countries or regions. (Sales.CountryRegionCurrency)
