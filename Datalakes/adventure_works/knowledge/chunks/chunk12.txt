 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Department Transfers Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Assign employees to departments based on job requirements and department needs (DepartmentID).
- Record the start date for an employee's transfer to a new department (StartDate).
- Update employee department transfers when an employee leaves a department or moves to a current department (EndDate).
- Document any changes made to employee department history records (ModifiedDate).

## 2. Employee Shift Allocation

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Determine the shift that each employee works based on their job responsibilities and business needs (ShiftID).
- Update employee shift allocation when an employee changes shifts or starts a new position (ModifiedDate).

## 3. Employee Payroll Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Finance Team

Activity:
- Record the hourly rate for each employee (Rate).
- Set the pay frequency for employees (PayFrequency).
- Update employee pay history when there is a change in pay or salary (ModifiedDate).

## 4. Job Candidate Resume Submission

Performed By: Job Applicants, Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Submit resumes to the Human Resources department in XML format (Resume).
- Update job candidate records with the date and time of submission (ModifiedDate).

## 5. Shift Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Define work shifts with descriptions, start times, and end times (Name, StartTime, EndTime).
- Update shift information when necessary (ModifiedDate).

## 6. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, HR Assistants

Activity:
- Register new employees in the system by recording their identification numbers, personal details, and employment details (BusinessEntityID, NationalIDNumber, LoginID, OrganizationNode, OrganizationLevel, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag).
- Assign employees to departments and shifts upon hire (DepartmentID, ShiftID).

## 7. Employee Department History Tracking

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, HR Assistants

Activity:
- Maintain a history of an employee's department transfers, including start and end dates for each transfer (StartDate, EndDate).
- Record any changes made to the employee department history records (ModifiedDate).
