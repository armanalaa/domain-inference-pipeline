 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- Assign initial department and shift for the employee.
- Record the employee's address and contact information.

## 2. Employee Department Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Update an employee's department assignment by recording the new DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, and EndDate.
- Transfer employees between departments as needed.

## 3. Employee Salary Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Finance Team

Activity:
- Adjust an employee's salary rate by recording the RateChangeDate and new Rate.
- Update the pay frequency for an employee.

## 4. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, Customers, Vendors

Activity:
- Register a new address by providing the AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, and PostalCode.
- Update an existing address with new information as needed.

## 5. Address Type Management

Performed By: Data Administrators

Activity:
- Define types of addresses for use in the system by recording the Name.

## 6. Employee Contact Information Management

Performed By: Employees, Customers, Vendors

Activity:
- Record contact information associated with an employee's business entity.

## 7. Business Entity Management

Performed By: Data Administrators

Activity:
- Register new business entities (employees, customers, vendors) by recording their unique identifier and other relevant details.
