 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Human Resources managers register new employees by recording their national ID number, login ID, job title, hire date, and salaried flag.
- Warehouse staff track employee shifts by updating the start and end times.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to vendors based on employee requests.
- Sales representatives update employee department history when an employee transfers departments.

## 2. Employee Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- HR managers register new employee addresses by recording the address line1, city, state, postal code, and address type.
- Warehouse staff update employee addresses when an employee changes location.
- Sales representatives use employee addresses for customer relationship management.

## 3. Employee Contact Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- HR managers register employee contact information by recording the contact type and person's business entity ID.
- Sales representatives update employee contact information as needed for communication purposes.

## 4. Employee Payroll Processing

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records employee pay history by updating rate, pay frequency, and vacation/sick leave hours.

## 5. Vendor Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams register new vendors by recording their business entity ID and contact information.

## 6. Store Management

Performed By: Store Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Store managers update store information by recording the name, start time, end time, and other relevant details.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each store location.

## 7. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance processes invoices from vendors by reviewing purchase orders and approving payments.
