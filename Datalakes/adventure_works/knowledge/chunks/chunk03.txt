 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and department assignments.
- HR managers update employee information when necessary, modifying the relevant fields in the HumanResources Employee table.
- HR managers manage employee departures by updating the EndDate in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 2. Department Assignment Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to departments and update their organizational structure by recording the DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, and EndDate in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Warehouse staff manage employee shifts by updating the ShiftID in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 3. Employee Pay Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers update employee salaries and pay frequencies by recording the RateChangeDate, Rate, and PayFrequency in the HumanResources EmployeePayHistory table.
- Finance department records and manages employee payments using the information from the HumanResources EmployeePayHistory table.

## 4. Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- HR managers record employees' addresses by associating them with AddressID, AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation in the Person Address table.
- Customers provide their addresses during registration or order placement, which are recorded in the Person Address table.

## 5. Contact Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- HR managers record employees' contact information by associating it with AddressTypeID and rowguid in the Person BusinessEntityContact table.
- Customers provide their contact information during registration or order placement, which are recorded in the Person BusinessEntityContact table.

## 6. Product Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives manage product catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on product requirements.

## 7. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity of products in stock.
