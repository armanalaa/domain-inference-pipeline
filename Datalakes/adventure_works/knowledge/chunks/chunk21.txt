 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- Assign employees to departments by selecting a DepartmentID and recording the StartDate and EndDate in EmployeeDepartmentHistory.
- Record employee pay history by specifying RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency for each change.

## 2. Employee Information Update

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Modify employee information by updating their details such as AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and ModifiedDate.
- Record the changes in the ModifiedDate column for each table related to the employee.

## 3. Department Management

Performed By: Department Managers

Activity:
- Create new departments by registering a unique DepartmentID, Name, GroupName, and ModifiedDate.
- Assign employees to departments by selecting a BusinessEntityID and recording the ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in EmployeeDepartmentHistory.

## 4. Address Management

Performed By: Employees or Administrative Staff

Activity:
- Register personal addresses by providing AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and ModifiedDate.
- Specify the address type by selecting an AddressTypeID.

## 5. Contact Information Update

Performed By: Employees or Administrative Staff

Activity:
- Update contact information by modifying BusinessEntityAddress with new AddressID, AddressTypeID, and ModifiedDate.

## 6. Shift Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers or Supervisors

Activity:
- Define shifts by recording ShiftID, Name, StartTime, EndTime, and ModifiedDate.

## 7. Product Subcategory Creation

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Create product subcategories by registering a unique ProductSubcategoryID, ProductCategoryID, Name, and ModifiedDate.

## 8. Unit Measure Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers or Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Register unit measures by providing UnitMeasureCode and Name, and recording the ModifiedDate.
