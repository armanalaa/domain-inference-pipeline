 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- HR managers assign employees to departments by specifying the DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, and EndDate for each employee-department history record.
- HR managers update employee records with rate changes by recording the RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate for each employee pay history record.

## 2. Employee Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers update employee information such as address, contact details, or marital status by recording the relevant details in the appropriate BusinessEntityAddress and BusinessEntityContact records.
- Warehouse staff track employee shifts by updating the EndDate for each employee-department history record when an employee changes departments.

## 3. Address Management

Performed By: Person Address Staff

Activity:
- Person Address staff register new addresses by recording the AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation for each address record.
- Person Address staff update address details by modifying the relevant columns in an existing address record.

## 4. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price for each item.
- Product managers update product information such as cost or list price over time by creating records in the Production.ProductCostHistory and Production.ProductListPriceHistory tables respectively.

## 5. Assembly Management

Performed By: Production Managers

Activity:
- Production managers create new assembly items by recording the BillOfMaterialsID, ProductAssemblyID, ComponentID, AK_BillOfMaterials_ProductAssemblyID_ComponentID_StartDate, Quantity, UnitMeasureCode, BOMLevel, and ModifiedDate for each component in the Production.BillOfMaterials table.
- Production managers update assembly information such as cost or quantity over time by modifying the relevant columns in an existing assembly record.

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the EndDate for each component in the Production.BillOfMaterials table when an item is used or added to the inventory.

## 7. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders by specifying the required products and quantities for each order.
- Customer service agents update order statuses by modifying the relevant columns in an existing order record.
