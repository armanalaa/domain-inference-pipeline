 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources Team

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- Update employee personal information such as NationalIDNumber, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, and rowguid.
- Assign employees to departments by recording DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Update employee login details including OrganizationNode, LoginID, JobTitle, HireDate, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.

## 2. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Provide new employees with necessary equipment by updating the inventory levels for assigned items.
- Assign workstations to new employees based on their department and shift.
- Train new employees on company policies, procedures, and job-specific skills.

## 3. Employee Performance Evaluation

Performed By: Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Conduct regular performance evaluations for employees to assess their job performance and identify areas for improvement.
- Update employee performance records with evaluation results and feedback.

## 4. Employee Promotion and Transfer

Performed By: HR Managers, Department Heads

Activity:
- Identify suitable candidates for promotion or transfer based on performance evaluations, skill sets, and department needs.
- Update employee records to reflect new job titles, departments, and pay rates following a successful promotion or transfer.

## 5. Employee Termination

Performed By: HR Managers, Department Heads

Activity:
- Initiate termination process for employees who no longer meet company requirements or have violated company policies.
- Update employee records to reflect termination date and final pay details.

## 6. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, HR Managers

Activity:
- Register home addresses by recording AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, SpatialLocation, rowguid, and ModifiedDate.
- Update employee addresses as needed, including changing the address type (e.g., home, work).

## 7. Employee Search

Performed By: Recruiters, HR Managers

Activity:
- Search for candidates based on searchString, useInflectional, useThesaurus, and language parameters to find suitable applicants for open positions.
