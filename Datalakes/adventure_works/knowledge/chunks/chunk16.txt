 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new products by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product details such as color, size, weight, and inventory levels.
- Set safety stock level and reorder point for each product.

## 2. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Hire new employees by recording their personal information, job title, hire date, and salary details.
- Assign employees to specific departments and shifts based on organizational needs.
- Update employee records when they change departments or shifts.

## 3. Employee Payroll Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Record employee pay history, including rate changes and pay frequencies.
- Calculate vacation hours and sick leave hours for each employee.

## 4. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Register and update personal or business addresses associated with employees or customers.
- Specify the type of address (e.g., home, work) and its spatial location.

## 5. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Monitor inventory levels for each product and update them as items are sold or manufactured.
- Generate purchase orders or work orders when inventory reaches the reorder point.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Receive customer orders and verify their details.
- Pick, pack, and ship ordered items to customers.
- Update order status and track delivery progress.

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Finance Department

Activity:
- Generate invoices for completed customer orders based on product list price and quantity sold.
- Record invoice details in the system and send them to customers.

## 8. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Record financial transactions, including sales, expenses, and payroll costs.
- Generate reports and analyze financial data for decision making and compliance purposes.
