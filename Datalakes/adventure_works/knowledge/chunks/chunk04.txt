 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and department assignments.
- HR managers update employee information as needed, modifying the relevant fields in the Employee table.
- HR managers track employee movements within the organization by recording StartDate, EndDate, DepartmentID, and ShiftID in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- HR managers manage employee pay history by updating RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 2. Address Management

Performed By: Person Address Administrators

Activity:
- Address administrators add new addresses for employees or customers by recording AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation in the PersonAddress table.
- Address administrators update employee or customer addresses as needed, modifying the relevant fields in the PersonAddress table.
- Address administrators assign address types to each address by recording AddressTypeID in the PersonBusinessEntityAddress table.

## 3. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers and Warehouse Staff (for location-specific assignments)

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to departments, recording DepartmentID in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Warehouse staff assign employees to specific locations within the warehouse, updating ShiftID in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table as needed.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff and Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by recording ProductID, LocationID, OnHandQty, and AvailableQty in the Production.ProductInventory table.
- Purchasing teams order new products based on inventory levels, updating the relevant fields in the Sales.SalesOrderHeader and Sales.SalesOrderDetail tables.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives and Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by recording ProductID, Quantity, UnitPrice, and ModifiedDate in the Sales.SalesOrderHeader and Sales.SalesOrderDetail tables.
- Customer service agents handle order modifications or cancellations, updating the relevant fields in the Sales.SalesOrderHeader table.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department generates invoices by recording OrderID, DueDate, and TotalDue in the Sales.SalesInvoiceHeader table.

## 7. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Agents and Finance Department

Activity:
- Customer service agents process customer payments by updating the relevant fields in the Sales.CreditCard and Sales.PersonCreditCard tables.
- Finance department records payment details, updating the relevant fields in the Sales.SalesInvoicePayment table.

## 8. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts and Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and employee productivity by querying the relevant tables (e.g., Sales.SalesOrderHeader, Production.ProductInventory, HumanResources.Employee).
- Management reviews these reports to make informed decisions about business strategy and resource allocation.
