 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Model Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Technical Illustrators, Translators

Activity:
- Product managers register new product models by recording the name and detailed catalog information in xml format. (ProductModelID, Name, CatalogDescription)
- Technical illustrators upload visual representations of the product model. (IllustrationID)
- Translators provide product descriptions in various languages for each product model. (CultureID, ProductDescriptionID)

## 2. Product Model Illustration Management

Performed By: Graphic Designers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Graphic designers create and update illustrations for the product models. (IllustrationID)
- IT administrators link each illustration to its corresponding product model. (ProductModelID, IllustrationID)

## 3. Production Model Product Description Management

Performed By: Translators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Translators provide product descriptions in various languages for each product model. (CultureID, ProductDescriptionID)
- Content managers link the translated descriptions to their corresponding product models and product descriptions. (ProductModelID, ProductDescriptionID, CultureID)

## 4. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Payroll Specialists

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their personal information, job title, and employment details. (BusinessEntityID, NationalIDNumber, LoginID, OrganizationNode, OrganizationLevel, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag)
- Payroll specialists update employee pay history with rate changes and frequency. (BusinessEntityID, RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency)

## 5. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to departments and shifts. (DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate)
- Warehouse staff track employee department changes. (BusinessEntityID, DepartmentID, ShiftID, ModifiedDate)

## 6. Address Management

Performed By: Employees, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Employees provide their personal addresses for business purposes. (AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode)
- IT administrators store and manage employee addresses. (AddressID, rowguid, ModifiedDate)

## 7. Product Model Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Inventory managers update the quantity of each product model in stock. (ProductModelID)
- Warehouse staff track product model inventory levels and changes. (BusinessEntityID, DepartmentID, ProductModelID, ModifiedDate)
