 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- HR managers update employee information by modifying the relevant fields in the HumanResources Employee table.
- HR managers assign employees to departments by recording DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- HR managers manage employee pay history by updating RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency in the HumanResources EmployeePayHistory table.

## 2. Address Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Vendors, and Employees

Activity:
- Individuals provide their street address information by filling out the columns AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode in the Person.Address table.
- Individuals update their street address information by modifying the relevant fields in the Person.Address table.

## 3. Contact Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Vendors, and Employees

Activity:
- Individuals provide their contact details by filling out the columns rowguid, ModifiedDate in the Person BusinessEntityContact table.

## 4. Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to departments based on job roles and department availability.
- Warehouse staff manage department assignments for warehouse employees.

## 5. Shift Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers define shifts by recording ShiftID, Name, StartTime, EndTime in the HumanResources Shift table.
- Warehouse staff assign employees to shifts based on work schedules and shift availability.

## 6. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices for customers by recording relevant details in the database.
- Accounting staff review and process customer invoices by updating their status and payment details.

## 7. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by providing order details such as product code, quantity, and shipping address.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and update the relevant information in the database.

## 8. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff manage inventory levels by updating stock quantities for each product.
- Purchasing teams order new products based on inventory levels and demand forecasts.

## 9. Error Logging

Performed By: System Administrators

Activity:
- System administrators log errors that occur in the system by recording relevant details in the dbo.ErrorLog table.
