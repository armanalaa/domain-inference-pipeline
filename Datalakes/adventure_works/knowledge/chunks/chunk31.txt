 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Sales Order Creation

Performed By: Sales Person

Activity:
- Registers new sales orders by entering customer details, order items, and shipping information (SalesOrderID, CustomerID, BillToAddressID, ShipToAddressID, ShippingMethodID).
- Records the sales subtotal, tax amount, and shipping cost (SubTotal, TaxAmt, Freight).
- Assigns an order number and status (OrderNumber, OrderDate, Status).
- Adds comments related to the order (Comment).

## 2. Customer Onboarding

Performed By: HR Manager

Activity:
- Creates new customer accounts by entering financial accounting numbers (AccountNumber).
- Assigns a customer identification number (CustomerID).

## 3. Employee Hiring

Performed By: HR Manager

Activity:
- Registers new employees by recording personal details, employment history, and job information (BusinessEntityID, NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag).
- Assigns an organizational role and level (OrganizationNode, OrganizationLevel).

## 4. Employee Department Assignment

Performed By: HR Manager

Activity:
- Assigns employees to departments by specifying department ID, shift ID, start date, and end date (EmployeeDepartmentHistory).

## 5. Employee Salary Management

Performed By: Finance

Activity:
- Updates employee salaries by entering rate change dates, rates, pay frequencies, and modifies the corresponding dates (EmployeePayHistory).

## 6. Product Registration

Performed By: Product Manager

Activity:
- Registers new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (Product).

## 7. Order Approval

Performed By: Sales Manager

Activity:
- Reviews and approves sales orders based on status (OrderDate, Status).

## 8. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Tracks inventory levels by recording product ID and quantities (ProductID, OrderQty).

## 9. Shipping Process

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Prepares orders for shipping by specifying ship date (ShipDate).

## 10. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance

Activity:
- Generates invoices based on total due from customer (TotalDue).
