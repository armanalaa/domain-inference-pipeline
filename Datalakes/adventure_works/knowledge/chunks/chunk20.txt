 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- Assign employees to departments by specifying DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, and EndDate in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Record employee pay history by entering RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 2. Employee Information Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Update employee information such as AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and ModifiedDate in the PersonAddress table.
- Record changes to employee addresses by specifying BusinessEntityID, AddressID, AddressTypeID, rowguid, and ModifiedDate in the PersonBusinessEntityAddress table.
- Update employee contact information by entering BusinessEnityID, EmailAddress, and ModifiedDate in the PersonBusinessEntityContact table.

## 3. Department Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Create or modify departments by recording DepartmentID, Name, GroupName, and ModifiedDate in the HumanResources Department table.

## 4. Shift Scheduling

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Define shift details like ShiftID, Name, StartTime, EndTime, and ModifiedDate in the HumanResources Shift table.

## 5. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.

## 6. Product Image Management

Performed By: IT Staff

Activity:
- Upload small and large images of products to the ThumbNailPhoto and LargePhoto columns in the Production.ProductPhoto table respectively.

## 7. Product Photo Assignment

Performed By: IT Staff

Activity:
- Designate principal product photos by setting the appropriate bit value for each record in the Production.ProductProductPhoto table.

## 8. Customer Review Collection

Performed By: Customers

Activity:
- Submit reviews of purchased products, including ReviewerName, ReviewDate, EmailAddress, Rating, and ModifiedDate in the Production.ProductReview table.
