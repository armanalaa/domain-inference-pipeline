 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Department Management

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers assign departments to employees by inputting department IDs in the HumanResources.EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- HR managers update employee department assignments by modifying the EndDate column in the HumanResources.EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- HR managers create new departments by entering department details like name and group name into the HumanResources.Department table.

## 2. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal information such as name, birthdate, gender, marital status, and hire date in the HumanResources.Employee table.
- HR managers assign employees to departments by linking department IDs from the HumanResources.Department table to the HumanResources.EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Warehouse staff issue equipment to new employees by recording equipment details like item code, name, and category in the Production.ProductInventory table and updating the EmployeeID column accordingly.

## 3. Employee Salary Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance

Activity:
- HR managers update employee salaries by modifying the Rate column in the HumanResources.EmployeePayHistory table on specific rate change dates.
- Finance records employee salary payments by referencing the BusinessEntityID from the HumanResources.Employee table in the Financial.TransactionHistory table.

## 4. Employee Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers update employee addresses by recording address details like line1, city, state, and postal code in the Person.Address table and linking them to the corresponding BusinessEntityID from the HumanResources.Employee table.
- Employees update their personal addresses by providing new address information which is then recorded by HR managers as mentioned above.

## 5. Employee Performance Evaluation

Performed By: HR Managers, Supervisors

Activity:
- Supervisors evaluate employee performance and provide feedback to HR managers who record the evaluations in the HumanResources.EmployeePerformanceEvaluation table.

## 6. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating item quantities in the Production.ProductInventory table.
- Sales representatives update order statuses by referencing the OrderID from the Sales.SalesOrderHeader table in the Sales.SalesOrderDetail table.

## 7. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by recording customer details, item codes, quantities, and prices in the Sales.SalesOrderHeader and Sales.SalesOrderDetail tables.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for orders by updating the OnOrderQty column in the Production.ProductInventory table.
- Shipping clerks prepare shipments by recording shipping details like carrier, tracking number, and delivery date in the Sales.ShipMethods table and linking them to the corresponding OrderID from the Sales.SalesOrderHeader table.
