 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- New hires are assigned a unique ID by HR managers. (HumanResources Employee)
- Personal details of the new employee are recorded, including name, birthdate, and marital status. (HumanResources Employee)
- The employee's job title is specified, along with their department and level in the organizational hierarchy. (HumanResources Employee, HumanResources Department)
- The hiring date is logged, and the new employee's salary status is determined. (HumanResources Employee)
- The new hire's personal address is registered for future communication purposes. (Person Address)

## 2. Employee Departure Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- HR managers mark the current employment status of an employee as inactive when they leave the company. (HumanResources Employee)
- The end date of the employee's tenure is recorded, and their employment history is updated accordingly. (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory)

## 3. Employee Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Changes to an employee's address are logged by HR managers or warehouse staff. (Person BusinessEntityAddress)

## 4. Employee Contact Information Update

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers update the contact information of employees, such as phone numbers and email addresses. (Person BusinessEntityContact)

## 5. Employee Department Assignment & Transfer

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR managers assign new departments to employees or transfer them between departments. (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory)

## 6. Employee Salary Adjustment

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers collaborate with the finance department to adjust an employee's salary based on performance or company policy changes. (HumanResources EmployeePayHistory)

## 7. Document Management for Product Maintenance

Performed By: Engineers, Technical Writers

Activity:
- Engineers and technical writers create and manage documents related to product maintenance. (Production.Document)

## 8. Bicycle Assembly Diagram Creation

Performed By: Designers, Illustrators

Activity:
- Designers and illustrators create diagrams for bicycle assembly instructions. (Production.Illustration)

## 9. Product Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives manage the sale of products, while purchasing teams handle inventory replenishment and warehouse staff track product availability. (HumanResources Employee, Production.Product)
