 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, and CurrentFlag.
- Assign employees to departments by selecting a DepartmentID and ShiftID.
- Record employee's address information including AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and primary contact details such as EmailAddressID and ContactTypeID.

## 2. Employee Departure Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Update employee's end date in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table when an employee leaves a department.
- Record any changes to an employee's pay history, including RateChangeDate and Rate, as well as PayFrequency updates.

## 3. Contact Type Management

Performed By: Data Administrators

Activity:
- Maintain the list of contact types by adding or updating ContactTypeID and Name.

## 4. Country/Region Code Management

Performed By: Data Administrators

Activity:
- Add or update country and region codes in the CountryRegion table, including CountryRegionCode and Name.

## 5. Email Address Management

Performed By: Data Administrators

Activity:
- Assign email addresses to employees by recording BusinessEntityID, EmailAddress, and ModifiedDate.

## 6. Password Management

Performed By: IT Administrators

Activity:
- Store hashed passwords for employee email accounts by recording BusinessEntityID, PasswordHash, PasswordSalt, and ModifiedDate.

## 7. Person Data Management

Performed By: Data Administrators

Activity:
- Record primary person data including BusinessEntityID, rowguid, and ModifiedDate.
