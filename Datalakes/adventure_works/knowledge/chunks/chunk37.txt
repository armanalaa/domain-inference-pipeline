 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their personal details (NationalIDNumber, LoginID, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate), job title (JobTitle), and employment status (SalariedFlag).
- Update employee information when necessary, such as changes in marital status or job title.
- Track employee history within the organization by recording department transitions (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory) and pay history (HumanResources EmployeePayHistory).

## 2. Department Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Create and manage departments within the organization (DepartmentID, Name, GroupName, ModifiedDate).
- Assign employees to specific departments (HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory).

## 3. Address Management

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Record employee addresses in the Person Address table, including address lines (AddressLine1, AddressLine2), city, state, postal code, and country.
- Assign a type to each address (Person AddressType).

## 4. Store Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Store Managers

Activity:
- Register new stores by providing store details (BusinessEntityID, Name, Sales Person ID, Demographics).
- Update store information when necessary (ModifiedDate).

## 5. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Identify and register individual customers (vIndividualCustomer) by recording their names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, and demographic information.
- Update customer details when necessary.

## 6. Sales Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Record sales transactions for each store (SalesOrderDetail).
- Associate special offers with products (SpecialOfferIDProductID).

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Generate invoices based on recorded sales transactions (SalesOrderDetail).

## 8. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Record financial transactions, including employee salaries and store sales (HumanResources EmployeePayHistory, SalesOrderDetail).
- Maintain a record of the company's financial status (vPersonDemographics).
