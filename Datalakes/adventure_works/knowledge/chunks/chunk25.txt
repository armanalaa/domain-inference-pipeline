 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- HR managers update employee information such as MaritalStatus, Gender, or HireDate when necessary.
- HR managers track employee department changes by recording the DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 2. Employee Payroll Management

Performed By: Finance and HR Managers

Activity:
- Finance records employees' pay history including RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate.
- HR managers update employee salaries when necessary.

## 3. Work Order Creation

Performed By: Production Managers

Activity:
- Production managers create work orders by recording the WorkOrderID, ProductID, OperationSequence, LocationID, ScheduledStartDate, ScheduledEndDate, ActualStartDate, ActualEndDate, ActualResourceHrs, PlannedCost, and ActualCost.

## 4. Work Order Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track work order progress by updating the ActualStartDate, ActualEndDate, and ActualResourceHrs as work is completed.

## 5. Vendor Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams record vendor details such as BusinessEntityID, AverageLeadTime, StandardPrice, LastReceiptCost, LastReceiptDate, MinOrderQty, MaxOrderQty, OnOrderQty, UnitMeasureCode, and ModifiedDate.

## 6. Product Vendor Mapping

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams map vendors to the products they supply by recording the ProductID and BusinessEntityID in the ProductVendor table.

## 7. Purchase Order Creation

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams create purchase orders by recording the PurchaseOrderID, PurchaseOrderDetailID, DueDate, OrderQty, ProductID, UnitPrice, LineTotal, ReceivedQty, and ModifiedDate.

## 8. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the quantity of products in stock by recording the received Qty when new products are received.
