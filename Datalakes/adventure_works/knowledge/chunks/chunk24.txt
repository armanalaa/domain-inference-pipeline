 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers

Activity:
- Register new HR employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- Update employee information as needed, modifying the relevant fields such as MaritalStatus, Gender, or ModifiedDate.
- Assign employees to departments by recording DepartmentID in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Track employee salary changes by recording RateChangeDate, Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 2. Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Person BusinessEntityAddress administrators

Activity:
- Register employee addresses by recording AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, and SpatialLocation in the Person Address table.
- Define address types for employees (e.g., home, work) by recording Name in the Person AddressType table.
- Associate employee addresses with their respective address types by recording BusinessEntityID, AddressID, AddressTypeID, and ModifiedDate in the Person BusinessEntityContact table.

## 3. Employee Department History Management

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- Record employee department changes by documenting DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in the HumanResources EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.

## 4. Shift Management

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- Define shifts for employees by recording Name, StartTime, EndTime, and ModifiedDate in the HumanResources Shift table.

## 5. Product Scrap Reason Management

Performed By: Manufacturing managers

Activity:
- Document manufacturing failure reasons by recording the name of each reason in the Production.ScrapReason table.

## 6. Transaction History Management

Performed By: Sales representatives, Purchasing teams, and Work Order staff

Activity:
- Record transactions for purchase orders, sales orders, or work orders by documenting ProductID, ReferenceOrderID, ReferenceOrderLineID, TransactionDate, TransactionType, Quantity, ActualCost, and ModifiedDate in the Production.TransactionHistory table.

## 7. Archived Transaction History Management

Performed By: Data administrators

Activity:
- Archive transaction history from previous years by moving records to the Production.TransactionHistoryArchive table.

## 8. Work Order Management

Performed By: Manufacturing staff

Activity:
- Create manufacturing work orders by recording WorkOrderID, ProductID, OrderQty, StockedQty, ScrappedQty, StartDate, EndDate, DueDate, ScrapReasonID, and ModifiedDate in the Production.WorkOrder table.
