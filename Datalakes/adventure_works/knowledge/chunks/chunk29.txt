 # adventure_works — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources Managers

Activity:
- Register new employees by recording their NationalIDNumber, LoginID, JobTitle, BirthDate, MaritalStatus, Gender, HireDate, SalariedFlag, VacationHours, SickLeaveHours, CurrentFlag, and rowguid.
- Update employee information as needed, modifying the relevant fields such as MaritalStatus, Gender, or HireDate.
- Assign employees to departments by recording their BusinessEntityID, DepartmentID, ShiftID, StartDate, EndDate, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeeDepartmentHistory table.
- Record any changes in employee pay history by updating the Rate, PayFrequency, and ModifiedDate in the EmployeePayHistory table.

## 2. Address Management

Performed By: IT Administrators, Employees

Activity:
- Register personal addresses by recording AddressLine1, AddressLine2, City, StateProvinceID, PostalCode, SpatialLocation, rowguid, and ModifiedDate in the PersonAddress table.
- Associate an address with a specific employee by linking their BusinessEntityID to the AddressID, AddressTypeID, and ModifiedDate in the PersonBusinessEntityAddress table.

## 3. Contact Information Management

Performed By: Employees

Activity:
- Register personal contact information by recording it in the PersonBusinessEntityContact table.

## 4. Currency Management

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Maintain a list of standard ISO currencies by recording their CurrencyCode and Name, as well as the ModifiedDate, in the Sales.Currency table.

## 5. Exchange Rate Management

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Obtain exchange rates for various currencies on specific dates and record them as FromCurrencyCode, ToCurrencyCode, AverageRate, EndOfDayRate, CurrencyRateDate, and ModifiedDate in the Sales.CurrencyRate table.

## 6. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Register new customers by recording their CustomerID, CreditCardID (if applicable), and other relevant details.

## 7. Credit Card Management

Performed By: IT Administrators

Activity:
- Store credit card information for customers by recording the CardType, CardNumber, ExpMonth, ExpYear, and ModifiedDate in the Sales.CreditCard table.

## 8. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Place new orders on behalf of customers by recording relevant details such as order items, shipping information, and payment methods.
- Track inventory levels to ensure timely fulfillment of orders.

## 9. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounting Department

Activity:
- Generate invoices based on completed orders and record any necessary financial details.
