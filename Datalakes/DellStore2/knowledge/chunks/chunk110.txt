 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product when items are received or shipped.
- Product managers monitor sales data from the sales table to determine which products need reordering.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income into the customers table during account creation.
- HR managers update the customers table with changes to a customer's personal information or credit card details.
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantities, and providing their order and billing information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders in the orders table when a customer places an order.
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table as they pick and pack items for each order.
- Finance team calculates the netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order and updates the orders table accordingly.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table when items are received or shipped, adjusting the quan_in_stock column as needed.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on reorder information from the reorder table and current inventory levels.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick items for each order, pack them, and prepare shipping labels.
- Warehouse staff update the orderlines table with the quantity of each product shipped for each order.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team generates invoices based on the totalamount column from the orders table and sends them to customers.

## 7. Data Analysis

Performed By: Analytics Team, Management

Activity:
- Analytics team extracts data from various tables for analysis, such as sales trends, customer behavior, and inventory performance.
- Management reviews the findings and makes decisions based on the insights gained from the data analysis.
