 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update product details as necessary, including actor, common_prod_id, and special.
- Inventory team tracks quan_in_stock for each product to ensure availability.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product information and handling order details.
- Customer service team updates customer records with contact information, credit card details, age, income, gender, and region.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Order Processing Team

Activity:
- Order processing team creates new orders based on customer requests from the orderlines table.
- They calculate netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Team

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve products as requested, updating quan_in_stock accordingly.
- Inventory team monitors sales to forecast future product demand and reorder when necessary.

## 5. Reordering

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams place orders for new inventory based on the date_low and quan_low from the reorder table.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Data Analysts, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze customer histories using the cust_hist table to identify trends and opportunities.
- Sales representatives use this information to personalize customer interactions and improve sales.

## 7. Data Documentation

Performed By: Data Analysts, IT Department

Activity:
- Data analysts document all tables and columns in the DellStore2 database for easier understanding and querying.
- The IT department ensures that this documentation is up-to-date and accessible to relevant personnel.
