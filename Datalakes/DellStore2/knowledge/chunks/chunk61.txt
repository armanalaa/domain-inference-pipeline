 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or existing ones are sold.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, providing product information and handling orders.
- Customer service agents assist existing customers with inquiries, returns, or complaints.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers based on their preferences and inventory availability.
- Warehouse staff prepare and ship the ordered items to the customer's specified address.
- The finance department records the order details, calculates the net amount, tax, and total amount.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when products are sold or received.
- Purchasing teams order new stock when the quantity in stock falls below a predetermined threshold.

## 5. Reordering and Forecasting

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor inventory levels and reorder products based on historical sales data and forecasted demand.
- Data analysts analyze sales trends to improve inventory management and forecasting accuracy.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Marketing Department, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- The marketing department uses customer history data to segment customers for targeted promotions.
- Sales representatives access customer history to understand each customer's preferences and purchasing behavior.

## 7. Account Management

Performed By: Finance Department, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- The finance department manages customer accounts, including credit card information, billing, and payments.
- Customer service agents assist customers with account-related inquiries or issues.
