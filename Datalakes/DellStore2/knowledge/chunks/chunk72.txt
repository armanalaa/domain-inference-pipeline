 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received or shipped.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for promotional offers or similar products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details like firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and age during account creation.
- Customer service agents update contact information as needed.
- HR managers assign username, "password", creditcardexpiration, and other personal data when onboarding new employees.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and order dates from the catalog.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate orderids.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to prepare items for shipment.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on expected sales.
- Finance records netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when products are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams monitor date_low and quan_low to initiate reorders.
- Inventory staff record date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected for each reorder.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items based on orderlines.
- Shipping teams generate shipping labels and track orders until delivery.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards assign ownership to specific people or teams for each table, column, and dataset.
- IT administrators ensure proper documentation and accountability across the organization.

## 7. Impact Analysis

Performed By: Database Administrators, Developers

Activity:
- Before making database changes, database administrators use impact analysis to see every report, dashboard, and ETL job that uses affected columns or tables.
