 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update product details as needed, including title, actor, common_prod_id, and special.
- IT staff maintain the relationship between common_prod_id and prod_id for efficient data management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username.
- HR managers update customer details such as age, income, gender, and creditcardexpiration.
- Customers manage their own accounts by changing password and accessing personal information.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product (quan_in_stock) and update it as products are sold or received.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory levels based on date_low and quan_low.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate orderids.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment based on orderlines and inventory levels.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered products according to orderlines and shipment details.
- Shipping teams arrange for delivery of orders based on customer address and preferred shipping method.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance records netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table.
- Finance generates invoices and tracks payments from customers.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor inventory levels and update date_expected based on reorder history and sales trends.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish products with quan_reordered when the quantity falls below the reorder point (quan_low).

## 8. Data Analysis & Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business analysts extract and analyze data from the database to generate reports and insights on sales, inventory, customer behavior, and more.
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about product offerings, marketing strategies, and operational efficiency.
