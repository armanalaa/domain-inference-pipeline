 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set quan_low for each item to trigger reordering when stock falls below this level.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, and creditcard information during account creation.
- Customer service agents update customer records with new contact information or address changes.
- HR managers maintain employee data related to age, income, gender, and region in the customers table for internal reporting purposes.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by linking customerid, orderdate, and netamount.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient stock for each order.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.
- Finance records totalamount and tax for each order in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update sales when products are sold to customers.
- Purchasing teams monitor sales data to determine reorder quantities and expected delivery dates.

## 5. Reordering

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams place new orders for products with quan_reordered when the date_low is reached.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock and date_expected upon receipt of reordered items.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- The data governance team maintains a shared business glossary to ensure consistent terminology across the organization.
- They use this glossary to understand and locate customer data across all systems for analysis and reporting purposes.
