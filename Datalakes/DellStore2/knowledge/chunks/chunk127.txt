 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact details and credit card information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes in contact or billing information.
- Sales representatives assign each customer a unique username and password for account access.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording their product code, name, category, list price, and special features.
- Inventory specialists update product information when stock levels change or products go on sale.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar items for easier categorization and tracking.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.
- Sales representatives process orders by confirming availability, calculating totals, and recording order dates.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory and package them for shipping.
- Shipping agents generate shipping labels and arrange for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists track stock levels by updating quantities in real time as items are sold or received.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for reordering low-stock products based on predefined thresholds.

## 6. Financial Transactions

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance department records net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order in the financial system.
- Accounting staff generates invoices based on recorded transactions and sends them to customers.

## 7. License Agreement Management

Performed By: Legal Department, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Legal department reviews and approves license agreements for new customers.
- Sales representatives distribute signed agreements to customers and update customer records accordingly.

## 8. Technical Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, IT Staff

Activity:
- Customer service agents assist customers with technical issues related to the Products.
- IT staff troubleshoot and resolve any system-related problems that may arise.
