 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data analysts update product information based on market trends and customer feedback.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar products for easier categorization.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives reach out to potential customers via email or phone calls to promote products and offers.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries regarding orders, returns, or product information.
- Customer service agents update customer profiles with new contact details or preferences.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing complex or large orders.
- Orders are automatically generated with order IDs, order dates, customer IDs, net amounts, taxes, and total amounts.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product and update inventory records accordingly.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low-stock items based on sales data and reorder points.
- Inventory records are updated with new quantities and sales data.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on order line IDs, product IDs, and quantities.
- Shipping teams prepare shipments for delivery by printing shipping labels and arranging packages for pickup or dropoff.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions related to orders, such as net amounts, taxes, and total amounts.
- Finance teams generate invoices based on order data and send them to customers.
- Finance teams update customer profiles with payment history and credit card information.

## 7. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Teams

Activity:
- Data analysts extract and analyze sales, inventory, and financial data from the database for reporting purposes.
- Business intelligence teams create reports and dashboards to visualize key performance indicators (KPIs) for various stakeholders.
- Reports are shared with relevant teams to inform decision-making and identify areas for improvement.
