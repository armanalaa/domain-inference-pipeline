 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Support Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact details and demographic information (customers).
- Customer support agents update customer records with any changes to contact or personal information (customers).
- Sales representatives assign each customer a unique username and password for account access (customers).

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product code, name, category, list price, and other relevant details (products).
- Inventory specialists update product information when necessary, such as changing prices or categories (products).
- Inventory specialists track inventory levels for each product and update the quan_in_stock column accordingly (inventory).

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog, select products, and place orders by specifying quantities and providing shipping information (orders, orderlines, customers).
- Sales representatives assist customers with placing orders, ensuring accurate product selection and addressing any issues that may arise (orders, orderlines, customers).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory based on the orderline details (inventory, orderlines).
- Inventory specialists update the quan_in_stock column for each product as it is shipped to the customer (inventory, orderlines).

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff package ordered products and prepare them for shipment (orders, orderlines).
- Shipping agents coordinate with carriers to arrange delivery of orders to customers (orders, orderlines).

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products when inventory levels reach a predetermined threshold (reorder).
- Inventory specialists receive and record deliveries of new stock, updating the quan_in_stock column accordingly (inventory).

## 7. Sales Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Finance Teams, Management

Activity:
- Finance teams generate sales reports by querying netamount, tax, totalamount columns from orders table (orders).
- Management reviews sales reports to identify trends, set goals, and make strategic decisions (orders).

## 8. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: HR Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- HR managers maintain a record of each customer's order history for future reference (cust_hist).
- Sales representatives use the customer's order history to tailor recommendations and offers (cust_hist).
