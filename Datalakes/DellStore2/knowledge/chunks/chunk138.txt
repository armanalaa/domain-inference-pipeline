 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.
- Product managers set reorder points (date_low and quan_low) for each item to ensure timely restocking.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, capturing their contact information (email, phone, address).
- HR managers create customer accounts in the system, assigning unique customerids and setting credit card details.
- Customers browse the catalog, add items to their cart, and place orders by specifying quantities for each product.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers and generate orderids.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items, updating the inventory accordingly.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for any products not in stock.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- They generate invoices containing the order details and send them to customers via email.

## 5. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Teams

Activity:
- Customers pay their invoices using credit cards, transferring the necessary information (creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration) to the system.
- Finance teams record these payments and update the customer accounts accordingly.

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- When inventory levels drop below reorder points, warehouse staff initiate reordering by updating date_reordered and quan_reordered for the relevant products.
- Purchasing teams process these reorders, placing them with suppliers and tracking their progress.

## 7. Performance Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze sales data (orderlines, orders, inventory) to identify trends, best-selling products, and areas for improvement.
- They present their findings to management, who use this information to make strategic decisions about product offerings and marketing strategies.
