 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category specialists update product categories based on market trends and customer preferences.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar products for easier tracking and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter new customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card information, and age.
- Customer service agents update customer contact information when necessary.
- HR managers maintain customer demographic data like income and gender for marketing analysis.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product and update sales when items are sold.
- Purchasing teams order new products based on inventory levels and expected demand.
- Warehouse staff record the reordered quantity and expected delivery date in the reorder table.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by selecting products, quantities, and order dates.
- Customer service agents process customer requests for order changes or cancellations.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping once they are ready to be dispatched.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- They record these values in the orders table along with the order date.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards ensure data accuracy, completeness, and consistency across all tables.
- IT administrators manage database backups, security, and access controls.

## 7. Analytics & Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Business analysts extract and analyze data from the various tables to generate reports on sales performance, inventory levels, customer demographics, etc.
- Marketing managers use these reports to identify trends, make data-driven decisions, and optimize marketing strategies.
