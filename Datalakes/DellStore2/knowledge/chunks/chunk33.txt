 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data stewards maintain product information accuracy by updating common_prod_id, title, actor, price, and special when necessary.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts with firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username.
- HR managers update customer details such as age, income, gender, and creditcardexpiration.
- Customers manage their personal information through the account settings page.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives review and confirm orders before they are recorded in the orders table with orderid, orderdate, customerid, netamount, tax, and totalamount.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock for each product in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on date_low and quan_low from the reorder table.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products and pack them for shipping.
- Shipping teams generate shipping labels and update orderlines with orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams generate invoices based on the netamount, tax, and totalamount from the orders table.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards maintain a business glossary with terms, KPIs, policies, and rules linked to data assets.
- IT administrators ensure user authentication through enterprise identity systems like Active Directory, SAML, and OAuth2 for secure access to the platform.
