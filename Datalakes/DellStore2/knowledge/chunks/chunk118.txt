 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when they receive shipments or process returns.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for promotional offers or related products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details during the onboarding process, including firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income.
- Customers update their personal information or password through the account settings.
- HR managers verify and approve new customer accounts based on credit checks and compliance with company policies.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders for customers by specifying orderdate, netamount, tax, totalamount, and customerid in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient inventory for each orderline.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when stock levels are low based on reorder data.
- Finance records invoices and payments related to each order.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update sales when products are sold or returned.
- Purchasing teams monitor date_low and quan_low to initiate reorders.
- Warehouse staff record date_reordered and quan_reordered once a new shipment arrives.
- Purchasing teams update date_expected for future inventory replenishment.

## 5. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards define and manage key business terms in the glossary for consistent understanding.
- IT administrators configure Data Quality checks to ensure data accuracy and completeness.
- Data stewards schedule tasks such as Metadata Download Imports to maintain the data catalog.

## 6. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Sales Managers, Marketing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales managers generate sales reports based on orderlines and inventory data.
- Marketing teams analyze customer demographics and purchasing patterns for targeted promotions.
- Finance department uses financial data from orders to create budgets and forecasts.
