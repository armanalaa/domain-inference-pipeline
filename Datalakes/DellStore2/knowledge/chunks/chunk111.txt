 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category specialists update product categories to ensure accurate organization of products.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar items for easier tracking and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as name, address, contact information, credit card data, and account credentials during onboarding.
- Customer service agents update customer profiles with changes in personal or financial information.
- Data stewards ensure the accuracy of customer data by validating and verifying entries.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels by recording the quantity of each product in stock.
- Purchasing teams order new products based on sales data and low stock alerts.
- Inventory managers reorder items when quantities drop below a specified threshold.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by selecting products, specifying quantities, and calculating prices.
- Customers place orders through the online store or over the phone.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment by picking items from inventory and packaging them.

## 5. Order Fulfillment & Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items according to shipping instructions.
- Shipping agents label packages and arrange for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accountants

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on product prices and shipping costs.
- Accountants record financial transactions related to orders in the company's accounting system.

## 7. Data Governance & Compliance

Performed By: Data Stewards, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- Data stewards maintain accurate, consistent data by monitoring and improving data quality.
- Compliance officers ensure the organization meets regulatory requirements and maintains audit-ready records for internal audits and external regulators.
