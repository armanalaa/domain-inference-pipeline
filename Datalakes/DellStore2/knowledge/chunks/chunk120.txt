 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new products based on supplier agreements and inventory levels.
- Product managers update product details when necessary, such as changing prices or categories.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customers place orders by providing required information like shipping address, payment details, and product preferences.
- Customer service agents handle inquiries related to order status, returns, or refunds using customer contact information stored in the database.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory and prepare them for shipping based on order details.
- Finance department records each order's net amount, tax, and total amount in the orders table.
- Orderlines are created to track individual product quantities within an order.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when products are received or shipped out.
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and place reorders when the quantity falls below a specified threshold.
- The reorder table is updated with expected delivery dates and quantities.

## 5. Sales Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department, Management

Activity:
- Finance department calculates sales for each product by summing up the sales column in the inventory table.
- Management analyzes sales reports to identify trends, best-selling products, and areas for improvement.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives access customer history to understand a customer's purchasing behavior and tailor recommendations accordingly.
- Marketing team uses customer data to segment the audience for targeted promotions and campaigns.
