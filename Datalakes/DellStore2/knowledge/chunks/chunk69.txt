 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set special or common_prod_id for promotional offers or similar items.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details like firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and age into the customers table during registration.
- Customer service agents update contact information when customers request changes.
- HR managers record income, gender, and region for employee profiles in the customers table.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and record netamount, tax, and totalamount in the orders table.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory based on orderlines.
- Shipping agents pack and label orders for delivery, updating orderdate in the orders table.

## 5. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are sold or returned.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on reorder data.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff, Accountants

Activity:
- Finance staff record sales transactions in the inventory and orders tables.
- Accountants calculate tax and totalamount for each order.

## 7. Audit & Compliance

Performed By: Data Governance Team, IT Security Personnel

Activity:
- Data governance team documents sensitive data using custom fields and classification tags in the customers, orders, inventory, and products tables.
- IT security personnel ensure compliance with regulations like GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA by monitoring data movement across systems.
