 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on predicted demand and inventory levels.
- Product managers update product details when necessary, such as changing the price or adding a special offer.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, providing product information and assisting with orders.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries about orders, returns, or account details.
- New customers are registered by sales representatives, who input their personal and financial information into the customers table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders for customers based on their preferences and available inventory.
- Warehouse staff track order fulfillment by picking items from stock and preparing them for shipment.
- The finance department records the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table when items are added or removed from stock.
- Purchasing teams monitor low stock levels and reorder products using the reorder table.

## 5. Order Line Item Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff create order line items by associating each product with a quantity in an order.
- Sales representatives update order line items if a customer requests changes to their order.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Marketing Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives record customer orders and interactions in the cust_hist table for future reference.
- The marketing department analyzes customer history data to identify trends and opportunities for targeted promotions.

## 7. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Governance Team

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure the security and integrity of DellStore2's data by implementing access controls and backups.
- The data governance team monitors data lineage and quality to maintain regulatory compliance and data accuracy.
