 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update existing item details as needed, such as changing the title or adjusting the price.
- IT administrators ensure the product catalog is accessible to authorized users and maintain its overall integrity.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information, including name, address, contact details, credit card information, and account preferences.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any changes to personal or billing information.
- HR managers maintain employee records related to customers, such as age, income, and gender for demographic analysis.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.
- Sales representatives review and confirm customer orders before they are processed further.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory levels when products are received or shipped, adjusting the quantity in stock accordingly.
- Purchasing teams monitor low inventory levels and initiate reorders for restocking purposes.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on the orderlines and ship them to the customer's specified address.
- Sales representatives update the order status in the system when an order is shipped.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate the net amount for each order based on the product prices and quantities.
- Accounting staff record the total amount for each order, including taxes and any applicable fees.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators catalog all data sources, including databases, ETLs, BI tools, data lakes, applications, and cloud & on-premises data sources and catalogs like Databricks, Cloudera, and Microsoft Purview.
- Data analysts use the centralized repository to monitor data quality, define bespoke data quality rules, and perform continuous monitoring for quick issue detection and trend analysis.
