 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives update customer contact information by modifying the firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and creditcardexpiration columns in the customers table.
- Sales representatives register new customers by entering their details into the customers table, including customerid, age, income, gender, and creating a username and password for them.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price in the products table.
- Inventory managers update the quan_in_stock column for a specific product in the inventory table when stock levels change.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by entering customerid, orderdate, netamount, tax, and totalamount into the orders table.
- Warehouse staff update the quan_in_stock column in the inventory table when an order is shipped, reducing the quantity available for that product.

## 4. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record each product included in an order by adding a new row to the orderlines table with orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.

## 5. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products that are running low by checking the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table and placing orders accordingly.
- Inventory managers update the quan_reordered column in the reorder table when a new order is placed, indicating the quantity to be replenished.

## 6. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate sales reports by querying the orders and inventory tables for total sales, best-selling products, and average order value.
- Management reviews the sales reports to make informed decisions about pricing strategies, marketing efforts, and inventory management.
