 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data stewards document business and technical data elements for each product, including title, actor, common_prod_id, and special attributes.

## 2. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product and update it accordingly as items are sold or received.
- Inventory managers set reorder points based on quan_low and date_low for each product to ensure timely restocking.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details.
- Customer service agents confirm order placement, provide order status updates, and handle any issues that arise during the transaction.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory and prepare them for shipment.
- Shipping clerks package and label orders, then coordinate with carriers to arrange delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 5. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Sales representatives maintain relationships with existing customers by following up on orders, offering promotions, and addressing any concerns.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries, complaints, and returns to ensure a positive shopping experience.
- Data stewards collect and organize metadata related to customer interactions for analysis and improvement of the sales process.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Accounting Staff, Finance Managers

Activity:
- Accounting staff record transactions such as netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table.
- Finance managers analyze financial data to monitor revenue, expenses, and profitability of the business.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards ensure data remains usable and trustworthy by defining standards, monitoring compliance, and documenting metadata across all tables in DellStore2.
- IT administrators maintain the database infrastructure, perform backups, and troubleshoot any issues that arise.
