 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data stewards ensure product information is accurate and up-to-date by reviewing and updating product details as needed.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, providing product recommendations based on their interests and preferences.
- Customer service agents assist existing customers with inquiries, troubleshoot issues, and process returns or refunds (cust_hist).

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm orders with customers, providing order details such as order date, customer ID, and net amount.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure items are in stock before shipping orders to customers.
- Finance team records the total amount for each order, including tax, and updates the customer's account accordingly.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table with the quantity of each product in stock (inventory).
- Purchasing teams monitor low stock levels and issue purchase orders for reordering when necessary.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack customer orders based on the orderlines table.
- The shipping department labels and ships the packages to the customer's specified address.

## 6. Reordering and Supply Chain Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams reorder products based on the reorder table, which includes information such as product ID, date low, and quantity low.
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table with the new quantities once the reordered items are received.

## 7. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: Data Stewards, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- Data stewards maintain accurate, consistent data by monitoring and improving data quality using tools like Data Profiling and Data Lineage (Dataedo).
- Compliance officers ensure the organization meets regulatory requirements and maintains audit-ready records for internal audits and external regulators.
