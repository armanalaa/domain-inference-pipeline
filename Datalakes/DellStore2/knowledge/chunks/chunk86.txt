 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when receiving shipments of newly registered products.
- Product managers review sales data from orderlines to identify best-selling products and adjust pricing accordingly.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details into the customers table during account creation or updates.
- Customer service agents assist customers with placing orders by verifying their information and shipping addresses.
- HR managers update creditcard, creditcardexpiration, username, and "password" when onboarding new employees.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders by linking customerid, orderdate, and product details from the customers and products tables.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient stock for each order.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels are low based on reorder data.
- Finance records netamount, tax, and totalamount in the orders table upon order completion.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when shipping products to customers or receiving new inventory.
- Inventory managers monitor sales and reorder data to forecast future demand and adjust inventory levels accordingly.

## 5. Reporting & Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract and analyze data from the orders, orderlines, customers, products, and inventory tables for performance reports.
- Management reviews these reports to make informed decisions about pricing strategies, marketing efforts, and operational efficiency.
