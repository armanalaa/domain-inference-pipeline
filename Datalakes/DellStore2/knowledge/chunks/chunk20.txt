 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their details (firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, username).
- Customer service agents update customer information (email, phone) when contacted.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific regions (region).

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update product details (title, actor, price) as needed.
- Product managers mark common products (common_prod_id) for cross-selling opportunities.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details (customerid, orderdate).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order availability (quan_in_stock) for each product selected by the customer.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items, updating inventory (quan_in_stock).
- Warehouse staff generate order lines (orderlineid, prod_id, quantity, orderdate) for each item in an order.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Shipping Department

Activity:
- Shipping department prepares orders for shipment by generating shipping labels and tracking numbers.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team calculates the net amount (netamount) based on order lines and taxes.
- Finance team generates invoices (orderid, netamount, tax, totalamount).

## 7. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Team

Activity:
- Customers pay for their orders using the provided payment methods (creditcard).
- Finance team records customer payments and updates customer accounts accordingly.

## 8. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory (quan_in_stock) when new items are received or existing items are sold.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels (date_low, quan_low).

## 9. Reorder Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record reordered quantities (quan_reordered) and expected delivery dates (date_expected) when purchase orders are fulfilled.

## 10. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Analytics Team

Activity:
- Analytics team analyzes sales data to identify trends, best-selling products, and customer behavior patterns.
- Analytics team generates reports for management based on the analyzed data.
