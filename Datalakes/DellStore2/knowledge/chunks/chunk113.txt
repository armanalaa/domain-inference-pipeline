 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new products based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.
- Product managers update product details when suppliers provide updated information or when product specifications change.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer contact information during lead generation and nurturing processes.
- Customers place orders by providing shipping details, order preferences, and payment information.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries related to orders, returns, or account updates.
- HR managers maintain customer records for internal reporting and analytics purposes.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items based on order details from the inventory table.
- Sales representatives or customer service agents confirm order status with customers.
- Warehouse staff ship orders to the specified address and update order status accordingly.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product ID.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock items based on reorder data.
- Sales representatives update sales data for each product ID when orders are fulfilled.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on product prices and customer location.
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to sales, expenses, and inventory costs in the appropriate ledgers.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data governance team maintains business glossaries, data quality rules, and catalogs for all data assets within DellStore2.
- They ensure data is accurate, secure, and trusted across the organization by monitoring data lineage and enforcing data quality standards.

## 7. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Team

Activity:
- Data analysts create reports and dashboards to provide insights into sales performance, inventory levels, customer behavior, and other key metrics.
- Business intelligence team uses these insights to inform strategic decisions and identify opportunities for improvement.
