 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for newly added or existing products based on their characteristics.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and age into the customers table during new account creation.
- HR managers update income, gender, and other personal information when necessary.
- Customers access their accounts using username and "password" to view order history or make changes to their personal data.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders by linking customerid, orderdate, netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Customers place orders by specifying products (prod_id), quantity, and orderdate in the orderlines table.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when they ship ordered items.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track sales by updating sales for each product (prod_id) in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on date_low and quan_low from the reorder table.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff, Accounting Teams

Activity:
- Finance records transactions related to orders (orders) and customer accounts (customers).
- Accounting teams generate financial reports based on order data for analysis and decision making.
