 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category specialists update and maintain the categories table with appropriate product categories.

## 2. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives create new customer accounts by capturing customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card information, age, income, and gender.
- HR managers update the customer table with any changes in customer details.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantities, and providing order details like shipping address.
- Sales representatives review and confirm orders before they are processed.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity in stock for each product in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish products when the quantity in stock falls below a certain threshold.

## 5. Order Processing and Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items from inventory.
- Shipping teams prepare shipments, label packages, and coordinate with carriers for delivery.

## 6. Order Tracking and Customer Service

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives track order status and provide updates to customers as needed.

## 7. Invoice Generation and Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Teams, Customers

Activity:
- Finance teams generate invoices based on orders and calculate net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.
- Customers make payments using their preferred credit card or other payment methods.

## 8. Reordering and Inventory Forecasting

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Purchasing teams reorder products based on the expected date of arrival and minimum quantity required as indicated in the reorder table.
- Data analysts forecast inventory needs by analyzing sales data and trends to determine when and how much to reorder for each product.
