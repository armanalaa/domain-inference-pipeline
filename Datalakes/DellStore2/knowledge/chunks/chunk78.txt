 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received or sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income during the sales process.
- Customer service agents update contact information when customers request changes.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders by linking customerid, orderdate, and netamount.
- Customers place orders by specifying orderlineid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate in the orderlines table.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when orders are fulfilled.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff monitor sales by tracking sales in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on date_low and quan_low from the reorder table.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff, Accounting Teams

Activity:
- Finance records totalamount for each order in the orders table.
- Accounting teams calculate tax based on order details.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Dataedo Users

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data quality and lineage using Dataedo's self-service analytics solution.
- Dataedo users browse the catalog to understand data definitions and usage guidance.
