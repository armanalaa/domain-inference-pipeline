 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received or shipped out.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details during the onboarding process, including firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, and creditcard information.
- Customer service agents update customer information as needed, such as address changes or contact preferences.
- HR managers maintain employee records, which may include customerid for employees who are also customers.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by associating orderid with the appropriate customerid and netamount.
- Customers place orders online or over the phone, providing necessary details like orderdate and quantity for each product.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient inventory for order fulfillment.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels are low.

## 4. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update sales when products are sold to customers.
- Purchasing teams monitor quan_in_stock and reorder prod_id when the quantity falls below a specified threshold (quan_low).

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack products based on orderlines, ensuring the correct quantity for each prod_id is included in each shipment.
- Shipping teams generate shipping labels and track packages until delivery.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record totalamount for each order based on netamount, tax, and any applicable shipping costs.
- They also update the creditcard information for customers as needed.

## 7. Data Quality Assurance

Performed By: Data Analysts, BI Teams, Data Governance Staff

Activity:
- Data analysts and BI teams run automated quality checks on DellStore2 to uncover errors, anomalies, and inconsistencies in the data.
- They monitor and improve data quality over time by assigning fixes and crowdsource feedback from the community.
