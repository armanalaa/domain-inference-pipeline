 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are received, referencing the quan_in_stock column.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product recommendations and order details.
- HR managers create new customer accounts, entering personal information such as name, address, email, phone, credit card details, and age.
- Customers browse the catalog using various search methods (by actor, category, or title) to find desired products.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives finalize orders by confirming customer details, order items, and shipping information.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered products, ensuring the correct quantity is included for each orderline.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.

## 4. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record sales transactions by entering net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.
- Finance teams update customer accounts with payment history, including credit card type, expiration date, and transaction details.

## 5. Reorder Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and mark products as low stock when the quantity in stock falls below a specified threshold.
- Purchasing teams review reorder requests and issue purchase orders to replenish stock.

## 6. Inventory Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze sales data to identify trends, best-selling products, and opportunities for improvement in inventory management.

## 7. Security Management

Performed By: IT Administrators

Activity:
- IT administrators manage user accounts by creating, updating, and deleting customer and employee accounts, as well as handling password resets.
