 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Support Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers and register new accounts by capturing customer details such as name, address, email, phone, credit card information, and password.
- Customer support agents assist existing customers with account inquiries, updates, or issues by accessing their personal data.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product code, name, category, list price, and other relevant details.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when products are received or sold, adjusting quantities in stock accordingly.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog and specifying quantities.
- Sales representatives review customer orders for accuracy before confirming them.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment once they are confirmed.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and reorder low stock items based on predefined thresholds.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for necessary products.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.
- Shipping agents label packages and arrange for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- They record these financial details in the orders table for future reference.

## 7. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Data analysts collect and analyze data from various sources to generate reports on sales performance, customer behavior, and inventory levels.
- Marketing teams use these insights to optimize marketing strategies and improve the overall customer experience.
