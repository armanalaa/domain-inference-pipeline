 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their first name, last name, address details, contact information, credit card details, username, password, age, income, gender, and region.
- HR managers update customer account details when necessary.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, list price, and any special offers or common product IDs.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity in stock for each product.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by recording order details such as order date, customer ID, net amount, tax, total amount, and orderlines (product ID, quantity).
- Customer service representatives handle any issues or adjustments related to the order.

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on orderlines (product ID, quantity).
- Shipping department ships the orders by recording shipping details such as tracking number and delivery date.

## 6. Reordering Inventory

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams reorder products when inventory levels reach a predefined low quantity threshold (quan_low).

## 7. Customer Support and Complaints Handling

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer complaints, inquiries, and returns by updating the relevant order or customer details.

## 8. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports based on data from the orders, customers, inventory, and sales tables for analysis and decision making.
- Management reviews these reports to make informed decisions about business operations and strategy.
