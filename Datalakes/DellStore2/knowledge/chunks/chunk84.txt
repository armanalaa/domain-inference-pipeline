 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.
- Product managers monitor sales data to identify best-selling products and adjust pricing strategies accordingly.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, capturing their contact information and preferences.
- Customers place orders by specifying the desired products, quantities, and delivery addresses.
- HR managers process new customer accounts, verifying identity and creditworthiness based on provided details.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers and generate corresponding order IDs.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on the orderlines table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for any necessary components or materials.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance, Accounting Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices, quantities, and taxes.
- Accounting teams generate invoices for customers, including order details, payment terms, and due dates.

## 5. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Teams

Activity:
- Customers make payments using their preferred credit card or other payment methods.
- Finance teams record customer payments, update account balances, and mark orders as paid in the orders table.

## 6. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and trigger reorder requests when stock reaches a predefined threshold.
- Purchasing teams review reorder requests, determine appropriate quantities, and issue purchase orders to suppliers.

## 7. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management Teams

Activity:
- Business analysts extract data from the database for analysis, focusing on sales trends, customer behavior, and inventory performance.
- Management teams use insights gained from data analysis to make informed decisions about pricing strategies, marketing efforts, and operational improvements.
