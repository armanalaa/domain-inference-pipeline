 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category specialists update product categories based on the assigned categoryname from the categories table.
- Product managers assign common_prod_id to products with similar characteristics for easier tracking.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details including firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income during account creation.
- HR managers update customer information such as address, contact details, and credit card information when necessary.
- Customer service agents record customerid for tracking purposes in the cust_hist table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by recording orderid, customerid, netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock for each product based on the inventory table.
- When an order is ready to be shipped, warehouse staff update the sales column in the inventory table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record quan_in_stock for each product in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on the date_low and quan_low from the reorder table.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items according to orderlines in the orderlines table.
- Shipping department records shipment details such as tracking number for each order.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department generates invoices based on the orders table and sends them to customers.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, Technical Support

Activity:
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries and complaints related to products or orders.
- Technical support assists customers with product-related issues.

## 8. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze sales data from the orders table to generate reports on customer behavior, sales trends, and inventory performance.
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about product offerings, pricing strategies, and marketing campaigns.
