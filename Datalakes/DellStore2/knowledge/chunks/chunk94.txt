 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category managers update the categories table with new or modified product categories.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar products for easier tracking and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details into the customers table during account creation or updates.
- Customer service agents update customer contact information as needed in the customers table.
- HR managers add new employees to the customers table when they are granted access to the store's platform.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory with the quantity of each product in stock.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on low stock levels tracked in the inventory table.
- Warehouse staff record sales and adjust inventory quantities accordingly.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by linking customer details, products, quantities, and order dates in the orders and orderlines tables.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on orderline information.
- Customer service agents update order statuses and handle any issues or changes to orders.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order and record them in the orders table.
- Finance teams generate invoices based on order information and send them to customers.

## 6. Reorder Processing

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update reorder with products that need to be replenished.
- Purchasing teams review the reorder table and issue purchase orders for necessary items.

## 7. Data Quality Assurance

Performed By: Data Quality Analysts

Activity:
- Data quality analysts monitor data consistency across tables, ensuring accurate and up-to-date information in DellStore2.
