 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product details and processing payments.
- Customers place orders by selecting items, specifying quantities, and entering their contact information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Order Processing Team

Activity:
- The order processing team verifies customer information and checks inventory availability before confirming orders.
- Upon confirmation, the team generates an order ID and updates the cust_hist table with the new order details.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- After order confirmation, the finance department calculates the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.
- The calculated values are recorded in the orders table.

## 5. Order Line Item Creation

Performed By: Order Processing Team

Activity:
- For each order, the order processing team creates a corresponding orderlines record, including the orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.

## 6. Inventory Adjustment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- When an order is shipped, warehouse staff update the inventory table by decreasing the quan_in_stock for the relevant product.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and reorder products when the quantity drops below a specified threshold (quan_low).
- Upon reordering, the team updates the reorder table with the new date_reordered, quan_reordered, and expected delivery date.

## 8. Data Management

Performed By: Data Management Team

Activity:
- The data management team maintains the integrity of the database by ensuring accurate and up-to-date information across all tables.
- They also use tools like Dataedo to document, analyze, and share data lineage diagrams for better understanding and compliance.
