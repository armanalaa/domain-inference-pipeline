 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update them when new products are received or sold.
- Product managers monitor sales data to identify best-selling products and adjust pricing strategies accordingly.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives interact with customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- HR managers maintain customer records, including personal details, contact information, credit card data, and purchase history.
- Customers place orders through the online store by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping details.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on orderlines data.
- Sales representatives confirm order status to customers and handle any issues that arise during the process.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with low inventory levels according to reorder data.

## 4. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Customers

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on orderlines data.
- Customers make payments using their preferred credit card methods and provide billing information as needed.

## 5. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Data managers ensure that all customer data is securely stored and complies with relevant regulations such as GDPR or CCPA.
- IT staff maintain the database schema and metadata repository, keeping documentation up to date automatically.

## 6. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business analysts analyze sales data to identify trends, customer preferences, and areas for improvement.
- Management uses these insights to make informed decisions about product offerings, pricing strategies, and marketing campaigns.
