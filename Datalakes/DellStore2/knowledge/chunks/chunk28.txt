 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update existing item details as needed, such as title, actor, or special designation.
- The data governance team ensures data quality and consistency across the product catalog.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information, including first name, last name, address, contact details, credit card information, username, and password.
- Customer service agents update customer records with new contact information or changes to account status.
- HR managers maintain employee data related to customers (age, income, gender) for internal reporting purposes.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock and sales of each product.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish inventory when quan_in_stock falls below a certain threshold.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by specifying the order date, customer ID, net amount, tax, and total amount.
- Customers place orders online or through other channels, providing necessary details such as order date, quantity, and product ID.
- Warehouse staff fulfill orders by selecting the appropriate products from inventory and shipping them to the customer.

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record each line item in an order, including the orderline ID, order ID, product ID, quantity, and order date.

## 6. Reordering Inventory

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update the reorder table when quan_in_stock falls below a predetermined threshold (quan_low).
- Purchasing teams use this information to issue purchase orders for replenishing low-stock items.

## 7. Data Lineage and Quality Assurance

Performed By: Data Governance Team, IT Administrators

Activity:
- The data governance team tracks how data flows across systems and visualizes transformations, dependencies, and impacts to ensure transparency, support compliance, and reduce errors.
- IT administrators perform automated data quality checks to detect errors, anomalies, and missing values before they propagate downstream.
