 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data stewards maintain reliable data for analytics, AI, and ensure up-to-date metadata for the catalog.
- Data stewards group related assets, link tables to terms, auto-generate descriptions, and monitor quality trends.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with customers, capturing their contact information, credit card details, and preferences.
- HR managers create customer profiles, including personal data, age, income, gender, and region.
- Customers place orders by specifying products, quantities, shipping addresses, and payment methods.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders based on customer requests and update order statuses.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and prepare items for shipment upon order confirmation.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when quan_in_stock falls below a certain threshold.
- Finance records order details, calculates net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when items are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and reorder products when date_low is reached.

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff create order lines for each product in an order, specifying quantity and orderdate.
- Sales representatives update orderlines with any changes to the order, such as cancellations or modifications.

## 6. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Purchasing teams update date_reordered and quan_reordered when products are reordered.
- Data stewards forecast future inventory needs based on sales trends and reorder patterns.

## 7. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: Data Stewards, HR Managers, Business Users

Activity:
- Data stewards discover and classify sensitive data, assign ownership, and manage roles for audit-readiness and regulatory compliance.
- HR managers ensure that employee access to sensitive data is properly controlled and monitored.
- Business users flag issues, assign tasks, and contribute insights, turning the data catalog into a living, collaborative resource.
