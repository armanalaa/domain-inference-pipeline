 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Maria (Product Manager), Hubert (Metadata Engineer, Tech Lead)

Activity:
- Maria registers new products by specifying the title, category, actor, price, and special attributes.
- Hubert updates product common_prod_id based on the unique identifier assigned to the product.
- Maria assigns a common_prod_id to each variant of a product with different attributes.

## 2. Inventory Management

Performed By: Sven (IT Admin & Support), Miłosz (Developer)

Activity:
- Sven updates inventory quan_in_stock for each product based on the latest stock count.
- Miłosz records sales data for each product, updating the sales column accordingly.
- Sven sets reorder points and levels for each product, determining when to restock items.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by entering customerid, orderdate, netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Customers place orders by providing orderid, orderdate, customerid, and product details (prod_id, quantity).
- Warehouse staff track orderlines, updating quan_in_stock as products are shipped to customers.

## 4. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Maria (Product Manager), Sven (IT Admin & Support)

Activity:
- Maria updates customer information such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income.
- Sven records customer history in cust_hist, detailing previous orders and interactions.

## 5. Order Reordering

Performed By: Miłosz (Developer), Marcin (QA Engineer)

Activity:
- Miłosz updates reorder with date_low, quan_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected for each product.
- Marcin verifies that the reordering process is functioning correctly and adjusts as necessary.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team records financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- They generate reports based on this data for analysis and decision making.

## 7. Quality Assurance

Performed By: QA Engineers (Marcin, Maciej, Sławomir)

Activity:
- QA engineers test the functionality of DellStore2, ensuring accurate data processing and proper workflows.
- They identify and report any issues or bugs that may impact the system's performance.
