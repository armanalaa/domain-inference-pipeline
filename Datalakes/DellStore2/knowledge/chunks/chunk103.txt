 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update product details as needed, such as title, actor, or common_prod_id.
- The data governance team ensures all product information is accurate and up-to-date.

## 2. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product and update it accordingly when items are received or sold.
- Inventory managers set reorder points and dates based on quan_low and date_low columns.
- Inventory managers process reorders, updating quan_reordered and date_expected as needed.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order dates.
- Customer service agents confirm order details with customers and create new orders in the system.
- Customer service agents update order statuses as needed throughout the order lifecycle.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack customer orders based on orderlines data.
- Shipping department prepares shipments for delivery by recording tracking numbers and estimated delivery dates.

## 5. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Finance Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives maintain customer relationships by updating contact information, credit card details, and communication preferences.
- HR managers onboard new customers by creating accounts and assigning usernames and passwords.
- The finance team records customer income and age to help with targeted marketing efforts.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team, Accounting Department

Activity:
- The finance team calculates net amounts for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- The accounting department records tax and total amounts for each order.
- The finance team generates financial reports to track sales performance and make data-driven decisions.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team, IT Department

Activity:
- The data governance team ensures all data in the DellStore2 database is accurate, complete, and up-to-date.
- The IT department implements security measures to protect customer and company data.
- The data governance team maintains a public roadmap for continuous improvement and supports community voting on new features.
