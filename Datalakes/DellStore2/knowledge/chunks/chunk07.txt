 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representative

Activity:
- Registers new customers by entering their first name, last name, email, phone number, username, password, address details, region, credit card type, credit card identification number, credit card expiration date, age, and income.

Performed By: Database Administrator

Activity:
- Validates the entered data for integrity and uniqueness before inserting it into the customers table.

## 2. Login Authentication

Performed By: Customer

Activity:
- Enters their username and password to access their account.

Performed By: System

Activity:
- Verifies the entered credentials against the stored data in the customers table, returning a refcursor containing customer information and related purchases if valid, or 0 if invalid.

## 3. Purchase Processing

Performed By: Customer

Activity:
- Selects DVD products to purchase and specifies the quantity for each.

Performed By: Sales Representative (or Automated System)

Activity:
- Calculates the net amount, tax amount, and total amount based on the selected products and quantities.

Performed By: Order Management Team

Activity:
- Creates a new order with the calculated amounts, customer ID, and order date.

Performed By: Inventory Management Team

Activity:
- Checks the inventory for availability of the selected products and updates the quan_in_stock column accordingly.

Performed By: Order Management Team

Activity:
- Adds the purchased products and their quantities to the orderlines table.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Retrieves DVD products from storage when the quan_in_stock column for a product falls below the specified quantity_low.

Performed By: Purchasing Team

Activity:
- Issues purchase orders for replenishing the depleted inventory.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Picks and packages the ordered DVD products.

Performed By: Shipping Department

Activity:
- Labels and ships the packaged orders to the customer's address.

## 6. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representative

Activity:
- Addresses customer inquiries, complaints, or issues related to their orders or accounts.

## 7. Sales Reporting

Performed By: Data Analyst

Activity:
- Generates sales reports based on data from the orders and inventory tables.
