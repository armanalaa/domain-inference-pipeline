 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product details when necessary, such as changing the title, actor, or common_prod_id.
- Monitor sales data for each product to assess performance and adjust pricing strategies accordingly.

## 2. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Track the quantity in stock for each product and update it as items are sold or received.
- Issue purchase orders when inventory levels fall below a specified threshold.
- Reorder products that have reached their minimum stock level by recording the date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Place orders by selecting desired products, specifying quantities, and providing customer details.
- Review order details before submitting them for processing.
- Record the netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the system.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Retrieve ordered items from inventory when they are ready to be shipped.
- Package and label orders for efficient shipping and delivery.
- Update the quan_in_stock column as items are fulfilled and shipped to customers.

## 5. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Maintain customer records by updating contact information, credit card details, and account history.
- Respond to customer inquiries regarding orders, returns, or product information.
- Track customer interactions and preferences for personalized service and targeted marketing efforts.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Record financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Generate invoices based on order details and send them to customers.
- Monitor sales revenue and expenses to ensure accurate financial reporting.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Maintain the integrity of data by ensuring proper data lineage, quality, and cataloging.
- Implement security measures to protect sensitive customer information.
- Use trusted reporting tools to identify and resolve inconsistencies in KPIs across different reports.
