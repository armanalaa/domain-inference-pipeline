 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives retrieve customer details by searching for a specific username or email address in the customers table.
- Sales representatives update customer information such as address, phone number, and credit card details in the customers table.
- Sales representatives add new customers to the customers table after verifying their identity and completing the registration process.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price in the products table.
- Inventory specialists update the quan_in_stock column in the inventory table when products are sold or restocked.
- Inventory specialists set the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table to trigger automatic reordering of low-stock items.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by linking customer details, product information, and quantities in the orderlines table.
- Warehouse staff update the quan_in_stock column in the inventory table when products are shipped to customers.
- Warehouse staff mark orders as shipped in the orders table after verifying that all items have been packaged and sent.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department calculates the netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table based on product prices and quantities.
- The finance department generates invoices by extracting order details from the orders table and sending them to customers via email or mail.

## 5. Account Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers update the customer's age, income, gender, and employment status in the customers table.
- The finance department records credit card transactions and updates the creditcard column in the customers table.

## 6. Data Maintenance and Compliance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data integrity by maintaining database backups and implementing security measures to protect customer data.
- Compliance officers review customer data to ensure it complies with regulations like GDPR and CCPA, and take appropriate actions when necessary.
