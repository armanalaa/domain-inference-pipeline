 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new products based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- HR managers create customer profiles containing personal information, credit card details, and contact information.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping details.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory based on order details.
- Sales representatives confirm order fulfillment and update order status in the system.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when the quantity falls below the specified threshold (quan_low).

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- Finance teams record financial transactions related to orders in the appropriate columns of the orders table.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data quality by validating and cleaning customer and product data.
- IT administrators document data lineage, data quality, and data catalog information for the DellStore2 database.
