 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income into the customers table.
- HR managers update customer age and gender information in the customers table.
- Customers place orders by specifying orderdate, customerid, and netamount in the orders table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orderlines by linking products to orders with prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.
- Warehouse staff update the quan_in_stock column for each product as items are shipped out.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on low inventory levels and update the reorder table with prod_id, date_low, quan_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records totalamount for each order in the orders table.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items based on orderlines, preparing them for shipment.

## 6. Shipping and Delivery

Performed By: Shipping Department

Activity:
- Shipping department updates the orderdate column in the orders table once an order has been shipped.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and update customer details as needed in the customers table.
