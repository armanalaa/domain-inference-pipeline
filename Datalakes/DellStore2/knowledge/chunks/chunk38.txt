 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for stocking up on products with low quantity in inventory.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage customers by providing product information and assisting with order placement.
- Customer service agents handle customer queries regarding orders, returns, or account details.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Order Processing Team

Activity:
- Order processing team verifies and confirms received orders against the customer history.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items based on orderlines and prepare them for shipping.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when the quantity falls below the specified threshold (quan_low).

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team, Accounting Department

Activity:
- Finance records net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.
- Accounting department generates invoices based on order details and customer information from customers.

## 6. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Team

Activity:
- Data analysts create reports using data from various tables (cust_hist, orders, inventory) to analyze sales trends and customer behavior.
- Business intelligence team presents key performance indicators (KPIs) and insights to stakeholders for decision making.

## 7. Compliance and Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- Data stewards maintain the business glossary, ensuring universal definitions of key concepts are up-to-date and consistent.
- Compliance officers review and approve changes to the glossary, ensuring adherence to internal policies and external regulations.
