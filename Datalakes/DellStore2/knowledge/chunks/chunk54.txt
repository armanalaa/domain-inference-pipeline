 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are received or existing ones are sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as name, address, email, phone, and credit card information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any changes to contact or payment information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by selecting products, quantities, and delivery details.
- Warehouse staff prepare the ordered items for shipment once an order is confirmed.
- Customer service agents handle any issues that arise during the order processing phase.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update the database when stock quantities change.
- Purchasing teams place orders to replenish low or depleted items based on reorder points and expected demand.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to orders, including payments and returns.

## 6. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: Data Governance Team, IT Security Team

Activity:
- The data governance team ensures that all data is accurate, complete, and up-to-date within the DellStore2 database.
- The IT security team implements measures to protect sensitive customer information from unauthorized access or breaches.
