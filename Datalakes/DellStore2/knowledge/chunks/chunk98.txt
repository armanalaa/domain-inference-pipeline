 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering contact details, credit card information, and preferences.
- HR managers update customer records with personal information changes such as address, email, or phone number.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, providing shipping information, and making payments.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders for customers based on their selections.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items from inventory for each order.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when inventory levels fall below quan_low.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on the netamount and tax calculated for each order.
- Finance records the totalamount, payment details, and account receivable information in the appropriate columns.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by assembling items, printing labels, and updating orderlines with tracking numbers.
- Shipping teams deliver orders to customers according to the provided address and tracking information.

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams place orders for products when quan_in_stock falls below a certain level (quan_low).
- Warehouse staff receive and update inventory levels when new shipments arrive.

## 7. Reorder Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor the date_expected for each reorder to ensure timely replenishment of products.
- Purchasing teams adjust purchase orders and expected delivery dates based on changes in the date_expected column.

## 8. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries, complaints, and returns by updating relevant columns in cust_hist and customers tables.
