 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their first name, last name, address details, contact information, credit card details, age, income, gender, and username/password.
- IT administrators manage customer accounts by updating account information, password resets, and account deactivation or deletion when necessary.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, list price, and special offers (if any).
- Inventory specialists update product information, such as quantity in stock, sales, and common product ID when necessary.

## 3. Category Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Product managers assign products to appropriate categories for easy navigation.
- IT administrators manage the category database by adding or removing categories as needed.

## 4. Inventory Management and Replenishment

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists track inventory levels and sales to determine when products need to be reordered.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for the necessary quantities of each product that needs replenishing.

## 5. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives (if needed)

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog and place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details such as shipping address and payment information.
- If a customer encounters difficulties during the ordering process, sales representatives assist them in completing their order.

## 6. Order Processing and Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory and prepare them for shipping.
- Shipping teams package the orders and arrange for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 7. Order Tracking and Customer Service

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries about order status, shipping details, and returns or exchanges.
- IT administrators monitor the order processing system to ensure smooth operation and address any technical issues that may arise.

## 8. Financial Management and Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Finance teams record net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.
- IT administrators generate reports on sales, inventory levels, customer demographics, and other key performance indicators to help inform business decisions.
