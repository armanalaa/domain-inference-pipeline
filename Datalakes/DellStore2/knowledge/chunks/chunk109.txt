 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage potential customers and register new accounts by capturing contact details (email, phone) from the customers' section in the customers table.
- Customer service agents assist existing customers with account-related queries, updating customer information as necessary.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details (product code, name, category, list price) in the products table.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels (quan_in_stock) when receiving shipments of new or restocked products.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product details and processing orders through the online store.
- Customers browse the catalog, select items, and submit orders via the website, which are recorded in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items, updating inventory levels accordingly.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels (quan_in_stock) and notify purchasing teams when stock for a product falls below the reorder point (date_low).
- Purchasing teams place orders to replenish inventory, updating the reorder table with details such as date_reordered and quan_reordered.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team calculates order totals (netamount, tax, totalamount) based on product prices and quantities in the orderlines table, and records these values in the orders table.

## 6. Customer Service

Performed By: Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Customer service agents handle customer complaints or issues related to orders, updating the cust_hist table with relevant details as necessary.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Dataedo Team

Activity:
- The dataedo team maintains the integrity and accuracy of the DellStore2 database by monitoring data quality, lineage, and catalog using Dataedo's data governance solution.
