 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.
- When inventory reaches a predefined low level, warehouse staff initiate reordering process using the prod_id.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with customers to understand their needs and place orders accordingly.
- HR managers maintain customer records including personal details, credit card information, and contact information.
- Customers view their order history, update their profile, and make payments using the provided username and password.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders based on customer requests and record them in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items according to orderlines and update inventory accordingly.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products not available in stock.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on the netamount from the orders table.
- Finance records the invoice details including tax and total amount.

## 5. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance

Activity:
- Customers make payments using their credit card information stored in customers table.
- Finance updates the payment status for each order.

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- When inventory reaches a predefined low level, warehouse staff initiate reordering process using the prod_id.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products not available in stock.
- Upon receipt of new stock, warehouse staff update the quan_in_stock column.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer queries and complaints related to orders, payments, or products.

## 8. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales, inventory levels, and customer behavior using data from the various tables.
- Management uses these reports for decision making and strategic planning.
