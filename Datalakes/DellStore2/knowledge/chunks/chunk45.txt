 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for promotional or related products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts with firstname, lastname, address, contact details, credit card information, and login credentials.
- Customer service agents update customer information as needed.
- HR managers assign age, income, gender, and region to customers for targeted marketing efforts.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate orderids in the cust_hist table.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Order Processing Team

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items based on orderlines and quan_in_stock.
- Order processing team updates netamount, tax, totalamount, and orderdate in the orders table.

## 5. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff update sales when products are sold to customers.
- Purchasing teams reorder items when quan_low is reached and date_low is due.

## 6. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff update date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected when items are restocked.
- Purchasing teams monitor date_expected to plan future orders.

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Finance, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Finance records financial transactions related to orders, taxes, and net amounts.
- Data analysts generate reports and insights from the data in the DellStore2 database for business decision making.
