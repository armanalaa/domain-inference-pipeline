 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering contact information, credit card details, and login credentials.
- HR managers update customer records with personal data changes such as address or employment status.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders based on customer requests and update the orderlines table with product IDs, quantities, and order dates.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment according to the orderlines details.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on inventory levels and reorder requirements.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records invoices for customers, including order IDs, net amounts, taxes, and total amounts.

## 5. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Team

Activity:
- Data analysts create reports and dashboards using data from the orders, orderlines, inventory, customers, and products tables to monitor sales performance and customer behavior.

## 6. Data Maintenance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Database Administrators

Activity:
- IT administrators manage database schema changes, including renaming columns, modifying data types, or retiring tables as systems evolve.

## 7. Impact Analysis

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data governance team uses impact analysis tools to identify reports, dashboards, and processes that will be affected by database changes before they are implemented.
