 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update the quan_in_stock for each product based on incoming shipments.
- Product managers assign common_prod_id to similar products for easier categorization and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address details, email, phone, creditcardtype, and password.
- HR managers update customer information such as age, income, gender, and region.
- Customers log in to their accounts using username and password for browsing products and placing orders.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog, select desired products, and add them to their shopping carts.
- Customers proceed to checkout, enter shipping information, and confirm order details.
- Sales representatives review orders for accuracy before they are processed.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on customer orders and record netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items, updating quan_in_stock accordingly.
- Warehouse staff prepare shipments for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 5. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with quan_low or date_low.
- Inventory staff receive and verify incoming shipments, updating quan_in_stock and sales accordingly.
- Inventory staff reorder products when necessary, recording date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected.

## 6. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Finance staff generate reports on sales, revenue, and expenses for analysis and decision making.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and complaints regarding orders, products, or account issues.
- Customer service representatives assist customers with password resets and other account management tasks.
