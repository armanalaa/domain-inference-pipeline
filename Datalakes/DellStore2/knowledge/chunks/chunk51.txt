 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, inputting their contact details (email, phone) into the customers table.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries and update the username and password columns for each customer.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog and specifying quantities.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and record order details (orderid, orderdate, netamount, tax, totalamount) in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items and update the quan_in_stock column accordingly.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with low stock levels based on the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table.
- Warehouse staff receive and record incoming shipments, updating the quan_in_stock column for each product.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by packing items and printing shipping labels.
- Shipping teams deliver orders to customers and update the orderlines table with tracking information.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records sales transactions (orderid, netamount) in the orders table and calculates total revenue.
- Finance generates invoices based on order details and updates customer accounts accordingly.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Management Team

Activity:
- Data management team ensures data quality by validating and correcting entries in the customers, orders, and inventory tables.
- Data management team documents data lineage and metadata in a shared repository for future reference.
