 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set special offers or discounts for common_prod_id.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address details, and contact information.
- Customer service agents update customer details when customers request changes.
- Sales representatives assign creditcardtype, creditcard, and creditcardexpiration for each customer account.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate orderlines with prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient inventory for order fulfillment.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick products from the shelves based on orderlines and update quan_in_stock accordingly.
- Shipping department packages orders for delivery and records orderid in the tracking system.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices by calculating netamount, tax, and totalamount based on orders.
- Finance records these amounts in the orders table for each customer.

## 6. Reordering

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff monitor date_low and quan_low to determine when products need reordering.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for the required prod_id and update date_reordered and quan_reordered.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- The data governance team documents data lineage, quality, and dependencies across the organization.
- They ensure compliance with regulations and industry standards by maintaining a business glossary, ER diagrams, and reference data management.
