 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or are sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate customer profiles during sales interactions, including contact details, credit card information, and purchase history.
- HR managers maintain customer records with personal data such as name, address, age, income, gender, and employment history.
- Customers create accounts by providing their email, phone number, and setting a username and password.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by selecting products, quantities, and delivery addresses.
- Customers confirm their order details and make payments using credit cards or other accepted methods.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor stock levels and update the inventory table when items are sold or received from suppliers.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when quantities drop below a predetermined threshold.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives calculate net amounts and taxes for each order based on the product list price and any applicable fees.
- The finance team records total amounts, taxes, and payments in the orders table.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data governance team maintains data quality by validating and correcting inconsistencies in customer, product, order, and inventory data.

## 7. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and customer behavior using data from the orders, inventory, and customers tables.
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about pricing strategies, marketing campaigns, and operational improvements.
