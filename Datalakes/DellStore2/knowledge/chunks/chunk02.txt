 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representative

Activity:
- Registers new customers by entering their first name, last name, address details, email, phone number, credit card type, and password.

Performed By: IT Administrator

Activity:
- Assigns a unique customer ID to each new customer upon registration.

## 2. Customer Profile Management

Performed By: Customers (self-service)

Activity:
- Updates personal information such as address, email, phone number, and password.

Performed By: Customer Service Representative

Activity:
- Modifies customer details when requested by the customer or upon verification of identity.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers (self-service)

Activity:
- Selects products, specifies quantities, and provides shipping information to place an order.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Retrieves the selected products from inventory based on the order details.

Performed By: Order Fulfillment Team

Activity:
- Packages the ordered items and prepares them for shipping.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Updates the inventory levels when products are removed due to order fulfillment or returns.

Performed By: Purchasing Team

Activity:
- Orders new stock for products that have reached their reorder point.

## 6. Financial Transactions

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Records the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representative

Activity:
- Responds to customer inquiries regarding orders, products, or account issues.
