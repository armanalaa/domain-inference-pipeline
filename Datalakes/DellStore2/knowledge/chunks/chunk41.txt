 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.
- When inventory reaches a low threshold, warehouse staff initiate a reorder request for the corresponding prod_id in the reorder table.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and creditcard details.
- HR managers update the username, "password", age, income, and gender fields as part of employee onboarding or account maintenance.
- Customers place orders by specifying orderdate, customerid, netamount, tax, totalamount, and selecting products from the orderlines table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on the totalamount column in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items according to the prod_id and quantity columns in the orderlines table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with quan_in_stock less than quan_low, as recorded in the reorder table.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update sales columns in the inventory table when products are sold.
- Purchasing teams update the date_reordered and quan_reordered columns in the reorder table upon receiving new shipments.

## 5. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount, for analysis and reporting purposes.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- The data governance team ensures sensitive information, like credit card details, is protected and classified according to PCI guidelines.

## 7. Data Collaboration

Performed By: Various Roles (Data Users)

Activity:
- Users collaborate on data assets and accumulate valuable knowledge about data in the Dataedo platform.
