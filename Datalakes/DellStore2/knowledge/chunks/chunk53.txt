 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product.
- When a product reaches its reorder point, warehouse staff update the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income.
- HR managers assign a unique username and password to each customer.
- Customers place orders by specifying the product id, quantity, and order date in the orderlines table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives calculate the netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order.
- Warehouse staff confirm product availability and update the sales column in the inventory table when an order is processed.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on the quan_reordered and date_expected columns in the reorder table.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department generates invoices for customers by referencing the orderid, customerid, netamount, tax, and totalamount from the orders table.

## 5. Credit Card Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Customers provide their creditcard, creditcardexpiration, and creditcardtype when creating a new account or placing an order.
- The finance department processes payments by verifying the provided information against the stored data.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: HR Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- HR managers record customer history in the cust_hist table, including customerid, orderid, and prod_id.
- Sales representatives use this information to identify customer preferences and tailor future offerings.

## 7. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract data from various tables (customers, orders, inventory, etc.) for analysis and reporting purposes.
- Management uses the generated reports to make informed decisions about product offerings, pricing strategies, and marketing campaigns.
