 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category specialists update product categories to ensure accurate organization and searchability.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to related products for easier tracking and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card information, and password during account creation.
- HR managers maintain customer records including age, income, gender, and region for targeted marketing efforts.
- Customer service agents update customer contact information when necessary.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details like shipping address and payment method.
- Sales representatives process orders, confirming availability, calculating net amount, tax, and total amount before finalizing the transaction.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when stock reaches a predetermined low level (quan_low) and reorder products accordingly.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on orderlines and customer details.
- Shipping department prepares shipments for delivery by recording shipping information, such as tracking numbers and estimated delivery dates.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records invoices for each order, including net amount, tax, total amount, and customer details.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update reorder with date_low, quan_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected to facilitate timely restocking.
- Purchasing teams monitor reorder data to plan and execute product replenishment.
