 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when receiving shipments related to specific prod_id.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for each item based on its characteristics.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts with firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and creditcard details.
- Customer service agents update contact information (email, phone) for existing customers.
- HR managers record username, "password", age, income, gender, and creditcardexpiration for new employees.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products (prod_id) and specifying quantities.
- Sales representatives confirm order details, including orderdate, netamount, tax, and totalamount, before finalizing the order.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve products (prod_id) from inventory based on orderlineid and quantity.
- Shipping agents package orders for delivery and record the orderdate.

## 5. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when products (prod_id) are sold or returned.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on date_low and quan_low for each prod_id.

## 6. Reordering

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected when restocking products (prod_id).
- Purchasing teams update date_low and quan_low for each prod_id based on reordering information.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record sales data from inventory (sales) to calculate netamount, tax, and totalamount for orders.
- Finance staff generate financial reports for analysis and decision-making purposes.

## 8. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data integrity by validating and correcting inconsistent definitions for key metrics like "Graduation Rate" or "Retention Rate".
- IT administrators implement access controls to protect sensitive customer data (PII, student records, research data) from unauthorized access.
