 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their first name, last name, address details, email, phone number, credit card type, and password.
- HR managers update customer information such as age, income, gender, and region when necessary.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, list price, and common product ID.
- Inventory specialists update product information like quantity in stock and sales.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details such as order date and billing information.
- Sales representatives review and confirm customer orders before they are processed further.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory based on the order ID.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for reordering low stock items as needed.

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Delivery Service Providers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pack and prepare the ordered products for shipping.
- Delivery service providers pick up the packages and deliver them to the customer's specified address.

## 6. Invoice Generation and Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Teams, Customers

Activity:
- Finance teams generate invoices based on order details and calculate net amount, tax, and total amount.
- Customers review invoices and make payments using their credit cards or other payment methods.

## 7. Customer Support and Complaints Handling

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer complaints and provide support for product issues, returns, or refunds.

## 8. Data Collection and Analytics

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators collect data about the event, product details, metadata repository type, license key ID, user ID/email, public IP address for analytics purposes.
- Data analysts analyze customer behavior, sales trends, and system performance to improve the overall business operations.
