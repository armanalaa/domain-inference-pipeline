 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their first name, last name, address details, email, phone number, credit card type, and password.
- HR managers update customer information such as age, income, gender, and region when necessary.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, list price, and common product ID.
- Inventory specialists update product details like quantity in stock and sales when necessary.

## 3. Order Placement and Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate order IDs.
- Warehouse staff track ordered items and prepare them for shipment based on the order ID.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment and Tracking

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock products.
- Inventory specialists update product quantities in stock when new items arrive.

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on the order ID.
- Shipping teams prepare shipments and generate shipping labels for delivery.

## 6. Order Tracking and Delivery Confirmation

Performed By: Customers, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customers track their orders using the tracking number provided in the shipping label.
- Customer service representatives assist customers with order tracking inquiries.

## 7. Invoice Generation and Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Teams, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on orders and send them to customers.
- Finance teams record payments received from customers against the corresponding invoices.

## 8. Reorder Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists monitor low stock products and update reorder information such as product ID, date low, quantity low, and expected delivery date.
- Purchasing teams place reorders for items with low stock based on the reorder information.

## 9. Security and Compliance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Security Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators implement security measures to protect customer data and prevent unauthorized access.
- Security analysts monitor website activity for potential threats and take necessary actions to maintain security and integrity.
