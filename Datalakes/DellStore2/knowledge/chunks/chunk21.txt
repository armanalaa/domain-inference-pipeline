 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Data Management

Performed By: Data Management Team, HR Managers

Activity:
- Data Management Team maintains customer records by updating contact details and personal information from customers.
- HR Managers verify and confirm the identity of new employees during onboarding process using customer data.

## 2. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers create accounts by providing necessary details such as name, address, email, phone number, and password.
- Sales Representatives assist customers in setting up their accounts during product purchases or demos.

## 3. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Team

Activity:
- Product Managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory Team updates inventory levels when a new product is added to the catalog.

## 4. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.
- Sales Representatives process orders for customers who require assistance or have complex requests.

## 5. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Team

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity of products in stock after order fulfillment.
- Inventory Team monitors sales and adjusts inventory levels accordingly to maintain optimal stock.

## 6. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack customer orders based on orderlines.
- Shipping Department prepares shipments for delivery by labeling packages and arranging transportation.

## 7. Order Processing

Performed By: Finance Team, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Finance Team calculates net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices and shipping costs.
- Sales Representatives review orders to ensure accuracy before they are processed and sent to customers.

## 8. Reorder Management

Performed By: Inventory Team, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory Team identifies products that need replenishment based on reorder data.
- Purchasing Teams place orders for new inventory when product quantities reach the reorder point.

## 9. Privacy Request Handling

Performed By: Customer Support, Data Management Team

Activity:
- Customer Support assists customers in making privacy requests such as accessing, correcting, or deleting their personal information.
- Data Management Team processes and fulfills customer privacy requests according to company policy and regulations.
