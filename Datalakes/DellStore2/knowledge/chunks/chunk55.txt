 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers assign common_prod_id to similar products for easier categorization.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income during account creation.
- Customer service agents update contact information when customers report changes.
- Sales representatives assign username and password for customer login.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate orderids.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipping.

## 4. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when products are shipped out or received.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory levels based on reorder data.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare shipments by packaging ordered items and printing shipping labels.
- Shipping agents send orders to the appropriate carriers for delivery.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table.
- Finance teams update creditcard, creditcardexpiration, and other financial details as needed.

## 7. Data Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Intelligence Teams

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze sales data to identify trends and patterns in customer behavior.
- Business intelligence teams create reports and dashboards for decision-makers based on the analyzed data.
