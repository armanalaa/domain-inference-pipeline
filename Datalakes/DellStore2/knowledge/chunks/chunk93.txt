 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer profiles with firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and creditcard details.
- HR managers update the username, "password", age, income, gender fields when a new employee is onboarded or existing information needs to be updated.
- Customers place orders by providing orderdate, customerid, netamount, tax, totalamount, and shipping information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders based on customer requests and update the orderlines table with orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment once an order is ready.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the sales column in the inventory table whenever products are sold or returned.
- Purchasing teams monitor the reorder table to determine when to place orders for restocking based on date_low, quan_low, and quan_reordered.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance records financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount in the orders table.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives update the cust_hist table with customerid, orderid, and prod_id to track customer purchasing behavior.
- Marketing teams analyze the data in the cust_hist table to identify trends and opportunities for targeted promotions.

## 7. Product Information Maintenance

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers update product details like title, actor, price, special, and common_prod_id in the products table as needed.
- Marketing teams use the actor column to associate products with specific actors or brands for marketing purposes.
