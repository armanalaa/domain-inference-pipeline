 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration

Performed By: Sales Representatives, IT Support

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by entering their details (firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone) into the customers table.
- IT support assigns a unique customerid and username to each new customer.
- Sales representatives provide customers with their login credentials (username, "password").

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price in the products table.
- IT support updates the common_prod_id column when a new item is added or an existing one is modified.

## 3. Trial and POC Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service

Activity:
- Sales representatives offer customers free trials or paid POCs for product evaluation.
- Customers consent to the terms and conditions outlined in the 092_license.pdf document.
- Customer service manages trial and POC expiration and early termination as needed.

## 4. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog, select products, and place orders by specifying orderlineid, orderdate, customerid, prod_id, quantity for each item in the orderlines table.
- Sales representatives review and confirm orders before they are processed.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Customer Service

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory (quan_in_stock) based on the orderlines table.
- Warehouse staff update the quan_in_stock column as items are shipped to customers.
- Customer service communicates order status and estimated delivery dates to customers.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance, Customer Service

Activity:
- Finance calculates netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order based on the products' prices and quantities.
- Customer service generates invoices using the calculated amounts and customer details (customerid, firstname, lastname, email) from the customers table.

## 7. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams monitor reorder (prod_id, date_low, quan_low) and issue purchase orders for necessary items.
- Warehouse staff receive and update inventory (quan_in_stock) when new shipments arrive.

## 8. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service, IT Support

Activity:
- Customer service handles customer inquiries and complaints related to products, orders, or accounts.
- IT support troubleshoots technical issues reported by customers.
