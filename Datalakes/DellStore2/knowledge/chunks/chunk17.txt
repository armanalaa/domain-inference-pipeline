 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their personal details, contact information, and account credentials.
- HR managers maintain customer profiles, updating demographic data, income levels, and preferences as needed.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, list prices, and special offers.
- Inventory specialists update product information related to quantity in stock, sales, and common product IDs.

## 3. Order Placement and Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives review orders, confirm availability, and process payments.
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship ordered items to customers.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment and Tracking

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with low stock levels.
- Warehouse staff receive and verify incoming shipments, updating inventory accordingly.
- Inventory specialists monitor stock levels and reorder items when quantities drop below specified thresholds.

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Providers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment by packing items, affixing shipping labels, and updating order statuses.
- Shipping providers transport orders to customers' specified addresses.

## 6. Financial Management and Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accountants

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Accountants record financial transactions related to sales, expenses, and inventory costs.

## 7. Customer Support and Complaints Handling

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries, complaints, and returns.
- They update order statuses, product information, and customer profiles as needed.
