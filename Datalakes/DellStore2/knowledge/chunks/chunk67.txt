 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product details and processing payments.
- Customers browse the catalog, add items to their cart, and confirm purchases using their account information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Order Processing Team

Activity:
- The order processing team generates order IDs, calculates net amount, tax, and total amount based on customer orders.
- They update the orders table with the new order details and create corresponding entries in the cust_hist and orderlines tables.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Inventory Management Team

Activity:
- The inventory management team monitors sales data to determine when products are running low on stock.
- They update the reorder table with the date_low, quan_low, and expected restocking information.

## 5. Reordering and Restocking

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams place orders for new inventory based on the reorder table's recommendations.
- Warehouse staff receive and record incoming shipments, updating the inventory table accordingly.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team records transactions related to customer orders, including payments and taxes, in the orders table.
- They generate financial reports based on this data for analysis and auditing purposes.

## 7. Customer Service

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and complaints regarding orders, returns, or product issues.
- They update the customers table with any relevant information gathered during interactions with customers.
