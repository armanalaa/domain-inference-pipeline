 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or are sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts with personal details, contact information, and credit card data.
- HR managers maintain customer records, including age, income, gender, and region.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping details.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders based on customer requests and inventory availability.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment according to order details.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with low stock levels.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory quantities as products are sold or received.
- Purchasing teams monitor reorder points and trigger replenishment orders when necessary.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Finance records financial transactions related to sales, inventory, and purchases.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators maintain the database schema and ensure data integrity.
- Data analysts catalog all data sources, including databases, ETLs, BI tools, data lakes, applications for better governance and collaboration.
