 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category managers update product categories to ensure accurate organization of the product offerings.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card information, and create new accounts.
- HR managers maintain customer data privacy by updating sensitive information like age, income, gender, and region.
- Customers update their own account information through the self-service portal.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity in stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish products when the quantity in stock falls below a specified threshold.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by specifying the product ID, quantity, and order date.
- Customers place orders through the self-service portal or over the phone.
- Warehouse staff update the order status to "shipped" once the order is ready for delivery.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on the order ID and product details.
- Shipping teams prepare shipping labels and arrange for delivery of the ordered items.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order to generate invoices.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update reorder information when inventory levels fall below a specified threshold.
- Purchasing teams review the reorder information and place purchase orders to replenish low stock items.
