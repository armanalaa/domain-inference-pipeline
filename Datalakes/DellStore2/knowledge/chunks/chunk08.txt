 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by entering their personal details (firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone) and creating a unique username and password.
- IT administrators assign each customer a unique customerid and creditcard information (creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration).

## 2. Customer Profile Management

Performed By: Customers, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customers update their personal details (address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone) and creditcard information (creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration) through their account settings.
- Customer service representatives modify customer details (firstname, lastname, age, income, gender) as requested by customers or based on verification processes.

## 3. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code (prod_id), title, category, actor, price, and special or common product attributes (common_prod_id).
- IT administrators update product information (price) based on market fluctuations or promotional offers.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock (quan_in_stock) and sales of each product (sales).
- Purchasing teams order new inventory when the quantity in stock falls below a predetermined threshold (date_low, quan_low), and update the expected delivery date (date_expected) once confirmed.

## 5. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products (prod_id), specifying quantities (qty_in8 or qty_in9), and providing their customerid.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process orders by generating a unique orderid, calculating the netamount, tax, and totalamount based on the selected products and quantities, and recording the order date (orderdate).
- Warehouse staff retrieve the ordered items from inventory, update the quantity in stock, and record the sales.

## 7. Order History Tracking

Performed By: Customers, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customers view their order history by accessing their account details (cust_hist).
- Customer service representatives review customer orders (cust_hist) to address inquiries or disputes.

## 8. Reorder Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the reorder (prod_id, date_reordered, quan_reordered) when inventory levels are depleted or low (date_low).
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on the reorder information.
