 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set quan_low for each item to trigger reordering when stock reaches a minimum level.

## 2. Customer Registration

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and age.
- HR managers verify and approve customer information for account activation.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details such as billing and shipping addresses.
- Sales representatives confirm orders in the system, generating an orderid and updating cust_hist with customerid, orderid, and prod_id.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on orderlines and quan_in_stock.
- Shipping department prepares shipments for delivery by recording orderid, customerid, netamount, tax, totalamount, and shipping details in the orders table.

## 5. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when products are shipped to customers or returned.
- Inventory staff monitor sales and adjust quan_in_stock accordingly.

## 6. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Inventory staff check reorder for low stock items and place purchase orders with suppliers when quan_reordered is reached.
- Purchasing teams process the orders and update inventory accordingly.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Technical Support

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries related to orders, returns, or product issues.
- Technical support assists customers with product-related problems and provides solutions or escalates issues as needed.

## 8. Billing and Payment

Performed By: Finance Department, Customers

Activity:
- Finance records creditcard, creditcardexpiration, and other payment details for each customer account.
- Customers make payments using their stored credit card information or other approved methods.

## 9. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales, inventory levels, customer behavior, and other key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Management reviews the reports to make informed decisions about pricing, marketing strategies, and operational improvements.
