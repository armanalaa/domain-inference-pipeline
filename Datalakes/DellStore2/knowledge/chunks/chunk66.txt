 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set sales targets for each item based on historical data and market trends.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customers place orders by providing necessary details such as address, payment information, and shipping preferences.
- HR managers create customer profiles containing personal and demographic data for future reference.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers and generate order IDs.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items according to the orderlines.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products that are out of stock or need to be replenished.
- Finance records invoices and calculates netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock levels to identify products that need replenishment.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for the required quantity of each product.
- Inventory managers review reorder information and adjust date_low and quan_low accordingly.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare shipments by packing ordered items and affixing shipping labels.
- Shipping department schedules deliveries based on customer preferences and estimated delivery times.

## 6. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries related to orders, returns, or product information.

## 7. Compliance and Regulatory Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department, Legal Team

Activity:
- Finance records creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration, and other sensitive data securely in accordance with regulatory requirements.
- The legal team ensures that all data handling practices comply with relevant laws and industry standards.
