 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product when items are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customer service agents assist existing customers with inquiries, process orders, and update customer details in the customers table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by specifying products, quantities, and order dates.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack the ordered items and update the inventory accordingly.
- Customer service agents confirm order details with customers and mark orders as shipped in the orders table.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team calculates the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices, quantities, and taxes.
- They generate invoices containing order details and send them to customers via email or mail.

## 5. Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Customers make payments using their preferred credit card methods, which are recorded in the customers table.
- The finance team verifies payments and updates the order status as paid in the orders table.

## 6. Reordering Inventory

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update the reorder table when stock for a product falls below the quan_low threshold.
- Purchasing teams place orders with suppliers to replenish low-stock items based on the date_reordered and quan_reordered in the reorder table.

## 7. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract data from various tables (customers, orders, inventory, etc.) to generate reports on sales performance, customer behavior, and inventory levels.
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about pricing strategies, marketing campaigns, and operational improvements.
