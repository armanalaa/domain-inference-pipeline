 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or existing ones sell out, referencing the quan_in_stock column.

## 2. Customer Onboarding

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering contact details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, and creditcard information.
- HR managers assign unique customerids to each account for internal tracking purposes.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details like orderdate and netamount.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and update the cust_hist table with the corresponding customerid, orderid, and prod_id.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory based on the prod_id and quantity specified in the orderlines table.
- Shipping teams package and ship orders to customers, updating the orderdate column as they do so.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records totalamount for each order by calculating netamount plus tax from the orders table.

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory levels based on the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table.
- Warehouse staff receive new stock and update the inventory table with the updated quan_in_stock for each product.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives respond to customer inquiries, accessing relevant information from the customers table as needed.
