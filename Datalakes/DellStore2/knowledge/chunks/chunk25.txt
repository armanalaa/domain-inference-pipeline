 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category managers maintain the categories hierarchy and assign products to appropriate categories.
- Product managers update product details such as title, actor, price, special, and common_prod_id when necessary.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information including firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username during the onboarding process.
- Customer service agents update customer details like address, email, or phone when customers request changes.
- HR managers record creditcard, creditcardexpiration, age, income, gender, and region for new employees.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock and sales for each product in the inventory.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish products when quan_in_stock falls below a certain threshold.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock upon receiving new shipments.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by specifying orderdate, customerid, and netamount.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing orderdate.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock and orderlines when an order is fulfilled.

## 5. Reordering Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update date_low and quan_low for products that are running low on stock.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when date_reordered is triggered based on date_expected and quan_reordered.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate tax and totalamount for each order.
- Accounting staff record these values along with netamount in the orders table.

## 7. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data governance team ensures data quality by implementing configurable thresholds or reusable rules to monitor and manage data.
