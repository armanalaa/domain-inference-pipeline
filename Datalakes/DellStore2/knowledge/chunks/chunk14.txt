 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their personal details, contact information, and account preferences. (customers)
- IT administrators assign unique identifiers to each customer upon registration. (customers)
- Customer service representatives update customer profiles with changes in contact information or account settings. (customers)

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, and list prices. (products)
- Inventory specialists update product details when changes occur, such as price adjustments or discontinuation of an item. (products)
- Inventory specialists categorize products based on their attributes and intended use. (categories)

## 3. Order Placement and Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information. (orderlines, customers)
- Sales representatives review customer orders for accuracy and confirm availability of selected products. (inventory, orderlines)
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment by picking items from stock, packing them, and labeling packages with the appropriate shipping information. (inventory, orderlines)

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Specialists, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams place orders for new inventory based on sales data and projected demand. (orders, inventory)
- Inventory specialists update the quantity in stock when items are received or sold. (inventory)
- Warehouse staff track the movement of products within the warehouse to ensure accurate inventory levels. (inventory)

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Providers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment by picking items from stock, packing them, and labeling packages with the appropriate shipping information. (inventory, orderlines)
- Shipping providers pick up orders from the warehouse and deliver them to customers according to the specified delivery address and method. (orders, orderlines)

## 6. Reordering Inventory

Performed By: Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Inventory specialists monitor inventory levels and reorder products when they reach a predetermined low threshold. (reorder, inventory)
- Inventory specialists update the expected delivery date for reordered items. (reorder)

## 7. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on product prices and shipping costs. (orders)
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to orders, including revenue, expenses, and tax payments. ("password")

## 8. Customer Support and Complaints Handling

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and complaints regarding orders, products, or account issues. (customers, orders)
- Customer service representatives update customer profiles with any relevant information gathered during support interactions. (customers)
