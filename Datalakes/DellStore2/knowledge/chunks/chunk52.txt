 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product when items are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income into the customers table during account creation.
- Customer service agents update the username and password columns when assisting customers with account management.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by linking customerid, orderdate, netamount, tax, and totalamount to the orders table.
- Customers place orders by specifying productid, quantity, and orderdate in the orderlines table.
- Warehouse staff update the quan_in_stock column when an order is fulfilled.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record sales by updating the sales column for each product in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table.

## 5. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount, in the orders table.

## 6. Data Documentation and Lineage

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Users

Activity:
- Data analysts document table relationships, visualize the data model, and share everything with the team using Dataedo's web portal or interactive HTML documents.

## 7. Data Collaboration and Knowledge Sharing

Performed By: Data Analysts, Business Users

Activity:
- Data analysts and business users collaborate on data assets, accumulate valuable knowledge about data in one place, and build a universal vocabulary of business terms with their definitions and mapping to the data.
