 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data analysts monitor product data quality to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- Product managers update product details as needed based on feedback or market trends.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information such as name, address, contact details, credit card data, and account details into the customers table.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes in contact information or account status.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by linking them to customer accounts and specifying products, quantities, and order dates.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and prepare items for shipment based on order details.
- Finance team records net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when items are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on reorder data.

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives add order lines for each product in an order.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock and sales when items are shipped or returned.

## 6. Data Quality Assurance

Performed By: Data Analysts, Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data analysts run automated quality checks to uncover errors, anomalies, and inconsistencies in the database.
- Data governance team monitors data quality scores over time and assigns fixes as needed.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Customer service agents respond to customer inquiries or complaints related to orders, products, or account information.

## 8. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data governance team establishes and enforces data quality standards across the organization.
- They also crowdsource feedback from data consumers to improve data quality and trustworthiness.
