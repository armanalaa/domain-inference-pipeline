 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for the procurement of new products based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.
- Product managers update product details, such as price and special offers, when necessary.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customers place orders through the online store or by contacting sales representatives directly.
- Customer service agents handle order inquiries, process returns, and address any customer complaints.
- HR managers maintain employee records, including customer service agent details like personal information, employment history, and performance reviews.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and pick items for orders based on orderlines data.
- Sales representatives or customer service agents confirm order details with customers before processing payment.
- Orders are fulfilled by packaging and shipping the selected products to the customer's specified address.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels in real-time as items are received or shipped out.
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and reorder products when the quantity falls below a predefined threshold.
- Inventory managers analyze sales data to forecast demand and adjust order quantities accordingly.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department, Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives and customer service agents record customer payments in the system upon order fulfillment.
- Finance department calculates net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on the recorded data.
- Finance records financial transactions, generates invoices, and manages accounts receivable and payable.

## 6. Data Management

Performed By: IT Department, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT department maintains the DellStore2 database and ensures its security and integrity.
- Data analysts extract insights from the data stored in the database to support decision-making processes across various departments.
- Business Glossary is used to define and manage business terms, policies, and rules consistently throughout the organization.
