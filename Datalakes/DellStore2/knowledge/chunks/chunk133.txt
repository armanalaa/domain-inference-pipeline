 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Sales Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their personal details, contact information, and account credentials.
- Sales managers update customer accounts with their credit card details, income level, and gender for targeted marketing campaigns.
- Customer service representatives verify customer identities using email or phone numbers during account recovery processes.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory specialists update product details with actual prices based on sales data.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar products for easier categorization and search.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing customer details.
- Warehouse staff track order statuses and update them in the system when items are picked, packed, and shipped.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists monitor stock levels and reorder low-stock products based on predefined thresholds.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for replenishing inventory.

## 5. Financial Transactions

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance department records net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order in the system.
- Accounting staff generate invoices based on order details and send them to customers.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives access customer histories to assist with queries or complaints.
- Marketing managers analyze customer purchase patterns to develop targeted marketing strategies.

## 7. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: IT Administrators, System Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators monitor system performance using tools like plink and mpstat to ensure smooth operation.
- System analysts troubleshoot issues and implement solutions to maintain system efficiency.
