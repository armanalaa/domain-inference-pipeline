 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact details and demographic information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes to contact information or account status.
- Sales representatives assign each customer a unique username and password for account access.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, list prices, and descriptions.
- Inventory specialists update product information when stock levels change or products go on sale.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar items for easier categorization and search.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.
- Sales representatives review and confirm customer orders before they are processed.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory and prepare them for shipment.
- Shipping agents package orders and arrange for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists track stock levels by updating the quantity of each product in inventory.
- Purchasing teams order more products when inventory reaches a predetermined low level.

## 6. Financial Transactions

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance department calculates net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Accounting staff records financial transactions related to orders, including payments and refunds.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries and complaints about orders, products, or account issues.

## 8. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data integrity by maintaining the database schema and enforcing security policies.
- Data analysts use the database to generate reports and insights for business decision making.
