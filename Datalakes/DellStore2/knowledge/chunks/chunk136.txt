 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or existing ones sell out.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customer service agents assist existing customers with queries about orders, returns, or account information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders online by selecting items, providing shipping details, and making payments.
- Sales representatives review and confirm orders before they are processed for fulfillment.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure adequate stock is available for customer orders.
- Purchasing teams order additional products when inventory falls below a predetermined threshold.

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Providers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment by packaging items securely and attaching shipping labels.
- Shipping providers transport orders to the specified delivery address.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record transactions related to sales, expenses, and payments in the financial system.
- They generate invoices for customers based on order details and calculate taxes accordingly.

## 7. Error Handling and Maintenance

Performed By: IT Support, System Administrators

Activity:
- IT support addresses technical issues reported by users or identified through monitoring tools.
- System administrators perform routine maintenance tasks to ensure the smooth operation of the system.
