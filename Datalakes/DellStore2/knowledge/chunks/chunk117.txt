 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data analysts monitor product performance by analyzing sales data from the inventory table.
- Product managers update product information when necessary, adjusting the price, special offers, or common_prod_id.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers and assist them in placing orders.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries, update customer details in the customers table, and resolve any issues related to orders or products.
- HR managers maintain employee records, including customer-related information such as contact details and credit card data stored securely in the customers table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by specifying order details like order date, customerid, and netamount.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels in the inventory table and fulfill orders by adjusting quan_in_stock accordingly.
- Finance department records totalamount and tax for each order in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels by recording quan_in_stock for each product in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams monitor low stock levels using the reorder table and place orders to replenish inventory when necessary.

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff create orderlines by associating each product with its corresponding quantity in an order.
- Sales representatives update orderlines if a customer requests changes to their order, such as adding or removing items.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: HR Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- HR managers maintain a record of each customer's order history in the cust_hist table for future reference and analysis.
- Data analysts use the cust_hist table to generate reports on customer behavior and preferences.

## 7. Account Management

Performed By: Finance Department, IT Department

Activity:
- Finance department manages customer accounts by recording creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration, username, and "password" securely in the customers table.
- IT department ensures data security and privacy by implementing appropriate measures to protect sensitive customer information.
