 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their details (firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration, username, "password", age, income, gender) and credit card information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any changes in contact or billing details.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific categories (category) based on their preferences or purchasing history.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory specialists update product information (title, actor, price, special) when there are changes or promotions.
- Inventory specialists track product inventory levels (quan_in_stock, sales) to ensure accurate stock availability.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details (orderdate).
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate invoices (netamount, tax, totalamount) for customers.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items from inventory (prod_id, quan_in_stock) and prepare them for shipment.

## 4. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record the details of each order line item (orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity, orderdate) as they are prepared for shipment.
- Sales representatives update the status of orders and order lines when they are shipped or delivered to customers.

## 5. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with low stock (quan_low) and monitor vendor deliveries.
- Inventory specialists update inventory levels (quan_in_stock, sales) as new shipments arrive.

## 6. Reorder Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Inventory specialists track product reorders (prod_id, date_low, quan_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, date_expected) to ensure timely restocking of popular items.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions (orders, sales) and generate reports on revenue, expenses, and profitability.
