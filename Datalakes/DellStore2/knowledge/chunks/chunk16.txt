 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact details and account information. (customers)
- Customer service agents update customer profiles with changes in contact information or preferences. (customers)
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific regions based on their location. (customers, region)

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details and categorizing them. (categories, products)
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new shipments arrive or products are sold. (inventory)
- Product managers set special offers or promotions for selected products in the catalog. (products)

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog and specifying quantities. (orderlines)
- Sales representatives process customer orders and confirm order details with customers. (orders)

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for customer orders. (inventory)
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold. (reorder)

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack customer orders from the warehouse shelves. (inventory, orderlines)
- Shipping teams prepare shipments for delivery by labeling packages and arranging for transportation. (orders)

## 6. Financial Processing

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each customer order. (orders)
- Finance teams record financial transactions related to sales, expenses, and inventory costs. ("password")

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries and complaints regarding orders, products, or account issues. (customers)
- Customer service agents provide assistance with password resets or account recovery. ("password")
