 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on the required quantity of common_prod_id products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product details and handling order modifications.
- Customers place orders by specifying the desired products, quantities, and shipping information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Order Processors

Activity:
- Order processors review and confirm incoming customer orders against inventory levels.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment based on orderline details.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity of products in stock by updating quan_in_stock when items are received or shipped.
- Inventory managers monitor sales and reorder levels to ensure adequate stock for popular products.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to orders, including payments and revenue recognition.

## 6. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: HR Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- HR managers maintain customer records, including personal information, credit card details, and purchase history.
- Marketing teams analyze customer data to identify trends and opportunities for targeted promotions.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers set reorder levels for each product based on sales history and expected demand.
- Purchasing teams place reorders when inventory falls below the specified quan_low threshold for a given product.
