 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new products based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.
- Product managers update product information when changes occur, such as price adjustments or discontinuation.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details into the system during account creation, including name, address, contact information, and credit card data.
- HR managers maintain customer records, updating personal information, income, gender, age, and region as needed.
- Customers access their accounts to view order history, update profile information, or make payments.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity in stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams monitor low stock levels and issue purchase orders to replenish items when necessary.
- Warehouse staff update sales data as products are sold or returned.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by specifying the product, quantity, and order date.
- Customers place orders through the online store or over the phone.
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship ordered items to the customer's specified address.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product price, quantity, and applicable taxes.
- Finance teams record financial transactions in the system, including revenue, expenses, and payments.

## 6. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers set reorder points for each product based on historical sales data and inventory levels.
- The system automatically generates reorder recommendations when inventory falls below the reorder point.
- Purchasing teams review and approve reorder requests to ensure accurate forecasting and efficient supply chain management.

## 7. Data Classification and Compliance

Performed By: Data Management Teams

Activity:
- Data management teams use automated tools to discover sensitive data across the entire data catalog, such as personal, financial, or health-related information.
- Data management teams review and refine classifications for maximum accuracy, ensuring compliance with major regulations like GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, FERPA, and PCI.
- Data management teams apply consistent sensitivity labels to data sources and monitor classification levels automatically.
