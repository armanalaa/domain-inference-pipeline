 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set quan_low for each item to trigger reordering when stock reaches a minimum level.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, and creditcard information during account creation.
- Customer service agents update the contact information of existing customers when necessary.
- HR managers assign unique customerid for each registered user.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog and specifying quantities.
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers before processing them in the system.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders with orderid, orderdate, customerid, netamount, tax, and totalamount based on confirmed customer orders.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack the ordered items and update the quan_in_stock accordingly.

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff create orderlines with orderlineid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate for each item in an order.
- Sales representatives update the sales column when orders are shipped to customers.

## 6. Inventory Control

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff monitor the inventory level by tracking quan_in_stock and sales.
- Purchasing teams order new products based on the current stock levels and reorder history.

## 7. Reordering

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff update the date_low, quan_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected when products need to be replenished.
- Purchasing teams place orders for the required quantity of items based on the reorder information.

## 8. Data Privacy Compliance

Performed By: IT Administrators, HR Managers

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data privacy by deploying Dataedo on-premises or in a private cloud to keep sensitive information within the organization's environment.
- HR managers implement role-based access control and user authentication protocols to secure data catalogs.
