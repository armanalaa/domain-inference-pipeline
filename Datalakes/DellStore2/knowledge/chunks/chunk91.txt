 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new products based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers through demos or consultations to understand their needs.
- HR managers process new customer applications by verifying identity and creditworthiness.
- Customers place orders for products by specifying quantity, delivery address, and payment method.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items according to the order lines and ship them to the customer's specified address.
- Finance department records the order details in the orders table, calculates net amount, tax, and total amount based on the orderlines and prices.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams reorder low stock products based on the date_low and quan_low in the reorder table.

## 5. Customer Support

Performed By: Support Team

Activity:
- Support team responds to customer queries or complaints via email or live chat, aiming to resolve issues within 2 business days as per SLA.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Dataedo Success Manager

Activity:
- Dataedo Success Manager provides individual onboarding and review meetings to help customers smoothly transition from the trial to a standard license without losing progress.
