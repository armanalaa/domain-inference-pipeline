 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Dataedo Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- The Dataedo team scans data dictionary tables to identify and update product details.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card type, and income during registration.
- HR managers update customer details like age, gender, and region in the customers table.
- Customers access their accounts using a username and password for various actions.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives review and confirm the order details before it is recorded in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity of products in stock and update the inventory table accordingly.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low-stock items based on the reorder table.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered products from inventory.
- Shipping teams prepare and ship orders to customers using address details from the customers table.

## 6. Billing and Payment

Performed By: Finance, Customers

Activity:
- Finance records net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.
- Customers pay for their orders using credit cards or other payment methods.

## 7. Data Profiling and Quality Assurance

Performed By: Dataedo Team

Activity:
- The Dataedo team executes predefined data profiling SQL queries to analyze column data profiles.
- They test data quality by defining and executing custom tests for the DellStore2 database.
