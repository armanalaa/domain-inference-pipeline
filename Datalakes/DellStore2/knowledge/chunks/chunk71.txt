 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their details and credit card information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with address changes or contact preferences.
- Sales representatives assign customer IDs to newly registered clients.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Inventory specialists update product information when stock levels change or special offers are introduced.
- Product managers link common product IDs for variants of the same item.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders by selecting products, quantities, and customer details.
- Warehouse staff track order fulfillment by marking items as shipped and updating inventory levels.
- Finance department records the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists monitor stock levels by tracking quantities in stock and sales.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory items.
- Inventory specialists update reorder information for each product, including expected delivery dates.

## 5. Order Tracking

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service agents respond to customer inquiries about order status.
- Warehouse staff update order tracking details when items are shipped or delayed.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Data governance team assigns owners to every table and column for accountability.
- They track data quality issues, improvements, and compliance updates.
- Data governance team notifies owners when a question is asked or an issue flagged on their data assets.
