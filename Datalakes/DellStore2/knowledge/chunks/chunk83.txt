 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category managers update the product categories to ensure accurate organization of the product offerings.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as name, address, and contact information during registration.
- Customer service agents update customer profiles with any changes to personal or billing information.
- HR managers maintain records of customer age, income, gender, and credit card details for internal analysis.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders by linking customer profiles with order details like order date, net amount, tax, and total amount.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for each order.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when the quantity of a product falls below the reorder point.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory with the number of units in stock and sales data for each product.
- Purchasing teams monitor low inventory levels and initiate reorders when necessary.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on the orderlines and available inventory.
- Shipping agents prepare and dispatch orders to customers according to their specified shipping address.

## 6. Reorder Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams update the reorder table with the product ID, date low, quantity low, date reordered, and quantity reordered.
- Inventory managers monitor the expected delivery date for each reordered product.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams, Auditors

Activity:
- Finance teams generate reports on net amount, tax, total amount, and other financial metrics for each order.
- Auditors review the financial reports to ensure accuracy and compliance with regulations.
