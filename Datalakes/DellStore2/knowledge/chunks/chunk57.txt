 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for newly introduced or related products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details like firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and age during account creation.
- HR managers update income, gender, and region in the customers table upon employment changes.
- Customer service agents record customerid when handling support requests or complaints.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders for customers by specifying orderid, netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient stock for order fulfillment.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.
- Finance records orderdate, sales, and tax in the orders table upon order confirmation.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when products are shipped to customers or returned.
- Inventory managers monitor sales and adjust quan_in_stock accordingly.

## 5. Reordering

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers set date_low and quan_low for each product to trigger reorders when stock falls below a certain level.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders based on the date_reordered and quan_reordered in the reorder table.

## 6. Data Management

Performed By: IT Administrators, Database Administrators

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure data integrity by backing up the DellStore2 database regularly.
- Database administrators optimize database performance and implement security measures to protect sensitive customer information.
