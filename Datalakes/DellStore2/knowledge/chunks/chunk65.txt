 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set quan_low for each item to trigger reordering when stock reaches a certain level.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details into the customers table during onboarding.
- Customers place orders by specifying products, quantities, and shipping information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with new contact information or address changes.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Order Processing Team

Activity:
- Order processing team creates orderlines for each item in an order and calculates the netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Order processing team assigns a unique orderid to each customer order.

## 4. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory when products are shipped out or received from orders.
- Inventory staff monitor sales and adjust quan_in_stock accordingly.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Team

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.
- Shipping team labels packages with the correct orderid and shipping information.

## 6. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff track reorder levels and place orders when quan_reordered is reached.
- Purchasing teams negotiate prices with suppliers for the ordered items.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance records transactions related to orders, such as netamount, tax, and totalamount, in the orders table.
- Finance generates reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and customer behavior using data from multiple tables.
