 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product when new shipments arrive.
- Product managers monitor sales data from the orders table to determine which products need reordering.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and creditcard details.
- HR managers update the username and password for each customer account as needed.
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantity, and providing order date.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on customer orders and record netamount, tax, and totalamount in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items, updating the quan_in_stock column for each product accordingly.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on reorder data from the reorder table.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the sales column in the inventory table when products are sold.
- Purchasing teams monitor the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table to determine when to place new orders for low-stock items.

## 5. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams generate financial reports by analyzing data from the orders, inventory, and reorder tables.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- The data governance team ensures data quality by monitoring and enforcing data standards across all tables in DellStore2.
