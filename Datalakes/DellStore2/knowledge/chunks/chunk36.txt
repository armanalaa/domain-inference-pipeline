 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when receiving shipments for newly registered products.
- Product managers assign common_prod_id to similar products for easier categorization and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username during account creation.
- Customer service agents update customer information when customers contact them for changes or inquiries.
- HR managers verify and approve new customer accounts based on age, income, gender, and region criteria.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders by linking customerid, orderdate, and netamount for each order.
- Customers place orders by specifying productid and quantity from the catalog.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when shipping ordered products to customers.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff monitor sales by tracking sales for each productid in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when quan_low is reached for a given prod_id.
- Inventory staff update quan_reordered and date_expected when receiving new shipments.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff, Accountants

Activity:
- Finance staff calculate totalamount by adding netamount and tax for each order.
- Accountants record financial transactions related to orders in the orders table.

## 6. Data Quality Assurance

Performed By: Data Quality Analysts

Activity:
- Data quality analysts use data profiling to understand the quality of data stored in the database.
- They ensure high data quality and increase trust in data by implementing appropriate data governance practices.

## 7. Compliance and Security

Performed By: IT Administrators, Legal Teams

Activity:
- IT administrators deploy Dataedo on-premises or in private clouds to maintain full control over the data and meet security requirements.
- Legal teams ensure that data privacy and security are prioritized at every step, with no raw data ever sent outside the systems.
