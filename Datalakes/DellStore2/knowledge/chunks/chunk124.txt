 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams negotiate prices with suppliers for common products based on sales data.
- Product managers update product information when a supplier discontinues or introduces a new item.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering contact details and credit card information.
- HR managers assign usernames and passwords to new employees for accessing the system.
- Customers update their personal information, such as address or email, in their account settings.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders on behalf of customers by selecting products, quantities, and shipping addresses.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for order fulfillment.
- Customers confirm their orders and make payments through the online store.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the quantity in stock when products are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams reorder low-stock items based on sales data and expected delivery times.
- Warehouse staff record the date expected for restocked items in the reorder table.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on the orderlines table.
- Shipping teams prepare shipments by printing labels and arranging packages for delivery.
- Warehouse staff update the inventory when products are shipped out to customers.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on product prices and quantities.
- Sales representatives issue invoices to customers with the calculated totals and payment due dates.
- Finance teams record payments received from customers in the customers table.

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate sales reports based on order data for product performance analysis.
- Management reviews sales reports to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement.
- Data analysts create category reports to monitor the performance of different product categories.
