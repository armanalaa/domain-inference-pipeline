 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when receiving shipments of newly registered products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate customer profiles by entering customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income.
- Customer service agents update the username and password for customers who request account access changes.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantities, and providing order details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and record the netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order.
- Warehouse staff track orderlines and update quan_in_stock when shipping an order.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on the date_low and quan_low for each product.
- Inventory staff update sales when products are sold, and reorder products when they reach the date_expected.

## 5. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance records financial transactions related to orders, including netamount, tax, and totalamount.
