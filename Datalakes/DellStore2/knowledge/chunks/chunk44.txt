 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers assign common product IDs to new items based on their category.
- Category managers update the categories table with new or modified product categories.
- Product managers record product details such as title, actor, price, and special in the products table.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by entering their personal information into the customers table.
- HR managers update customer details like address, email, phone, and credit card information in the customers table.
- Customers access their account information and make changes to their profile using their username and password.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table with the quantity of each product in stock.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low-stock items based on the quan_low column in the reorder table.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by entering order details into the orders table.
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table with the quantity sold from each orderline.
- Customers track their orders using the orderid in the cust_hist and orders tables.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts for each order based on the totalamount and tax columns in the orders table.
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to orders in the appropriate ledger or accounting system.
