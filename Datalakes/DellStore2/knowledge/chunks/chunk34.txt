 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Governance Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- They update product details when necessary, including title, actor, and common_prod_id.
- The data governance team ensures all product information is accurate, secure, and trustworthy using Dataedo's data quality tools.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and password.
- HR managers update customer information such as age, income, gender, and credit card details.
- Customers manage their own accounts by modifying personal details and credit card information.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order dates.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and record the netamount, tax, totalamount, and customerid in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish stock when quan_in_stock falls below a certain threshold.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered products based on orderlines.
- Shipping teams prepare shipments, record tracking numbers, and update order statuses.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team generates invoices based on orders and records them in the appropriate system.

## 7. Reorder Process

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update reorder when quan_in_stock falls below a specified level.
- Purchasing teams process the reorder by recording date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected in the reorder table.
