 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- IT staff update common_prod_id for variants of a product.
- Product managers assign actors to products based on their role in the product's lifecycle.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new customer accounts with required details.
- HR managers update customer information as needed.
- Customers manage their account details such as address, email, and password.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory levels.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when new shipments arrive.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create and confirm orders for customers.
- Customers place orders through the online store or over the phone.
- Warehouse staff prepare and ship ordered products to customers.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to orders and inventory.

## 6. Reorder Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor inventory levels and update date_low and quan_low for products with low stock.
- Purchasing teams review reorder information and issue purchase orders to replenish stock.

## 7. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: IT Staff, Legal Teams

Activity:
- IT staff ensure data privacy and security by implementing appropriate measures.
- Legal teams review and update consent details as needed to comply with regulations.
