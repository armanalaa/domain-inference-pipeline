 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category managers update product categories to ensure accurate organization of the product catalog.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card information, and demographic data.
- HR managers maintain customer account information, including age, income, gender, and region.
- Customers update their personal information through the self-service portal.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details like shipping address and payment method.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the quantity in stock for each product as items are received or shipped out.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory levels based on sales data and reorder points.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.
- Shipping teams generate shipping labels, package orders, and arrange delivery with carriers.

## 6. Financial Transactions

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance records net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Team

Activity:
- Accounting team generates invoices based on order details and sends them to customers.

## 8. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards define and maintain the business glossary, ensuring consistent definitions across domains.
- IT administrators implement data lineage to provide transparency into data dependencies and downstream impact.
