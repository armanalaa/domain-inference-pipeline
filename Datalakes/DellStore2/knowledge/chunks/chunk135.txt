 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Authentication

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Support

Activity:
- Customer service representatives assist new customers in registering accounts by entering their personal details (firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, username).
- IT support staff secure customer passwords during account creation.
- Customer service representatives verify customer identities using provided credit card information and email addresses.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers categorize new items in the catalog (category) and assign them product codes, names, categories, and list prices (title, actor, price).
- Inventory specialists track product availability by monitoring quan_in_stock for each item (inventory).
- Product managers update common_prod_id when products are rebranded or discontinued.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog and select desired items (title), adding them to their shopping carts.
- Sales representatives assist customers with order placement by confirming product availability and calculating netamount, tax, and totalamount based on selected items and shipping destination.
- Customers finalize orders by providing payment information (creditcard, creditcardexpiration).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate order documents (orders) containing customer and product details, as well as netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory based on orderlineid and quantity.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by packing them into boxes and labeling them with orderid.

## 5. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for restocking low inventory (quan_in_stock).
- Warehouse staff receive shipments from suppliers and update quan_in_stock accordingly.
- Inventory specialists monitor reorder levels (date_low, quan_low) and initiate reorders when necessary.

## 6. Order Tracking and Delivery

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives update order statuses based on warehouse staff reports of shipping and delivery (orderlines).
- Warehouse staff track orders as they move through the fulfillment process (orderid, orderdate) and notify customers of estimated delivery dates.

## 7. Customer Service and Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Support

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries regarding orders, returns, and account issues.
- IT support staff troubleshoot technical problems related to the DellStore2 platform (email, password).

## 8. Performance Monitoring and Analysis

Performed By: System Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- System administrators configure and run driver programs to distribute workloads across multiple target machines (ds2webdriver.exe) based on specified parameters in configuration files.
- Data analysts analyze performance data collected by the driver program, including opm, response times, CPU%, etc., to identify areas for optimization.
