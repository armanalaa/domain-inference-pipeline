 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, record their contact information in the customers table, and follow up on leads.
- Customer service agents assist existing customers with queries, update customer details as necessary, and log interactions in the cust_hist table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by referencing the customers and inventory tables.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items based on orderlines and update the quan_in_stock column accordingly.
- Finance team records the netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on reorder data from the reorder table.
- Warehouse staff receive and store new inventory items, updating the quan_in_stock column as needed.

## 5. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team generates financial reports by querying the orders and customers tables for relevant data.

## 6. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: IT Department, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- IT department ensures that sensitive customer information is securely stored in the customers table and properly encrypted.
- Compliance officers monitor access to sensitive data and log any potential breaches or unauthorized activity.

## 7. Data Analysis and Insights

Performed By: Data Analysts, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Data analysts extract insights from the orders, customers, and inventory tables using various analytical techniques.
- Marketing team uses these insights to develop targeted marketing campaigns and strategies.
