 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or sold.
- Product managers set reorder points for each item based on date_low and quan_low.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives interact with customers to understand their needs and place orders accordingly.
- HR managers maintain customer records, including firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income.
- Customers create accounts by providing necessary personal information and setting up a username and password.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders based on customer requests and order history.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items according to the orderlines table, ensuring correct quantity and product IDs.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products not in stock, updating inventory accordingly.

## 4. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table.
- They also track sales data from the inventory table to analyze performance and make strategic decisions.

## 5. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the sales column in the inventory table when products are sold.
- Purchasing teams reorder items when they reach the specified quan_low and update the quan_reordered and date_reordered columns in the reorder table.

## 6. Data Management

Performed By: IT Staff, Dataedo Users

Activity:
- IT staff maintain the database schema and ensure data integrity across tables.
- Dataedo users document the database structure and provide easy access to this information for all business users.
