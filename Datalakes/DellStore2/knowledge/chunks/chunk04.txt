 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Categorization

Performed By: Category Managers

Activity:
- Assign products to appropriate categories based on their content or genre. (categories)
- Update category names and descriptions as needed to reflect changes in product offerings. (categories)

## 2. New Product Registration

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Input new product details, including title, actor, price, and common product ID. (products)
- Assign the product to a category for easy browsing by customers. (products, categories)

## 3. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Create new customer accounts with required personal information such as name, address, email, and phone number. (customers)
- Set up credit card details for the customer's account. (customers)

## 4. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers

Activity:
- Browse products by category or search for specific items. (products, categories)
- Add desired products to the shopping cart and proceed to checkout. (orderlines)
- Enter shipping and billing information to complete the order. (customers, orders)

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Retrieve ordered items from inventory based on order details. (inventory, orderlines)
- Package the items for shipping and update their stock levels accordingly. (inventory)

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Monitor product quantities in real-time to ensure adequate stock levels. (inventory)
- Reorder low-stock items when necessary, providing expected delivery dates. (reorder)

## 7. Order Fulfillment and Delivery

Performed By: Shipping Department

Activity:
- Prepare orders for shipping by attaching labels and addressing packages appropriately.
- Coordinate with courier services to arrange pickups and track deliveries. (orders)
