 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product details when necessary, including title, actor, common_prod_id, and special status.
- Monitor product sales to track inventory levels and adjust quan_in_stock accordingly.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Onboard new customers by entering their personal details (firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone) and credit card information.
- Update customer records with age, income, gender, and contact preferences.
- Maintain a history of customer orders (cust_hist).

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Track inventory levels for each product based on quan_in_stock.
- Replenish stock when quan_in_stock falls below a predetermined threshold.
- Record sales to update the sales column in the inventory table.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details (orderdate, customerid).
- Record order information in the orders table, including netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Generate orderlines to detail each product included in an order.

## 5. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Monitor inventory levels to determine when products need to be reordered.
- Set low quantity thresholds (quan_low) and expected delivery dates (date_expected).
- Update the reorder table with relevant information when a reorder is initiated.

## 6. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Finance, Data Governance Teams

Activity:
- Generate reports based on data from various tables (customers, orders, orderlines, inventory, products).
- Analyze sales trends to identify opportunities for growth and improvement.
- Ensure compliance with regulations by maintaining accurate and traceable data lineage.
