 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or existing ones are sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, and credit card information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any changes to contact or payment information.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping details.
- Sales representatives process orders for customers who require assistance.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on orderlines.
- Shipping agents prepare shipments for delivery by recording tracking numbers and shipping dates.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department generates invoices based on orders, calculating net amount, tax, and total amount.

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Customers pay for their orders using the provided payment methods.
- The finance department records payments and updates customer accounts accordingly.

## 7. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for products with low stock levels.
- Warehouse staff receive and store incoming shipments from suppliers.

## 8. Reorder Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor inventory levels and reorder products when they reach a predetermined threshold.

## 9. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business analysts generate reports on sales, inventory, and customer behavior for analysis and decision making.
- Management reviews reports to make informed decisions about pricing, marketing, and product offerings.
