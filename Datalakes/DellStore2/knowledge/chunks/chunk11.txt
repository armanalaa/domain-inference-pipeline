 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Support Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers and register new accounts by recording customer details such as name, address, contact information, and credit card details.
- Customer support agents assist existing customers in managing their accounts, updating personal information, and resolving any issues related to orders or payments.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details like title, category, actor, price, special features, and common product ID.
- Inventory specialists track inventory levels and update the system when stock quantities change.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives (if assisting)

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog, select items, and place orders by specifying quantity for each item and providing order details such as shipping address and payment information.
- Sales representatives may help customers with their orders, ensuring accurate product selection and smooth checkout process.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Order Processing Team

Activity:
- Upon receiving an order, warehouse staff pick the items from inventory and prepare them for shipping.
- The order processing team reviews each order, calculates net amount, tax, and total amount, and updates the system with the new order details.

## 5. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and place orders for restocking when quantities reach a predefined threshold.
- Warehouse staff receive new shipments, count the items, and update the system with the updated stock quantities.

## 6. Shipping and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Courier Services

Activity:
- Once an order is ready for shipping, warehouse staff package the items and hand them over to courier services for delivery.
- Courier services transport the packages to the specified addresses and update the system with tracking information and delivery status.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Support Agents

Activity:
- Customer support agents handle customer inquiries related to orders, returns, refunds, or any other issues that may arise during the purchasing process.

## 8. Reordering and Inventory Forecasting

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Inventory specialists monitor sales data and reorder items when they reach a predefined low quantity threshold.
- Data analysts forecast future inventory needs based on historical sales data and market trends to ensure optimal stock levels.
