 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- IT staff maintain the product information by updating the title, actor, special, and common_prod_id as needed.
- Product managers monitor inventory levels and update product details when stock becomes depleted or replenished (inventory).

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card type, and password during account creation or updates.
- HR managers update customer details like age, income, gender, and region when necessary.
- Customers access their accounts to view order history, change personal information, or make payments (creditcard).

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details like shipping address and payment method.
- Sales representatives review and confirm customer orders before they are processed (orders).

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track the quantity in stock for each product and update it accordingly when items are received or shipped out (inventory).
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low inventory levels based on sales data and reorder points (reorder).

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items, update the quan_in_stock for each product, and record the orderlines.
- Sales representatives or customer service agents confirm shipment and provide tracking information to customers (orders).

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff calculate the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices, quantities, and taxes (orders).
- Finance staff record financial transactions related to orders, such as payments and refunds.

## 7. Data Governance & Analytics

Performed By: IT Staff, Business Analysts

Activity:
- IT staff document the data ecosystem, including relationships and data flows, using a data catalog tool (metadata).
- Business analysts use the documented data to gain insights, make data-driven decisions, and prepare data for AI & ML models (metadata).
