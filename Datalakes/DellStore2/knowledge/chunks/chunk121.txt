 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column for each product when items are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering contact information, credit card details, and other relevant data from potential clients.
- HR managers update customer profiles with personal details, employment history, and income levels.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders for customers based on their selections and submit them to the warehouse for fulfillment.
- Warehouse staff pick items from inventory, pack orders, and update the orderlines table with product IDs, quantities, and order dates.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records each order in the orders table, calculating net amount, tax, and total amount based on product prices, quantities, and shipping costs.

## 5. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Customers pay for their orders using credit cards or other payment methods, which are recorded in the customers table.
- Finance department verifies payments and updates order statuses accordingly.

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and place orders for replenishments when necessary.
- Warehouse staff receive shipments from suppliers, update the inventory table with new quantities, and process incoming products into stock.

## 7. Reorder Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Inventory managers set reorder points for each product based on historical sales data and expected demand.
- When inventory levels fall below the reorder point, inventory managers generate purchase orders to replenish stock.

## 8. Security and Compliance

Performed By: IT Department, Dataedo Team

Activity:
- IT department ensures secure connections and implements encryption practices for data protection.
- Dataedo team performs regular security testing and vulnerability response to maintain the system's integrity.
