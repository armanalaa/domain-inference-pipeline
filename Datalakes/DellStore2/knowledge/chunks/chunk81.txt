 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when products are received or shipped out.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income during new account creation.
- Customer service agents update customer information when customers contact them for changes or inquiries.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products and quantities from the catalog.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and generate orderids.
- Warehouse staff track orderlines as they prepare and ship orders.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Agents

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on the orderlines.
- Shipping agents assign carrier services and generate shipping labels for orders.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance records netamount, tax, and totalamount for each order in the orders table.

## 6. Inventory Monitoring

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish products when quan_in_stock falls below a certain level.
- Inventory staff update sales and quan_in_stock as products are sold or received.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff monitor reorder to determine when to restock low-quantity items.
- Purchasing teams place orders for the reordered products based on date_reordered and quan_reordered.

## 8. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Analysts, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data analysts use Dataedo to catalog tables, views, dashboards, and reports in the centralized data catalog for easier discovery and validation.
- IT administrators ensure that all business definitions, calculation rules, and metrics are published to the Dataedo Portal for transparency and understanding across teams.
