 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact information and demographic details.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes to contact information or account status.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific regions based on their location.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when products are received or sold.
- Product managers mark common products as such for easier identification and management.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by associating customers with specific products and quantities.
- Customers place orders through the online store or over the phone.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping once they are ready.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and sales to ensure accurate stock counts.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.
- Warehouse staff receive and record new shipments of products.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on the items requested and their current inventory levels.
- Shipping teams prepare orders for delivery by printing shipping labels and arranging for courier pickup.
- Warehouse staff update order statuses to reflect that they have been shipped.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order based on the products ordered and their prices.
- Accounting staff record financial transactions related to orders, including payments from customers and expenses related to shipping and inventory management.
- Finance teams generate reports on sales, expenses, and profitability for analysis and decision-making purposes.

## 7. Reordering Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor inventory levels and reorder products when they fall below a certain threshold.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for the necessary quantities of each product to be reordered.
- Inventory managers update expected delivery dates for reordered products.
