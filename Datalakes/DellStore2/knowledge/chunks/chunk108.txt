 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on inventory levels and demand forecasts.
- Product managers update product details when new versions or variations are released.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, providing product information and assisting with orders.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries, address concerns, and process returns or refunds.
- Marketing personnel create targeted campaigns to attract new customers and retain existing ones.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm orders with customers and update the order status in the system.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment based on order details.
- Finance teams record the order details, calculate taxes, and generate invoices.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels as items are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams monitor stock levels and place reorders when quantities drop below the minimum threshold.
- Inventory managers analyze sales data to forecast demand and optimize stock levels.

## 5. Account Management

Performed By: Account Managers, Finance Teams

Activity:
- Account managers handle account renewals or upgrades for existing customers.
- Finance teams process payments from customers and update their accounts accordingly.
- Customer service agents address billing inquiries and disputes.

## 6. Technical Support

Performed By: Technical Support Specialists

Activity:
- Technical support specialists assist customers with product-related issues or technical questions.
- They troubleshoot problems, provide solutions, and escalate complex issues when necessary.

## 7. Data Management

Performed By: Data Analysts, IT Staff

Activity:
- Data analysts gather and analyze data from various sources to generate reports and insights.
- IT staff maintain the database infrastructure, ensuring its security, performance, and scalability.
- They also implement new features or improvements based on business needs.
