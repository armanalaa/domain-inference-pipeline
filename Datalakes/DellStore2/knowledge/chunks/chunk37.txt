 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product details when necessary, including title, actor, price, special, and common_prod_id.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Register new customers by providing firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and creditcard.
- Update customer details such as username, password, age, income, gender, creditcardexpiration.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Track the quantity of products in stock (quan_in_stock) and sales for each product.
- Update inventory levels when products are sold or received.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Place orders by specifying orderid, orderdate, customerid, netamount, tax, totalamount, and related orderlines (orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity).
- Update the orders table with date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected when necessary.

## 5. Reordering Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Monitor product inventory levels to determine when products need to be reordered (date_low, quan_low).
- Update the reorder table with date_reordered and quan_reordered for each product.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Record financial transactions related to orders in the orders table (netamount, tax, totalamount).
- Maintain accurate data lineage and quality through regular audits and updates.

## 7. Business Glossary Management

Performed By: Business Users

Activity:
- Agree on vocabulary before discussing data by maintaining a business glossary with definitions and mapping to the data.
- Link definitions to their actual technical data and meaning for easy understanding.
