 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update the quantity in stock for each product based on incoming shipments or sales.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar products for easier categorization and management.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as name, address, contact information, credit card data, and account information into the customers table.
- Customer service agents update customer profiles with changes to personal or billing information.
- HR managers add employee details related to a customer (e.g., age, income, gender) to the customers table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by specifying the order date, customer ID, and products with their respective quantities.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment based on order details from the orderlines table.
- Finance department calculates the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Inventory Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory staff monitor product quantities in stock by referring to the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams place orders to replenish low-stock items based on the quan_low column in the reorder table.
- Inventory staff update the sales column in the inventory table when products are sold.

## 5. Reference Data Management

Performed By: Data Administrators, IT Department

Activity:
- Data administrators define and document lookup values for categories, currencies, units of measure, departments, statuses, calendars, and more.
- The IT department ensures that the defined lookups are correctly linked to multiple columns in relevant tables.
- Data administrators explain values with titles, descriptions, and custom fields for better understanding across the organization.
