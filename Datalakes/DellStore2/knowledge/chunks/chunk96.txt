 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product information when necessary, such as changing the price or category.
- Monitor sales data to identify popular products and adjust inventory accordingly.

## 2. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Track current stock levels by updating quan_in_stock for each product.
- Issue purchase orders when inventory falls below a certain threshold (quan_low).
- Reorder products based on reorder data (date_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, date_expected).

## 3. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Register new customers by recording their details (firstname, lastname, address1, etc.) and creating a unique customerid.
- Update customer information as needed, such as changes in contact details or credit card information.
- Process orders for existing customers, linking orderid to the relevant customerid.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Create new orders by recording the orderid, customerid, netamount, tax, and totalamount.
- Associate each order with relevant products through orderlines (orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity).
- Update order status as it progresses through the fulfillment process.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Record financial transactions related to orders, such as netamount and totalamount.
- Calculate taxes based on relevant rules or regulations.
- Maintain a history of customer transactions (cust_hist) for future reference and analysis.
