 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on inventory levels and demand forecasts.
- Product managers update product information when receiving updated specifications or feedback from customers.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers, providing product recommendations and processing orders.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries, process returns, and manage complaints.
- HR managers recruit new employees, onboard them, and maintain employee records.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers and update the order status in the system.
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship orders to customers based on the order details.
- Finance teams record the order details, calculate taxes, and generate invoices for customers.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when receiving new shipments or processing returns.
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and place reorders when the quantity falls below a predefined threshold.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams, Accountants

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions, update account balances, and generate financial reports.
- Accountants review financial records for accuracy and compliance with accounting standards.

## 6. Security Management

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators implement security measures to protect the database from unauthorized access or data breaches.
- Data analysts monitor system logs for suspicious activities and take appropriate action when necessary.

## 7. Performance Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Data analysts extract and analyze sales data to identify trends, patterns, and areas for improvement.
- Marketing teams use the insights gained from the analysis to optimize marketing strategies and improve customer engagement.
