 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their first name, last name, address details, email, phone number, credit card type, and password.
- HR managers update customer information such as age, income, gender, and region when necessary.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, list price, and common product ID.
- Inventory specialists update product information like quantity in stock and sales when necessary.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing order details such as shipping address.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and record the net amount, tax, total amount, and order date.

## 4. Order Line Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quantity in stock when an order is fulfilled.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products with low quantities based on reorder information like date_low and quan_low.

## 5. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive and record new shipments of products, updating the quantity in stock accordingly.
- Purchasing teams update reorder information like date_reordered and quan_reordered when a purchase order is fulfilled.

## 6. Order Fulfillment and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and prepare orders for shipping.
- Shipping teams record the shipment details like tracking number and delivery date.

## 7. Customer Support and Complaints Handling

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer complaints by recording the issue, providing solutions, and updating the customer's history accordingly.

## 8. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales, inventory levels, customer behavior, and other key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about pricing, marketing strategies, and operational improvements.
