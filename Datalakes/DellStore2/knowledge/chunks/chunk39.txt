 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new products based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with customers to understand their needs and preferences.
- HR managers maintain customer records, including personal details, contact information, and credit card data.
- Customers place orders by specifying products, quantities, shipping addresses, and payment methods.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and pick items for customer orders.
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers and update the order status in the system.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record the quantity of each product in stock and update sales data.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when inventory levels fall below a predefined threshold.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance records order details, calculates net amount, tax, and total amount for each order.

## 6. Customer Service

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries related to orders, returns, or product information.

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business analysts generate reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and customer behavior.
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about pricing, marketing strategies, and operational efficiency.
