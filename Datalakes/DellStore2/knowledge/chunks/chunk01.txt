 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Category Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Category managers assign products to appropriate categories based on their genre or type.
- Product managers update product details such as price, special offers, and common product IDs when necessary.

## 2. Customer Registration and Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives help new customers create accounts by capturing their personal information (name, address, email, phone number), credit card details, and setting up a username and password.
- HR managers verify customer identities and approve or deny account creation based on age, income, and other eligibility criteria.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog, select desired products, and add them to their shopping carts.
- Sales representatives assist customers with any issues or questions during the ordering process.
- Customers confirm their orders by providing shipping information and completing the checkout process.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory shelves based on order details.
- Inventory managers update product quantities in stock after fulfilling orders.

## 5. Order Fulfillment and Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shippers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff package the ordered items securely for shipping.
- Shippers determine the most efficient method of delivery (e.g., standard mail, expedited shipping) based on customer preferences and order details.

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor stock levels and reorder products when quantities drop below a predetermined threshold.
- Purchasing teams negotiate with suppliers for the best prices and delivery times for new inventory.

## 7. Customer Support and Complaints Handling

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, HR Managers

Activity:
- Customer service representatives address customer inquiries or complaints regarding orders, products, or account issues.
- HR managers investigate and resolve any disputes or fraudulent activities that may arise during the customer interaction process.
