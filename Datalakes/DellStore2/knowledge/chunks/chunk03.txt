 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price. (products)
- Inventory staff update the quantity in stock for each product based on incoming shipments or sales. (inventory)
- Product managers set reorder levels for each product, specifying the minimum quantity that triggers a reorder. (reorder)

## 2. Category Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Product managers assign categories to products based on their content or genre. (categories)
- Data analysts monitor sales trends across different product categories to identify popular and underperforming areas.

## 3. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, IT Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives assist new customers in creating accounts by capturing their personal information, credit card details, and setting up usernames and passwords. (customers)

## 4. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog, select desired products, and add them to their shopping cart.
- Sales representatives help customers with complex orders or special requests, adjusting quantities, product options, and delivery details as needed. (orderlines)

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on the customer's order and send them to the customer for review.
- Customer service representatives address any issues or concerns raised by customers during the order review process.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders according to the details provided in the orderlines table, ensuring each item is correctly accounted for.

## 6. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare shipments by bundling products, applying shipping labels, and arranging them in appropriate packaging.
- Shipping staff coordinate with carriers to schedule pickups and track the progress of orders en route to customers.

## 7. Invoice Settlement

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order in the orders table.
- Finance staff reconcile payments received from customers with outstanding invoices, marking them as paid in the orders table.

## 8. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and complaints regarding orders, products, or account information. (customers)
- Customer service representatives update customer records with any changes to contact details or preferences.
