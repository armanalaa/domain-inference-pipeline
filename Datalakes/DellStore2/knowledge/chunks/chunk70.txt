 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when receiving shipments of newly registered products.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for each product based on its unique features and market positioning.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts by entering firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username.
- HR managers update creditcard, creditcardexpiration, age, income, gender, and other personal details as needed.
- Customers place orders by selecting products from the catalog, specifying quantity, and providing orderdate.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orders with orderid, netamount, tax, totalamount, and customerid based on customer orders.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient inventory for fulfilling orders.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when quan_in_stock falls below a certain threshold.
- Finance records invoices and payments related to each order.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update sales when products are sold to customers.
- Purchasing teams monitor sales and reorder products when date_low is reached or quan_low is depleted.

## 5. Compliance and Audit

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance, External Auditors

Activity:
- HR managers maintain sensitive customer information in accordance with data privacy regulations.
- Finance ensures accurate record keeping for audit purposes.
- External auditors review the database to verify compliance with GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, and other regulations.
