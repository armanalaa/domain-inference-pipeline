 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on inventory levels and expected demand.
- Product managers update product details when changes occur, such as price adjustments or discontinuation.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customers place orders through the online store using their personal information stored in the customers table.
- Customer service agents handle order inquiries, address issues, and provide updates on order statuses.
- HR managers maintain employee records, including customer service representatives' contact details and performance evaluations.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive and store incoming products according to the quan_in_stock column in the inventory table.
- Warehouse staff track product quantities as they are sold or returned, updating the sales column in the inventory table.
- Purchasing teams monitor inventory levels and reorder products when the quantity falls below the quan_low threshold.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by generating order IDs and populating the cust_hist table with relevant details.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered products for shipment based on the orderlines table.
- Customer service agents confirm order shipments to customers, updating the orders table with tracking information.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate net amounts due from customers based on order details and prices stored in the orders and products tables.
- Finance teams apply taxes according to customer location and product category, updating the tax column in the orders table.
- Finance teams record total amounts received from customers after taxes, updating the totalamount column in the orders table.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: IT Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- IT administrators maintain data integrity by ensuring accurate and consistent data across all tables.
- Data analysts document key information about databases, tables, columns, relationships, and data models using tools like Data Dictionary & Data Catalog.
- Data analysts publish this documentation to the analyst community for easy access and understanding of the DellStore2 database structure.
