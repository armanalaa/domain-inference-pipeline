 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Inventory staff update quan_in_stock when receiving shipments of newly registered products.
- Product managers set special and common_prod_id for each item based on its features and market positioning.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, HR Managers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer profiles with firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username.
- Customer service agents update customer details when customers contact them for changes or inquiries.
- HR managers add creditcard, creditcardexpiration, age, income, gender to customer profiles during onboarding.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, Finance

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders by specifying orderid, netamount, tax, totalamount, and customerid.
- Warehouse staff track quan_in_stock to ensure sufficient stock for each order.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when inventory levels are low.
- Finance records orderdate, sales, and updates the reorder table with date_low, quan_low, date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orderlines based on orderid and prod_id.
- Shipping department assigns shipping labels and sends orders to customers.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance

Activity:
- Finance generates invoices for each order using customer details, order information, and sales data.

## 6. Data Governance

Performed By: Data Stewards, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data stewards maintain the business glossary by defining terms, KPIs, policies, and rules linked to relevant data assets.
- IT administrators ensure user authentication through enterprise identity systems like Active Directory, SAML, and OAuth2.

## 7. Compliance and Security

Performed By: Compliance Officers, Data Privacy Team

Activity:
- Compliance officers monitor data usage to maintain industry standards and support regulatory compliance.
- Data privacy team ensures data privacy by design, keeping all governance data within the organization's environment.
