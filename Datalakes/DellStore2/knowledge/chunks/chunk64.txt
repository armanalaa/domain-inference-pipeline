 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or existing ones are sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives engage with potential customers to understand their needs and recommend suitable products.
- Customer service agents assist customers with inquiries about orders, returns, or account information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Order Processors

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products and providing shipping details.
- Sales representatives review and confirm customer orders.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment based on order details.
- Order processors generate invoices, update inventory levels, and mark orders as shipped.

## 4. Financial Management

Performed By: Accounting Staff, Finance Managers

Activity:
- Accounting staff record transactions related to sales, returns, and payments.
- Finance managers analyze financial data to monitor performance and make strategic decisions.

## 5. Reordering and Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and notify purchasing teams when stock is low.
- Purchasing teams order additional products to maintain optimal inventory levels.

## 6. Compliance and Regulatory Reporting

Performed By: HR Managers, Legal Team

Activity:
- HR managers ensure employee data is accurate and up-to-date in compliance with regulations.
- The legal team generates reports for regulatory bodies as required.

## 7. Data Governance and Lineage

Performed By: Data Governance Team

Activity:
- The data governance team documents the lineage, quality, and context of data across all processes to ensure compliance and transparency.
