 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- IT team updates the common_prod_id for a product when it undergoes a rebranding or renaming.
- Product managers mark special products with appropriate tags to highlight promotions or seasonal offers.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents, IT Team

Activity:
- Sales representatives input customer details such as name, address, contact information, and credit card data.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes in personal or billing information.
- IT team ensures the security of sensitive customer data by implementing encryption and access controls.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping details.
- Sales representatives process orders for customers with special requests or complex order requirements.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams, IT Team

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the quantity in stock for each product as items are received or shipped out.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers when inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.
- IT team automates reordering of products based on sales data and predefined thresholds.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders according to the orderlines and shipping details.
- Shipping teams prepare and ship orders to customers, updating the order status accordingly.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Team, IT Team

Activity:
- Accounting team calculates the net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices and shipping costs.
- IT team generates invoices using the calculated amounts and customer details from the orders and customers tables.

## 7. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Team

Activity:
- Customers pay their invoices using the provided payment methods (credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
- Finance team records payments and updates customer accounts accordingly.

## 8. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business analysts use data from orders, customers, inventory, and products to generate reports and insights for decision making.
- Management reviews the reports and makes strategic decisions based on the analysis.
