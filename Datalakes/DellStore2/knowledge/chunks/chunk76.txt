 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact details and demographic information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes to contact information or account status.
- Sales representatives assign each customer a unique username and password for account access.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, list prices, and other relevant details.
- Inventory specialists update product information when stock levels change or products go on sale.
- Product managers assign common product IDs to similar items for easier tracking and management.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.
- Sales representatives process orders by confirming availability, calculating totals, and finalizing transactions.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory and prepare them for shipping.
- Shipping teams package orders and arrange for delivery to the customer's specified address.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists track stock levels by updating quantities in real time as items are sold or received.
- Purchasing teams order new inventory when stock levels fall below predetermined thresholds.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance department records net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Accounting staff generates invoices based on order data and processes payments from customers.

## 7. Reordering and Forecasting

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Inventory specialists monitor stock levels and reorder items when quantities fall below minimum thresholds.
- Data analysts forecast future demand based on historical sales data and adjust inventory orders accordingly.
