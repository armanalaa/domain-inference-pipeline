 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update quan_in_stock when items are received or shipped.
- Product managers set reorder points for each item based on date_low and quan_low.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and username into the customers table.
- Customer service agents update customer information when changes occur.
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by specifying orderdate, netamount, tax, and totalamount in the orders table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives add products to customer orders by referencing prod_id, quantity, and orderdate in the orderlines table.
- Warehouse staff remove items from inventory when they are shipped to customers.
- Finance department records invoices for each order, including orderid, netamount, tax, and totalamount.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update sales when items are sold to customers.
- Purchasing teams place orders for new inventory based on quan_in_stock and reorder points.
- Warehouse staff receive and record new inventory, updating quan_in_stock accordingly.

## 5. Reordering and Forecasting

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Purchasing teams update date_reordered, quan_reordered, and date_expected when new inventory is ordered.
- Finance department forecasts future sales based on historical data from the orders table.

## 6. Compliance and Data Governance

Performed By: HR Managers, IT Department

Activity:
- HR managers ensure customer information complies with privacy regulations such as FERPA and GDPR.
- IT department maintains visibility into where sensitive data resides and how it moves across campus systems.

## 7. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Researchers, Administrators, Compliance Teams

Activity:
- Researchers analyze customer behavior patterns using data from the orders table.
- Administrators generate reports on student outcomes, financial aid, and accreditation data.
- Compliance teams respond to audits by validating metrics and providing stakeholders with confidence in every report.
