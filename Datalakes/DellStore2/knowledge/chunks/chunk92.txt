 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Data stewards maintain product information accuracy by updating common_prod_id, actor, price, special when necessary.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers, Finance

Activity:
- Sales representatives create customer accounts with firstname, lastname, address1, address2, city, state, zip, country, region, email, phone, creditcardtype, creditcard, creditcardexpiration, username, and password.
- HR managers update customer details such as age, income, gender.
- Customers place orders by providing orderdate, netamount, tax, totalamount.
- Finance records payments for each order.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels (quan_in_stock) and process orders by updating sales.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock based on date_low and quan_low.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Warehouse staff record the quantity of each product in stock (quan_in_stock).
- Data stewards update reorder levels (date_reordered, quan_reordered, date_expected) when necessary.

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives create order lines for each customer order with orderlineid, orderid, prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.
- Warehouse staff update the quantity of each product sold (sales) when an order is fulfilled.

## 6. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack customer orders based on orderid.

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance

Activity:
- Finance generates invoices for each order with netamount, tax, totalamount.

## 8. Customer History Tracking

Performed By: Data Stewards, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Data stewards record customer purchase history in cust_hist with customerid, orderid, and prod_id.
- Sales representatives use this data to analyze customer behavior and preferences.

## 9. Category Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Data Stewards

Activity:
- Product managers create and manage product categories (category, categoryname).
- Data stewards update category information when necessary.
