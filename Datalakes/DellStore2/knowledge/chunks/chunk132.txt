 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and complaints by accessing customer details from the customers table.
- Sales representatives register new customers by entering their personal information, contact details, and credit card information into the customers table.
- Sales representatives update customer information when necessary, such as changes to address or credit card details.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the product catalog by entering product code, name, category, list price, and other relevant information into the products table.
- Inventory specialists track inventory levels by monitoring quan_in_stock in the inventory table for each product.
- Inventory specialists update sales data in the inventory table when a sale is made.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders on behalf of customers by entering order details (customerid, orderdate, netamount, tax, totalamount) into the orders table.
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment by checking inventory levels in the inventory table and updating quan_in_stock as necessary.
- Finance department records financial transactions related to each order, such as payments and refunds.

## 4. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products that are running low on stock by checking the date_low and quan_low in the reorder table.
- Warehouse staff receive and store incoming inventory, updating quan_in_stock in the inventory table accordingly.

## 5. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Business Intelligence Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business intelligence analysts generate reports on sales performance, customer behavior, and inventory levels by querying data from multiple tables (orders, customers, products, inventory).
- Management reviews these reports to make informed decisions about business strategy.
