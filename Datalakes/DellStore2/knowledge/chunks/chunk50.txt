 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the quan_in_stock column when products are received or shipped.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as firstname, lastname, address, email, phone, creditcardtype, and income into the customers table during account creation or updates.
- HR managers maintain up-to-date contact information for each customer in the customers table.
- Customers place orders by providing order details like orderdate, netamount, tax, totalamount, and shipping address in the orders table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate orderlines for each customer's order by specifying prod_id, quantity, and orderdate.
- Warehouse staff retrieve the specified products from inventory and update the quan_in_stock column accordingly.
- The finance department records the order details in the orders table and calculates the netamount, tax, and totalamount based on the orderlines.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the inventory table when products are received or shipped, adjusting the quan_in_stock column accordingly.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish low stock levels based on the date_low and quan_low columns in the reorder table.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack customer orders according to the orderlines and ship them to the specified address.
- The shipping department updates the orderdate, orderid, and orderstatus in the orders table when an order is shipped.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department generates invoices for each customer's order based on the order details in the orders table and sends them to the customer's email address.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries, update customer contact information as needed, and escalate issues to relevant teams when necessary.
