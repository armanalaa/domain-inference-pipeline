 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products arrive or existing ones are sold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as first name, last name, address, email, phone, credit card information, and age during registration.
- HR managers maintain updated contact and personal information for each customer.
- Customers update their own account details through the self-service portal.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping information.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order details and process payments.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on inventory levels and sales forecasts.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the quantity in stock for each product after shipments are received or dispatched.
- Purchasing teams reorder products when the quantity in stock falls below a predefined threshold.

## 6. Order Fulfillment and Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Providers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by packaging items and affixing shipping labels.
- Shipping providers deliver orders to customers according to the specified address and delivery method.

## 7. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record order details, calculate net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Finance teams update account balances and generate invoices for customers.

## 8. Data Governance and Compliance

Performed By: Data Governance Teams

Activity:
- Data governance teams ensure that all data is accurate, secure, and compliant with relevant regulations such as GDPR, CPPA, and HIPAA.
- Data governance teams document data lineage and impact analysis for AI models to maintain transparency and compliance.
