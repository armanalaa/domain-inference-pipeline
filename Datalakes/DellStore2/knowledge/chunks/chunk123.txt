 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams negotiate prices with suppliers for common products and update the price column accordingly.
- Product managers assign actors to each product and update the actor column as needed.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new customer accounts by entering first name, last name, address details, email, phone, credit card type, and other relevant information.
- HR managers update customer details such as age, income, gender, and region when necessary.
- Customers manage their own account information through the self-service portal.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders on behalf of customers by selecting products, quantities, and order dates from the inventory.
- Customers browse the catalog, add items to their cart, and submit orders through the self-service portal.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from stock and update the quan_in_stock column accordingly.
- Sales representatives generate orderlines by associating each product with its respective quantity, order date, and customer ID.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff package ordered products for shipment and update the sales column to reflect the fulfilled orders.
- Shipping teams prepare shipping labels, track packages, and update orderlines with tracking numbers.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team calculates net amount, tax, and total amount for each order based on product prices, quantities, and taxes.
- Finance team generates invoices and sends them to customers via email or other communication channels.

## 7. Reordering

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Inventory managers monitor stock levels and update the quan_in_stock column when necessary.
- Purchasing teams reorder low-stock products by setting date_low, quan_low, and initiating the reordering process.

## 8. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract data from various tables to create reports on sales, inventory levels, customer behavior, and other key performance indicators.
- Management reviews these reports to make informed decisions about product offerings, pricing strategies, and operational improvements.
