 # DellStore2 — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their contact details and account information.
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes to contact information or account status.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific sales regions based on their location.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details and categorizing them.
- Inventory specialists update product information when stock levels change or products go out of production.
- Product managers mark common products as such for easier customer browsing.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog and specifying quantities.
- Sales representatives process orders by confirming availability, calculating prices, and recording order details.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Specialists

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick items from inventory based on order details.
- Shipping specialists package orders for delivery and record shipping information.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Specialists, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Inventory specialists update stock levels when items are sold or received.
- Purchasing teams order replenishments based on low inventory thresholds and expected demand.

## 6. Financial Transactions

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance department calculates net amounts, taxes, and total amounts for each order.
- Accounting staff records financial transactions in the system and generates invoices for customers.

## 7. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries related to orders, products, or account issues.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any relevant support notes.

## 8. Compliance and Legal Matters

Performed By: Legal Department, Dataedo Team

Activity:
- Legal department reviews and manages contracts and agreements with customers and partners.
- Dataedo team ensures compliance with data privacy regulations and handles any related issues.
