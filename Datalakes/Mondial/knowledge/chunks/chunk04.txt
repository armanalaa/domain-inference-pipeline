 # Mondial — Business Workflows

## 1. Country Management

Performed By: Country Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Country managers register new countries by recording the name, code, capital, province, area, and population.
- Data analysts update country information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the country table.

## 2. City Management

Performed By: City Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- City managers register new cities by recording the name, country, province, population, longitude, and latitude.
- Data analysts update city information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the city table.

## 3. Continent Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers register new continents by recording the name and area.
- Data analysts update continent information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the continent table.

## 4. Lake Management

Performed By: Hydrologists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Hydrologists register new lakes by recording the name, area, depth, altitude, type, river (if any), longitude, and latitude.
- Data analysts update lake information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the lake table.

## 5. River Management

Performed By: Hydrologists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Hydrologists register new rivers by recording the name, country, province, longitude, and latitude (source and merge points if applicable).
- Data analysts update river information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the geo_river table.

## 6. Sea Management

Performed By: Maritime Specialists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Maritime specialists register new seas by recording the name, country, province, and mergesWith (if applicable).
- Data analysts update sea information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the geo_sea table.

## 7. Mountain Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers register new mountains by recording the name, country, province, and location on an island (if applicable).
- Data analysts update mountain information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the geo_mountain table.

## 8. Island Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers register new islands by recording the name, country, province, and location (sea, lake, or river).
- Data analysts update island information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the geo_island table.

## 9. Desert Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers register new deserts by recording the name, area, longitude, and latitude.
- Data analysts update desert information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the desert table.

## 10. Organization Management

Performed By: Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Administrators register new organizations by recording the abbreviation, name, city, country, province, and establishment details.
- Data analysts update organization information as needed, referencing the relevant columns in the organization table.
