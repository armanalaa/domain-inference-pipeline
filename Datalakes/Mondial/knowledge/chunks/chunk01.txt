 # Mondial — Business Workflows

## 1. City Management

Performed By: City Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- City administrators register new cities by recording their name, country, province, population, longitude, and latitude.
- Data analysts update city population data based on the latest census reports.
- City administrators assign cities to provinces or regions as necessary.

## 2. Country Management

Performed By: Government Officials, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Government officials register new countries by recording their name, code, capital, province, area, and population.
- Data analysts update country demographic data based on the latest reports from government agencies or international organizations.
- Government officials establish diplomatic relations with other countries through joining international organizations.

## 3. Continent Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers register new continents by recording their name and area.
- Data analysts update continent data based on the latest geographical research.
- Geographers categorize countries according to their continents.

## 4. Desert Management

Performed By: Geologists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geologists register new deserts by recording their name, area, longitude, and latitude.
- Data analysts update desert data based on the latest geological surveys.
- Geographers assign deserts to countries or regions.

## 5. Economy Management

Performed By: Economists, Government Officials

Activity:
- Economists calculate GDP, agriculture, service, industry, and inflation rates for each country.
- Government officials use these economic indicators to make informed policy decisions.
- Economists analyze the impact of various policies on the economy.

## 6. Ethnic Group Management

Performed By: Sociologists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Sociologists register new ethnic groups by recording their name, country, and percentage of the population they represent.
- Data analysts update ethnic group data based on the latest census reports or surveys.
- Sociologists study the cultural, social, and economic aspects of different ethnic groups.

## 7. Geographical Feature Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers register new geographical features like rivers, lakes, seas, mountains, and islands by recording their name, area, longitude, latitude, type, and associated country or region.
- Data analysts update geographical feature data based on the latest surveys or satellite imagery.
- Geographers analyze the spatial relationships between different geographical features.
