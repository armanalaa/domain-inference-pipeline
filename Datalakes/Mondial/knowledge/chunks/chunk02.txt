 # Mondial — Business Workflows

## 1. City Management

Performed By: City Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- City managers register new cities by recording the name, country, province, population, longitude, and latitude.
- City managers update city details when necessary, such as changes in population or administrative division.
- Data entry clerks verify and input geographical coordinates provided by various sources.

## 2. Country Management

Performed By: Country Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Country managers register new countries by recording the name, code, capital, area, population, and relevant provinces.
- Country managers update country details when necessary, such as changes in government or administrative division.
- Data entry clerks verify and input geographical coordinates provided by various sources.

## 3. Continent Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Geographers categorize countries under their respective continents based on geographical and political criteria.
- Data entry clerks record the name and area of each continent.

## 4. Desert Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Geographers identify and register new deserts by recording their names, areas, longitudes, and latitudes.
- Data entry clerks verify and input geographical coordinates provided by various sources.

## 5. Economic Analysis

Performed By: Economists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Economists calculate and record the GDP, agriculture, service, industry, and inflation rates for each country.
- Data analysts update economic data as new information becomes available.

## 6. Ethnic Group Management

Performed By: Sociologists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Sociologists identify and register ethnic groups in each country along with their names, percentages, and other relevant demographic details.
- Data entry clerks verify and input the data provided by various sources.

## 7. Geographical Feature Association

Performed By: Geographers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Geographers associate deserts, rivers, lakes, seas, mountains, islands, and other geographical features with their respective countries and provinces.
- Data entry clerks verify and input the associations provided by various sources.

## 8. Language Management

Performed By: Linguists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Linguists identify and register languages spoken in each country along with their names, percentages, and other relevant linguistic details.
- Data entry clerks verify and input the data provided by various sources.

## 9. Organization Membership

Performed By: Diplomats, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Diplomats record the membership of countries in international organizations, along with their types and roles.
- Data entry clerks verify and input the data provided by various sources.
