 # Mondial — Business Workflows

## 1. City Management

Performed By: City Planners, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- City planners determine the name and location of a city based on its longitude and latitude.
- City planners assign a country to a city by providing the corresponding country code.
- City planners record the population, province, and area of a city.
- Data entry clerks verify and update city information as needed.

## 2. Country Management

Performed By: Government Officials, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Government officials determine the name, code, capital, province, area, and population of a country.
- Government officials assign ethnic groups to countries based on their percentage distribution.
- Government officials record the GDP, agriculture, service, industry, and inflation rates for each country.
- Data entry clerks verify and update country information as needed.

## 3. Continent Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Geographers determine the name and area of a continent.
- Data entry clerks record the continents in the database.

## 4. Desert Management

Performed By: Geologists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Geologists determine the name, area, longitude, and latitude of deserts.
- Data entry clerks record the deserts in the database.

## 5. Lake Management

Performed By: Hydrologists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Hydrologists determine the name, area, depth, altitude, type, river (if connected), longitude, and latitude of lakes.
- Data entry clerks record the lakes in the database.

## 6. River Management

Performed By: Hydrologists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Hydrologists determine the name, country, province, and connection points (sea, lake, or river) of rivers.
- Data entry clerks record the rivers in the database.

## 7. Sea Management

Performed By: Oceanographers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Oceanographers determine the name, country, province, and connection points (merging seas or other water bodies) of seas.
- Data entry clerks record the seas in the database.

## 8. Island Management

Performed By: Geographers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Geographers determine the name, country, province, and location (sea, lake, or river) of islands.
- Data entry clerks record the islands in the database.

## 9. Organization Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- HR managers determine the abbreviation, name, city, country, province, and establishment date of organizations.
- Data entry clerks record the organizations in the database.

## 10. Geographical Relationship Management

Performed By: GIS Specialists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- GIS specialists establish relationships between cities, provinces, countries, rivers, lakes, seas, islands, and geographical features (mountains, deserts).
- Data entry clerks record the relationships in the database.
