 # Mondial — Business Workflows

## 1. City Management

Performed By: City Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- City managers register new cities by recording the name, country, province, population, longitude, and latitude.
- Data entry clerks update city details when changes occur, such as population or administrative boundaries.
- City managers assign cities to provinces and countries based on geographical and political considerations.

## 2. Continent Management

Performed By: Geographers, Research Analysts

Activity:
- Geographers add new continents by specifying the name and area.
- Research analysts update continent details when necessary, such as changes in borders or population distribution.
- Geographers categorize continents based on geographical features, historical events, and cultural characteristics.

## 3. Country Management

Performed By: Diplomats, Economists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Diplomats register new countries by providing the name, code, capital, province, area, population, and GDP.
- Economists update country details related to economic indicators like agriculture, service, industry, and inflation.
- Data entry clerks maintain accurate records of each country's ethnic groups and languages.

## 4. Geographical Feature Management

Performed By: Cartographers, Geologists, Research Analysts

Activity:
- Cartographers add new geographical features like deserts, rivers, lakes, mountains, seas, and islands to the database.
- Geologists provide details about each feature's area, longitude, latitude, depth, altitude, type, and associated bodies of water.
- Research analysts update geographical features when changes occur, such as borders or names.

## 5. Organization Management

Performed By: Diplomats, Business Representatives, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Diplomats register new international organizations and their members.
- Business representatives add corporate entities to the database.
- Data entry clerks maintain accurate records of each organization's type, location, and establishment details.

## 6. Language Management

Performed By: Linguists, Research Analysts

Activity:
- Linguists add new languages to the database by specifying the country, name, and percentage of speakers.
- Research analysts update language details when necessary, such as changes in distribution or official status.

## 7. Economic Analysis

Performed By: Economists, Financial Analysts

Activity:
- Economists analyze economic trends by examining GDP, agriculture, service, industry, and inflation data for each country.
- Financial analysts forecast future economic conditions based on historical and current data.

## 8. Geopolitical Analysis

Performed By: Diplomats, Political Scientists, Research Analysts

Activity:
- Diplomats assess geopolitical risks and opportunities by examining relationships between countries, organizations, and territories.
- Political scientists analyze political structures, power dynamics, and conflicts within and between nations.
- Research analysts update geopolitical data when changes occur, such as border disputes or diplomatic agreements.
