 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Parent Information Registration

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Data entry clerks record parent identification details such as name and contact information.
- They input the date of birth of the parent, along with the format used for recording it.
- The clerk records the age of the parent at the time of the event, specifying the unit of measurement.
- Height and weight of the parent are also recorded in centimeters and kilograms respectively.
- The sex of the parent is noted down as well.

## 2. Parent Medical History Documentation

Performed By: Healthcare Professional

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals document any relevant medical history and concurrent conditions of the parent, excluding the reaction/event.
- They provide structured information about past medical episodes, including the name of the episode, start and end dates, and comments if necessary.
- The professional records any text related to the relevant medical history and concurrent conditions of the parent.

## 3. Drug Information Recording

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Data entry clerks record the name of the drug as administered to the parent.
- They input the start date of the drug administration, along with the format used for recording it.
- The end date of the drug administration is also recorded, if applicable.
- The clerk records any indication for which the drug was administered and any known reactions experienced by the parent.

## 4. Reaction/Event Reporting

Performed By: Reporter (Primary Source)

Activity:
- The reporter provides a description of the reaction or event as reported by the primary source.
- They highlight any term in the report that they consider significant.
- The date of start of the reaction or event is recorded, along with the format used for recording it.

## 5. Data Validation and Quality Assurance

Performed By: Quality Assurance Officer

Activity:
- Quality assurance officers verify the accuracy and completeness of the data entered in the FDA_AdverseEvents database.
- They ensure that all required fields are filled out and that the data conforms to the specified formats.
- Any discrepancies or errors are corrected before the data is accepted into the system.
