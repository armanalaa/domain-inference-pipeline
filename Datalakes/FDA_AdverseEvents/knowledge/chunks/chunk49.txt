 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers, Patients

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies register new drugs by providing details such as product code, name, dosage form, and route of administration.
- Healthcare providers record adverse events related to a specific drug, including the reaction, gestation period at exposure, and date of start of drug.
- Patients or their representatives report adverse reactions experienced with a particular drug, providing details like the drug's name, dosage text, and action taken with the drug.

## 2. Drug Dosage Information Management

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies specify structured dosage units for each drug, including separated dosage numbers, interval dosage unit numbers, and cumulative dose to first reaction.
- They also provide the definition of the dosage interval and the pharmaceutical form (dosage form) of the drug.

## 3. Adverse Event Timeline Documentation

Performed By: Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Healthcare providers document the time interval between beginning of drug administration and start of reaction/event, as well as the time interval between last dose of drug and start of reaction/event.
- They also record the date of last administration and the duration of drug administration.

## 4. Adverse Event Assessment and Action

Performed By: Medical Experts, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Medical experts assess the recurrence of reactions on readministration and the reaction that recurred if applicable.
- They also evaluate the adverse event and document the source of assessment.

## 5. Adverse Event Report Submission

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Pharmaceutical Companies, Regulatory Bodies

Activity:
- Healthcare providers submit adverse event reports to regulatory bodies, including all relevant details about the drug and the adverse event.
- Pharmaceutical companies monitor adverse event reports and respond with any necessary actions or updates.
