 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Patients, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals record patient details and adverse event information (patientdemographics, reactionmeddraversionllt).
- Patients or their representatives report adverse events related to a specific drug (primarysourcereaction).
- Adverse event terms are translated into MedDRA terminology (reactionmeddrallt).
- The seriousness of the reported adverse event is highlighted by the reporter (termhighlighted).
- The date of start and end of the adverse event, as well as its duration, are recorded (reactionstartdate, reactionenddate, reactionduration, reactiondurationunit).
- Time intervals between suspect drug administration and the start/last dose of the adverse event are documented (reactionfirsttimeunit, reactionlasttimeunit).

## 2. Case Report Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Submission Teams

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies compile adverse event reports into a case report (casereportid).
- The case report includes all relevant adverse events, patient information, and drug details (casereportdata).

## 3. Case Review and Validation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Safety Assessment Teams

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies review each submitted case report for accuracy and completeness (casestatus).
- The safety assessment team validates the adverse event reports, patient details, and drug information (validatedby).

## 4. Case Analysis and Investigation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Medical and Scientific Teams

Activity:
- Medical and scientific teams analyze the validated case report to determine if the adverse event is related to the drug (adverseeventanalysis).
- Relevant test procedures are conducted on the patient to support the investigation (testdate, testname, testresult, testunit).

## 5. Case Outcome Determination

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Medical and Scientific Teams

Activity:
- The medical and scientific teams determine the outcome of the adverse event (reactionoutcome).

## 6. Case Report Updates and Archiving

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Data Management Teams

Activity:
- Updated case reports are archived for future reference (casereportarchive).
