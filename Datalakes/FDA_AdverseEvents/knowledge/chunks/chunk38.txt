 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Case Report Submission

Performed By: Researchers, Sponsors, Investigators

Activity:
- Researchers record case details such as patient demographics, adverse event description, and drug information.
- Sponsors review and validate the accuracy of the reported cases before submission.
- Investigators confirm the authenticity of the submitted reports and attach any supporting documents.

## 2. Case Review and Validation

Performed By: FDA Reviewers

Activity:
- FDA reviewers assess the completeness and validity of each case report according to ICH guidelines.
- They verify if the reported adverse events are related to the specific drug or medical product.
- If necessary, they request additional information from the submitter for a comprehensive evaluation.

## 3. Case Categorization and Analysis

Performed By: FDA Analysts

Activity:
- FDA analysts categorize adverse events based on their severity, nature, and potential impact on public health.
- They analyze the trends and patterns of reported cases to identify any potential safety concerns or risks associated with specific drugs or medical products.

## 4. Case Follow-up and Investigation

Performed By: FDA Investigators

Activity:
- FDA investigators conduct further investigation on complex or serious adverse events to determine their cause, frequency, and potential solutions.
- They collaborate with relevant stakeholders, such as drug manufacturers and healthcare providers, to gather additional information.

## 5. Case Closure and Reporting

Performed By: FDA Managers

Activity:
- FDA managers review the findings of the investigation and make decisions on whether to take regulatory action or close the case.
- They prepare and distribute reports summarizing the adverse event, its impact, and any recommended actions to relevant parties, including drug manufacturers and healthcare providers.

## 6. Regulatory Action and Compliance Monitoring

Performed By: FDA Enforcement Officers

Activity:
- FDA enforcement officers take appropriate regulatory action based on the findings of the adverse event investigation.
- They monitor compliance with regulations and guidelines related to drug safety, ensuring that manufacturers address identified issues promptly.

## 7. Data Management and Maintenance

Performed By: FDA Database Administrators

Activity:
- FDA database administrators maintain the integrity and accuracy of the Adverse Events database by regularly updating and backing up data.
- They ensure that the system is secure, accessible, and scalable to accommodate growing amounts of case reports.
