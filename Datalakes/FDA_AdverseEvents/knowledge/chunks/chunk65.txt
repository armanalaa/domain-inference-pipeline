 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: FDA Officers, Manufacturers, and Consumers

Activity:
- FDA officers monitor adverse events related to FDA-regulated products.
- Manufacturers report adverse events associated with their products.
- Consumers submit reports about adverse experiences with FDA-regulated products.

## 2. Event Classification

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- FDA officers classify adverse events based on the level of hazard involved.
- They assess the severity and potential impact on public health.

## 3. Recall Initiation

Performed By: Manufacturers, FDA Officers

Activity:
- If necessary, manufacturers initiate a recall to address defective or potentially harmful products.
- FDA officers may request or order a recall in rare cases.

## 4. Recall Strategy Oversight

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- FDA officers oversee the company's strategy for recalling defective or potentially harmful products.
- They ensure the recall is appropriate and effective.

## 5. Enforcement Reporting

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- Once classified, FDA officers post recall information in the Enforcement Reports.
- Recall information includes details about the recalled products and the level of hazard involved.

## 6. Recall Information Updates

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- If necessary, FDA officers make corrections or changes to recall information previously disclosed in a past Enforcement Report.
- This may include expanding the recall to include more batches or lots of the same recalled product.

## 7. Public Health Protection

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- FDA officers work with industry and state partners to protect public health from products that present a risk of injury or gross deception.
- They publish press releases and other public notices about recalls that may potentially present a significant or serious risk to the consumer or user of the product.
