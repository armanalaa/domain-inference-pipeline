 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Employees (Safety Officers, Data Entry Clerks)

Activity:
- Safety officers investigate adverse events and gather relevant details such as patient information, event description, and product details.
- Data entry clerks record the collected data into the AdverseEvents database, including the report ID, reporter type, report date, and event start date.
- Safety officers assign a severity level to each reported adverse event based on its impact on the patient's health.

## 2. Case Report Processing

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Employees (Safety Officers)

Activity:
- Safety officers review and validate incoming case reports for completeness and accuracy, ensuring all required data elements are present.
- If a report is incomplete, safety officers follow up with the reporter to obtain missing information.
- Once validated, safety officers update the status of the case report in the AdverseEvents database.

## 3. Data Transmission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company IT Staff (System Administrators)

Activity:
- System administrators transmit validated adverse event reports to the FDA using the Electronic Transmission protocol, including the ICSR Acknowledgment Message.

## 4. Data Reception and Validation

Performed By: FDA IT Staff (System Administrators)

Activity:
- System administrators receive adverse event reports from pharmaceutical companies and validate them for compliance with the ICH ICSR Specifications.

## 5. Case Review and Analysis

Performed By: FDA Employees (Medical Officers, Data Analysts)

Activity:
- Medical officers review adverse event reports to assess their potential impact on public health and determine any necessary follow-up actions.
- Data analysts analyze the collected data to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with specific products or patient groups.

## 6. Follow-Up and Communication

Performed By: FDA Employees (Medical Officers, Communications Staff)

Activity:
- Medical officers initiate follow-up actions if necessary, such as contacting the pharmaceutical company for additional information or requesting a risk assessment.
- Communications staff inform the public and healthcare professionals about potential risks associated with specific products, based on the analysis of adverse event reports.

## 7. Report Archiving and Retrieval

Performed By: FDA IT Staff (System Administrators)

Activity:
- System administrators archive adverse event reports in a secure database for future reference and retrieval as needed.
