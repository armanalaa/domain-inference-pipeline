 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Report Submission

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Pharmaceutical Companies' Representatives

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals record patient details and adverse event information (patientid, productcode, reaction) upon observation.
- Pharmaceutical companies' representatives input the received reports into the system (reportdate, reportsource, reporterdetails).
- Reports are reviewed for completeness and seriousness criteria (serious, seriousnessdeath, seriousnesslifethreatening, seriousnesshospitalization, seriousnessdisabling, seriousnesscongenitalanomali, seriousnessotherimportantcondition).

## 2. Adverse Event Report Aggregation

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Duplicate reports are identified and consolidated (reportduplicate, duplicatesource, linkedreportnumb).
- Reports are grouped by product for analysis (productcode).

## 3. Adverse Event Report Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Regulatory Affairs Specialists

Activity:
- Trends and patterns in adverse events are analyzed (receiptdate, fulfillexpeditecriteria).
- Reports requiring expedited review are flagged (fulfillexpeditecriteria).

## 4. Adverse Event Report Confirmation

Performed By: Medical Professionals, Regulatory Affairs Specialists

Activity:
- Reports without initial medical confirmation from health professionals are verified (medicallyconfirm).
- Confirmations are recorded in the system (confirmerid, confirmdate).

## 5. Adverse Event Report Archiving

Performed By: Data Management Personnel

Activity:
- Archived reports are stored with relevant documentation (additionaldocument, documentlist, authoritynumb, companynumb).

## 6. Adverse Event Report Retrieval

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Medical Professionals

Activity:
- Reports are retrieved for further analysis or review (linkreportnumb).

## 7. Adverse Event Report Nullification

Performed By: Data Management Personnel

Activity:
- Reports are nullified when no longer needed (casenullification, nullificationreason).
