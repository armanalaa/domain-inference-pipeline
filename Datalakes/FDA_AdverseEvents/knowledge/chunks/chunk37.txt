 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff register adverse events by recording event details, product information, and reporter contact.
- Healthcare providers document patient symptoms, medication history, and treatment outcomes related to the reported adverse event.
- Pharmaceutical company staff assign MedDRA version for assessing the adverse event.
- Staff evaluate the relatedness of the drug to the adverse event using specified methods.
- They record additional information on the drug, such as dosage and duration of administration.

## 2. Case Narrative Creation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Staff compile a comprehensive narrative case summary that includes clinical course, therapeutic measures, outcome, and additional relevant information.
- They include the reporter's comments in the narrative.

## 3. Diagnosis Recording

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Staff record the sender's diagnosis using MedDRA version.
- They document any reclassification of the reaction/event based on additional information.

## 4. Assessment and Result Documentation

Performed By: External Assessors (e.g., Medical Experts)

Activity:
- External assessors evaluate the adverse event, determining its relatedness to the drug, assessment method, and result.
- They document their findings and conclusions in the database.

## 5. Additional Information Collection

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Staff collect any additional information on the drug that may be relevant to the adverse event investigation.

## 6. Case Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Staff submit the complete case report, including narrative, diagnosis, assessment, and additional information, for further analysis and action.
