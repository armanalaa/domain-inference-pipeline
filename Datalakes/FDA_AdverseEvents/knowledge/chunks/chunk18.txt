 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies document adverse events related to their products and submit reports to regulatory bodies.
- Healthcare providers record details of patient's adverse reactions to medications and forward the reports to pharmaceutical companies.
- Adverse event reports are reviewed by pharmaceutical companies for medical confirmation before submission.
- Duplicate reports from different sources are identified and consolidated.
- Reports are submitted in a standardized format, following ICH ICSR Specifications.

## 2. Duplicate Report Management

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Duplicate reports from various sources are compared and matched based on common identifiers.
- The source of duplicate reports is documented for future reference.
- Duplicates are marked as such in the database to prevent redundancy.

## 3. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Adverse event reports are investigated to determine if there is a causal relationship between the event and the product.
- Findings from the investigation are documented and used for further analysis or action.

## 4. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Data Analysts, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Adverse event data is analyzed to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with the product.
- Results of the analysis are used for risk assessment and management decisions.

## 5. Regulatory Compliance Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Legal Teams

Activity:
- Regulatory bodies' requirements and guidelines are followed during adverse event reporting and management.
- Reports are prepared to demonstrate compliance with regulatory standards.

## 6. Adverse Event Follow-up

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Customer Service Representatives, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Patients or healthcare providers are contacted for additional information if needed during the adverse event investigation.
- Updates on the investigation findings and any necessary actions are communicated to patients or healthcare providers.

## 7. Adverse Event Database Maintenance

Performed By: IT Staff, Data Analysts

Activity:
- The adverse event database is regularly updated with new reports and information.
- Data integrity is ensured through proper data entry, validation, and error handling procedures.
