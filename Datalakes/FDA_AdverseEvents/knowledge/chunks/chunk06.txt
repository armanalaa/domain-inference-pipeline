 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: External Stakeholders (e.g., healthcare providers, patients), Internal FDA Staff

Activity:
- Healthcare providers and patients submit adverse event reports using the FAERS Electronic Submissions system.
- Internal FDA staff review and validate incoming reports for completeness and accuracy.
- FDA staff assign MedDRA terms to describe adverse events and medication errors.

## 2. Case Processing

Performed By: Internal FDA Staff (Case Processors)

Activity:
- Case processors analyze adverse event reports, assessing their potential impact on public health.
- They determine whether the reported event requires further investigation or action.
- Case processors document their findings and decisions in the case processing workflow.

## 3. Data Integration

Performed By: Internal FDA IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff integrate adverse event data from various sources into the AEMS database.
- They ensure data consistency across different product categories (e.g., medical products, vaccines, devices).

## 4. Data Analysis and Trend Identification

Performed By: Internal FDA Analysts

Activity:
- Analysts use advanced analytics tools to identify trends and patterns in adverse event data.
- They produce reports that highlight potential safety concerns or areas for further investigation.

## 5. Regulatory Decision Making

Performed By: FDA Regulatory Bodies (e.g., Center for Drug Evaluation and Research)

Activity:
- Regulatory bodies review the findings and recommendations from analysts.
- They make decisions regarding regulatory actions, such as product recalls or label changes.

## 6. Cross-Product Surveillance

Performed By: Internal FDA Staff (e.g., Safety Surveillance Officers)

Activity:
- Safety surveillance officers monitor adverse event data across diverse product categories.
- They identify and address potential safety issues that may affect multiple products or product types.

## 7. Consumer Complaints Management

Performed By: Internal FDA Staff (e.g., Consumer Complaint Coordinators)

Activity:
- Consumer complaint coordinators receive, review, and respond to consumer complaints related to FDA-regulated products.
- They document the complaints in the AEMS database for future analysis and trend identification.

## 8. Regulatory Misconduct Reporting and Whistleblower Submissions

Performed By: Internal FDA Staff (e.g., Investigators)

Activity:
- Investigators receive and process regulatory misconduct reports and whistleblower submissions.
- They investigate allegations of illegal or unethical activities within the FDA or by regulated entities.
