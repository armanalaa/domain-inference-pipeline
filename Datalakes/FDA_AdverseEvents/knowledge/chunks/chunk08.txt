 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers, Consumers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies record adverse event details for their products, including the product name, event description, and date of occurrence.
- Healthcare providers submit reports on patient adverse events related to FDA-regulated products, including patient demographics and event severity.
- Consumers report personal experiences with adverse events caused by FDA-regulated products, providing their contact information for follow-up.

## 2. Adverse Event Review

Performed By: FDA Staff (Medical Officers, Safety Evaluators)

Activity:
- Medical officers review adverse event reports to assess potential safety concerns and determine if further investigation is necessary.
- Safety evaluators analyze the reported events to identify patterns or trends across multiple products or product categories.

## 3. Case Processing

Performed By: FDA Staff (Case Managers, Regulatory Affairs Specialists)

Activity:
- Case managers assign adverse event reports to appropriate regulatory affairs specialists for further investigation and follow-up.
- Regulatory affairs specialists gather additional information from various sources, such as medical literature or manufacturer data, to evaluate the potential causality between the reported event and the FDA-regulated product.

## 4. Data Verification and Confirmation

Performed By: FDA Staff (Medical Reviewers)

Activity:
- Medical reviewers verify the accuracy of adverse event reports, ensuring that all necessary information is present and that the report meets established criteria for validity.

## 5. Adverse Event Classification

Performed By: FDA Staff (Classification Specialists)

Activity:
- Classification specialists categorize adverse events based on their nature, severity, and potential impact on public health.

## 6. Regulatory Action

Performed By: FDA Staff (Regulatory Affairs Managers, Legal Counsel)

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs managers determine appropriate regulatory actions in response to adverse event reports, such as issuing warnings or mandating label changes for affected products.
- Legal counsel reviews and approves regulatory actions to ensure compliance with relevant laws and regulations.

## 7. Public Data Access and Analysis

Performed By: General Public (Researchers, Healthcare Professionals)

Activity:
- The general public accesses and analyzes adverse event data through the FDA AEMS Public Dashboard to identify trends, patterns, or potential safety concerns related to FDA-regulated products.
