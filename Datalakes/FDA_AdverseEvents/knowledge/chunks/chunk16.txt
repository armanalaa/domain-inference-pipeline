 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Regulatory Authorities

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies record product details and adverse event information for each report in the ICH ICSR message.
- Companies assign unique identifiers to each adverse event report.
- Regulatory authorities receive adverse event reports from pharmaceutical companies.
- Authorities verify the authenticity of received adverse event reports.
- Authorities store adverse event reports in their respective databases.

## 2. Adverse Event Data Management

Performed By: Data Managers, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- Data managers organize and maintain adverse event data in a structured format for easy retrieval.
- Compliance officers ensure that all adverse event reports comply with regulatory standards.
- Data managers update the database with new adverse event reports regularly.

## 3. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: Research Analysts, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Research analysts review adverse event reports to identify patterns and trends.
- Safety officers assess the severity of adverse events and their potential impact on public health.
- Research analysts categorize adverse events based on their nature and effects.

## 4. Adverse Event Communication

Performed By: Public Relations, Regulatory Affairs

Activity:
- Public relations teams prepare communications to inform the public about significant adverse events.
- Regulatory affairs liaise with regulatory authorities to share adverse event data and updates.
- Public relations teams distribute press releases and update websites with adverse event information.

## 5. Adverse Event Response

Performed By: Product Managers, Regulatory Affairs

Activity:
- Product managers evaluate the need for product modifications in response to adverse events.
- Regulatory affairs coordinate with regulatory authorities to obtain necessary approvals for product changes.
- Product managers implement changes to the product as required, ensuring compliance with regulations.

## 6. Adverse Event Follow-up

Performed By: Case Managers, Research Analysts

Activity:
- Case managers investigate adverse events further to gather additional information.
- Research analysts analyze follow-up data to assess the effectiveness of any corrective actions taken.
- Case managers document findings and recommendations for future action.

## 7. Adverse Event Report Archiving

Performed By: Data Managers, Compliance Officers

Activity:
- Data managers archive adverse event reports that are no longer actively monitored.
- Compliance officers ensure that archived data is stored securely and can be retrieved when needed.
