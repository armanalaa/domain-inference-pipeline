 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Report Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, Healthcare Professionals, Consumers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff compile adverse event details and generate an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR).
- Healthcare professionals or consumers submit the ICSR to the FDA through the openFDA API.
- FDA staff receive the report and record its unique identifier, sender, receiver, date, and acknowledgment code.

## 2. Adverse Event Acknowledgement

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff acknowledge receipt of the ICSR by sending an acknowledgement message containing the report's unique identifier, sender, receiver, date, and acknowledgment code.
- If errors are detected in the report, FDA staff record error messages for correction before re-submission.

## 3. Adverse Event Error Resolution

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff review and correct errors identified by FDA in the ICSR, ensuring field lengths and ISO codes comply with specifications.
- Once corrected, the updated ICSR is re-submitted to the FDA for processing.

## 4. Adverse Event Data Processing

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff process the corrected ICSR, extracting relevant data such as safety report ID, safety report version, local report number, company number, receipt date, and report acknowledgment code.

## 5. Adverse Event Data Storage

Performed By: Database Administrators

Activity:
- Database administrators store the extracted adverse event data in the FDA_AdverseEvents database for future analysis and reporting.

## 6. Adverse Event Report Analysis

Performed By: Researchers, Analysts

Activity:
- Researchers and analysts access and analyze the stored adverse event data to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with specific drugs or indications.

## 7. Adverse Event Report Retrieval

Performed By: Users (Researchers, Analysts, Public)

Activity:
- Users retrieve adverse event reports from the FDA_AdverseEvents database for various purposes such as research, analysis, or public inquiry.
