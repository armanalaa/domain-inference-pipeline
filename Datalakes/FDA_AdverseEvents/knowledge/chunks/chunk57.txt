 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, FDA Employees

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff record adverse event details, including product information and patient data. (A.1.2 Local Message Number, A.1.3 ICSR Message Sender Identifier)
- Staff assign unique identifiers to each report sent to the FDA. (B.1.1 Safety Report ID)
- FDA employees receive and acknowledge receipt of adverse event reports. (B.1.7 a and b Date of Receipt of the Most Recent Information)
- FDA staff process reports, determining if they were successfully loaded into the database or require correction. (B.1.8 Acknowledgment Code for a Report)

## 2. Error Handling

Performed By: FDA Employees

Activity:
- In case of errors during report loading, FDA staff provide error messages and relevant field information to help the pharmaceutical company correct issues. (B.1.9 Error Message or Comment)

## 3. Case Safety Report Processing

Performed By: FDA Employees

Activity:
- FDA employees review adverse event reports for potential safety concerns and take appropriate action if necessary. (Not specified in the provided materials)

## 4. Database Management

Performed By: IT Staff, FDA Employees

Activity:
- IT staff manage the database structure and ensure it can handle adverse event report data. (Not specified in the provided materials)

## 5. Regulatory Compliance Monitoring

Performed By: FDA Employees

Activity:
- FDA employees monitor compliance with regulatory standards for adverse event reporting. (Not specified in the provided materials)
