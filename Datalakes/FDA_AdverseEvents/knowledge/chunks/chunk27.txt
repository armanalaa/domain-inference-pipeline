 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Representatives, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company representatives submit adverse event reports to the FDA using the ICH ICSR format in their native language.
- Healthcare providers document and verify adverse events in the patient's native language.

## 2. Adverse Event Translation

Performed By: Translation Specialists

Activity:
- Translation specialists translate adverse event reports from various languages into English for processing by the FDA.

## 3. Adverse Event Validation

Performed By: FDA Reviewers

Activity:
- FDA reviewers validate the accuracy and completeness of translated adverse event reports.

## 4. Adverse Event Categorization

Performed By: FDA Analysts

Activity:
- FDA analysts categorize adverse events based on their nature, severity, and potential impact on public health.

## 5. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: FDA Investigators

Activity:
- FDA investigators investigate the root cause of adverse events to determine if they are related to the drug or medical device in question.

## 6. Adverse Event Response Planning

Performed By: FDA Management

Activity:
- FDA management plans responses to adverse events, including communication with stakeholders and potential recall or labeling changes for affected products.

## 7. Adverse Event Report Archiving

Performed By: FDA Data Managers

Activity:
- FDA data managers archive adverse event reports for future reference and analysis.
