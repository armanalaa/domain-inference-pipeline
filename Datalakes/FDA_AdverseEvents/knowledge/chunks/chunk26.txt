 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Medical Writers

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists receive adverse event reports from various sources.
- Medical writers review and analyze the reported events for potential safety concerns.
- Regulatory affairs specialists assign unique identifiers to each adverse event report.
- Medical writers classify adverse events based on their nature, severity, and product involvement.

## 2. Data Entry and Validation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input the received adverse event reports into the FDA_AdverseEvents database.
- They ensure all required fields are filled, including reporter details, event description, and product information.
- Data entry clerks validate the entered data for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## 3. Language Translation and Localization

Performed By: Translators, Localization Specialists

Activity:
- Translators translate adverse event reports from various languages into English.
- Localization specialists ensure that all translated reports maintain their original context and meaning.

## 4. Case Review and Assessment

Performed By: Safety Assessors

Activity:
- Safety assessors review the translated and validated adverse event reports.
- They assess the potential risks associated with each reported event and determine if further investigation is needed.

## 5. Investigation and Follow-up

Performed By: Investigators, Medical Officers

Activity:
- Investigators conduct in-depth investigations on adverse events requiring further examination.
- Medical officers provide medical expertise during the investigation process.

## 6. Risk Communication and Management

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Managers, Product Safety Officers

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs managers communicate risk information to relevant stakeholders, such as product manufacturers.
- Product safety officers develop and implement risk management strategies based on the assessment results.

## 7. Report Generation and Archiving

Performed By: Data Analysts, Database Administrators

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports summarizing adverse event data for various purposes, such as trend analysis or regulatory reporting.
- Database administrators archive the adverse event reports securely for future reference and auditing purposes.
