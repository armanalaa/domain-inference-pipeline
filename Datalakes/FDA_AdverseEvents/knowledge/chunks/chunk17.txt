 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies submit adverse event reports to regulatory bodies by entering the report type, product details, and event description.
- Healthcare providers document adverse events experienced by patients, including symptoms, duration, and severity.
- Adverse event reporters attach any relevant supporting documentation, such as lab results or medical images.

## 2. Case Identification and Duplicate Checking

Performed By: Regulatory Body Staff

Activity:
- Regulatory body staff identify duplicate adverse event reports based on product details and event descriptions.
- They compare new reports with existing ones to ensure no duplicates are being processed.

## 3. Data Validation and Verification

Performed By: Regulatory Body Staff

Activity:
- Regulatory body staff verify the accuracy and completeness of adverse event data, checking for missing or inconsistent information.
- They validate the source of the report to ensure it is from a reliable provider.

## 4. Adverse Event Coding and Classification

Performed By: Regulatory Body Staff

Activity:
- Regulatory body staff assign codes to adverse events based on their nature, severity, and system affected.
- They classify the events according to predefined categories for further analysis.

## 5. Risk Assessment and Signal Detection

Performed By: Regulatory Body Analysts

Activity:
- Analysts assess the risk associated with each adverse event, considering factors such as frequency, severity, and potential impact on public health.
- They detect signals indicating a possible safety concern or product issue that may require further investigation.

## 6. Signal Evaluation and Investigation

Performed By: Regulatory Body Experts

Activity:
- Experts evaluate signals to determine if they represent a true safety concern or are false positives.
- They investigate the causes, mechanisms, and potential solutions for any identified issues.

## 7. Risk Management and Communication

Performed By: Regulatory Body Staff, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Regulatory body staff communicate risk management decisions to pharmaceutical companies, including any necessary label changes or product restrictions.
- Companies update their product information and inform healthcare providers about the new safety guidelines.
