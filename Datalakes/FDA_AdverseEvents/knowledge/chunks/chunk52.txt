 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Patients, Manufacturers

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals document adverse events observed in patients during treatment.
- Patients report adverse events they experience after using a medical product.
- Manufacturers enter adverse event reports received from healthcare providers or patients.

## 2. Adverse Event Categorization

Performed By: Safety Officers, Medical Experts

Activity:
- Safety officers classify adverse events based on their severity and relationship to the product.
- Medical experts review and validate categorizations made by safety officers.

## 3. Data Verification and Validation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Quality Assurance Specialists

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input adverse event data into the system following verification checks.
- Quality assurance specialists review entered data for accuracy and completeness.

## 4. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: Research Analysts, Statisticians

Activity:
- Research analysts analyze patterns and trends in reported adverse events.
- Statisticians perform statistical analysis on the collected data to identify potential risks or concerns.

## 5. Case Report Writing

Performed By: Medical Writers

Activity:
- Medical writers compile all relevant information about an adverse event into a comprehensive case report.

## 6. Case Report Review and Approval

Performed By: Medical Experts, Regulatory Affairs Officers

Activity:
- Medical experts review the accuracy and completeness of case reports.
- Regulatory affairs officers approve or request revisions to case reports before submission to regulatory bodies.

## 7. Case Report Submission

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Officers, Data Managers

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs officers submit approved case reports to the appropriate regulatory agencies.
- Data managers ensure that all submitted case reports are properly recorded and archived for future reference.
