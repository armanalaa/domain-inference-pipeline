 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists review incoming adverse event reports to determine their relevance and completeness.
- Data entry clerks input the approved reports into the FDA_AdverseEvents database, including report ID, product details, and patient information.
- Data entry clerks assign each report a status (e.g., received, reviewed, accepted).

## 2. Data Mapping and Validation

Performed By: IT Specialists, Quality Assurance Analysts

Activity:
- IT specialists map the data from the FDA_AdverseEvents database to the ICH ICSR DTD, ensuring compatibility with the E2BM document.
- Quality assurance analysts validate the mapped data for accuracy and completeness according to the ICH ICSR DTD specifications.

## 3. Data Transformation and Packaging

Performed By: IT Specialists

Activity:
- IT specialists transform the validated data into an SGML format compliant with the ICH ICSR Specifications.
- IT specialists package the SGML data files for electronic transmission via EDI envelope or physical media.

## 4. Adverse Event Submission

Performed By: Data Transmission Specialists

Activity:
- Data transmission specialists submit the packaged SGML data files to the intended recipient, such as the FDA.

## 5. Acknowledgement and Record Keeping

Performed By: Data Transmission Specialists, Regulatory Affairs Specialists

Activity:
- Data transmission specialists request and record acknowledgement of receipt from the recipient.
- Regulatory affairs specialists update the FDA_AdverseEvents database with the acknowledgement details and report status.

## 6. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: Safety Analysts, Medical Officers

Activity:
- Safety analysts analyze the adverse event data to identify trends, patterns, and potential safety concerns.
- Medical officers evaluate the reported events for their impact on product safety and make recommendations for further action.

## 7. Report Follow-up and Response

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Medical Officers

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists follow up with sponsors or manufacturers regarding incomplete or missing information in adverse event reports.
- Medical officers collaborate with regulatory affairs specialists to respond to requests for additional information from the FDA or other regulatory bodies.
