 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting by Health Professionals

Performed By: Health Professional (Doctor, Pharmacist, etc.)

Activity:
- Record the adverse event details such as product code, patient information, and reaction description. (A.2.1.2a reporterorganization, A.2.1.2b reporterdepartment, A.2.1.3 reportercountry, A.2.2 literaturereference, A.2.3.1 studyname, A.2.3.2 sponsorstudynumb, A.2.3.3 observestudytype)
- Specify the qualification of the reporter. (A.2.1.4 Qualification)
- Document the location and contact details of the reporter. (A.2.1.2c reporterstreet, A.2.1.2d reportercity, A.2.1.2e reporterstate, A.2.1.2f reporterpostcode, A.3.1.4a senderstreetaddress, A.3.1.4b sendercity, A.3.1.4c senderstate)
- Include any relevant literature references. (A.2.2 literaturereference)

## 2. Adverse Event Reporting by Consumers or Other Non-Health Professionals

Performed By: Consumer or other non-health professional

Activity:
- Register adverse events related to the product, providing necessary details like product code, patient information, and reaction description. (A.2.1.2a reporterorganization, A.2.1.2b reporterdepartment, A.2.1.3 reportercountry, A.2.2 literaturereference, A.2.3.1 studyname, A.2.3.2 sponsorstudynumb, A.2.3.3 observestudytype)
- Specify the qualification of the reporter as a consumer or other non-health professional. (A.2.1.4 Qualification)
- Document the location and contact details of the reporter. (A.2.1.2c reporterstreet, A.2.1.2d reportercity, A.2.1.2e reporterstate, A.2.1.2f reporterpostcode, A.3.1.4a senderstreetaddress, A.3.1.4b sendercity, A.3.1.4c senderstate)

## 3. Adverse Event Report Reception by Pharmaceutical Companies

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Receivers)

Activity:
- Receive adverse event reports from health professionals and consumers. (A.3.2.2a receiverorganization, A.3.2.2b receiverdepartment)
- Review the received reports for validity and completeness. (Not specified in provided sources)
- Assign the reports to relevant teams for further investigation. (Not specified in provided sources)

## 4. Adverse Event Report Reception by Regulatory Authorities

Performed By: Regulatory Authority Staff (Receivers)

Activity:
- Receive adverse event reports from health professionals and consumers. (A.3.2.2a receiverorganization, A.3.2.2b receiverdepartment)
- Review the received reports for validity and completeness. (Not specified in provided sources)
- Collaborate with pharmaceutical companies on investigations and potential product recalls. (Not specified in provided sources)

## 5. Adverse Event Investigation by Pharmaceutical Companies or Regulatory Authorities

Performed By: Investigative Team (Pharmaceutical Company or Regulatory Authority Staff)

Activity:
- Conduct a thorough investigation of the adverse event report, including reviewing medical records and product batch information. (Not specified in provided sources)
- Determine if there is a causal relationship between the product and the adverse event. (Not specified in provided sources)
- Document findings and recommendations for further action. (Not specified in provided sources)

## 6. Adverse Event Report Analysis by Pharmaceutical Companies or Regulatory Authorities

Performed By: Data Analysts (Pharmaceutical Company or Regulatory Authority Staff)

Activity:
- Analyze adverse event reports to identify trends and potential safety concerns. (Not specified in provided sources)
- Compile reports summarizing the findings for internal use and regulatory reporting. (Not specified in provided sources)

## 7. Adverse Event Report Submission to Regulatory Authorities

Performed By: Compliance Officer or Legal Representative (Pharmaceutical Company Staff)

Activity:
- Prepare adverse event reports for submission to regulatory authorities, including all relevant details and findings from the investigation and analysis phases. (A.3.1.4l senderemailaddress, A.3.2.2a receiverorganization, A.3.2.2b receiverdepartment)
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and deadlines for report submission. (Not specified in provided sources)
