 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Medical Professionals, Patients, Sponsors

Activity:
- Medical professionals document adverse events observed in patients.
- Patients report their own adverse events to healthcare providers.
- Sponsors collect and submit reports from various sources.

## 2. Data Entry and Validation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Quality Assurance Specialists

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input received reports into the system.
- Quality assurance specialists verify the accuracy of entered data.

## 3. Case Processing

Performed By: Case Processors

Activity:
- Case processors review and assess each adverse event report for validity.

## 4. Coding and Classification

Performed By: Coders, Classifiers

Activity:
- Coders assign relevant medical codes to the reported events.
- Classifiers categorize the events based on their nature and severity.

## 5. Safety Signal Detection

Performed By: Data Analysts, Risk Managers

Activity:
- Data analysts identify potential safety signals from the coded and classified data.
- Risk managers evaluate these signals for potential risks associated with the product.

## 6. Regulatory Reporting

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Legal Counsel

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists prepare reports for submission to regulatory bodies.
- Legal counsel review and approve the reports before submission.

## 7. Follow-up and Response Management

Performed By: Case Managers, Product Safety Teams

Activity:
- Case managers follow up on reported adverse events with relevant parties.
- Product safety teams take appropriate actions based on the findings.
