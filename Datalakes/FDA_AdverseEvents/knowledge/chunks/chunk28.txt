 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies document adverse events related to their products.
- Healthcare providers report adverse events they've observed in patients.
- Both parties input event details such as patient information, event description, and product involved.

## 2. Data Validation

Performed By: Quality Assurance Teams

Activity:
- Quality assurance teams verify the accuracy and completeness of reported adverse events.
- They ensure all required fields are filled out and that data conforms to established standards.

## 3. Event Categorization

Performed By: Adverse Event Analysts

Activity:
- Adverse event analysts categorize reported events based on their nature, severity, and potential impact.

## 4. Transmission Preparation

Performed By: IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff prepare the ICH ICSR message by encoding adverse event data according to specified standards.

## 5. Transmission

Performed By: Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) Systems

Activity:
- EDI systems transmit the prepared ICH ICSR messages to the FDA for processing.

## 6. Acknowledgement Receipt

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff receive and acknowledge receipt of transmitted ICH ICSR messages.

## 7. Event Review and Analysis

Performed By: FDA Adverse Event Reviewers

Activity:
- FDA adverse event reviewers assess the reported events for potential safety concerns.

## 8. Risk Assessment and Communication

Performed By: FDA Regulatory Teams

Activity:
- Regulatory teams evaluate the risk posed by adverse events and determine appropriate actions, such as product label updates or recall.
- They communicate their findings to relevant parties, including pharmaceutical companies and healthcare providers.
