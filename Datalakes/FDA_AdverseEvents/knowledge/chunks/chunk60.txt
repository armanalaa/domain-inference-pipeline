 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Report Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff compile adverse event details and submit reports to the FDA. (icsrmessagedate, icsrmessagesenderidentifier)
- Healthcare providers document adverse events and share relevant information with pharmaceutical companies for reporting. (productcode, patientdemographics, adversetype, dosage)
- Pharmaceutical company staff verify the accuracy of received reports before submission to the FDA. (reportacknowledgmentcode)

## 2. Adverse Event Acknowledgement

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff receive and acknowledge receipt of adverse event reports from pharmaceutical companies. (icsrmessagedate, icsrmessagereceiveridentifier, reportacknowledgmentcode)
- FDA staff review the acknowledgement messages for errors or issues. (parsingerrormessage)

## 3. Adverse Event Data Processing

Performed By: FDA IT Systems

Activity:
- FDA IT systems store and process incoming adverse event reports in the FDA_AdverseEvents database. (icsrmessagedate, productcode, patientdemographics, adversetype, dosage)

## 4. Adverse Event Case Review

Performed By: FDA Medical Officers

Activity:
- FDA medical officers assess the severity and potential impact of adverse events on public health. (adversetype, patientoutcome)
- FDA medical officers determine if further investigation is necessary based on the assessment. (investigationrequired)

## 5. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: FDA Investigators

Activity:
- FDA investigators gather additional information about adverse events requiring further investigation. (additionalinformation, followupactions)
- FDA investigators collaborate with pharmaceutical companies to obtain necessary data for the investigation. (companycontactinformation)

## 6. Adverse Event Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: FDA Data Analysts

Activity:
- FDA data analysts analyze adverse event data to identify trends, patterns, or potential risks associated with specific products. (productcode, adversetype, patientdemographics)
- FDA data analysts prepare reports summarizing their findings and recommendations for further action. (reportsummary, recommendedactions)

## 7. Adverse Event Regulatory Action

Performed By: FDA Regulatory Officers

Activity:
- FDA regulatory officers review the analysis and recommendations provided by data analysts. (reportsummary, recommendedactions)
- FDA regulatory officers determine appropriate regulatory actions based on the findings, such as product warnings or recalls. (regulatoryaction)
