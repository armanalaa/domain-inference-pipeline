 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers, Patients

Activity:
- Healthcare providers document adverse events related to pharmaceuticals.
- Pharmaceutical companies collect and verify reports from various sources.
- Patients or their representatives submit self-reported adverse event details.
- Reports are categorized based on the nature of the adverse event.
- Each report is assigned a unique identifier (ICSRID).

## 2. Data Entry and Validation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Quality Assurance Specialists

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input received reports into the system.
- Quality assurance specialists review entered data for accuracy and completeness.
- Incomplete or inconsistent reports are flagged for follow-up.
- Corrected reports are re-entered, and original entries are archived.

## 3. Case Review and Assessment

Performed By: Medical Officers, Safety Evaluators

Activity:
- Medical officers review adverse event reports to determine if they are valid cases.
- Safety evaluators assess the severity of each valid case and its potential relationship to the drug.
- Cases are classified as serious or non-serious based on their impact on the patient's health.

## 4. Aggregation and Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Statisticians

Activity:
- Data analysts aggregate adverse event reports by drug, event type, and geographical location.
- Statisticians analyze aggregated data to identify trends, patterns, or potential safety concerns.
- Findings are presented in regular reports to relevant stakeholders.

## 5. Regulatory Reporting

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Legal Counsel

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists prepare regulatory submissions based on the analysis results.
- Legal counsel reviews submissions for compliance with applicable regulations and laws.
- Submissions are sent to relevant regulatory authorities (e.g., FDA).

## 6. Internal Communication and Coordination

Performed By: Project Managers, Cross-Functional Teams

Activity:
- Project managers coordinate the workflow among different teams involved in adverse event reporting.
- Cross-functional teams collaborate to address any issues or questions that arise during the process.
- Regular meetings are held to discuss progress and identify areas for improvement.

## 7. Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement

Performed By: Compliance Officers, Auditors

Activity:
- Compliance officers monitor adherence to internal policies and procedures related to adverse event reporting.
- Auditors conduct periodic reviews of the adverse event reporting process to ensure compliance with regulations.
- Non-compliant actions are addressed through corrective action plans.
