 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies submit adverse event reports to regulatory bodies.
- Healthcare providers document and report adverse events related to specific drugs.
- Adverse event details are recorded, including patient information, drug details, and event description.
- Reports are reviewed by pharmacovigilance teams for validation and assessment.
- Validated reports are forwarded to regulatory authorities for further action.

## 2. Acknowledgement of Report Receipt

Performed By: Regulatory Authorities

Activity:
- Regulatory authorities acknowledge receipt of adverse event reports.
- Acknowledgements include report identification numbers and timestamps.
- Acknowledged reports are stored for future reference and analysis.

## 3. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: Pharmacovigilance Teams, Regulatory Authorities

Activity:
- Pharmacovigilance teams investigate adverse events to determine causality.
- Data from multiple sources is analyzed to support investigation findings.
- Findings are documented and shared with relevant parties for further action.

## 4. Risk Assessment and Management

Performed By: Regulatory Authorities, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Regulatory authorities assess the risk posed by adverse events to public health.
- Pharmaceutical companies review their product safety profiles based on adverse event data.
- Risk management plans are developed and implemented to mitigate identified risks.

## 5. Adverse Event Updates and Follow-ups

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Regulatory Authorities

Activity:
- Healthcare providers provide updates on adverse events as they become available.
- Regulatory authorities follow up on reported adverse events for additional information.
- Updated information is incorporated into the adverse event records.

## 6. Adverse Event Database Maintenance

Performed By: Data Management Teams, IT Staff

Activity:
- Adverse event databases are maintained to ensure data integrity and accuracy.
- Duplicate entries are removed and inconsistencies are resolved.
- Database backups are performed regularly for disaster recovery purposes.

## 7. Adverse Event Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Regulatory Authorities

Activity:
- Adverse event data is analyzed to identify trends and patterns.
- Findings are reported to regulatory authorities and pharmaceutical companies.
- Reports inform risk management decisions and guide further investigation.
