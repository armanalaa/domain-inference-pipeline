 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers, Patients

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies register new drug indications (B.4.k.11a).
- Healthcare providers report adverse events related to specific drugs (B.4.k.11b).
- Patients or their representatives submit individual case safety reports (ICSRs) detailing the adverse event, drug indication, and route of administration (B.4.k.9).
- ICSRs include information about the patient's demographics, reaction gestation period, and drug start and end dates (B.4.k.12b, B.4.k.14b, B.4.k.10a, B.4.k.10b).
- ICSRs also provide details about the time interval between drug administration and the start of the reaction/event (B.4.k.13).

## 2. Data Validation and Standardization

Performed By: FDA Data Analysts, Quality Assurance Specialists

Activity:
- FDA data analysts validate the accuracy and completeness of ICSR submissions (B.4.k.9 - B.4.k.14b).
- Quality assurance specialists ensure that the MedDRA version for drug indications matches the case (B.4.k.11a).
- FDA data analysts standardize the date formats of start and end dates to CCYYMMDD, CCYYMM, or CCYY (B.4.k.12b, B.4.k.14b, B.4.k.10a, B.4.k.10b).

## 3. Data Integration and Storage

Performed By: FDA Database Administrators

Activity:
- FDA database administrators integrate ICSR data from various sources into the FDA_AdverseEvents database.
- They store the integrated data in appropriate tables, maintaining the relationship between related columns (e.g., linking drug indications to adverse events).

## 4. Data Analysis and Investigation

Performed By: FDA Safety Analysts, Epidemiologists

Activity:
- FDA safety analysts investigate adverse events associated with specific drugs or drug classes (B.4.k.9 - B.4.k.14b).
- Epidemiologists analyze the data to identify patterns and trends related to drug safety (e.g., frequency of adverse events, severity, and potential risk factors).

## 5. Risk Assessment and Communication

Performed By: FDA Risk Management Team, Regulatory Affairs Specialists

Activity:
- The FDA risk management team assesses the risk associated with identified adverse events (B.4.k.9 - B.4.k.14b).
- Regulatory affairs specialists communicate the findings and any necessary actions to pharmaceutical companies, healthcare providers, and the public.

## 6. Regulatory Action and Compliance Monitoring

Performed By: FDA Enforcement Officers, Compliance Specialists

Activity:
- FDA enforcement officers take regulatory action against non-compliant pharmaceutical companies (e.g., issuing warnings or fines).
- Compliance specialists monitor the actions taken by pharmaceutical companies in response to regulatory findings and ensure ongoing compliance with safety regulations.
