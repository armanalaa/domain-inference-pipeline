 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers, Patients

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies record adverse event details such as reaction start date, end date, duration, and outcome for their products.
- Healthcare providers document the time interval between suspect drug administration and start of reaction/event, last dose and start of reaction/event, and results of relevant tests and procedures.
- Patients report adverse events related to a specific drug, including the drug's characterization, batch number, and any more information available.

## 2. Drug Characterization

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies provide the proprietary medicinal product name, active drug substance names, and country where the drug was obtained.
- They record the batch/lot number, authorization/application number, country of authorization/application, and name of holder/applicant for each drug.
- Pharmaceutical companies also document the dose (number) and dose (unit) for each drug.

## 3. Data Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, FDA Representatives

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies prepare and submit individual case safety reports to the FDA containing all relevant adverse event and drug characterization data.
- FDA representatives verify and process the submitted reports for further analysis and action.

## 4. Data Validation

Performed By: FDA Representatives

Activity:
- FDA representatives validate the submitted individual case safety reports for accuracy, completeness, and compliance with ICH ICSR specifications.
- They identify any missing or inconsistent data and request corrections from the reporting pharmaceutical companies.

## 5. Data Analysis

Performed By: FDA Analysts

Activity:
- FDA analysts analyze the validated individual case safety reports to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with specific drugs or drug combinations.
- They generate reports summarizing their findings for further review and action by regulatory committees.

## 6. Risk Assessment and Management

Performed By: FDA Regulatory Committees

Activity:
- FDA regulatory committees review the analysis reports generated by FDA analysts, assessing the potential risks posed by the identified adverse events and trends.
- They make recommendations for risk management strategies, such as updating product labels, conducting further studies, or initiating recall actions if necessary.

## 7. Regulatory Action

Performed By: FDA Enforcement Agents

Activity:
- FDA enforcement agents implement the recommended regulatory actions, working with pharmaceutical companies to address identified risks and ensure compliance with regulations.
- They monitor the implementation process and take further action as needed to protect public health.
