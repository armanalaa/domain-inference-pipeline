 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Healthcare Professionals

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists input adverse event reports into the system based on received ICSR messages. (safetyreport)
- Healthcare professionals record details of patient's medical history related to the adverse event. (medicalhistoryepisode)
- Regulatory affairs specialists link multiple reports for the same adverse event. (linkedreport)

## 2. Case Review and Assessment

Performed By: Adverse Event Analysts, Medical Officers

Activity:
- Adverse event analysts evaluate the severity of reported adverse events based on patient symptoms and outcomes.
- Medical officers review the medical history of patients to determine if the adverse event is related to a specific drug or substance. (drugreactionrelatedness)

## 3. Drug Information Management

Performed By: Pharmacovigilance Specialists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Pharmacovigilance specialists maintain records of active substances and their associated adverse events. (activesubstance)
- Data entry clerks input information about drug recurrences related to the reported adverse event. (drugrecurrence)

## 4. Duplicate Report Detection

Performed By: Adverse Event Analysts, System Administrators

Activity:
- Adverse event analysts check for duplicate reports within the system based on patient and event details. (reportduplicate)
- System administrators configure the system to prevent duplicate report submissions.

## 5. Data Validation and Quality Control

Performed By: Quality Assurance Specialists, System Administrators

Activity:
- Quality assurance specialists verify the accuracy of data entered into the system. (safetyreport, medicalhistoryepisode, linkedreport)
- System administrators implement data validation rules to ensure consistent and reliable data entry.

## 6. Autopsy Report Processing

Performed By: Medical Examiners, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Medical examiners conduct autopsies on deceased patients and record their findings. (patientautopsy)
- Data entry clerks input the results of autopsies into the system.

## 7. Death Case Investigation

Performed By: Adverse Event Analysts, Medical Officers

Activity:
- Adverse event analysts investigate deaths related to adverse events and determine their cause. (patientdeath)
- Medical officers review medical history and autopsy reports to identify the contributing factors.

## 8. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Pharmacovigilance Specialists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Pharmacovigilance specialists analyze adverse event data to identify trends and potential safety concerns. (summary)
- Data analysts generate reports on adverse events for regulatory compliance and internal analysis.
