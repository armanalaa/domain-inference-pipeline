 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Patients, Manufacturers

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals document adverse events experienced by patients.
- Patients report adverse events directly to the FDA through various channels.
- Manufacturers submit reports on adverse events related to their products.
- Reports include demographic and administrative information.
- Reports detail drug information from the case reports.
- Reports provide reaction information from the reports.
- Reports record patient outcome information from the reports.

## 2. Adverse Event Data Collection

Performed By: FDA IT Staff

Activity:
- Collect adverse event data from various sources, including initial report image ID numbers (if available).
- Store collected data in the FDA_AdverseEvents database.

## 3. Adverse Event Data Processing

Performed By: FDA Data Analysts

Activity:
- Process and organize adverse event data for analysis.
- Ensure data accuracy and completeness before further processing.

## 4. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: FDA Researchers, Data Scientists

Activity:
- Analyze processed adverse event data to identify trends and patterns.
- Evaluate the potential relationship between drugs and adverse events.

## 5. Adverse Event Report Distribution

Performed By: FDA Communication Officers

Activity:
- Distribute summarized adverse event reports to relevant stakeholders, such as drug manufacturers.
- Provide individual case reports upon request through the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA).

## 6. Adverse Event Database Maintenance

Performed By: FDA IT Staff

Activity:
- Maintain and update the FDA_AdverseEvents database regularly.
- Ensure data integrity, security, and accessibility for authorized users.

## 7. FOIA Request Handling

Performed By: FDA Administrative Staff

Activity:
- Handle FOIA requests related to adverse event reports.
- Provide summarized FAERS reports or individual case reports upon request.
