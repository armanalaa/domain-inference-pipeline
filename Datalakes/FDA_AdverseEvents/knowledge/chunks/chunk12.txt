 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting by Clinical Investigators

Performed By: Clinical Investigators

Activity:
- Record adverse event details such as patient information, drug name, and symptoms.
- Document the start and end date of the adverse event.
- Describe the nature and severity of the adverse event.
- Include any relevant medical history or concomitant medications.

## 2. Adverse Event Transmission to Sponsors

Performed By: Clinical Investigators, Sponsors

Activity:
- Package the adverse event reports for transmission to sponsors.
- Ensure all necessary information is included in the report.
- Submit the adverse event reports electronically to the sponsor.

## 3. Adverse Event Review by Sponsors

Performed By: Sponsors, Medical Safety Officers

Activity:
- Review the received adverse event reports for completeness and validity.
- Assess the potential impact of the adverse event on product safety.
- Determine if further investigation is required.

## 4. Adverse Event Report Submission to Ethics Committees

Performed By: Sponsors, Ethics Committees

Activity:
- Forward the approved adverse event reports to ethics committees.
- Ensure all necessary information is included in the report.
- Provide updates on the status of the adverse event investigation.

## 5. Adverse Event Reporting to Regulatory Authorities

Performed By: Sponsors, Regulatory Authorities

Activity:
- Submit the approved adverse event reports to regulatory authorities.
- Include any relevant findings from the investigation.
- Ensure timely and accurate reporting in compliance with regulations.

## 6. Adverse Event Analysis by Regulatory Authorities

Performed By: Regulatory Authorities, Safety Evaluators

Activity:
- Analyze the received adverse event reports for trends or patterns.
- Assess the potential impact of the adverse events on public health.
- Determine if regulatory action is required.

## 7. Adverse Event Report Archiving

Performed By: Regulatory Authorities, Data Managers

Activity:
- Store the archived adverse event reports securely for future reference.
- Ensure all necessary information is included in the archive.
- Maintain the integrity and accessibility of the archived data.
