 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies document adverse events related to their products. (Product_Details)
- Healthcare providers record patient information and details of the adverse event. (Patient_Info, Adverse_Event_Details)
- Both parties assign a unique identifier to each report. (Report_ID)

## 2. Data Preparation for Transmission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies map adverse event data to the ICH ICSR DTD. (ICH_ICSR_DTD_Mapping)
- They populate required fields with unit measurements using ICH M2 numeric codes. (Unit_Measurements, E2BM_Unit_Codes)
- They specify routes of administration using ICH M2 numeric codes. (Routes_of_Administration, E2BM_Route_Codes)

## 3. SGML Declaration Preparation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies choose the appropriate DCL file based on the character set used in the report. (DCL_File)
- They include information about the character encoding, delimiters, and SGML features used in the report. (SGML_Declaration)

## 4. Transmission of Adverse Event Reports

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, FDA

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies transmit adverse event reports to the FDA using the ICH ICSR message specification. (Transmitted_Report)
- The FDA receives and validates the report based on the provided SGML declaration. (Received_and_Validated_Report)

## 5. Acknowledgement of Received Report

Performed By: FDA

Activity:
- The FDA sends an acknowledgment message to the pharmaceutical company confirming receipt of the report. (Acknowledgement_Message)

## 6. Adverse Event Review and Analysis

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff reviews adverse event reports for potential safety concerns. (Reviewed_Report)
- They analyze the data to identify trends, patterns, or signals that may indicate a safety issue. (Analyzed_Data)
