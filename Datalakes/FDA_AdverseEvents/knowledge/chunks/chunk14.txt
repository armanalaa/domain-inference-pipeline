 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Safety Officers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Safety officers collect and document adverse event details according to the ICH ICSR specifications. (A.1, A.2, A.3.1, A.3.2)
- Data entry clerks input the collected data into the FDA_AdverseEvents database. (All relevant columns)
- Safety officers verify and confirm the accuracy of entered data. (A.1, A.2, A.3.1, A.3.2)

## 2. Case Identification and Duplicate Checking

Performed By: Data Analysts, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Data analysts cross-reference new reports with existing cases to identify duplicates. (A.1.11.1, A.1.11.2)
- Safety officers review and confirm the identification of duplicate cases. (A.1.11.1, A.1.11.2)

## 3. Patient Characteristics Documentation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input patient characteristics into the FDA_AdverseEvents database. (B.1.10, B.1.10.7.1)
- Safety officers verify and confirm the accuracy of entered patient data. (B.1.10, B.1.10.7.1)

## 4. Event/Reaction Documentation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input event/reaction details into the FDA_AdverseEvents database. (B.2)
- Safety officers verify and confirm the accuracy of entered event data. (B.2)

## 5. Drug Information Documentation

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Safety Officers

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input drug information into the FDA_AdverseEvents database. (B.4, B.4.k.17.2, B.4.k.18)
- Safety officers verify and confirm the accuracy of entered drug data. (B.4, B.4.k.17.2, B.4.k.18)

## 6. Case Report Analysis

Performed By: Safety Officers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Safety officers analyze adverse event reports to assess potential risks. (B.5)
- Data analysts support safety officers by providing relevant data and insights. (B.5)

## 7. Case Report Review and Approval

Performed By: Safety Officers, Supervisors

Activity:
- Safety officers review and evaluate completed case reports. (All sections)
- Supervisors approve or request revisions for the submitted case reports. (All sections)
