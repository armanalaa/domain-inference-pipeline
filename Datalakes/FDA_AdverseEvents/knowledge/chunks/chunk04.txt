 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Drug Safety Officers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Drug safety officers monitor adverse event reports submitted by various sources.
- Data analysts extract and clean data from the FDA_AdverseEvents database for analysis.
- Drug safety officers review adverse event reports to assess potential risks associated with specific drugs.
- Data analysts update the FDA_AdverseEvents database with new reports, ensuring data accuracy and completeness.
- Drug safety officers collaborate with pharmaceutical companies to address identified issues and implement corrective actions.

## 2. Adverse Event Querying

Performed By: Researchers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Researchers formulate queries using the openFDA API to retrieve specific adverse event data from the FDA_AdverseEvents database.
- Data analysts run queries on the FDA_AdverseEvents database to extract relevant information for analysis or reporting purposes.
- Researchers analyze the retrieved data to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with certain drugs or pharmacologic classes.
- Data analysts generate reports based on the analyzed data, providing insights to drug safety officers and other stakeholders.

## 3. Adverse Event Classification

Performed By: Drug Safety Officers, Medical Experts

Activity:
- Drug safety officers review adverse event reports and classify them according to their severity and potential impact on public health.
- Medical experts provide guidance on the classification of adverse events based on their medical knowledge and understanding of drug effects.
- Drug safety officers update the FDA_AdverseEvents database with the classified adverse event information.

## 4. Adverse Event Follow-up

Performed By: Drug Safety Officers, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Drug safety officers initiate follow-ups with pharmaceutical companies to gather additional information about reported adverse events.
- Pharmaceutical companies provide the requested information and collaborate with drug safety officers to address identified issues.
- Drug safety officers assess the effectiveness of corrective actions taken by pharmaceutical companies and monitor the ongoing safety of their products.

## 5. Adverse Event Report Dissemination

Performed By: Communication Specialists, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports summarizing adverse event data for various audiences, such as healthcare professionals, patients, and the general public.
- Communication specialists review and edit the reports to ensure clarity and accessibility.
- Communication specialists disseminate the reports through appropriate channels, such as websites, newsletters, or conferences.

## 6. Adverse Event Database Maintenance

Performed By: Data Administrators, IT Specialists

Activity:
- Data administrators ensure the FDA_AdverseEvents database is properly maintained and backed up to prevent data loss.
- IT specialists troubleshoot any technical issues that may arise in the database or related systems.
- Data administrators perform regular audits of the FDA_AdverseEvents database to identify and address any inconsistencies or errors.

## 7. Adverse Event Analysis and Reporting (Advanced)

Performed By: Researchers, Data Scientists

Activity:
- Researchers conduct advanced analysis on adverse event data to uncover hidden patterns, trends, and correlations.
- Data scientists develop predictive models to forecast potential risks associated with specific drugs or pharmacologic classes.
- Researchers publish their findings in scientific journals or present them at conferences to contribute to the broader understanding of drug safety.
