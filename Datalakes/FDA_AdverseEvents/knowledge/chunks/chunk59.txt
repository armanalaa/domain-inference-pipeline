 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies submit adverse event reports to regulatory bodies by providing report ID, product details, and event description.
- Healthcare providers document adverse events experienced by patients, including patient demographics, event details, and outcome information.

## 2. Case Safety Report Acknowledgement

Performed By: Regulatory Bodies

Activity:
- Regulatory bodies acknowledge receipt of individual case safety reports with an acknowledgment number.

## 3. Adverse Event Categorization

Performed By: Regulatory Bodies

Activity:
- Regulatory bodies categorize adverse events based on their severity, nature, and relationship to the product.

## 4. Case Safety Report Review

Performed By: Regulatory Bodies

Activity:
- Regulatory bodies review case safety reports for completeness, accuracy, and relevance to the product's safety profile.

## 5. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Regulatory Bodies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies investigate adverse events reported for their products by gathering additional information and assessing causality.
- Regulatory bodies may request further investigation from the pharmaceutical company to ensure thorough evaluation of the adverse event.

## 6. Adverse Event Response

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Regulatory Bodies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies respond to adverse events by updating product labeling, modifying manufacturing processes, or initiating additional studies as necessary.
- Regulatory bodies may issue communications to healthcare providers and the public regarding the adverse event and any actions taken in response.

## 7. Adverse Event Database Maintenance

Performed By: IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff maintain the database by ensuring data integrity, performing backups, and addressing any technical issues that may arise.
