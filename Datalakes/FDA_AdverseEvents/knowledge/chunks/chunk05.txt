 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: FDA Staff, Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- FDA staff review adverse event reports submitted by pharmaceutical companies and healthcare providers.
- Healthcare providers record patient details, drugs taken, and reactions experienced in the Patient Information, Drugs, and Reactions sections of the report.
- Pharmaceutical companies submit adverse event reports to FDA through the API using search queries or count queries.

## 2. Adverse Event Data Processing

Performed By: FDA IT Staff

Activity:
- FDA IT staff process incoming adverse event reports and store them in the FDA_AdverseEvents database.
- They ensure data quality and consistency by applying standardized reporting protocols.

## 3. Adverse Event Data Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, FDA Staff

Activity:
- Data analysts extract adverse event reports from the FDA_AdverseEvents database for analysis.
- They identify trends across diverse product categories to support timely regulatory decision-making.

## 4. Adverse Event Report Verification and Validation

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff verify and validate adverse event reports to ensure their accuracy and completeness.
- They may request additional information from the reporting party if necessary.

## 5. Adverse Event Case Processing

Performed By: FDA Case Processors

Activity:
- FDA case processors review and classify adverse event cases based on their severity, nature, and potential impact on public health.
- They may escalate cases to relevant FDA centers for further investigation or action.

## 6. Adverse Event Report Redaction

Performed By: AI Tools

Activity:
- AI tools automatically redact sensitive information from adverse event reports before they are stored in the FDA_AdverseEvents database.

## 7. Consumer Complaints Management

Performed By: FDA Staff, Consumers

Activity:
- Consumers submit consumer complaints related to FDA-regulated products through various channels.
- FDA staff review and process these complaints, investigating potential issues and taking appropriate action if necessary.

## 8. Regulatory Misconduct Reporting

Performed By: Whistleblowers, FDA Staff

Activity:
- Whistleblowers report regulatory misconduct related to FDA-regulated products.
- FDA staff investigate these reports and take appropriate action if misconduct is confirmed.
