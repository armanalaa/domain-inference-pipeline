 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Patients, Manufacturers

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals document adverse events observed in patients using the event date, patient demographics, and event details.
- Patients or their representatives report adverse events experienced with a specific product, providing personal information and event description.
- Manufacturers receive reports from healthcare professionals and patients, reviewing them for accuracy and completeness before submission to regulatory bodies.

## 2. Case Processing

Performed By: FDA Reviewers

Activity:
- FDA reviewers assess the received adverse event reports for relevance to the product under investigation, verifying the report's authenticity and completeness.
- Reviewers categorize adverse events based on their severity, nature, and potential impact on public health.
- FDA reviewers identify any trends or patterns in the reported adverse events to determine if further investigation is necessary.

## 3. Data Entry and Management

Performed By: FDA Data Entry Staff

Activity:
- FDA data entry staff input the verified adverse event reports into the database, ensuring accurate recording of all relevant details such as report ID, product information, and event description.
- Data entry staff maintain the integrity and consistency of the database by resolving any discrepancies or inconsistencies in the reported data.

## 4. Case Follow-up

Performed By: FDA Investigators

Activity:
- FDA investigators follow up on adverse event reports requiring further investigation, contacting healthcare professionals and patients for additional information.
- Investigators analyze the collected data to determine the causality between the product and the reported adverse event.
- If necessary, investigators recommend actions such as product recall or label changes based on their findings.

## 5. Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: FDA Analysts

Activity:
- FDA analysts analyze the data in the database to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with specific products.
- Analysts generate reports summarizing their findings for internal use by regulatory bodies or external dissemination to the public.
- Reports may include recommendations for product safety improvements or updates on ongoing investigations.

## 6. Compliance Monitoring

Performed By: FDA Compliance Officers

Activity:
- Compliance officers monitor manufacturers' adherence to reporting requirements, reviewing their submissions for completeness and accuracy.
- Officers take enforcement actions against non-compliant manufacturers, such as fines or product recalls.
- Compliance officers collaborate with other departments to ensure consistent application of regulations across the industry.

## 7. Stakeholder Communication

Performed By: FDA Public Affairs Staff

Activity:
- Public affairs staff communicate adverse event information and regulatory updates to various stakeholders, including manufacturers, healthcare professionals, and the general public.
- They maintain transparency by providing regular updates on ongoing investigations and product safety issues.
- Public affairs staff also manage media inquiries related to adverse events and FDA actions.
