 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies record new adverse events related to their products by providing details such as event description, date of occurrence, and patient information.
- Healthcare providers document adverse events experienced by patients, including symptoms, severity, and duration.
- Both parties assign unique identifiers to each reported adverse event.

## 2. MedDRA Term Assignment

Performed By: Pharmacovigilance Teams

Activity:
- Pharmacovigilance teams classify adverse events using Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (MedDRA) terms, ensuring accurate and consistent coding.

## 3. Active Substance and Product Association

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies associate each reported adverse event with the appropriate active substance and product.

## 4. Result Tests Procedures Documentation

Performed By: Investigators, Laboratory Technicians

Activity:
- Investigators or laboratory technicians document any tests and procedures relevant to the investigation of the patient. (Optional)

## 5. Safety Report Versioning

Performed By: Data Managers

Activity:
- Data managers assign version numbers to safety reports for tracking different versions of an adverse event report.

## 6. MedDRA Version Recording

Performed By: Pharmacovigilance Teams

Activity:
- Pharmacovigilance teams record the MedDRA version number used for each term in the adverse event report.

## 7. Report Identification and Sender Identification

Performed By: Data Managers, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Data managers ensure unique identifiers are assigned to each safety report, while pharmaceutical companies provide their own identification details.
