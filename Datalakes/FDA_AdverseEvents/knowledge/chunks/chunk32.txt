 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Patient Data Registration

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Records patient initials (B.1.1.1a) and email address (B.1.1.1b) for future communication.
- Registers patient's medical record numbers from GP, specialist, hospital, and investigation sources (B.1.1.1c, B.1.1.1d).
- Documents the patient's date of birth (B.1.2.1b) and age at time of onset (B.1.2.2a) in a standard format.

## 2. Patient Demographic Information Collection

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Records patient's weight (B.1.3), height (B.1.5), and sex (B.1.6) for demographic analysis.
- Documents the last menstrual period date (B.1.6b) if applicable.

## 3. Medical History Documentation

Performed By: Medical Historian

Activity:
- Compiles relevant medical history and concurrent conditions (B.1.7.2) for each patient episode.
- Records the start and end dates of each medical episode (B.1.7.1b, B.1.7.1c, B.1.7.1e).
- Documents any continuation or comments related to the medical history (B.1.7.1d, B.1.7.1g).

## 4. Past Drug Therapy History Recording

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Records the name of reported drugs (B.1.8a) and their start and end dates (B.1.8c, B.1.8e).
- Documents the indication for each drug (B.1.8f.2) and any associated reactions (B.1.8g.2).

## 5. Death Reporting

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Records the date of death (B.1.9.1b) for deceased patients.
