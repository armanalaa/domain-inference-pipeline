 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Patients, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals document adverse events observed in patients (occurcountry).
- Patients or their representatives report personal experiences of adverse events (additionaldocument).
- Pharmaceutical companies collect and verify reports from various sources.
- Adverse event details are recorded including date of occurrence (receivedate, receiptdate), type (reporttype), seriousness (serious, seriousnessdeath, seriousnesslifethreatening, seriousnesshospitalization, seriousnessdisabling, seriousnesscongenitalanomali, seriousnessother).
- The transmission date of the report is recorded (transmissiondate).

## 2. Transmission of Adverse Event Reports

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, EDI Systems

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies prepare and transmit adverse event reports in a standardized format (ICH ICSR Specifications).
- EDI systems package the data for secure transmission via physical media or over the Internet.
- A unique message number is assigned to each transmission (Message Number).
- The sender's identification is included in the transmission (Message Sender Identifier).

## 3. Receipt and Validation of Adverse Event Reports

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff receives adverse event reports via secure EDI or physical media.
- The received reports are validated for syntactic correctness using an SGML parser (nsgmls).
- Invalid reports are flagged and corrected as necessary.

## 4. Tracking and Routing of Adverse Event Reports

Performed By: FDA Systems, FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA systems track the status of each adverse event report (Message Number).
- Reports are routed to appropriate departments based on their severity and type (reporttype, serious).

## 5. Analysis and Investigation of Adverse Event Reports

Performed By: FDA Scientists, FDA Regulatory Staff

Activity:
- FDA scientists analyze adverse event reports for patterns and potential risks.
- FDA regulatory staff investigate the reported events to determine if further action is needed (seriousnessdeath, seriousnesslifethreatening, seriousnesshospitalization).

## 6. Response and Communication Regarding Adverse Event Reports

Performed By: FDA Regulatory Staff, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- FDA regulatory staff determine appropriate responses to adverse event reports (seriousnessdeath, seriousnesslifethreatening, seriousnesshospitalization).
- Communication is sent to the reporting party and the pharmaceutical company involved.

## 7. Update of Adverse Event Database

Performed By: FDA Data Entry Staff

Activity:
- FDA data entry staff update the FDA_AdverseEvents database with validated reports.
