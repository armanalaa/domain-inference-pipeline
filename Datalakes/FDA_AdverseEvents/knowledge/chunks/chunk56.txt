 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Report Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Safety Officers, Regulatory Agencies' ICSR Processing Teams

Activity:
- Safety officers compile adverse event details and submit individual case safety reports (ICSRs) to regulatory agencies. (icsr_submission)
- Regulatory teams verify the receipt of ICSRs and acknowledge their successful loading into the database. (receipt_acknowledgement)
- In case of errors, regulatory teams provide specific error messages for rectification. (error_message)

## 2. Error Handling and Resubmission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' IT Support, Regulatory Agencies' ICSR Processing Teams

Activity:
- Upon receiving error messages, pharmaceutical companies' IT support rectifies the issues and resubmits the ICSRs. (error_rectification)
- Regulatory teams reprocess the corrected ICSRs and update their status in the database. (icsr_reprocessing)

## 3. ICSR Acknowledgment Generation

Performed By: Regulatory Agencies' Automated Systems, Regulatory Agencies' ICSR Processing Teams

Activity:
- Upon successful loading of ICSRs, automated systems generate acknowledgement messages for each submitted report. (acknowledgement_generation)
- Regulatory teams review the generated acknowledgements and ensure their accuracy before sending them back to the pharmaceutical companies. (acknowledgement_review)

## 4. ICSR Database Management

Performed By: Regulatory Agencies' Database Administrators

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the database structure and ensure its integrity, allowing for efficient storage of ICSRs. (database_management)
- Database administrators monitor the database for errors or inconsistencies and take corrective actions as necessary. (error_monitoring)

## 5. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: Regulatory Agencies' Safety Officers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Safety officers and data analysts analyze the stored ICSRs to identify trends, patterns, or potential safety concerns related to specific drugs or medical devices. (adverse_event_analysis)
- Based on their findings, they may recommend further investigation or regulatory actions. (recommendation_for_investigation)
