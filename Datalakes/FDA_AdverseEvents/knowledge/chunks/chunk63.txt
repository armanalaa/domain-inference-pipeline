 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Drug Labeler Registration and Submission

Performed By: Drug Manufacturers, Repackagers, Relabelers, Private Labelers

Activity:
- Register as a drug labeler with the FDA to obtain an establishment number.
- Submit electronic listing files containing product information for each drug.
- Assign unique three-segment National Drug Code (NDC) numbers to each drug.
- Update the NDC Directory daily with new or modified drug listings.

## 2. FDA Data Verification and Approval

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- Review submitted electronic listing files for accuracy and completeness.
- Verify that all required information is included in each NDC Directory entry.
- Ensure that the content of each NDC Directory entry is the responsibility of the labeler submitting the SPL file.

## 3. Drug Product Identification and Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Pharmacies, Distributors

Activity:
- Use the unique NDC number to identify and report on final marketed drugs.
- Access the NDC Directory for up-to-date information on drug products.

## 4. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Patients, Manufacturers

Activity:
- Document any adverse events associated with a specific drug product.
- Submit adverse event reports to the FDA through the MedWatch Safety Information and Adverse Event Reporting program.

## 5. FDA Adverse Event Data Management

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- Receive, review, and process adverse event reports submitted by various sources.
- Investigate reported adverse events to determine if they are related to a specific drug product.
- Update the FDA_AdverseEvents database with new or modified adverse event information.

## 6. Adverse Event Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Researchers, Public Health Officials

Activity:
- Analyze data from the FDA_AdverseEvents database to identify trends and patterns in adverse events.
- Generate reports on adverse event findings for various stakeholders, including drug manufacturers, healthcare providers, and the general public.

## 7. Compliance Monitoring and Enforcement

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- Monitor compliance with regulations related to drug labeler registration, product listing, and adverse event reporting.
- Take enforcement action against non-compliant entities, including issuing warnings, fines, or other penalties.
