 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs Specialists, Case Processors

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs specialists receive adverse event reports from various sources.
- Case processors review the received reports to ensure completeness and validity.
- Case processors assign unique identifiers to each report for tracking purposes.
- Case processors verify the patient's relevant medical history and concurrent conditions.
- Case processors record details about the reaction(s) or event(s).

## 2. Drug Information Collection

Performed By: Case Processors, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Case processors collect information about the drug involved in the adverse event.
- Data entry clerks input the drug's characterization, medicinal product, and dosage details.
- Data entry clerks record the drug's authorization number and country of origin.
- Data entry clerks document the drug's administration route, dosage form, and paradministration (if applicable).
- Data entry clerks input the reaction gestation period and related information.

## 3. Test Results Recording

Performed By: Case Processors, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Case processors collect test results relevant to the investigation of the patient.
- Data entry clerks record the test date, name, result, unit, and any additional information.
- Data entry clerks input low and high test range values (if applicable).

## 4. Parent Information Documentation

Performed By: Case Processors, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Case processors document the parent's identification details.
- Data entry clerks record the parent's birthdate, age, sex, weight, and height.
- Data entry clerks input the parent's medical history and concurrent conditions.
- Data entry clerks record the relevant past drug therapy of the parent.

## 5. Death Reporting and Autopsy Information Recording

Performed By: Case Processors, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Case processors document details about the patient's death (if applicable).
- Data entry clerks record the death date and autopsy information (if applicable).
- Data entry clerks input the cause of death report and determine autopsy findings.
