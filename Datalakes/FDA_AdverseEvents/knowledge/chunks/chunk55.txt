 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Pharmaceutical Companies' Safety Officers

Activity:
- Healthcare providers document adverse events related to drug usage (adversetevent).
- Pharmaceutical companies' safety officers input the event details into the FDA Adverse Events database.

## 2. Case Report Creation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Safety Officers

Activity:
- Safety officers compile adverse event data into a structured case report (casereport).
- They include relevant patient information, drug details, and event description.

## 3. Case Report Transmission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff electronically transmit the completed case reports to the FDA (transmittedata).
- They use the Individual Case Safety Reports Message Specification for transmission.

## 4. Acknowledgement of Transmission

Performed By: FDA IT Staff

Activity:
- FDA IT staff receive and validate the transmitted data set (transmittedata).
- Upon successful validation, they send an acknowledgment message to the pharmaceutical company.

## 5. Case Review and Investigation

Performed By: FDA Safety Officers

Activity:
- Safety officers review received adverse event case reports (casereport).
- They investigate potential safety concerns related to the reported drug.

## 6. Risk Assessment and Action

Performed By: FDA Regulatory Committees

Activity:
- Regulatory committees assess the risk level of the adverse event (risklevel).
- Based on the assessment, they decide on appropriate actions such as warnings or drug recalls.

## 7. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: FDA Data Analysts

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze trends in reported adverse events (trendanalysis).
- They generate reports for further investigation, risk assessment, and public dissemination.
