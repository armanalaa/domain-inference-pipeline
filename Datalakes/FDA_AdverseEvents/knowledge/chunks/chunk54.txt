 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical companies document adverse events related to their products. (Product_Details)
- Healthcare providers record patient information and details of the adverse event. (Patient_Info, Adverse_Event_Details)
- Both parties submit the reports to the FDA through the Electronic Submission Gateway. (Submission_Details)

## 2. Case Processing

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff verify the authenticity of the submitted adverse event reports. (Report_Verification)
- They assign a unique identifier to each report for tracking purposes. (Report_ID)
- FDA staff review the reported adverse events for potential safety concerns. (Safety_Review)

## 3. Data Analysis and Aggregation

Performed By: FDA Data Analysts, Epidemiologists

Activity:
- Data analysts aggregate adverse event reports based on common factors such as product, patient demographics, and symptoms. (Data_Aggregation)
- Epidemiologists analyze the aggregated data to identify trends and potential risks associated with specific products or patient groups. (Risk_Analysis)
- They prepare reports summarizing their findings for further review by FDA management. (Report_Preparation)

## 4. Risk Assessment and Management

Performed By: FDA Management, Regulatory Affairs

Activity:
- FDA management evaluates the risk posed by the identified adverse events and determines appropriate actions. (Risk_Evaluation)
- Regulatory affairs team communicates with pharmaceutical companies regarding any necessary product updates or safety warnings. (Communication_with_Pharma)
- They monitor the implementation of these changes to ensure compliance. (Compliance_Monitoring)

## 5. Adverse Event Database Maintenance

Performed By: FDA IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff maintain and update the FDA_AdverseEvents database with new reports, aggregated data, and risk assessments. (Database_Updates)
- They ensure the database remains secure and accessible only to authorized personnel. (Security_Management)
- IT staff perform regular backups and troubleshoot any issues that may arise. (Backup_and_Troubleshooting)
