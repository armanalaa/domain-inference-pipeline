 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Patients, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Healthcare providers record patient details and adverse event information.
- Patients report their own adverse events related to medication usage.
- Pharmaceutical companies input data from internal sources or external reports.

## 2. Case Data Entry

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input the received adverse event reports into the system.

## 3. Case Validation and Verification

Performed By: Case Validators, Medical Reviewers

Activity:
- Case validators verify the authenticity of the reported cases.
- Medical reviewers assess the medical relevance and accuracy of the data.

## 4. Case Categorization and Classification

Performed By: Case Analysts

Activity:
- Case analysts categorize and classify adverse events based on their nature and severity.

## 5. Data Analysis and Aggregation

Performed By: Data Scientists, Statisticians

Activity:
- Data scientists analyze the aggregated data to identify trends and patterns.
- Statisticians perform statistical analysis to determine the significance of findings.

## 6. Risk Assessment and Communication

Performed By: Risk Management Team, Regulatory Affairs

Activity:
- The risk management team assesses the potential risks associated with the adverse events.
- Regulatory affairs communicate the identified risks to relevant stakeholders.

## 7. Adverse Event Follow-up and Investigation

Performed By: Investigators, Medical Experts

Activity:
- Investigators conduct follow-ups on reported cases for additional information.
- Medical experts review the investigation findings and provide expert opinions.

## 8. Regulatory Reporting and Compliance

Performed By: Regulatory Affairs, Legal Team

Activity:
- Regulatory affairs prepare and submit reports to regulatory bodies as required.
- The legal team ensures compliance with relevant regulations and guidelines.
