 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Patients, Manufacturers

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals document adverse events observed in patients.
- Patients report their own adverse events directly to the FDA.
- Manufacturers submit reports on adverse events related to their products.
- Reports include details about the drug product, reactions, indications for use, route of administration, and dose.

## 2. Data Submission

Performed By: Manufacturers, Healthcare Professionals, FDA Staff

Activity:
- Manufacturers submit adverse event reports to the FDA through various channels.
- Healthcare professionals report adverse events via the MedWatch Safety Information and Adverse Event Reporting Program.
- FDA staff process and store incoming adverse event reports in the FDA_AdverseEvents database.

## 3. Data Validation and Verification

Performed By: FDA Staff

Activity:
- FDA staff do not extensively validate or verify adverse event reports.
- Reports are not medically validated, and a causal relationship cannot be established between product and reactions listed in a report.

## 4. Data Analysis and Interpretation

Performed By: Researchers, Public Health Officials, FDA Staff

Activity:
- Researchers and public health officials analyze adverse event reports to identify trends and potential safety issues.
- FDA staff use the data for regulatory decision making and product risk assessment.

## 5. API Usage

Performed By: Developers, Researchers, Public Health Officials

Activity:
- Users read the API Basics documentation to learn how to access the FDA_AdverseEvents data.
- Users review the list of searchable fields available in the Drug Adverse Event dataset.
- Users construct queries using the openFDA API to retrieve specific adverse event reports.

## 6. Data Limitations Awareness

Performed By: Users, FDA Staff

Activity:
- Users are aware that the reported events do not necessarily represent a cause and effect relationship.
- Users understand that the data has limitations, such as potential underreporting and lack of certainty about the causal relationship between products and events.
