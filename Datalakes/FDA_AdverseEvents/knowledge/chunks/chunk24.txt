 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. ICSR Submission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff compile adverse event details and generate an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR).
- They assign a unique identifier to the ICSR for tracking purposes.
- Staff record the date of receipt of the most recent information about the adverse event.

## 2. ICSR Transmission

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company IT Staff, Regulatory Authority IT Staff

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company IT staff encrypt and transmit the ICSR to the regulatory authority's EDI gateway.
- Regulatory authority IT staff decrypt and parse the SGML data file upon receipt.

## 3. ICSR Validation

Performed By: Regulatory Authority IT Staff

Activity:
- If there are no SGML parsing errors, the regulatory authority's application processes the ICSR further.
- If there is an SGML parsing error, the whole file is rejected and the sender must re-transmit the ICSR.

## 4. ICSR Acknowledgment Generation

Performed By: Regulatory Authority IT Staff

Activity:
- Upon successful validation, regulatory authority IT staff create an SGML message to comply with the ICSR Acknowledgement DTD.
- The sender of the ICSR can then extract data from this ICSR Acknowledgement Message to determine the status of each ICSR.

## 5. ICSR Status Tracking

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, Regulatory Authority Staff

Activity:
- The sender of the ICSR can track the status of each ICSR based on data extracted from the ICSR Acknowledgement Message.
- Regulatory authority staff use this information for monitoring and managing adverse event reports.
