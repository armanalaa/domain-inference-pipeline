 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Report Submission

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Regulatory Authorities, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals record adverse event details following patient encounters (A.2.1.1a - reportertitle, A.2.1.1b - reportergivename, A.2.1.1c - reportermiddlename, A.2.1.1d - reporterfamilyname).
- Regulatory authorities receive adverse event reports from healthcare professionals (A.2.1.2a - reporterorganization).
- Pharmaceutical companies submit adverse event reports to regulatory authorities (A.1.10.2 - companynumb).

## 2. Adverse Event Report Identification and Linking

Performed By: Regulatory Authority Staff

Activity:
- Regulatory authority staff assign unique case numbers to each received adverse event report (A.1.12 - linkreportnumb).
- If multiple reports exist for the same case, regulatory authority staff identify and link them (A.1.11 - duplicate).

## 3. Adverse Event Report Nullification

Performed By: Regulatory Authority Staff

Activity:
- Regulatory authority staff nullify adverse event reports when necessary (casenullification).
- They provide reasons for nullification (nullificationreason).

## 4. Adverse Event Report Medically Confirmation

Performed By: Healthcare Professionals, Regulatory Authority Staff

Activity:
- Healthcare professionals confirm if the adverse event was medically confirmed (A.1.14 - medicallyconfirm).
- If not initially from a health professional, regulatory authority staff may seek confirmation (A.1.14).

## 5. Adverse Event Report Source Verification

Performed By: Regulatory Authority Staff

Activity:
- Regulatory authority staff verify the source(s) of case identifiers in previous transmissions (duplicatesource).

## 6. Duplicate Case Identifier Management

Performed By: Regulatory Authority Staff

Activity:
- If multiple reports exist for the same case, regulatory authority staff maintain one number as the case number in its original field (A.1.11 - duplicate).
- They list the second number as a suspected duplicate.

## 7. Adverse Event Report Transmission

Performed By: Regulatory Authorities, Pharmaceutical Companies

Activity:
- Regulatory authorities transmit adverse event reports to other regulatory authorities (A.1.10.1 - authoritynumb or A.1.10.2 - companynumb).
- Pharmaceutical companies retransmit ICSRs if necessary, using their own sender's case report number (A.1.10.2 - companynumb).
