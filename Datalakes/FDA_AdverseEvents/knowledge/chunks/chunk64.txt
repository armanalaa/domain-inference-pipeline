 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: FDA Officers, Manufacturers, and Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- FDA officers review adverse event reports submitted by manufacturers or healthcare providers.
- Manufacturers document adverse events related to their products in internal systems.
- Healthcare providers record adverse events experienced by patients and report them to the FDA.
- FDA officers assign unique NDC codes to each reported adverse event based on product, manufacturer, and package size.
- Manufacturers update their internal records with the assigned NDC codes.

## 2. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: FDA Officers, Manufacturers, and Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- FDA officers investigate adverse events to determine if they are related to specific products.
- Manufacturers collaborate with the FDA during investigations by providing product information and batch details.
- Healthcare providers provide additional information about the adverse event and its impact on patients.

## 3. Adverse Event Classification

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- FDA officers classify adverse events based on their severity, type, and potential impact on public health.

## 4. Adverse Event Recall Initiation

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- If an adverse event is determined to be related to a specific product, the FDA initiates a recall of the affected products.

## 5. Recall Information Documentation

Performed By: Manufacturers and FDA Officers

Activity:
- Manufacturers document recall information in their internal systems, including affected products, reasons for recall, and corrective actions.
- FDA officers record recall information in the FDA Recall Enterprise System (RES).

## 6. Recall Information Dissemination

Performed By: FDA Officers

Activity:
- FDA officers disseminate recall information to the public through various channels, including press releases and online databases.

## 7. Adverse Event Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts and Researchers

Activity:
- Data analysts and researchers analyze adverse event data to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with specific products or manufacturers.

## 8. Adverse Event Reporting System Maintenance

Performed By: IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff maintain the FDA Adverse Events database, ensuring its accuracy, security, and accessibility for authorized users.
