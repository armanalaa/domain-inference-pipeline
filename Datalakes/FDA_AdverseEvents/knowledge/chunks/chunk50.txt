 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, Healthcare Providers, Patients

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff register new adverse events by recording the event details, product information, and MedDRA version for reaction/event term (reactionmeddraversionpt).
- Healthcare providers report adverse events to pharmaceutical companies by providing case narrative including clinical course, therapeutic measures, outcome, and additional relevant information (narrativeincludeclinical).
- Patients or their representatives submit reports of adverse events experienced with a specific product (reportercomment).

## 2. MedDRA Term Assignment

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff assign MedDRA version for medical history (patientepisodenamemeddraversion) and indication (patientindicationmeddraversion) to the patient's medical history.
- They also assign MedDRA version for autopsy-determined cause(s) of death (patientdetermautopsmeddraversion) when applicable.

## 3. Adverse Event Assessment

Performed By: Medical Experts, Pharmacovigilance Team

Activity:
- Medical experts assess the adverse event and determine the drug assessment method (drugassessmentmethod) and result (drugresult).
- They also record additional information on the drug (drugadditional).

## 4. Case Closure and Follow-up

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff, Medical Experts

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company staff close the case by recording the final assessment and outcome (drugreactionassesmeddraversion).
- Medical experts follow up on the adverse event to monitor its progression and potential impact (sendercomment).

## 5. Sender's Diagnosis/Syndrome Recording

Performed By: Healthcare Providers, Patients

Activity:
- Healthcare providers record their diagnosis or syndrome related to the adverse event (senderdiagnosis).
- Patients or their representatives provide information about their health status and any relevant syndromes (senderdiagnosismeddraversion).

## 6. Data Management and Analysis

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company IT Staff, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Pharmaceutical company IT staff manage the database and ensure data integrity (not specified in provided materials).
- Data analysts analyze adverse event data to identify trends, patterns, and potential risks associated with specific products (not specified in provided materials).
