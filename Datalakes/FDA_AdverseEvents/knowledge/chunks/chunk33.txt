 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Safety Officers, Medical Writers)

Activity:
- Safety officers gather information about adverse events from various sources.
- Medical writers review and analyze the gathered data to determine if it meets the criteria for an Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR).
- If an ICSR is deemed necessary, medical writers prepare and submit the report to the FDA.

## 2. Patient Death Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Safety Officers)

Activity:
- Safety officers record the reported cause(s) of on death for each patient death case.
- They determine whether an autopsy was performed and document the results.

## 3. Parent Information Recording

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Data Entry Clerks)

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input parent identification, age, date of birth, weight, height, sex, and relevant medical history into the database.

## 4. Parent Medical History Documentation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Medical Writers)

Activity:
- Medical writers document the parent's medical history and concurrent conditions in a repeatable block within the database.

## 5. Parent Past Drug Therapy Recording

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Data Entry Clerks)

Activity:
- Data entry clerks record the name of drugs, start date, end date, and indication for each drug therapy in a repeatable block within the database.

## 6. Adverse Event Investigation

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Safety Officers)

Activity:
- Safety officers investigate adverse events to determine if they are related to the product and document their findings in the database.

## 7. Regulatory Compliance Monitoring

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Company Staff (Compliance Officers)

Activity:
- Compliance officers ensure that all adverse event reports are submitted to the FDA in compliance with regulatory requirements.
