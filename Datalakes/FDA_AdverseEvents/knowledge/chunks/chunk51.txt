 # FDA_AdverseEvents — Business Workflows

## 1. Adverse Event Reporting

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Safety Officers, Healthcare Providers

Activity:
- Safety officers within pharmaceutical companies record adverse event details such as patient demographics, product information, and event description. (AdverseEvent, PatientDemographics, ProductInfo, EventDescription)
- Healthcare providers submit reports on observed adverse events to the relevant regulatory bodies. (ReportSubmission)

## 2. Case Processing

Performed By: FDA Case Processors

Activity:
- Case processors at the Food and Drug Administration review incoming adverse event reports for completeness and validity. (CaseReview, ReportValidation)
- If necessary, case processors request additional information from the reporting party to ensure a thorough investigation. (InformationRequest)

## 3. Adverse Event Coding

Performed By: FDA Coders

Activity:
- Coders within the FDA assign Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (MedDRA) codes to adverse events based on their nature and effects. (MedDRACoding)

## 4. Case Analysis

Performed By: FDA Analysts

Activity:
- Analysts at the FDA analyze coded adverse event cases to identify potential safety concerns or trends related to specific products. (CaseAnalysis, SafetyConcernIdentification)

## 5. Risk Assessment

Performed By: FDA Risk Assessors

Activity:
- Risk assessors evaluate the identified safety concerns and determine the level of risk associated with each adverse event. (RiskAssessment, RiskLevelDetermination)

## 6. Communication with Sponsors

Performed By: FDA Liaisons

Activity:
- Liaisons between the FDA and pharmaceutical companies communicate findings from the risk assessment process, including any necessary actions to address potential safety issues. (CommunicationWithSponsors)

## 7. Adverse Event Follow-up

Performed By: Pharmaceutical Companies' Safety Officers

Activity:
- Safety officers within pharmaceutical companies follow up on FDA communications and take appropriate actions to address identified safety concerns, such as updating product labels or conducting further investigations. (FollowUpActions)
