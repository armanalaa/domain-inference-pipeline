 # IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark — Business Workflows

## 1. Movie Data Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the integrity and performance of the database by monitoring system metrics and optimizing queries.
- Content managers input new movies into the system by recording title, production year, kind, and associated companies.
- Content managers assign roles to actors in movies and record their names, character names, and notes about their performances.

## 2. Actor Data Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators ensure the accuracy of actor data by verifying MD5 sums and maintaining unique identifiers for each actor.
- Content managers register new actors by recording their names, genders, and associated movies.

## 3. Company Data Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the integrity of company data by verifying MD5 sums and ensuring unique identifiers for each company.
- Content managers input new companies into the system by recording their names, countries, types, and associated movies.

## 4. Keyword Assignment

Performed By: Content Managers

Activity:
- Content managers assign relevant keywords to movies based on their titles, genres, or themes.

## 5. Link Creation

Performed By: Content Managers

Activity:
- Content managers establish relationships between movies by linking them based on shared actors, companies, or themes.

## 6. Query Execution and Optimization

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Database administrators optimize query performance by adjusting join orders and estimating cardinalities to minimize errors.
- Data analysts execute queries to extract insights from the database for various purposes such as trend analysis or performance evaluation.
