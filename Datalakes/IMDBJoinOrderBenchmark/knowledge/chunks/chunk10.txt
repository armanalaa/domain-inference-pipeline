 # IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark — Business Workflows

## 1. Content Management and Data Entry

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Content managers register new movies by recording title, production year, kind, and related information.
- Data entry clerks input actor details such as name, role, and character name.
- Data entry clerks assign appropriate categories to each movie and actor.

## 2. Actor and Character Information Management

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Content managers update aka_name table with aliases for actors and actresses.
- Data entry clerks record additional information about actors like gender and nationality.
- Data entry clerks link characters to their respective movies using the cast_info table.

## 3. Company Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Company Representatives

Activity:
- HR managers register company details including name, country, type, and imdb_id.
- Company representatives update company_name with any changes or additions to the company's name.

## 4. Movie Information Management

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Content managers input movie details like title, production year, and kind into the aka_title table.
- Data entry clerks record additional information about movies such as episode number, season, and note.

## 5. Keyword and Link Association

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Content managers assign relevant keywords to movies using the keyword table.
- Data analysts link related movies together through the movie_link table based on common themes or genres.

## 6. Movie Information and Metadata Management

Performed By: Data Analysts, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Data analysts add and update various types of information about movies using the movie_info and movie_info_idx tables.
- IT administrators ensure proper indexing for efficient data retrieval and query performance.

## 7. Query Optimization and Analysis

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Database administrators analyze query performance and identify potential issues or bottlenecks.
- Data analysts optimize queries to improve efficiency and reduce response time.
