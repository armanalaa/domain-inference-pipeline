 # IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark — Business Workflows

## 1. Movie Catalog Management

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Content managers register new movies by recording title, production year, and genre.
- Data analysts assign unique identifiers to each movie for easy tracking.
- Content managers update movie information as necessary, including cast details and keywords.
- Data analysts maintain the integrity of the database by validating and verifying all updates.

## 2. Actor/Actress Management

Performed By: Casting Directors, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Casting directors add new actors and actresses to the system with their names, roles, and notable works.
- Data analysts assign unique identifiers to each actor or actress for easy tracking.
- Casting directors update actor/actress profiles as necessary, including biographical information and images.
- Data analysts validate and verify all updates to ensure accuracy.

## 3. Company Management

Performed By: Production Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Production managers add production companies to the system with their names, countries of origin, and types.
- Data analysts assign unique identifiers to each company for easy tracking.
- Production managers update company information as necessary, including contact details and affiliated movies.
- Data analysts validate and verify all updates to ensure accuracy.

## 4. Character Management

Performed By: Scriptwriters, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Scriptwriters create characters for movies with their names, roles, and relationships.
- Data analysts assign unique identifiers to each character for easy tracking.
- Scriptwriters update character details as necessary, including descriptions and backstories.
- Data analysts validate and verify all updates to ensure accuracy.

## 5. Join Estimation and Optimization

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Database administrators analyze the structure of the database to optimize join operations.
- Data analysts develop models for estimating the size and cost of joins based on historical data.
- Database administrators implement these models to improve query performance.
- Data analysts monitor the system's performance and adjust models as needed.

## 6. Query Execution and Monitoring

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Database administrators execute queries on the database to retrieve specific information.
- Data analysts analyze query results to gain insights into movie data trends.
- Database administrators monitor query performance and identify bottlenecks for optimization.
- Data analysts use visualization tools to present findings in an easily understandable format.

## 7. Quality Assurance and Error Handling

Performed By: Quality Assurance Analysts, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Quality assurance analysts test the system for errors and inconsistencies.
- Data analysts analyze error reports to identify patterns and root causes.
- Quality assurance analysts work with content managers, casting directors, production managers, and scriptwriters to correct any issues.
- Data analysts implement fixes and verify their effectiveness to maintain data integrity.
