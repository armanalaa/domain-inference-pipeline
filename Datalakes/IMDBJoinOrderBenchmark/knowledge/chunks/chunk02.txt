 # IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark — Business Workflows

## 1. Movie Data Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the schema and indexes for efficient data management (schema.sql, fkindexes.sql).
- Content managers register new movies by entering title, production year, kind, and associated companies (aka_title, company_name).
- Content managers assign unique identifiers to each movie (movie_id).

## 2. Actor Data Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the schema for actor data (char_name, aka_name).
- Content managers register new actors by entering name, gender, and associated movies (cast_info, char_name).
- Content managers assign unique identifiers to each actor (person_id).

## 3. Keyword Assignment

Performed By: Content Managers

Activity:
- Content managers categorize movies and actors by assigning relevant keywords (keyword).

## 4. Link Creation

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the schema for linking movies (movie_link).
- Content managers create links between related movies based on specified criteria (linked_movie_id, link_type_id).

## 5. Company Data Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the schema for company data (company_name, company_type).
- Content managers register new companies by entering name, country code, and associated movies (movie_companies).

## 6. Information Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the schema for movie information (movie_info, movie_info_idx).
- Content managers record various types of information about movies (info_type, info).

## 7. Complete Cast Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Content Managers

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the schema for complete cast data (complete_cast).
- Content managers mark which actors are part of a movie's complete cast (subject_id, status_id).
