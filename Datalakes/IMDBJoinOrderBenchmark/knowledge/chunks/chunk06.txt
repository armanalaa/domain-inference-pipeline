 # IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark — Business Workflows

## 1. Data Management and Integration

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Database administrators create and maintain the IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark database structure.
- Data analysts import and clean data from various sources, ensuring consistency and accuracy.
- Database administrators optimize database performance by creating appropriate indexes on foreign keys.

## 2. Actor and Title Management

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Content managers add new actors and titles to the respective catalogs, providing necessary details like names, roles, and production years.
- Data entry clerks input actor and title information into the aka_name, aka_title, char_name tables.

## 3. Company Management

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Content managers register movie companies, specifying their names, countries, and types.
- Data entry clerks input company information into the company_name table.

## 4. Cast Information Management

Performed By: Content Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Content managers assign roles to actors in specific movies and record related notes.
- Data entry clerks input cast information into the cast_info table.

## 5. Keyword and Link Association

Performed By: Content Taggers

Activity:
- Content taggers associate relevant keywords with movies based on their content.
- Content taggers establish links between related movies using appropriate link types.

## 6. Query Optimization and Analysis

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Database administrators develop and optimize queries to extract specific data from the IMDBJoinOrderBenchmark database.
- Data analysts analyze query performance, identifying areas for improvement in cardinality estimation and join ordering.
