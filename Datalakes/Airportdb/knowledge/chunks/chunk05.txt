 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Administrators, Airline Managers

Activity:
- Airline administrators register new airlines by entering the IATA code, airline name, and base airport.
- Airline managers update airline details as needed, including changing the airline name or base airport.
- Airline administrators assign airplanes to their respective airlines.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Airplane Administrators, Maintenance Staff

Activity:
- Airplane administrators register new airplanes by entering the capacity, type, and airline association.
- Maintenance staff track airplane maintenance records, including repairs and inspections.
- Airplane administrators update airplane details as needed, such as changing the airplane type or capacity.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airplane Administrators

Activity:
- Airplane administrators register new airplane types by entering the identifier and description.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Managers, Ground Staff

Activity:
- Airport managers register new airports by entering the IATA, ICAO, name, city, country, latitude, longitude, and geolocation.
- Ground staff update airport details as needed, such as changing the airport name or geolocation.

## 5. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Flight Schedulers, Airline Managers

Activity:
- Flight schedulers create new flight schedules by entering the flight number, from and to airports, departure and arrival times, days of operation, and associated airline.
- Airline managers update flight schedules as needed, such as changing the departure or arrival time.

## 6. Flight Booking

Performed By: Passengers, Travel Agents, Airlines Staff

Activity:
- Passengers or travel agents book flights by selecting a flight, choosing a seat, and providing passenger information.
- Airlines staff confirm the booking and record the booking details, including the flight ID, seat, passenger ID, price, and associated flight.

## 7. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Weather Stations Staff

Activity:
- Weather stations staff collect weather data by recording temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, direction, and weather conditions at specific stations.
