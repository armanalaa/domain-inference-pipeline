 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Administrators, Airline Managers

Activity:
- Airline administrators register new airlines by providing the IATA code, airline name, and base airport.
- Airline managers update airline details as needed, including changes to the airline name or base airport.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Airplane Administrators, Maintenance Staff

Activity:
- Airplane administrators register new aircraft by specifying the capacity, type, and assigning them to an airline.
- Maintenance staff track airplane maintenance schedules and record any necessary repairs or updates.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airplane Administrators

Activity:
- Airplane administrators define new aircraft types by providing a unique identifier and description.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Managers, Ground Staff

Activity:
- Airport managers register new airports with their IATA, ICAO, and name.
- Ground staff update airport details such as city, country, and geolocation.

## 5. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Airline Managers, Ground Staff

Activity:
- Airline managers create flight schedules for specific routes, including departure and arrival times, days of operation, and assigning aircraft.
- Ground staff update flight schedules as needed due to changes in operations or cancellations.

## 6. Booking Management

Performed By: Travel Agents, Passengers

Activity:
- Travel agents book flights for passengers by selecting a flight, assigning seats, and recording passenger information.
- Passengers confirm their bookings and pay the associated price.

## 7. Passenger Information Management

Performed By: Passenger Service Representatives

Activity:
- Passenger service representatives record passenger details such as passport number, name, birthdate, sex, address, email address, and telephone number.

## 8. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Meteorologists, Ground Staff

Activity:
- Meteorologists collect weather data from various stations, recording temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, direction, and weather conditions.
- Ground staff update the weather data for specific airports as needed.
