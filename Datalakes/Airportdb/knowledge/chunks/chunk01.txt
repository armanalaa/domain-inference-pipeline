 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Airline Management

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airlines by recording airline ID, IATA code, airline name, and base airport.
- Airline management updates airline details as needed, such as changing the airline name or base airport.
- Airline management assigns airplanes to specific airlines based on capacity and type.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Maintenance Staff

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airplanes by recording airplane ID, capacity, type, and assigned airline.
- Maintenance staff tracks airplane maintenance history, including repairs and inspections.
- Maintenance staff updates airplane details as needed, such as changing the airplane type or capacity.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airplane types by recording type ID, identifier, and description.
- Airline operations team updates airplane type details as needed, such as changing the description or adding a new identifier.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airport operations team registers new airports by recording airport ID, IATA code, ICAO code, and name.
- Airport operations team updates airport details as needed, such as changing the name or adding a new IATA code.

## 5. Geolocation Management

Performed By: Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airport operations team records geolocation data for each airport, including city, country, latitude, and longitude.

## 6. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Flight Operations Staff

Activity:
- Airline operations team creates new flight schedules by recording flight number, from and to airports, departure and arrival times, and assigned airline.
- Flight operations staff updates flight schedules as needed, such as changing the departure or arrival time.

## 7. Flight Registration

Performed By: Flight Operations Staff

Activity:
- Flight operations staff registers new flights by recording flight ID, flight number, assigned airplane, and associated flight schedule.

## 8. Booking Management

Performed By: Passengers, Travel Agents, Airline Reservation Staff

Activity:
- Passengers or travel agents place bookings by recording booking ID, flight ID, seat, and passenger details.
- Airline reservation staff updates bookings as needed, such as changing the passenger details or seat assignment.

## 9. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Weather Station Operators

Activity:
- Weather station operators record weather data by recording log date, time, station, temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed and direction, and weather conditions.
