 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Airline Management

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airlines by recording the airline ID, IATA code, airline name, and base airport.
- Airline management assigns airplane types to each airline based on capacity requirements.
- Airline management updates airline details when changes occur, such as modifying the airline name or base airport.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Maintenance Staff

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airplanes by recording the airplane ID, capacity, type, and associated airline.
- Maintenance staff schedules routine maintenance checks for each airplane based on its type and usage.
- Maintenance staff records airplane maintenance history, including repairs and inspections.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airplane types by recording the type ID, identifier, and description.
- Airline operations team updates airplane type details when changes occur, such as modifying the description or adding a new type.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airport operations team registers new airports by recording the airport ID, IATA code, ICAO code, and name.
- Airport operations team updates airport details when changes occur, such as modifying the airport name or location.

## 5. Geolocation Management

Performed By: Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airport operations team records geolocation data for each airport, including city, country, latitude, and longitude.

## 6. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Flight Operations Staff

Activity:
- Airline operations team creates new flight schedules by recording the flight number, departure and arrival airports, and days of operation for each route.
- Flight operations staff updates flight schedules when changes occur, such as modifying departure or arrival times.

## 7. Booking Management

Performed By: Passenger Services Representatives, Airline Reservation System

Activity:
- Passenger services representatives assist customers in booking flights by recording the booking ID, associated flight, seat, passenger ID, and price.

## 8. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Weather Station Operators

Activity:
- Weather station operators record weather data at various stations, including temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, direction, and weather conditions.
