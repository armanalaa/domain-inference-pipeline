 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Administrators, Airline Managers

Activity:
- Airline administrators register new airlines by entering airline ID, IATA code, airline name, and base airport.
- Airline managers update airline details as needed, modifying the airline name or base airport.
- Airline administrators assign airplanes to specific airlines by linking them through the airline_id column in the airplane table.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Airplane Managers, Maintenance Staff

Activity:
- Airplane managers register new airplanes by entering airplane ID, capacity, type ID, and airline ID.
- Maintenance staff track airplane maintenance history, recording details such as service dates, repairs, and maintenance costs.
- Airplane managers update airplane information, including capacity or type, when necessary.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airplane Administrators

Activity:
- Airplane administrators register new airplane types by entering type ID, identifier, and description.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Managers, Ground Staff

Activity:
- Airport managers register new airports by entering airport ID, IATA code, ICAO code, and name.
- Ground staff update airport information, including city, country, or geolocation details.

## 5. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Flight Planners, Airline Managers

Activity:
- Flight planners create flight schedules by entering flight number, departure and arrival airports, departure and arrival times, and days of operation for each flight.
- Airline managers modify flight schedules as needed, adjusting departure or arrival times, or changing the days of operation.

## 6. Flight Registration

Performed By: Flight Operations Staff

Activity:
- Flight operations staff register new flights by linking them to their corresponding airplanes and flight schedules.

## 7. Booking Management

Performed By: Reservation Agents, Passengers

Activity:
- Reservation agents create bookings for passengers by entering booking ID, flight ID, seat number, passenger ID, and price.
- Passengers confirm or modify their bookings as needed, updating personal details or selecting different seats.

## 8. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Meteorologists, Ground Staff

Activity:
- Meteorologists collect weather data by entering log date, time, station, temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, wind direction, and weather conditions.
