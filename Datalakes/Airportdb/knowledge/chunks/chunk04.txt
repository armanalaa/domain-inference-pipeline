 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Airline Management

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airlines by providing the IATA code, airline name, and base airport.
- Airline management assigns airline_id to each registered airline.
- Airline management updates airline details as needed, modifying airlinename or base_airport.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Maintenance Team, Airline Operations Team

Activity:
- Maintenance team records the capacity and type of each airplane.
- Airline operations team assigns airplane_id to each registered airplane.
- Airline operations team updates airplane details as needed, modifying capacity or type.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team

Activity:
- Airline operations team adds new airplane types by providing the identifier and description.
- Airline operations team assigns type_id to each registered airplane type.
- Airline operations team updates airplane type details as needed, modifying the description.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airport operations team registers new airports by providing the IATA and ICAO codes, name, city, country, latitude, longitude, and geolocation.
- Airport operations team assigns airport_id to each registered airport.
- Airport operations team updates airport details as needed, modifying any attribute.

## 5. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airline operations team creates new flights by specifying flightno, from, to, departure, arrival, airline_id, and airplane_id.
- Airport operations team schedules flights for each day of the week (monday through sunday) using the provided flight information.

## 6. Passenger Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new passengers by recording passportno, firstname, lastname, and other personal details.

## 7. Booking Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Online Booking System

Activity:
- Customer service representatives or the online booking system assigns booking_id to each booking.
- Customer service representatives or the online booking system records flight_id, seat, passenger_id, and price for each booking.

## 8. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Weather Stations

Activity:
- Weather stations record temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, direction, and weather conditions at specific times and locations.
- Weather data is sent to the database for storage and analysis.
