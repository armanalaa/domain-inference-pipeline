 # Airportdb — Business Workflows

## 1. Airline Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Airline Management

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airlines by recording the airline code (iata), name, and base airport.
- Airline management assigns airplanes to registered airlines based on capacity and type.
- Airline management updates airline details when changes occur, such as modifying the airline name or base airport.

## 2. Airplane Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Maintenance Staff

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airplanes by recording the airplane code (airplane_id), capacity, type, and assigned airline.
- Maintenance staff performs routine checks and maintenance on airplanes to ensure they are safe for operation.
- Maintenance staff records any necessary repairs or updates in the database.

## 3. Airplane Type Management

Performed By: Airline Operations Team

Activity:
- Airline operations team registers new airplane types by recording the type code (type_id), identifier, and description.

## 4. Airport Management

Performed By: Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airport operations team registers new airports by recording the airport code (airport_id), IATA, ICAO, name, city, country, latitude, longitude, and geolocation.

## 5. Flight Scheduling

Performed By: Airline Operations Team, Airport Operations Team

Activity:
- Airline operations team creates flight schedules by specifying the flight number, from airport, to airport, departure time, arrival time, airline, and assigned airplane.
- Airport operations team updates flight schedules when changes occur, such as modifying departure or arrival times.

## 6. Flight Booking

Performed By: Passengers, Travel Agents, Airline Staff

Activity:
- Passengers or travel agents book flights by selecting a flight, choosing seats, and providing passenger information (passport number, first name, last name).
- Airline staff records the booking details, including the booking ID, flight ID, seat, passenger ID, and price.

## 7. Weather Data Collection

Performed By: Meteorological Staff

Activity:
- Meteorological staff collects weather data by recording the log date, time, station, temperature, humidity, air pressure, wind speed, wind direction, and weather conditions.
