 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock when products are received from suppliers.
- Product managers set the ReorderLevel for each product to ensure timely restocking.
- Warehouse staff mark a product as Discontinued when it is no longer available.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives add new customers to the database with their contact and address details.
- Customer service agents update customer information as needed.
- Sales representatives assign territories to employees for sales coverage.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new purchase orders for customers based on their needs and product availability.
- Customers place orders by selecting products, quantities, and shipping details in the Purchase Order List or through the Reorder Product button on the Products form.

## 4. Order Approval

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Management (with Purchase Approval privilege)

Activity:
- Purchasing teams review and approve purchase orders based on budget constraints and product availability.
- Management with Purchase Approval privilege can also approve orders as needed.

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff locate and pick the ordered products from inventory.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsOnOrder when products are allocated for an order.
- Shipping department packages and labels the orders for shipment.

## 6. Order Tracking

Performed By: Shipping Department, Customers

Activity:
- Shipping department records the ShipVia, ShipName, ShipAddress, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, ShipCountry, and ShippedDate when an order is shipped.
- Customers can track their orders using the hyperlinks in the Purchase Order List.

## 7. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounting Department

Activity:
- Accounting department generates invoices based on approved purchase orders and sends them to suppliers.

## 8. Supplier Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, HR Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams add new suppliers to the database with their contact and address details.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories for supplier relationships management.
