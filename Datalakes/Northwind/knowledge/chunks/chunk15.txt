 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for restocking discontinued products.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives maintain customer relationships by updating contact information and order history.
- Customer service agents assist customers with inquiries about orders, shipments, or returns.

## 3. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by filling out employment forms and assigning territories.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the database when stock is depleted or restocked.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders for customers by selecting products, quantities, and shipping details.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items before they are shipped to the customer.
- Shipping clerks update order statuses when shipments are sent out.

## 5. Shipment Tracking

Performed By: Shipping Clerks, Customers

Activity:
- Shipping clerks record shipment details such as carrier, tracking number, and delivery date.
- Customers track their orders using the provided tracking numbers.

## 6. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff generate invoices based on order details and send them to customers.

## 7. Payment Processing

Performed By: Accounting Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Accounting staff receive payments from customers via various methods (e.g., credit card, check) and update the database accordingly.
- Customers make payments on their outstanding invoices through the provided payment options.
