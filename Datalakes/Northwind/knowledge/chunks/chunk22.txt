 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock when products are received or shipped.
- Product managers set the ReorderLevel to trigger restocking when inventory falls below a certain threshold.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives add new customers by entering their details such as CompanyName, ContactName, and Address.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any changes to contact information or account status.
- Sales representatives assign territories to employees for sales coverage.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by specifying the CustomerID, required delivery date, and shipping details.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack order items based on the OrderID and ProductID.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for products that are out of stock or have low inventory levels.

## 4. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff generate invoices by calculating the total order value, applying any discounts, and adding freight charges.
- Invoices are sent to customers with their OrderID as reference.

## 5. Shipping and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shippers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by printing shipping labels with the ShipName, ShipAddress, and ShipCity.
- Shippers pick up or deliver orders based on the OrderID and ShipVia.

## 6. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their personal details such as LastName, FirstName, Title, and HireDate.
- Finance department records the HomePhone and Extension for payroll purposes.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories based on their skills and expertise.

## 7. Performance Evaluation

Performed By: Managers, Employees

Activity:
- Managers evaluate employee performance by reviewing Notes and ReportsTo relationships.
- Employees update their Notes to reflect their achievements and areas for improvement.
