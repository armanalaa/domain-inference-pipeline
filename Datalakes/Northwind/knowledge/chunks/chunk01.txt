 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Company Management

Performed By: Administrator, Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Administrators manage company types by updating the type of a company in the Company table.
- Data Entry Clers register new companies by recording the company name, contact information, and type in the Company table.
- Administrators add multiple contacts for a company by entering their details in the Contact table associated with the corresponding company ID.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales Representatives register new customers by recording customer information and contact details in the Customers table.
- Customer Service Agents update customer information as needed in the Customers table.

## 3. Shipper Management

Performed By: Logistics Coordinators, Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Logistics Coordinators register new shippers by recording shipper details in the Shippers table.
- Data Entry Clerks update shipper information as needed in the Shippers table.

## 4. Vendor Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Data Entry Clerk

Activity:
- Purchasing teams register new vendors by recording vendor details in the Suppliers table.
- Data Entry Clerks update vendor information as needed in the Suppliers table.

## 5. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Product Managers register new products by recording product details and assigning them to categories in the Products table.
- Inventory Specialists update product information as needed in the Products table, including stock levels and reorder quantities.

## 6. Order Placement

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales Representatives place orders on behalf of customers by recording order details, customer ID, and employee ID in the Orders table.
- Customers place orders directly through the system by entering their information and order details in the Orders table.

## 7. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and pick items for orders from the warehouse based on order details in the Order_Details table.
- Shipping clerks prepare shipments by recording shipping information, including shipper ID, in the Orders table.

## 8. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff generate invoices based on order details and customer information from the Orders and Customers tables.

## 9. Payment Processing

Performed By: Accounting Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Accounting staff record payments received from customers in the Payments table.
- Customer Service Agents assist customers with payment issues and update customer account information accordingly.

## 10. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Management, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Management reviews sales reports generated from the Orders and Customers tables to monitor business performance.
- Data Analysts perform detailed analysis of sales trends, inventory levels, and other key performance indicators using data from various tables in the Northwind database.
