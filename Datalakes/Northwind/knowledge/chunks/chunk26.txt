 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the units in stock when products are received or shipped.
- Product managers set the reorder level to trigger automatic replenishment orders.
- Warehouse staff mark a product as discontinued when it is no longer available.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as company name, contact information, and shipping address.
- Customer service agents update customer records with new contact information or address changes.
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by selecting products, setting quantities, and specifying shipping details.
- Customer service agents process returns and issue refunds or credits to customers.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm order details with customers and mark orders as shipped when they are ready for dispatch.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items based on the order details.
- Shipping clerks label packages with the correct shipping information and send them to the designated shipper.
- Warehouse staff update the units in stock when products are shipped out.

## 4. Shipment Tracking

Performed By: Shipping Clerks, Customers

Activity:
- Shipping clerks assign a shipper to each order based on the chosen shipping method and location.
- Shippers update the shipped date and tracking number for each order once it has been dispatched.
- Customers track their orders using the tracking number provided by the shipper.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff generate invoices based on the order details and shipment information.
- Accounting staff send invoices to customers via email or mail.
- Customers review their invoices for accuracy and make payments as required.

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Accounting Staff, Banking Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff record customer payments and update the order status accordingly.
- Banking staff process incoming payments and credit the appropriate accounts.
- Accounting staff reconcile bank statements to ensure accurate records.

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Management, Sales Representatives, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Management reviews sales reports to identify trends and opportunities for growth.
- Sales representatives use customer reports to target marketing efforts and build relationships with key accounts.
- Accounting staff generate financial reports for internal analysis and external reporting purposes.
- Management uses analytical reports to make informed decisions about pricing, inventory levels, and business strategy.
