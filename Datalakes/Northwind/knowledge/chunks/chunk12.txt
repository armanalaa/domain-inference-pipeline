 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse product categories and select desired items.
- Sales representatives assist customers with their orders and provide recommendations.
- Sales representatives enter customer details, selected products, quantities, and shipping information.

## 2. Order Confirmation

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm the order details and check product availability.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels to reflect ordered items.

## 3. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive and store incoming shipments of products.
- Warehouse staff update the database with new product quantities on hand.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipping.
- Shipping department employees prepare shipments for delivery by selecting appropriate carriers.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Department

Activity:
- Accounting department generates invoices based on confirmed orders.
- Invoices are sent to customers along with shipping information.

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Accounting Department

Activity:
- Customers pay for their orders using various payment methods.
- Accounting department records payments and updates customer accounts accordingly.

## 7. Order Tracking

Performed By: Shipping Department, Customers

Activity:
- Shipping department tracks the status of shipments and updates the database with shipping information.
- Customers can track their orders using provided tracking numbers.

## 8. Customer Support

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries related to orders, returns, or other issues.
- Customer service representatives update the database with relevant information and assist customers in resolving their concerns.

## 9. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Management, Analytics Team

Activity:
- Management reviews sales reports and identifies trends or areas for improvement.
- Analytics team performs data analysis to optimize operations and make informed decisions.
