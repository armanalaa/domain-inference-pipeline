 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Company Management

Performed By: Administrators, HR Managers

Activity:
- Administrators register new companies by recording the company name, contact details, and region.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories based on company locations.

## 2. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers update employee information such as names, titles, addresses, and contact details.
- Employees modify their personal data like home phone numbers or notes.

## 3. Role Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers, Administrators

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to specific roles within the company hierarchy.
- Administrators update reporting relationships between employees.

## 4. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Warehouse staff update stock levels for individual products.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders on behalf of customers, specifying order details like quantities and discounts.
- Customer service agents process customer inquiries about existing orders.

## 6. Shipment Coordination

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare shipments by selecting products from inventory and calculating freight costs.
- Shipping teams arrange for the delivery of orders using designated carriers.

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department generates invoices based on order details, including product prices, quantities, and shipping costs.

## 8. Payment Processing

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff record customer payments and update order status accordingly.

## 9. Performance Tracking

Performed By: Management, HR Managers

Activity:
- Management reviews employee performance based on sales figures, customer feedback, and other key metrics.
- HR managers evaluate employee productivity and make recommendations for promotions or training needs.
