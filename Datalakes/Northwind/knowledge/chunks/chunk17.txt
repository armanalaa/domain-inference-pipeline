 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock when receiving shipments of products.
- Product managers modify product details as needed, including description, picture, QuantityPerUnit, and Discontinued status.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer information such as CompanyName, ContactName, Address, City, Region, PostalCode, Country, Phone, and Fax during account creation.
- Customer service agents update customer details when changes occur.
- Sales representatives assign territories to employees for sales coverage.

## 3. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their personal and professional information (LastName, FirstName, Title, BirthDate, HireDate, Address, City, Region, PostalCode, Country, HomePhone, Extension, Photo, Notes, ReportsTo).
- Employees update their own contact information as needed.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by specifying the CustomerID, EmployeeID, OrderDate, RequiredDate, and ShipVia.
- Warehouse staff update order details (OrderID, ProductID, UnitPrice, Quantity) as products are picked from inventory.
- Shipping teams record the ShippedDate and Freight cost for each order.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update UnitsOnOrder when new products are ordered from suppliers.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on ReorderLevel and track their progress.

## 6. Supplier Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Purchasing teams add new suppliers by recording the SupplierID, CompanyName, ContactName, ContactTitle, Address, City, Region, PostalCode, Country, Phone, and Fax.
- Finance department records payments to suppliers for invoices received.

## 7. Shipping Management

Performed By: Shipping Teams, Customers

Activity:
- Shipping teams record the ShipName, ShipAddress, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, and ShipCountry for each order.
- Customers confirm receipt of orders by updating the ShippedDate in their records.
