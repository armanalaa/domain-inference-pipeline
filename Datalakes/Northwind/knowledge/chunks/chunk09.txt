 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Registration

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Record product details such as name, category, and list price in the products table.
- Assign a unique product code to each item registered.
- Set initial quantities on hand and reorder level for the new product.

## 2. Order Placement

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Place orders by specifying customer details, selected products, and desired quantities.
- Record order date, required delivery date, and shipment details in the orders table.

## 3. Order Approval

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Review purchase orders for accuracy and approve them to initiate product procurement.
- Update the status of approved purchase orders in the order_details table.

## 4. Product Procurement

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Suppliers

Activity:
- Receive products from suppliers and update quantities on hand for each item in the products table.
- Record shipment details such as ship name, address, and country in the orders table.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Track quantities of products on hand and on order in the products and order_details tables.
- Update quantities when products are received or shipped out.

## 6. Reorder Process

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Monitor inventory levels and generate purchase orders for items with low stock.
- Set default quantity for each product in the purchase order.

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance

Activity:
- Generate invoices based on shipped orders and update their status accordingly in the orders table.

## 8. Return Management

Performed By: Customers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Process returns of products by updating quantities on hand and marking them as discontinued in the products table.

## 9. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Finance, Management

Activity:
- Generate reports on sales, inventory levels, and financial performance using data from the customers, orders, order_details, and products tables.
