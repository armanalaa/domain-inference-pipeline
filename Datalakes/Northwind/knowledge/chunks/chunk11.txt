 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product information when necessary, including category assignment, description, and discontinuation status.
- Monitor inventory levels and adjust reorder levels as needed to ensure timely restocking.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Add new customers by entering company name, contact details, address, and other relevant information.
- Update customer records with any changes in contact information or account status.
- Manage customer orders, including tracking order progress, addressing issues, and providing updates on order status.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Select a product category and product for an order, entering the required quantity.
- Verify that the selected products are in stock or on order before finalizing the order.
- Advance the order status from New to Invoiced once the order is ready for invoicing.

## 4. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Generate and send invoices based on approved orders.
- Update order status to Invoiced upon successful invoice generation.
- Monitor outstanding invoices and follow up with customers as needed.

## 5. Order Allocation

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Allocate products for orders when items are not in stock or on order.
- Create purchase orders to acquire necessary products and update order status accordingly.
- Ship allocated products to the warehouse once received.

## 6. Order Shipping

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Prepare orders for shipping by selecting a shipper and calculating shipping fees.
- Update order status to Shipped upon successful shipping.
- Ensure accurate shipping information is provided for each order.

## 7. Order Tracking and Closure

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Monitor the progress of shipped orders until they are delivered to customers.
- Update order status to Closed once an order has been delivered or cancelled.
- Address any issues that arise during order tracking and ensure timely resolution.

## 8. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Supervisors

Activity:
- Hire new employees by recording their personal and professional details, including address, contact information, and employment history.
- Assign territories to employees as needed for sales and distribution purposes.
- Update employee records with any changes in personal or employment information.

## 9. Supplier Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Add new suppliers by entering company name, contact details, and other relevant information.
- Monitor supplier performance and update supplier records accordingly.
- Negotiate pricing and terms with suppliers as needed to optimize inventory costs.

## 10. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Finance Staff, Management

Activity:
- Generate reports on sales, inventory, and financial performance using data from the Northwind database.
- Analyze reports to identify trends, opportunities, and areas for improvement in business operations.
- Use insights gained from analysis to inform strategic decision-making and drive business growth.
