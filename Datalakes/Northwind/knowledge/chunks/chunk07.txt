 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their first name, last name, job title, and other personal details.
- HR managers update employee records with changes in contact information or job titles.
- IT support ensures the system validates required fields for all employee records before saving.

## 2. Employee Territory Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories based on geographical coverage.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and recommend territory assignments for new hires.

## 3. Employee Performance Evaluation

Performed By: Supervisors, HR Managers

Activity:
- Supervisors evaluate employee performance and provide feedback on a regular basis.
- HR managers review evaluation results and make decisions about promotions or salary adjustments.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives place new orders for customers by specifying the required products, quantities, and shipping details.
- Customer service representatives update order statuses and handle customer inquiries related to existing orders.
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship ordered items according to the specified shipping details.

## 5. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, list price, and other relevant details.
- Purchasing teams manage supplier relationships and negotiate prices for products.

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update the system when stock changes occur.
- Purchasing teams order new products to replenish depleted inventory.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records financial transactions related to orders, payments, and expenses.
- Finance department generates reports on sales, profits, and other key performance indicators for analysis and decision making.
