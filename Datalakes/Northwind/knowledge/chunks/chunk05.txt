 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal details, titles, and contact information.
- HR managers update employee records with changes in personal or professional information.
- IT administrators manage employee access rights and permissions based on roles and responsibilities.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories for sales and support purposes.

## 2. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, New Hires

Activity:
- HR managers provide new hires with employment contracts, job descriptions, and company policies.
- New hires sign and return the necessary documents to confirm their acceptance of terms.
- HR managers enter the signed documents into the employee records for future reference.

## 3. Employee Performance Tracking

Performed By: Managers, Employees

Activity:
- Managers evaluate employees' performance based on set goals and objectives.
- Employees submit self-evaluations detailing their achievements and areas for improvement.
- Managers update employee records with the evaluation results and notes for future reference.

## 4. Employee Offboarding

Performed By: HR Managers, Exiting Employees

Activity:
- HR managers initiate offboarding procedures when an employee leaves the company.
- Exiting employees return company property such as laptops, ID cards, and uniforms.
- HR managers update employee records to reflect the termination date and exit status.

## 5. Employee Training and Development

Performed By: HR Managers, Trainers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers identify training needs for employees based on performance evaluations and department requirements.
- Trainers develop and deliver training programs to address identified needs.
- Employees participate in the training sessions and complete any required assessments or assignments.

## 6. Employee Benefits Administration

Performed By: HR Managers, Payroll Department

Activity:
- HR managers process employee benefit requests such as health insurance, retirement plans, and paid time off.
- Payroll department updates employee records with the approved benefits and corresponding deductions.

## 7. Employee Data Maintenance

Performed By: IT Administrators

Activity:
- IT administrators ensure accurate and up-to-date employee data by performing regular database backups and system maintenance.
