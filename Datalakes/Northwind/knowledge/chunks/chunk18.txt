 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal details and employment information. (employees table)
- IT administrators assign territories to employees for sales tracking purposes. (employee_territories table)
- HR managers update employee records with changes in contact information or job titles. (employees table)

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by entering their company details and contact information. (customers table)
- Customer service agents update customer records with changes in address or contact details. (customers table)
- Sales representatives track customer orders and shipments for follow-ups and sales analysis. (orders table)

## 3. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price. (products table)
- Warehouse staff update stock levels as products are received or sold. (products table)
- Product managers monitor discontinued products and adjust inventory accordingly. (products table)

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders on behalf of customers by selecting products, quantities, and shipping details. (orders table)
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from stock and prepare them for shipment. (order_details table)
- Shipping clerks package and label orders for delivery to the specified address. (orders table)

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff generate invoices based on order details, calculating total amounts due and applying any discounts. (order_details table)

## 6. Shipping and Delivery Tracking

Performed By: Shipping Clerks, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Shipping clerks update the shipped date and shipment details for each order. (orders table)
- Warehouse staff track the location of shipped orders until they are delivered to customers. (orders table)

## 7. Report Generation

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts create reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and customer behavior using data from various tables. (all tables)
- Management reviews reports for strategic decision making and business improvement initiatives. (reports not explicitly mentioned in source materials)
