 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their CompanyName, ContactName, and ContactTitle.
- Customer service representatives update customer details such as Address, City, Phone, and Email when needed.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific territories based on their location.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update product information like QuantityPerUnit, UnitsInStock, and UnitsOnOrder as needed.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by specifying the required products, quantities, and shipping details.
- Sales representatives confirm orders and update the OrderDate, RequiredDate, and ShippedDate.

## 4. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shippers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered products based on the OrderID and Quantity.
- Shippers prepare shipments by recording the ShipVia, Freight, ShipName, ShipAddress, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, and ShipCountry.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff generate invoices based on the orders and update the OrderID, CustomerID, and TotalAmountDue.

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records customer payments by updating the OrderID, CustomerID, and PaidStatus.

## 7. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Supervisors

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their EmployeeID, personal details, and employment history.
- Supervisors assign tasks to employees based on their roles and responsibilities.

## 8. Performance Evaluation

Performed By: Supervisors, HR Managers

Activity:
- Supervisors evaluate employee performance and update the EmployeeID, Title, and PerformanceRating.
- HR managers review employee evaluations and make decisions regarding promotions or terminations.

## 9. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Management, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Management reviews sales reports to track performance and identify trends.
- Data analysts generate custom reports based on specific business requirements.
