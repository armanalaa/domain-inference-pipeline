 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal details and employment information. (employees)
- IT administrators assign system access and set up employee profiles. (employees)
- HR managers define employee roles and reporting structure. (employees, employee_territories)
- IT administrators configure email accounts and set up network permissions for each employee. (employees)
- HR managers maintain employee records, including performance evaluations and training history. (employees, Notes)

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives identify potential customers and initiate contact. (customers)
- Customer service agents respond to customer inquiries and handle complaints. (customers)
- Sales representatives create new customer accounts, recording company details and contact information. (customers)
- Sales representatives update customer account information as needed. (customers)
- Customer service agents process returns and manage refunds or exchanges for existing customers. (customers)

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives receive orders from customers and confirm order details. (orders)
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered products from inventory. (products)
- Warehouse staff pack orders for shipping, ensuring accurate quantities and correct items. (order_details)
- Shipping clerks prepare shipments by labeling packages and arranging pickups with carriers. (orders, shippers)
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels to reflect shipped orders. (products, order_details)

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams negotiate with suppliers for product purchases and manage purchase orders. (orders, products, suppliers)
- Warehouse staff receive deliveries from suppliers and update inventory levels accordingly. (products)
- Warehouse staff perform regular stock checks to ensure accurate inventory counts. (products)
- Warehouse staff manage product storage and organization within the warehouse. (categories, products)
- Warehouse staff process returns and restock returned items when applicable. (orders, products)

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Accountants, Finance Managers

Activity:
- Accountants record financial transactions, including sales, purchases, and payments. (orders, order_details, suppliers)
- Finance managers generate invoices for customers based on completed orders. (orders, customers)
- Accountants process payments from customers and update account balances accordingly. (customers)
- Finance managers prepare financial reports for management review and analysis. (regions, employees, orders, order_details, suppliers)
- Accountants maintain accurate records of expenses and manage budgets for various departments. (employees, regions)
