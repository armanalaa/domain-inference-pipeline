 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their CompanyName, ContactName, and ContactTitle.
- Customer service representatives update customer details such as Address, City, Region, PostalCode, Country, Phone, and Fax when necessary.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific territories based on employee_territories.

## 2. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their LastName, FirstName, Title, BirthDate, HireDate, and Address details.
- Employees update their own contact information in the employees table.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories based on employee_territories.

## 3. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update product information such as QuantityPerUnit, UnitsInStock, and UnitsOnOrder.
- Product managers mark discontinued products by setting the Discontinued column to 'True'.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders for customers by recording CustomerID, EmployeeID, OrderDate, RequiredDate, and ShipVia.
- Warehouse staff update order details such as OrderID, ProductID, UnitPrice, Quantity, Discount, and Freight.
- Shipping clerks record the ShippedDate and ShipName, ShipAddress, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, and ShipCountry for each order.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by monitoring UnitsInStock and UnitsOnOrder for each product.
- Warehouse staff update the ReorderLevel when necessary to ensure timely restocking.

## 6. Shipment Tracking

Performed By: Shipping Clerks, Customers

Activity:
- Shipping clerks record shipment details such as ShipName, ShipAddress, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, and ShipCountry for each order.
- Customers track their orders using the OrderID and ShippedDate.

## 7. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record financial transactions such as payments and receipts related to orders.
