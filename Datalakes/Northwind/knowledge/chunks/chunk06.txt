 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Employee Management

Performed By: Human Resources (HR) Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- HR managers register new employees by recording their personal details, employment history, and qualifications.
- IT staff configure employee accounts with access permissions based on job roles and privileges.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories, ensuring no overlap or conflicts.

## 2. Employee Onboarding

Performed By: New Hires, Supervisors, HR Managers

Activity:
- New hires complete employment forms, including personal information and agreements.
- Supervisors review and approve new hire applications, setting up initial job assignments and training plans.
- HR managers process paperwork, ensuring compliance with legal requirements and company policies.

## 3. Employee Performance Tracking

Performed By: Supervisors, Employees, Finance Department

Activity:
- Supervisors monitor employee performance, providing feedback and setting goals for improvement.
- Employees update their timesheets with hours worked and tasks completed.
- Finance department calculates payroll based on hours worked, overtime, bonuses, and deductions.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives receive customer orders, confirm availability of products, and process payments.
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and prepare orders for shipment.
- Shipping department coordinates with carriers to schedule deliveries and updates order statuses.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Purchasing teams negotiate contracts with suppliers for product purchases.
- Warehouse staff receive shipments, update inventory levels, and manage storage.
- Finance department tracks costs associated with inventory, including purchasing and storage expenses.

## 6. Customer Service

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries, complaints, and returns.
- Sales representatives provide product information, answer questions, and assist with order placement.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department, Management

Activity:
- Finance department generates financial reports, including sales, expenses, and profitability analyses.
- Management reviews reports to make informed decisions about business strategy and resource allocation.
