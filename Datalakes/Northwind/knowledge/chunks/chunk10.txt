 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by providing company name, contact details, and address.
- Sales representatives update customer information as needed.
- Customer service agents handle customer inquiries and update contact details accordingly.

## 2. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products, specifying quantities, and providing shipping details.
- Sales representatives confirm order details and process the transaction.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory based on order ID.
- Shipping department packages orders for shipment and records shipping details.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse managers monitor stock levels and update the database when items are added or removed from inventory.
- Purchasing teams order new products when inventory reaches reorder level.

## 5. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording personal and professional details, assigning territories, and setting up employee profiles.
- Employees update their own contact information and report to their supervisors.

## 6. Supplier Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Purchasing teams maintain relationships with suppliers by recording supplier details and negotiating prices.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records financial transactions, including order totals, payments, and freight costs.
