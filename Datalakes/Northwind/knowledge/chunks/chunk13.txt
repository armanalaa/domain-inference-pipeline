 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update existing item details such as description, category, or price when necessary.
- IT administrators manage product images by uploading and updating them in the ProductCategories table.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives add new customers to the system with their contact information and address details.
- Customer service agents update customer records with any changes to contact or address information.

## 3. Employee Territory Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers, IT Administrators

Activity:
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories based on geographical coverage and business needs.
- IT administrators update the employee_territories table with the assigned territoryID for each employee.

## 4. Employee Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers enter employee details such as name, title, hire date, and contact information into the employees table.
- Employees update their personal information in the employees table as needed.

## 5. Order Placement

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders on behalf of customers by specifying the customerID, selected products, and quantities.
- Customers can also place orders directly through the system using their account information.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory based on order details in the order_details table.
- Shipping teams pack and prepare orders for shipment, updating the orders table with the ship date and shipping information.

## 7. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the unitsInStock column in the products table when items are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams order new stock based on the UnitsOnOrder and ReorderLevel columns in the products table.

## 8. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records transactions related to orders, such as freight costs and payments, in the orders table.
