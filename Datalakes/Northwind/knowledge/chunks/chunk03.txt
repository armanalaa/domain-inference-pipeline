 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Company Management

Performed By: Administrators, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Administrators register new companies by entering the company name, contact details, and address information into the customers table.
- Data Entry Clerks update existing company records with changes in contact information or addresses as needed.

## 2. Employee Assignment

Performed By: HR Managers

Activity:
- HR Managers assign employees to specific territories by matching employee IDs with corresponding territory IDs in the employee_territories table.

## 3. Employee Information Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- HR Managers input employee details such as name, title, contact information, and employment history into the employees table.
- Data Entry Clerks update employee records with changes in personal or professional data.

## 4. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product Managers add new items to the product catalog by recording the product code, name, category, description, and list price.

## 5. Category Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product Managers create and manage product categories by entering category names, descriptions, and pictures in the categories table.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales Representatives place orders for customers by specifying the required products, quantities, and shipping details in the orders table.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and fulfill orders by updating the units on order and units in stock columns in the products table as needed.

## 7. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting Staff generate invoices for customers based on their order history, calculating total amounts due and recording them in the orders table.

## 8. Shipping Coordination

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shippers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by selecting a shipper from the shippers table and updating the corresponding columns in the orders table.
- Shippers coordinate delivery of orders to customers based on the shipping details provided.
