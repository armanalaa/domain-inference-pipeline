 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock column when products are received or shipped.
- Warehouse staff adjust the ReorderLevel column to ensure timely restocking of popular items.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives enter customer details such as CompanyName, ContactName, and Address into the customers table during initial contact.
- Customer service agents update the Phone column when a customer contacts the company with changes to their contact information.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders by specifying the CustomerID, EmployeeID, and required delivery date.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsOnOrder column when an order is placed for a product.
- Shipping clerks record the ShipVia, ShipName, ShipAddress, ShipCity, ShipRegion, ShipPostalCode, and ShipCountry upon preparing an order for shipment.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock column when products are received or shipped.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on the UnitsOnOrder and ReorderLevel columns.

## 5. Supplier Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Purchasing teams add new suppliers by entering their CompanyName, ContactName, and other relevant details.
- Accounting staff record payments to suppliers in the Freight column when they receive invoices.

## 6. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Accounting staff record all financial transactions related to orders, such as payments to suppliers and sales revenue, in the appropriate columns.

## 7. Report Generation

Performed By: Management, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Management generates reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and other key metrics using data from various tables.
- Sales representatives use the data to identify trends, forecast future sales, and make informed decisions about pricing and promotions.
