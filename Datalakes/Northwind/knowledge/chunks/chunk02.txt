 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Company Management

Performed By: Administrators, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Administrators manage company data by adding new companies (CompanyName, ContactName, etc.) using the Add Company command button.
- Data Entry Clerks update existing company information as needed.

## 2. Company Filtering

Performed By: Users

Activity:
- Users filter the list of companies based on their requirements (Customers Only, Shippers Only, Vendors Only) using radio buttons or built-in, on-demand filtering options.

## 3. Company Detail View

Performed By: Users

Activity:
- Users view detailed information about a specific company by double-clicking anywhere in a detail row or clicking on the ID field (set up as a hyperlink).

## 4. Company Label Creation

Performed By: Users

Activity:
- Users create labels for companies using the Create Labels button and Access Label Wizard.

## 5. Company Deletion

Performed By: Administrators

Activity:
- Administrators delete a company after confirming referential integrity and business rules.

## 6. Company Type Change Control

Performed By: Administrators

Activity:
- Administrators control when a company type can be changed based on business rules.

## 7. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting products (ProductName, Quantity), providing shipping details, and clicking the Add Order button.
- Sales representatives review and confirm customer orders before they are processed.

## 8. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels (UnitsInStock) and update them as products are shipped or received.
- Purchasing teams order new products when the quantity falls below the ReorderLevel.

## 9. Invoice Processing

Performed By: Finance, Customers

Activity:
- Finance generates invoices based on orders (OrderID, Total Amount) and sends them to customers.
- Customers review and pay their invoices as needed.
