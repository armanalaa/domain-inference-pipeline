 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording the company name, contact name, and address details.
- Sales representatives update customer information when changes occur.
- Customer service representatives retrieve customer details for inquiries.
- Customer service representatives update customer contact information as provided by customers.

## 2. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update product details such as quantity in stock and units on order.
- Warehouse staff mark discontinued products when necessary.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by specifying the required items and shipping details.
- Warehouse staff confirm product availability before fulfilling orders.
- Warehouse staff update order status when products are shipped.
- Shipping clerks record shipment details including ship name, address, city, region, postal code, and country.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each product.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders when stock levels reach reorder level.

## 5. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their personal and professional details.
- Employees update their own contact information as needed.
- HR managers assign territories to employees for sales and support purposes.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Staff, Accounting Clerks

Activity:
- Finance staff record financial transactions such as orders, payments, and expenses.
- Accounting clerks generate invoices based on order details and customer information.

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Management, Analysts

Activity:
- Management reviews sales reports to assess business performance.
- Analysts generate custom reports for specific purposes such as inventory analysis or financial forecasting.
