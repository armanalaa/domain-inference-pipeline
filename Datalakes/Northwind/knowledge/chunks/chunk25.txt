 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new customers by recording their company name, contact information, and address details.
- Customer service representatives update customer records with changes in contact information or address.
- Sales representatives assign customers to specific territories based on geographical location.

## 2. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Employees

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by recording their personal and professional details, including birthdate, job title, and reporting structure.
- Employees update their own contact information and address within the employee database.
- HR managers assign employees to specific territories based on geographical location.

## 3. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Product managers update product details such as quantity per unit, units in stock, and reorder level when necessary.
- Product managers mark discontinued products as such.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives place orders for customers by selecting products, quantities, and shipping details.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure availability of ordered items.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for out-of-stock items.

## 5. Shipping and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shippers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipping by packing products and printing shipping labels.
- Shippers track shipments and update order status with the shipped date and shipping carrier.

## 6. Invoice Generation and Payment

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Finance Department

Activity:
- Sales representatives generate invoices based on orders and send them to customers.
- Customers pay their invoices by providing payment details such as credit card information or bank transfer details.
- Finance department records payments and updates customer accounts accordingly.

## 7. Supplier Management

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, HR Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams register new suppliers by recording their company name, contact information, and address details.
- HR managers update supplier records with changes in contact information or address.
