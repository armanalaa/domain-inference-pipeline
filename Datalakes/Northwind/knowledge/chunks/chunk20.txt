 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock column when products are received or shipped.
- Product managers assign ReorderLevel to ensure timely restocking of popular items.

## 2. Customer Relationship Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives add new customers by entering their details and assigning them a territory.
- Customer service agents update customer information such as address or contact details when changes occur.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers by selecting products, quantities, and shipping details.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsOnOrder column when orders are processed.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on order requirements.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the Discontinued column for products that are no longer available.
- Purchasing teams monitor the SupplierID to maintain relationships with reliable suppliers.

## 5. Shipping and Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shippers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the ShippedDate and ShipVia columns when orders are ready for shipment.
- Shippers provide shipping rates based on the Freight column and selected ShipperID.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records transactions related to orders, such as payments received or made, in appropriate columns.

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales performance, inventory levels, and customer behavior using data from the database.
- Management reviews these reports to make informed decisions about business strategy.
