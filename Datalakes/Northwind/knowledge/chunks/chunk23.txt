 # Northwind — Business Workflows

## 1. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update the UnitsInStock when receiving shipments of products.
- Product managers set the ReorderLevel to trigger restocks before inventory depletion.

## 2. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives add new customers by entering their details such as CompanyName, ContactName, and Address.
- Customer service agents update customer information like Phone or PostalCode when changes occur.
- Sales representatives assign territories to employees using the employee_territories table.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Sales representatives create orders for customers by specifying the CustomerID, OrderDate, and required delivery date.
- Warehouse staff retrieve products from inventory based on order details (OrderID, ProductID).
- Shipping clerks record the ShipVia, ShipName, ShipAddress, and other shipping details before dispatching orders.

## 4. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update UnitsOnOrder when placing orders with suppliers.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for products that are low on stock (UnitsInStock).

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance records transactions like sales and purchases in the orders and order_details tables.
- Accounting staff calculates and updates the total Freight for each order based on ShipVia.

## 6. Employee Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Supervisors

Activity:
- HR managers hire new employees by entering their details like FirstName, LastName, Title, and HireDate.
- Supervisors assign employees to report to a specific manager (ReportsTo).

## 7. Shipper Management

Performed By: Shipping Clerks, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Shipping clerks select the most appropriate shipper for an order based on ShipVia.
- Purchasing teams negotiate shipping rates with suppliers (Shippers).
