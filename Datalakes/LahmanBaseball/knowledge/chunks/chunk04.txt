 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Management

Performed By: Database Administrators, Baseball Statisticians

Activity:
- Database administrators maintain the integrity of the database by regularly updating it with new seasons' data.
- Baseball statisticians input historical player statistics into the Batting, Fielding, and Appearances tables.

## 2. Team Management

Performed By: Team Owners, General Managers

Activity:
- Team owners hire or fire managers based on team performance.
- General managers make strategic decisions about player acquisitions and trades.

## 3. Game Tracking

Performed By: Game Scorers, Statisticians

Activity:
- Game scorers record game events such as hits, runs, errors, and outs during live games.
- Statisticians verify and update the data in the AllstarFull, Batting, Fielding, and Appearances tables.

## 4. Award Determination

Performed By: Awards Committee, Voters

Activity:
- The awards committee determines the criteria for various baseball awards.
- Voters cast their votes for the most deserving players in each award category.

## 5. Award Allocation

Performed By: Awards Administrators

Activity:
- Awards administrators calculate and allocate points based on voters' choices.
- They update the AwardsShareManagers and AwardsSharePlayers tables with the results.

## 6. Hall of Fame Induction

Performed By: Hall of Fame Voters, Historians

Activity:
- Hall of Fame voters consider candidates based on their career statistics and contributions to baseball.
- Historians research and document the careers of players inducted into the Hall of Fame.

## 7. College Player Data Collection

Performed By: Researchers, College Baseball Committee Members

Activity:
- Researchers gather data about college baseball players' performances and careers.
- College Baseball committee members input this data into the CollegePlaying table.

## 8. Salary Data Management

Performed By: SABR Business of Baseball Committee, Database Administrators

Activity:
- The SABR Business of Baseball committee gathers salary data for baseball players.
- Database administrators input this data into the appropriate tables in the database.
