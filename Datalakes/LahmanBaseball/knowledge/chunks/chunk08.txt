 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Management

Performed By: Baseball League Administrators

Activity:
- Register new players by recording player ID, year of registration, and team assignment.
- Assign players to specific positions (e.g., pitcher, catcher, first baseman) based on their skills and team needs.
- Update player records with game statistics such as at bats, hits, runs, and errors.

## 2. Game Management

Performed By: Umpires, Scorers, Team Managers

Activity:
- Record the outcome of each game, including game number, team ID, league ID, and game result (win or loss).
- Designate players as game starters and record their positions for each game.
- Track player appearances in games, whether they are batting, playing defense, or pitching.

## 3. Field Management

Performed By: Fielders (e.g., outfielders, catchers)

Activity:
- Play defense during games by making putouts, assists, and errors.
- Record double plays, passed balls, wild pitches, stolen bases, and caught stealing.
- Track time played in the field expressed as outs.

## 4. Position-Specific Field Management (Outfielders)

Performed By: Left Fielders, Center Fielders, Right Fielders

Activity:
- Play games at specific outfield positions (left field, center field, right field).
- Record the number of games played at each position.

## 5. Position-Specific Field Management (Split)

Performed By: Outfielders

Activity:
- Track games played in split positions (e.g., left and center fields, center and right fields).

## 6. Appearances Tracking

Performed By: League Statisticians

Activity:
- Record total games played by each player, games started, and games played in various roles (batting, defense, pitching).

## 7. Awards and Recognition

Performed By: Award Committees

Activity:
- Evaluate players based on their performance and award them for outstanding achievements.
- Record the player ID, award ID, year of award, league ID, and any additional notes related to the award.

## 8. Managerial Performance Tracking

Performed By: League Statisticians

Activity:
- Track managerial performance by recording games managed, wins, losses, and final team position in standings.
