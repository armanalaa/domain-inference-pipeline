 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Data Management

Performed By: Baseball Executives, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Baseball executives assign unique playerIDs to each athlete in the People table.
- Data analysts record players' birthYear, birthMonth, birthDay, birthCity, birthCountry, birthState in the People table.
- Data analysts document players' deathYear, deathMonth, deathDay, deathCountry in the People table.

## 2. Player Registration

Performed By: Baseball Executives, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Baseball executives register new players by entering their playerID, birthYear, birthMonth, birthDay, birthCity, birthCountry, birthState, and deathYear, deathMonth, deathDay, deathCountry into the People table.

## 3. Player Information Updates

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Data analysts update player information in the People table as needed.

## 4. Team and Franchise Data Management

Performed By: Baseball Executives, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Baseball executives assign unique teamIDs to each team in the Teams table.
- Data analysts record yearly stats and standings for each team in the Teams table.

## 5. Ballpark Management

Performed By: Baseball Executives, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Baseball executives assign unique parkIDs to each ballpark in the Parks table.

## 6. Player Statistics Recording

Performed By: Statisticians, Umpires

Activity:
- Statisticians record batting statistics for each player in the Batting table.
- Umpires record pitching statistics for each player in the Pitching table.
- Fielding statistics are recorded for each player in the Fielding table.

## 7. Position Data Recording (Outfield)

Performed By: Statisticians, Umpires

Activity:
- Statisticians record outfield position data for players in the FieldingOF table.

## 8. Position Data Recording (Split)

Performed By: Statisticians, Umpires

Activity:
- Statisticians record LF/CF/RF game played splits for all years in the FieldingOFsplit table.

## 9. Appearance Tracking

Performed By: Statisticians, Umpires

Activity:
- Statisticians track positions a player appeared during a season in the Appearances table.

## 10. All-Star Game Recording

Performed By: Baseball Executives, Statisticians

Activity:
- Baseball executives assign unique allstarID to each All-Star game.
- Statisticians record player participation and team representation in the AllStarFull table.
