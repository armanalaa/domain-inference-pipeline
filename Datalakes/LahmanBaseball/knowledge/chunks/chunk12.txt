 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Team Management

Performed By: League Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Maintain league structure and team affiliations for each season.
- Assign teams to appropriate leagues based on their primary games played.
- Record wins, losses, and ties for each team within their respective leagues.

## 2. Player Registration

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Identify players who participated in the season's games, both major and independent.
- Register player details, including game numbers and primary teams, in the AllstarFull table.

## 3. Game Participation Tracking

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Record each player's appearances across all games within a season.
- Populate the Appearances table with relevant details such as games played and positions.

## 4. Batting Performance Recording

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Track batting statistics for each player in their respective games.
- Update the Batting table with game, at-bat, run, hit, and other relevant data.

## 5. Fielding Performance Recording

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Record fielding statistics for each player in their defensive games.
- Populate the Fielding table with position, games played, outs, assists, errors, and other relevant data.

## 6. All-Star Game Selection

Performed By: League Managers, Coaches

Activity:
- Select players to participate in North-South and East-West All-Star Games.
- Record player details and award information in the AwardsPlayers table.

## 7. Manager Assignment

Performed By: League Administrators

Activity:
- Assign managers to each team for the season, if available.
- Populate the Managers table with manager details.

## 8. Series and Championship Tracking

Performed By: League Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Record series postings, championships, and playoff results in the appropriate tables (SeriesPost, TeamsHalf).
