 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Information Management

Performed By: Data Administrators, Baseball Analysts

Activity:
- Data administrators update player information in the People table based on biographical data sources.
- Baseball analysts record new players' details such as name, birthdate, position, and college in the CollegePlaying table.
- Data administrators assign unique playerIDs to each registered player.
- Analysts input player statistics for each season into the Batting, Fielding, and Appearances tables.
- If a player receives an award, data administrators record the awardID, yearID, lgID, and notes in the AwardsPlayers or AwardsManagers table.

## 2. Award Allocation and Tracking

Performed By: Award Committees, Data Administrators

Activity:
- Award committees determine winners for various awards based on player performance.
- Data administrators assign points to managers and players in the AwardsShareManagers and AwardsSharePlayers tables.
- If a tie occurs, data administrators update the tie column in the AwardsManagers or AwardsPlayers table.
- Award committees document notes about the award process in the notes column of relevant award tables.

## 3. Game Tracking and Recording

Performed By: Game Statisticians, Data Administrators

Activity:
- Game statisticians record game details such as teamID, lgID, gameNum, gameID, and startingPos for each player in the AllstarFull table.
- Data administrators update the BattingPost table with post-season statistics for each round.

## 4. Team Management and Tracking

Performed By: Team Managers, Data Administrators

Activity:
- Team managers assign teamID to their respective teams in the AllstarFull and Appearances tables.
- Data administrators track team statistics such as innings pitched, outs made, and errors committed by fielders in the Fielding table.

## 5. Stadium and Park Information Management

Performed By: Stadium Administrators, Data Administrators

Activity:
- Stadium administrators provide accurate stadium information for each team in the appropriate tables.
- Data administrators update park data to reflect any changes in stadium names or locations.

## 6. Place of Birth and Death Recording

Performed By: Data Administrators, Researchers

Activity:
- Data administrators input players' places of birth and death into the People table.
- Researchers fill in missing state/province data for all places of birth and death.

## 7. Award Standardization and Maintenance

Performed By: Database Administrators, Researchers

Activity:
- Database administrators standardize award names across award tables.
- Researchers add historical data for new awards to the relevant tables.
