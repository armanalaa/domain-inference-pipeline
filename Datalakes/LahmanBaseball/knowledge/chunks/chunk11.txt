 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Management

Performed By: Baseball Operations Team, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Baseball operations team assigns a unique playerID to each athlete joining the league.
- Data entry clerks record player details such as name, schoolID, and yearID in the CollegePlaying table for players with college backgrounds.
- Data entry clerks input player statistics like G, AB, R, H, 2B, 3B, HR, RBI, SB, CS, BB, SO, IBB, HBP, SH, SF, and GIDP in the Batting table.
- For fielding statistics, data entry clerks record POS, G, GS, InnOuts, PO, A, E, DP, PB, WP, SB, CS, ZR in the Fielding table.

## 2. Team Management and Scheduling

Performed By: League Administrators, Schedulers

Activity:
- League administrators assign teamID and lgID to each baseball team participating in the league.
- Schedulers create game schedules for teams within a league (lgID) and record gameNum, gameID, teamID, and yearID in the AllstarFull table.

## 3. Game Tracking and Reporting

Performed By: Game Scorers, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Game scorers track game events like runs scored (R), hits (H), errors (E), etc., during live games.
- Data entry clerks record game statistics from game scores in the AllstarFull and Batting tables.

## 4. Award Nomination and Recognition

Performed By: Awards Committee, League Administrators

Activity:
- The awards committee nominates players for various awards based on their performance.
- League administrators record awardID, playerID, yearID, lgID, tie, and notes in the AwardsPlayers and AwardsManagers tables.

## 5. Award Point Allocation and Distribution

Performed By: Awards Committee, League Administrators

Activity:
- The awards committee allocates points to players based on voting results for each award.
- League administrators record pointsWon, pointsMax, and votesFirst in the AwardsSharePlayers and AwardsShareManagers tables.

## 6. College Data Management

Performed By: Data Entry Clerks, Database Administrators

Activity:
- Data entry clerks input college names for players without existing schoolIDs in the CollegePlaying table.
- Database administrators create new school records with year suffixes to represent old college names.

## 7. Team and League Analysis

Performed By: Statisticians, Analysts

Activity:
- Statisticians calculate team and player statistics like batting average (AVG), on-base percentage (OBP), slugging percentage (SLG), etc., using data from the Batting table.
- Analysts compare team records against all teams versus within league to identify strengths and weaknesses.
