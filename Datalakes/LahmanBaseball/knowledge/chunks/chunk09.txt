 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Management

Performed By: Data Administrators, League Officials

Activity:
- Data administrators assign unique playerIDs to new players joining the league.
- League officials update playerIDs for players who change teams or leagues.

## 2. Season Scheduling

Performed By: League Schedulers

Activity:
- League schedulers determine game schedules for each team in a season.
- They assign specific dates, times, and locations for each game.

## 3. Game Management

Performed By: Umpires, Players, Coaches

Activity:
- Umpires officiate games, calling balls, strikes, outs, and errors.
- Players perform on the field, batting, fielding, pitching, and running bases.
- Coaches strategize and make decisions during the game.

## 4. Game Results Recording

Performed By: Umpires, Scorers

Activity:
- Umpires record game results, including scores, hits, errors, and outs.
- Scorers verify and update game statistics in the Batting and Fielding tables.

## 5. Player Performance Tracking

Performed By: Statisticians, Coaches

Activity:
- Statisticians calculate and record player performance metrics such as batting averages, earned run averages, and fielding percentages.
- Coaches review player statistics to evaluate their team's performance and make strategic decisions.

## 6. Award Nomination and Voting

Performed By: League Committees, Managers, Players, Fans

Activity:
- League committees nominate players for various awards based on their performance.
- Managers, players, and fans cast votes to determine the winners of each award.

## 7. Award Allocation

Performed By: League Officials

Activity:
- League officials tally the votes and announce the winners of each award.
- They update the Awards Players and Awards Managers tables with the winning playerIDs, awardIDs, yearIDs, and leagueIDs.

## 8. Season Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Analysts, Journalists

Activity:
- Analysts and journalists analyze season statistics to identify trends, patterns, and key performances.
- They create reports and articles highlighting the most significant findings.
