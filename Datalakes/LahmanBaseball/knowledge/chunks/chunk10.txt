 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Management

Performed By: Baseball Operations Team, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Baseball operations team assigns player ID codes to new players joining the league.
- Data entry clerks input player details such as name, school, and salary into the Players table.
- Data entry clerks record player statistics from games in the Batting, Fielding, and Appearances tables.
- Baseball operations team updates player records with awards information from the AwardsPlayers and AwardsManagers tables.
- Data entry clerks mark players who have been inducted into the Hall of Fame in the HallOfFame table.

## 2. League Management

Performed By: League Administrators, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- League administrators assign league ID codes to each baseball team.
- Data entry clerks input league details such as name and location into the Schools table.
- Data entry clerks record college playing history for players in the CollegePlaying table.

## 3. Negro League Data Management

Performed By: Negro League Data Specialists, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Negro League data specialists verify and assign major league status to Negro League games and teams.
- Data entry clerks input Negro League game and team details into the AllstarFull, Batting, Fielding, and Appearances tables.

## 4. Award Voting and Tabulation

Performed By: Award Voters, Award Committee Members

Activity:
- Award voters cast their votes for players in each award category.
- Award committee members tabulate the votes to determine the winners of each award.
- Award committee members update the AwardsPlayers and AwardsManagers tables with the voting results.

## 5. Salary Management

Performed By: Finance Department, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- The finance department determines player salaries for each season.
- Data entry clerks input player salaries into the Salaries table.

## 6. Database Maintenance and Updates

Performed By: Database Administrators, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Database administrators ensure the database is running smoothly and securely.
- Data entry clerks update player records with new statistics as they become available.
- Database administrators make necessary changes to the database schema when needed.
