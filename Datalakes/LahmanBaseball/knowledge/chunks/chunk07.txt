 # LahmanBaseball — Business Workflows

## 1. Player Registration and Information Management

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk, Baseball Analyst

Activity:
- Data entry clerks record player details such as nameFirst, nameLast, weight, height, bats, throws, debut, bbrefID, finalGame, retroID into the Players table.
- Baseball analysts update player information in the CollegePlaying and Batting tables based on available data.

## 2. Team Management

Performed By: Team Owners, League Administrators

Activity:
- Team owners register their teams with the necessary details like name, park, attendance into the Teams table.
- League administrators assign teamIDs, lgID, and divID to each team in the corresponding tables.

## 3. Franchise Management

Performed By: League Administrators

Activity:
- League administrators link teams to their respective franchises using the TeamsFranchise table.

## 4. Ballpark Information Management

Performed By: Data Entry Clerk, League Administrators

Activity:
- Data entry clerks record park details like city, state, country into the Parks table.
- League administrators assign parkkey to each ballpark.

## 5. Game and Season Tracking

Performed By: Scorekeepers, Statisticians

Activity:
- Scorekeepers and statisticians record game results, player statistics (e.g., G, AB, R, H, BB, SO), and team statistics (e.g., W, L, R, ER) into the AllstarFull, Appearances, Batting, Fielding, and Teams tables.

## 6. Award Allocation

Performed By: Awards Committee

Activity:
- The awards committee assigns awards to players based on their performance in a given year using the AwardsPlayers and AwardsManagers tables.

## 7. Statistical Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Baseball Analysts, Data Scientists

Activity:
- Baseball analysts and data scientists analyze player and team statistics to generate reports and insights using the Batting, Pitching, Fielding, Teams, AwardsPlayers, AwardsManagers tables.
