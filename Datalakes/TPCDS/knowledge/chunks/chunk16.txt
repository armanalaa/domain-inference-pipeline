 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by recording their details (cc_employees) and assigning roles (cc_class).
- HR managers update employee information (cc_name, cc_division, cc_company) and manage payroll records.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming calls (cc_rec_start_date) and assist customers with queries or issues.
- They record call details (cc_closed_date_sk, cc_open_date_sk) and categorize calls based on type (cc_class).

## 3. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers oversee multiple call centers within a division (cc_division), setting targets and strategies.
- They allocate resources (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours) and monitor performance metrics (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details (cp_catalog_number, cp_description) and assigning categories (cp_department).

## 5. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog online (cp_catalog_page_sk) and place orders through sales representatives or directly via the website.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff process returned items (cr_item_sk) by updating inventory levels and tracking returns (cr_returned_date_sk).
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries about returns (cr_refunded_customer_sk) and issue refunds.

## 7. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Finance, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Finance records financial transactions (cc_tax_percentage) and generates reports on call center performance.
- Data analysts analyze sales data (cp_catalog_page_sk) to identify trends and make recommendations for improvement.
