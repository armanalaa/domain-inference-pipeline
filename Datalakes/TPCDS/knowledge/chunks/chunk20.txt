 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Membership Management

Performed By: Council, Administrator

Activity:
- The Council establishes requirements for new member payment of dues.
- The Administrator sends annual dues notifications to Primary Representatives.
- The Administrator determines the amount and times of payment based on Council's decisions.
- Individual bills are mailed for each payment by the Administrator.
- The Administrator suspends membership rights if payment becomes more than 30 days past due.

## 2. Membership Rights Suspension

Performed By: Administrator, Legal Department

Activity:
- The Administrator suspends membership privileges when the validity of a Primary Representative is in question.
- The Administrator notifies the legal department of the Member in question.
- Membership privileges remain suspended until the Member designates the appointment of the Primary Representative in writing to the Administrator.

## 3. Dues Payment Collection

Performed By: Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Members pay their annual dues on January 1st or within 60 days after notification is sent.
- The Administrator collects past due charges when membership rights and privileges are terminated.

## 4. Pro-Rated Dues Assessment

Performed By: Administrator

Activity:
- The Administrator assesses a prorated percentage of the annual dues for companies joining in the second half of the year.

## 5. Membership Rights and Privileges Reinstatement

Performed By: Administrator

Activity:
- The Administrator reinstates membership rights and privileges when the TPC receives payment for all past due charges.

## 6. Primary Representative Appointment

Performed By: Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Members designate the appointment of the Primary Representative in writing to the Administrator.

## 7. Pro-Rated Dues Calculation

Performed By: Administrator

Activity:
- The Administrator calculates the prorated percentage of the annual dues based on how many months remain in the year for companies joining in the second half of the year.
