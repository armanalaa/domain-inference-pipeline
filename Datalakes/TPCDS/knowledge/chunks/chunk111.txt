 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific warehouses based on the call center's market class and division (call_center).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center within their assigned warehouse (call_center).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers and route them to appropriate sales representatives (not specified in the provided materials).
- Sales representatives assist customers with product inquiries, place orders, and provide order details (not specified in the provided materials).

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve items from inventory based on order details received from sales representatives (not specified in the provided materials).
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new items needed to replenish inventory levels (not specified in the provided materials).

## 4. Order Delivery

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Delivery Personnel

Activity:
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for delivery by packaging them and assigning a carrier (O_CARRIER_ID) to each order.
- Delivery personnel pick up the prepared orders from the warehouse and deliver them to customers according to the assigned delivery dates (OL_DELIVERY_D).

## 5. Customer Account Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- HR managers update customer records with employment details such as address and demographic information (not specified in the provided materials).
- Finance department updates customer balances after each order is delivered and paid for by the customer (C_BALANCE).

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns initiated by customers, record the reason for return (cr_reason_sk), and assign a return number (cr_returned_date_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels after receiving returned items from customers.

## 7. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on various aspects of the business such as sales performance, customer demographics, and delivery statistics using data from multiple tables (not specified in the provided materials).
- Management reviews the generated reports to make informed decisions about business operations and strategy.
