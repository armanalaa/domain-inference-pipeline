 TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific markets based on market class and description.
- Call center managers determine the number of employees required for each call center.
- Call center managers allocate square footage for each call center.
- Call center managers set operating hours for each call center.
- Warehouse staff update warehouse details for each call center, including warehouse ID and address.

## 2. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their personal information such as first name, last name, street address, city, state, zip code, phone number, credit status, and credit limit.

## 3. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog page and specifying quantity.
- Sales representatives process customer orders by entering order details into the orders table.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each item by updating the order_line table with new quantities as orders are processed.

## 5. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record customer returns by entering return details into the catalog_returns table.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels for returned items by adjusting quantities in the order_line table.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team records financial transactions related to orders, such as amounts and payment dates, in the history table.
