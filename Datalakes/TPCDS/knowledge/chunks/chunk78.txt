 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific markets (cc_mkt_id).
- Market managers define the market class and description for each call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse assigned to each call center (cc_warehouse_sk).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees handle customer calls during operational hours (cc_hours).
- Call center managers record the start and end dates of each call center operation (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date).
- Call center managers close a call center when it is no longer in operation (cc_closed_date_sk).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with their respective codes and details (cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for each item in the catalog (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record customer returns and their reasons (cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for returned items (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Address Management

Performed By: HR Managers, Customers

Activity:
- HR managers register employee addresses in the system (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Customers provide their shipping and billing addresses during transactions.
