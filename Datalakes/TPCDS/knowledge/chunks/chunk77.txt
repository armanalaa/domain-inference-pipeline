 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Benchmark Subcommittee Management

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee Members

Activity:
- Propose software updates based on feedback and analysis (call_center, policies_v6.19.pdf).
- Coordinate with Council for formal review and approval of proposed updates (call_center, policies_v6.19.pdf).
- Assign problem reporting, development, testing, and release activities to subcommittee members or third parties (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).

## 2. Software Development

Performed By: Subcommittee Members, Third Parties

Activity:
- Develop TPC-Provided Software according to defined procedures (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).
- Contribute code to the software, ensuring a signed CLA is on file with the TPC Administrator (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).
- Assign copyright to the contributor or TPC, depending on contractual agreements (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).

## 3. Software Testing and Quality Assurance

Performed By: Subcommittee Members, Third Parties

Activity:
- Test the developed software for quality assurance according to defined procedures (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).
- Report any issues or problems found during testing to the Benchmark Subcommittee (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).

## 4. Software Approval and Publication

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, Council

Activity:
- Propose resolution of comments from formal review as an updated draft of the TPC-Provided Software (policies_v6.19.pdf).
- Bring forward a motion to the Council for approval of the TPC-Provided Software (policies_v6.19.pdf).
- Publish the approved TPC-Provided Software upon Council's approval (policies_v6.19.pdf).

## 5. Formal Review and Approval Process

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, Council, All Members, Public

Activity:
- Make the software available for formal review by All Members and the public (policies_v6.19.pdf).
- Consider comments and proposed changes generated from the review (policies_v6.19.pdf).

## 6. Software Maintenance and Support

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, TPC Administrator

Activity:
- Provide support via the problem reporting mechanism for TPC-Provided Software (procedures_v1.0.0.pdf).

## 7. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (catalog_page).

## 8. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Record returned items with relevant details such as returned date, time, item SKU, customer information, warehouse SKU, and reason for return (catalog_returns).
