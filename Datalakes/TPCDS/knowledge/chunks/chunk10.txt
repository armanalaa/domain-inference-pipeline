 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Technical Subcommittee Formation and Management

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittees, Working Groups, Membership Committee

Activity:
- Benchmark Subcommittees and Working Groups are formed to address specific technical issues (membership requirements).
- The Membership Committee manages the membership process, ensuring minimum membership requirements are met.

## 2. Meeting Organization and Conduct

Performed By: Technical Subcommittee Chairperson, Meeting Attendees

Activity:
- Technical Subcommittee meetings are scheduled according to the meeting schedule, with possible modifications if necessary.
- The chairperson conducts meetings following established rules, including duties such as leading discussions and enforcing meeting etiquette.

## 3. General Meetings Management

Performed By: Meeting Attendees, Chairperson

Activity:
- General meetings are held according to the frequency defined in the calendar of recurring events.
- Meeting minutes are recorded during general meetings for future reference.

## 4. Voting and Decision Making

Performed By: Meeting Attendees, Chairperson

Activity:
- Voting rules are applied during general meetings to make decisions on various matters.
- Mail ballots may be conducted when necessary, following the established process for conducting a mail ballot.

## 5. Benchmark Standard Development and Revision

Performed By: Technical Subcommittees, Working Groups

Activity:
- Technical Subcommittees and Working Groups develop and revise benchmark standards according to the rules outlined in the Technical Subcommittee Operating Rules.

## 6. Results Reporting and Publication

Performed By: Test Sponsor, Reporting Processor

Activity:
- The test sponsor collects processor information for reporting purposes.
- Pre-publication certification is conducted before TPC results are published on the TPC Results List.
