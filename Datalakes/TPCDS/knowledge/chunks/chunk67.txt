 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Benchmark Proposal Submission

Performed By: TPC Member Companies, TPC Council

Activity:
- TPC member companies submit draft benchmark proposals to the TPC Council for consideration (Specification).
- The Council reviews the contents, applicability, and potential of the proposal and presents a recommendation back to the Council.

## 2. Benchmark Subcommittee Establishment

Performed By: TPC Council

Activity:
- If the proposal is accepted for future work, the Council establishes a Benchmark Subcommittee to develop a formal benchmark Specification.

## 3. Benchmark Development

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee

Activity:
- The subcommittee develops a formal benchmark Specification based on the accepted proposal.
- The subcommittee may brief non-members for timely feedback to speed up the benchmark development cycle.

## 4. Status Updates and Council Feedback

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, TPC Council

Activity:
- At each General Meeting, the Benchmark Subcommittee provides a status update on its work, including a working draft of the Specification.
- The Council may provide direction and feedback to the subcommittee to further their work.

## 5. Draft Specification Release Authorization

Performed By: TPC Council

Activity:
- If deemed advisable, the Council may authorize the release of a draft Specification to the public for experimental data gathering and speeding up the approval process.

## 6. Benchmark Standard Approval

Performed By: TPC Council

Activity:
- The Council votes to formally accept the proposal for future work, leading to the creation of a new TPC-Enterprise Class Benchmark.

## 7. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific markets based on market class and description (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Sales representatives handle customer calls from the assigned call centers (cc_call_center_sk).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and ship orders accordingly (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 8. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when items are sold or returned (cr_item_sk).

## 9. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date and time, reason for return, and order number (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk, cr_order_number).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when items are returned (cr_item_sk).
