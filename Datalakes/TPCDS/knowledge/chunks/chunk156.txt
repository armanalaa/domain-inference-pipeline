 TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Inventory Analysts

Activity:
- Warehouse managers assign unique identifiers to each stock item.
- Inventory analysts record the quantity of each item in the warehouse.
- Warehouse managers store distribution center details for each item.
- Inventory analysts categorize items based on their distribution centers.
- Warehouse managers update the total number of orders for each item.

## 2. District Management

Performed By: District Managers, Geographic Information Specialists

Activity:
- District managers register new districts within a warehouse's jurisdiction.
- Geographic information specialists provide street addresses and city details for each district.
- District managers determine the applicable tax rate for each district.
- Geographic information specialists assign unique identifiers to each district.
- District managers maintain the next order identifier for each district.

## 3. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Data Entry Clerks

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers in the system.
- Data entry clerks record customer identification details.
- Customer service representatives assign districts to customers based on their location.
- Data entry clerks link customers to their corresponding warehouse.
- Customer service representatives generate unique identifiers for each customer history record.

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Agents, Customer Service Managers

Activity:
- Call center agents answer incoming calls from customers.
- Customer service managers assign calls to available call center agents.
- Call center agents provide information about products and services.
- Customer service managers monitor call center agent performance.
- Call center agents record call details, including date, time, and reason for the call.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing teams create and manage product descriptions on the catalog pages.
- Product managers categorize products based on their department.
- Marketing teams assign unique identifiers to each catalog page.
- Product managers track the number of orders for each catalog page.
