 TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific warehouses based on the warehouse's capacity and location (cc_division, cc_company).
- Warehouse staff update call center details such as name, class, employees count, square footage, operating hours, and manager assignment (cc_name, cc_class, cc_employees, cc_sq_ft, cc_hours, cc_manager).

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Manager, Call Center Manager

Activity:
- Market managers define market classes and descriptions for each call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers associate their call centers with specific markets based on the market class and description (cc_mkt_id).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by selecting items from the catalog and specifying quantities (ol_i_id, ol_quantity).
- Warehouse staff update stock levels as orders are processed (s_i_id, s_quantity).

## 5. Delivery Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff determine the delivery date for each order based on the next available delivery transaction for the district (d_next_o_id).
- Warehouse staff update the delivery date for each order line in the order_line table (ol_delivery_d).

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned item, refunded customer details, and reason for return (cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update stock levels as returns are processed (s_i_id, s_quantity).
