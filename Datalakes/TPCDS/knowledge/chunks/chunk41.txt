 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the required skills and open positions (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal information, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming customer calls and provide assistance (cc_rec_start_date).
- They record call details such as the reason for the call, product inquiries, and solutions provided (cc_class).
- Call center staff document customer complaints or issues that require further action (cc_closed_date_sk, cc_open_date_sk).

## 3. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Division Managers

Activity:
- Market managers oversee multiple call centers within a specific market segment (cc_mkt_id, cc_division).
- Division managers allocate resources and set performance targets for each division under their control (cc_division_name).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details like code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing teams update catalog pages with promotional content or seasonal offers (cp_start_date_sk, cp_end_date_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned item details and reason for return (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels to reflect returned items (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by confirming availability and shipping details (cr_order_number).
- Customer service representatives update order statuses as they progress through the fulfillment process (cc_rec_end_date).
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship ordered items to customers (cr_ship_mode_sk).

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions such as revenue, expenses, and taxes (cc_tax_percentage).
- They generate reports on call center performance, sales, and returns for analysis and decision making (not specified in the provided materials).
