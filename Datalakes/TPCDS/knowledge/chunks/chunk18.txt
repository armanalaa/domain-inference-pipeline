 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by recording their personal details and qualifications.
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing them with training materials and schedules.
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facility managers allocate square footage for new call centers according to the number of employees assigned.
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary equipment such as phones and computers.
- Facility managers set up call center locations based on market requirements and company divisions.

## 3. Market Allocation

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers determine the appropriate market class for each call center.
- Call center managers assign market classes to their centers based on the services they will provide.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added to the catalog.

## 5. Catalog Page Management

Performed By: Marketing Team, Web Developers

Activity:
- Marketing team creates and organizes catalog pages by department and product category.
- Web developers design and publish these pages on the company's website for customers to browse.

## 6. Customer Returns

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center representatives process customer returns by recording return details and reasons.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, update inventory levels, and prepare them for restocking or disposal.

## 7. Performance Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management Team

Activity:
- Data analysts collect and analyze performance data from call centers, such as call volume and average handling time.
- Management team reviews the analysis to identify areas for improvement and make necessary adjustments.
