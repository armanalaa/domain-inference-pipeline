 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. District Management

Performed By: District Managers, City Planners

Activity:
- District managers assign unique IDs (D_ID) to each district based on geographical boundaries.
- City planners update the street information (D_STREET_1, D_STREET_2) for each district.
- District managers record the city, state, and zip code (D_CITY, D_STATE, D_ZIP) for each district.
- District managers calculate and set the sales tax rate (D_TAX) based on local regulations.

## 2. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Inventory Specialists

Activity:
- Warehouse managers assign unique IDs (W_ID) to each warehouse.
- Inventory specialists track the quantity of items in stock (S_QUANTITY) for each warehouse.
- Warehouse managers determine the distribution information (S_DIST_01, S_DIST_02, ..., S_DIST_10) based on demand and location.

## 3. Customer Registration

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Sales Associates

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register new customers by recording their unique ID (C_ID), name, address, phone number (C_FIRST, C_LAST, C_STREET_1, C_STREET_2, C_CITY, C_STATE, C_ZIP, C_PHONE).
- Sales associates assign credit status (C_CREDIT) and set credit limit (C_CREDIT_LIM) for each customer.

## 4. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Associates

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog (I_ID), specifying quantities, and choosing shipping options.
- Sales associates enter order details into the ORDER table (O_ID, O_D_ID, O_W_ID, O_C_ID, O_ENTRY_D) and generate unique order numbers (NO_O_ID).

## 5. Order Line Management

Performed By: Sales Associates, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales associates create order lines by linking items to orders (OL_I_ID, OL_O_ID) and specifying quantities (OL_QUANTITY).
- Warehouse staff update the stock levels (S_QUANTITY) as items are sold.

## 6. Inventory Replenishment

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Warehouse Managers

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new inventory based on demand forecasts and supplier availability.
- Warehouse managers receive and update the stock levels (S_QUANTITY) as new items arrive.

## 7. Delivery Management

Performed By: Carriers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Carriers pick up orders from warehouses and deliver them to customers based on shipping options (O_CARRIER_ID).
- Warehouse staff update the delivery status (OL_DELIVERY_D) for each order line.

## 8. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return dates and times (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk), reasons for returns (cr_DIST_INFO), and updating the stock levels (S_QUANTITY) as items are returned.

## 9. Sales Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract sales data from the ORDER table (O_ID, O_D_ID, O_W_ID, O_C_ID) and analyze trends to inform business decisions.

## 10. Inventory Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract inventory data from the STOCK table (S_I_ID, S_W_ID, S_QUANTITY) and analyze trends to optimize stock levels and distribution.
