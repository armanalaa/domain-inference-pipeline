 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Policy Proposal and Approval

Performed By: Council Members, Steering Committee

Activity:
- Council members or Standing Committees propose policy modifications (cc_manager).
- The Steering Committee reviews the proposed changes and prepares a recommendation (cc_mkt_id).
- The proposal is added to the agenda for the next General Meeting (cp_catalog_page_number).
- At least ten working days prior to the next General Meeting, the proposal and recommendation are distributed to all Members for review (cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- At the next General Meeting, the SC presents the proposal and its recommendation for a vote by the Council (cp_catalog_page_id). If approved, the policy modification is effective immediately unless specified otherwise.

## 2. Policy Modification Review

Performed By: Steering Committee

Activity:
- The Steering Committee reviews proposed changes to associated documents (TPC Procedures, TPC Membership List, Fair Use Quick Reference Guide).

## 3. Document Maintenance and Approval

Performed By: TPC Administrator, Steering Committee

Activity:
- The TPC Administrator maintains the "TPC Membership List" document (TPC_Membership.pdf).
- The Steering Committee approves changes to the "TPC Procedures" and "Fair Use Quick Reference Guide" documents (TPC_Procedures.pdf, TPC_Fair_Use_Quick_Reference.pdf).

## 4. Policy Distribution

Performed By: Web Administrator

Activity:
- When distributed via the Web Site or as part of TPC-Provided Software, associated documents use prescribed file names and formats (CLA.pdf, EULA.txt, TPC_Fair_Use_Quick_Reference.pdf, TPC_Membership.pdf, TPC_Procedures.pdf).

## 5. Mission Statement Management

Performed By: Council Members, Steering Committee

Activity:
- The TPC is focused on developing data-centric benchmark standards and disseminating objective, verifiable performance data to the industry (TPC mission statement).
