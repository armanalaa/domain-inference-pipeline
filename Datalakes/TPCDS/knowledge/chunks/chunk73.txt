 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Benchmark Subcommittee Management

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee Members

Activity:
- Collaborate on the development of a new benchmark standard by discussing and providing feedback during General Meetings.
- Prepare a working draft of the Specification for presentation at General Meetings.
- Submit the Specification to the Council for approval to advance into formal review.

## 2. Benchmark Standard Approval Process

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, Council Members

Activity:
- Review the submitted Specification during a formal review period of 60 days.
- Consider and resolve comments generated from the review.
- Propose resolution of comments as an updated Benchmark Standard to the Council for approval.

## 3. TPC-Provided Kit Release

Performed By: Council Members

Activity:
- Authorize the release of a draft TPC-Provided Kit to the public when deemed advisable.
- Encourage companies to run the draft kit, document results, and discuss findings with All Members and customers.

## 4. Results Publication and Discussion

Performed By: Companies, All Members, Customers

Activity:
- Run the TPC-Provided Kit and publish technical articles or make presentations discussing results.
- Adhere to the conditions in Policies § 8.1 (Use of TPC Materials) and Policies § 8.3 (Fair Use of TPC Specifications).

## 5. Benchmark Standard Publication

Performed By: Council Members

Activity:
- Approve the updated Benchmark Standard proposed by the Benchmark Subcommittee.
- Publish the approved Benchmark Standard as a TPC Result that can be publicized.

## 6. Benchmark Development Work

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, Council Members

Activity:
- Continue work on developing a new benchmark standard if authorized by the Council.
- Disband the subcommittee at the conclusion of the General Meeting if the Council does not authorize continued work.
