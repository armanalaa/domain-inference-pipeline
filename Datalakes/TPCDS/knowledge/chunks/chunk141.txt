 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by recording their details such as name, qualifications, and employment history.
- HR managers process employee paperwork, including tax forms and benefits enrollment.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Management, IT Support

Activity:
- Facilities management assigns square footage and work hours to each call center.
- IT support configures call center systems, ensuring compatibility with the database and communication networks.

## 3. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers allocate call centers to specific markets based on geographical coverage and market segment.
- Market managers oversee the performance of assigned call centers, providing guidance and resources as needed.

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls, assist customers with inquiries or troubleshoot issues.
- Call center managers monitor call quality, coach employees on performance, and address customer complaints.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog by adding new items with product codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Marketing managers create and manage the layout of catalog pages for different departments.

## 6. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details such as date, time, reason, and item information.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update the inventory accordingly.

## 7. Sales Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts collect and analyze sales data from various sources, including call center transactions and catalog page views.
- Management reviews sales reports to identify trends, make informed decisions, and set strategic goals for the business.
