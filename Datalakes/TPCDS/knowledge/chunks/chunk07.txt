 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific warehouses based on geographical proximity (cc_division).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of a call center when it expands or contracts (cc_sq_ft).

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize call centers based on market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers update the market manager responsible for a specific call center (cc_market_manager).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls during business hours (cc_hours).
- Call center managers record the start and end dates of a call center's operation (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse where a specific item is stored when inventory levels change (not specified in provided schema).

## 5. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record return requests from customers, including the returned date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse where a returned item is stored (not specified in provided schema).

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team records the tax percentage for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).
