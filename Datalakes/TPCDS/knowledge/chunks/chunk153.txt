 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with details like name, class, employees, square footage, hours of operation, and market information.
- Warehouse staff update the year-to-date sales for a specific warehouse when a payment transaction is processed.

## 2. District Management

Performed By: District Managers

Activity:
- District managers register new districts with details such as street, city, state, zip code, and name.
- District managers update the year-to-date sales for a specific district when a payment transaction is processed.

## 3. Customer Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer payments by updating the customer's balance and recording the transaction details.
- If a customer has a credit card, customer service representatives check if it's blocked ("BC") before processing the payment.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives register new orders with details like order ID, carrier ID, and entry date.
- Warehouse staff track the quantity of each item ordered for a specific order.

## 5. Order Status Inquiry

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives retrieve the status of a customer's orders, including order ID, carrier ID, and entry date.
- For a specific customer, they also access details about each line item in the order, such as item ID, supplying warehouse ID, quantity, amount, and delivery date.

## 6. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price.
- They also manage the content of the catalog pages, including department, catalog number, catalog page number, description, and type.

## 7. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording returned date and time, item SKU, refunded customer details, and reason for return.
- They also update the warehouse and district's year-to-date sales when a return is processed.
