 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers, Division Managers

Activity:
- Assign call centers to specific markets and divisions based on market class, division name, and company name (call_center).
- Determine the number of employees required for each call center (call_center).
- Allocate square footage for each call center according to the number of employees (call_center).
- Set operating hours for each call center (call_center).
- Designate a manager for each call center (call_center).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Market Managers

Activity:
- Answer incoming calls during operational hours (call_center).
- Route calls to appropriate departments based on customer inquiries (not specified in the schema).
- Record start and end dates for each call (call_center).
- Close calls when issues are resolved or escalated (call_center).
- Update call center open and closed dates as needed (call_center).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (catalog_page).
- Assign each item to a specific department (catalog_page).
- Create unique catalog numbers for each item (catalog_page).
- Update catalog pages as needed (catalog_page).

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Record returned items and their quantities (catalog_returns).
- Assign returned items to appropriate warehouses (catalog_returns).
- Update the status of the order associated with the return (not specified in the schema).
- Determine the reason for each return (catalog_returns).
- Refund customers based on return reasons and applicable policies (not specified in the schema).

## 5. Policy Compliance Monitoring

Performed By: Council Members, TPC Administration

Activity:
- Review claims to ensure they adhere to TPC Policies (Policies_v6.19.pdf).
- Investigate alleged policy violations and take appropriate response measures if necessary (TPC Policies, Revision 6.19).
