 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by specifying the required skills, qualifications, and job roles.
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing training materials, company policies, and equipment.
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills, availability, and business needs.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers determine the necessary square footage for a new call center.
- Warehouse staff prepare and deliver equipment (phones, computers, etc.) to the designated call center location.
- Call center managers set up the call center infrastructure according to the company's standards.

## 3. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers and HR managers assign each call center to a specific market division based on geographical location or market segment.

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers and route them to the appropriate department (sales or customer service).
- Sales representatives assist customers in placing orders by providing product information, answering questions, and processing transactions.
- Customer service agents handle customer complaints, returns, and refunds by following established procedures and updating relevant records.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details (code, name, category, list price).
- Marketing managers create and update catalog pages to showcase products and provide relevant information to customers.

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service agents process customer returns by recording return details (date, time, reason) and updating the order status.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for damage or defects, and prepare them for restocking or disposal.

## 7. Auditor Certification

Performed By: TPC Council, Prospective Candidates

Activity:
- Prospective candidates apply to become apprentice auditors by submitting their background information, familiarity with benchmarking, and desired benchmarks for certification.
- The TPC Council reviews the applications and decides whether to accept or reject the candidates based on their qualifications and the number of existing Auditors.
