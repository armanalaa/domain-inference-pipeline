 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by specifying their roles, qualifications, and salaries. (cc_employees)
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing them with necessary training materials and equipment. (not specified)
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability. (cc_call_center_id)

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Facilities managers allocate space for new call centers by providing square footage, hours of operation, and necessary amenities. (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours)
- IT staff configure call center systems by setting up hardware, software, and network connections. (not specified)

## 3. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign each call center to a specific market division based on geographical location or market segment. (cc_division)

## 4. Market Classification

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers classify markets according to their size, competition, and potential for growth. (cc_mkt_class, cc_market_manager)

## 5. Call Center Description

Performed By: Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers provide detailed descriptions of each call center, including its purpose, services offered, and unique selling points. (cc_name, cc_description)

## 6. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price. (not specified)
- IT staff update the catalog database with the new item information. (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type)

## 7. Catalog Page Maintenance

Performed By: IT Staff

Activity:
- IT staff maintain catalog pages by updating product descriptions, images, and pricing information as needed. (not specified)

## 8. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording the returned date and time, item SKU, customer details, and reason for return. (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk)
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for damage or defects, and update the database with their condition. (not specified)

## 9. Audit Preparation

Performed By: Test Sponsors, IT Staff

Activity:
- Test sponsors prepare for audits by automating test environments and developing audit protocols. (not specified)
- IT staff generate audit trails of actions and system configurations to facilitate compliance with audit requirements. (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk, cr_warehouse_sk)
