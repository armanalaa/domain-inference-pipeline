 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, HR Manager

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on their skills and qualifications (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, contact information, and employment history (cc_name, cc_sq_ft, cc_hours, cc_manager, cc_mkt_id, cc_division, cc_company).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming calls and provide customer support (cc_call_center_sk, cc_open_date_sk, cc_closed_date_sk).
- They record call details such as product inquiries, complaints, or orders (cc_name, cc_class, cc_mkt_id, cr_item_sk, cr_order_number).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_page_id, cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold (cr_item_sk, cr_quan).

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return dates and reasons (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when processing customer returns (cr_item_sk, cr_quan).

## 5. Review and Approval Processes

Performed By: Council Members, TPC Staff

Activity:
- Council members review and vote on TPC recommendations (6.12, 6.13).
- TPC staff publish approved results and press releases (7.3, 7.4).

## 6. Confidentiality and Compliance Management

Performed By: TPC Staff, Auditors

Activity:
- TPC staff ensure confidentiality of TPC information and materials (7.1).
- Auditors review compliance with TPC policies and issue certifications (Independent Audit).
