 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the number of open positions and employee qualifications.
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, employment history, and performance evaluations.

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers determine market class and division for each call center based on geographical location and customer demographics.
- Call center managers update call center records with the assigned market class, division, and related details.

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Support

Activity:
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary equipment such as phones, computers, and furniture according to the specified square footage.
- IT support configures call center systems, including phone lines, internet connections, and software applications.

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries about products and services, providing information and processing orders.
- Customer service agents assist customers with account management, billing issues, and technical support.

## 5. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing team updates catalog pages with descriptions and promotional content for each product.

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service agents process returns by recording return date, time, reason, and customer information.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for damage, and update inventory accordingly.

## 7. Compliance Review

Performed By: TAB Members, Council Members

Activity:
- TAB members review Results to ensure they meet Benchmark Standards and recommend compliance or non-compliance to the Council.
- Council members discuss and vote on TAB recommendations during General Meetings.
