 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific call centers based on their expertise and workload (cc_division).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center (cc_sq_ft).
- Call center managers determine the operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Market managers categorize call centers based on market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Sales representatives assign specific markets to each call center (cc_mkt_id).
- Market managers appoint market managers for each division within the company (cc_market_manager).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers (cc_call_center_id).
- Customers place orders for products through call centers (not specified in the schema).
- Call center employees record product details such as code, name, category, and list price during order placement (not specified in the schema).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (not specified in the schema).
- Warehouse staff update the catalog with new pages containing product details (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns initiated by customers (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse with returned items (cr_warehouse_sk).
- Customer service representatives record reasons for product returns (cr_reason_sk).
