 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Steering Committee, Call Center Manager

Activity:
- The Steering Committee elects new call center managers during the Annual Meeting.
- The call center manager assigns employees to specific call centers based on their skills and expertise.
- The call center manager determines the number of employees needed for each call center.
- The call center manager sets the operating hours for each call center.
- The call center manager hires additional staff when necessary to meet demand.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming calls from customers during operational hours.
- Customers place orders or inquire about products over the phone.
- Call center staff record product details (name, category, list price) for new items in the catalog.
- Call center staff update customer information (address, contact details) when necessary.
- Call center staff handle returns and refunds according to the reason provided by customers.

## 3. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Chief Financial Officer, Administrator

Activity:
- The Chief Financial Officer prepares annual financial reports for the Corporation.
- The annual report includes a balance sheet, income statement, and statement of changes in financial position.
- The annual report is sent to each member upon request within 120 days after the close of the fiscal year.
- The Administrator sets reasonable reproduction costs for providing copies of reports or documents to members.

## 4. Record Keeping

Performed By: Corporation Staff, Members

Activity:
- The Corporation keeps adequate and correct records of accounts and minutes of Board meetings.
- The Corporation maintains a record of its members with their names and addresses.
- Minutes are kept in written form, while other books and records can be kept in any form capable of being converted into written form.
- Members have the right to receive a financial report yearly as per Corporations Code Section 8321(a).

## 5. Document Distribution

Performed By: Administrator, Members

Activity:
- Upon written request from any member, the Corporation provides copies of reports, studies, or other documents made available to members upon payment of reasonable reproduction costs.
- The Administrator affixes the annual statement of certain transactions and indemnifications to the annual report as required by Corporations Code Section 8322.
- The Corporation furnishes annually to its members a statement of any transaction or indemnification described in Corporations Code Section 8322(d) and (e).
