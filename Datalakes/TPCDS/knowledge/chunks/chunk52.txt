 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to warehouse staff based on their capacity (cc_employees).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center (cc_sq_ft).
- Call center managers determine the operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets based on market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- HR managers hire new employees for the assigned call centers (cc_employees).

## 3. Call Center Location Setup

Performed By: Administrative Staff, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Administrative staff register new call center locations with their respective details (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Warehouse staff set up the physical infrastructure for each call center.

## 4. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers classify each call center based on its type (cc_class).
- HR managers determine the number of employees required for each call center class.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with their respective codes, names, categories, and list prices (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing teams create and update catalog pages to display the products.

## 6. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record customer returns with their respective dates and times (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for returned items (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 7. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders and record order numbers (cr_order_number).
- Customer service representatives update the status of each order in the system.
