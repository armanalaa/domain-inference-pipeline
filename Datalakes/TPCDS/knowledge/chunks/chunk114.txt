 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse locations to new call centers based on the call center's division and company information.
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of a call center when it expands or contracts.
- Warehouse staff track the hours each call center operates daily.

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize and describe call centers based on market class and division.
- Call center managers associate a specific market manager with each call center.

## 3. Customer Service Operations

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and process orders for customers over the phone.
- Call center employees record the product code, name, category, and list price of items ordered by customers.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording their product code, name, category, and list price.
- Call center employees update the catalog page with the item's details when it is sold out or discontinued.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives confirm the availability of items in stock before processing customer orders.
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse's inventory when an order is shipped out or returned.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Call center employees record return details, including the reason for the return and the customer's information.
- Customers submit returns of purchased items through call centers.

## 7. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records the tax percentage associated with each warehouse location.
