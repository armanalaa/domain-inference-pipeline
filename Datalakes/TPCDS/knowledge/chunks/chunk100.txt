 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with specific details such as name, class, employees, square footage, hours, and market information.
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for each call center.

## 2. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price.
- Sales representatives create or update catalog pages with department, catalog number, catalog page number, description, and type.

## 3. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customers, Warehouse Staff, Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns by providing the returned date and time, item SKU, and reason for return.
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse inventory with the returned quantity.
- Call center representatives process refunds for customers based on their customer details and return information.

## 4. Error Handling and Reporting

Performed By: Application Developers, Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Application developers implement error handling mechanisms to detect and report errors that could result in an invalid TPC-C transaction.
- Call center representatives report detected errors to customers as part of the output screen or delivery log.

## 5. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets with market class and description.
- Call center managers update the division, division name, company, and company name for each call center based on market assignments.

## 6. Transaction Monitoring

Performed By: Application Developers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Application developers implement transaction monitoring to detect and terminate long-running transactions based on predefined time-out intervals.
- Call center managers ensure application transparency by managing message contexts between client and server applications without any knowledge of the underlying communication mechanisms.
