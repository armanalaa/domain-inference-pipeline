 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal information and employment details (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Configuration

Performed By: IT Administrators, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- IT administrators configure call center settings such as opening hours, square footage, and GMT offset (cc_hours, cc_sq_ft, cc_gmt_offset).
- Call center managers assign market classes and descriptions to each call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers oversee the division of markets and assign call centers to specific markets (cc_division, cc_market_manager).

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives handle incoming calls from customers and provide assistance (cc_call_center_id).
- Call center representatives record call details such as start date, end date, closed date, and reason for call (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date, cc_closed_date_sk).

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return date and time, item SKU, and customer details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when processing a return (cr_quan).
