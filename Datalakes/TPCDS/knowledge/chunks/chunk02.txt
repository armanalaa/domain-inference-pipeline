 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Member Management

Performed By: Corporation Administrator, Board of Directors

Activity:
- Review membership applications and make decisions on admission based on eligibility criteria.
- Approve or deny membership requests in accordance with the bylaws and board resolutions.
- Maintain a membership book containing the name, address, and status of each member.

## 2. Membership Fee Management

Performed By: Corporation Administrator, Members

Activity:
- Set annual dues for all classes of members as determined by the Board of Directors.
- Collect dues from new members within 90 days of admission and annually thereafter.
- Process resignations to avoid liability for unpaid dues.

## 3. Membership Voting Rights

Performed By: Members, Corporation Administrator

Activity:
- Determine eligibility of subsidiaries to vote in designated TPC committees or sub-groups.
- Cast votes on matters presented by the Board of Directors or Standing Committees.

## 4. Board of Directors Appointment

Performed By: Members, Corporation Administrator

Activity:
- Appoint one member to the Corporation's Board of Directors upon admission to membership.

## 5. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Establish and manage call centers for handling customer inquiries or complaints.
- Assign employees and allocate resources to call centers based on market needs.

## 6. Catalog Page Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update or modify existing catalog pages as needed.

## 7. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Handle customer returns by recording returned date, time, item SKU, and reason for return.
- Refund customers and update their records accordingly.
- Coordinate with warehouse staff to process and track returned items.
