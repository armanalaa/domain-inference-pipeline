 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the call center's capacity (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee details in the call center table (cc_name, cc_class, cc_sq_ft, cc_hours) upon hiring.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers set the operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and process payments during call center operations.

## 3. Customer Selection

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff select customers based on either their customer number or last name for processing payments.

## 4. Payment Processing - Case 1

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Upon selecting a customer based on their number, call center staff retrieve and update the customer's balance (C_BALANCE) and payment count (C_PAYMENT_CNT).

## 5. Payment Processing - Case 2

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Upon selecting a customer based on their last name, call center staff retrieve the relevant customer's details from the sorted set of selected rows.

## 6. Customer History Recording

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- If a customer's credit status is "BC", call center staff record the payment transaction in the customer's history (C_DATA).

## 7. Warehouse and District Balance Updates

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Upon successful payment, call center staff update the warehouse and district year-to-date balances (W_YTD, D_YTD).

## 8. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 9. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned item's details and updating the customer's balance (cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk, cr_BALANCE).
