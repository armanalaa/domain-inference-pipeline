 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with specific square footage and hours of operation based on market requirements (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse assigned to each call center (cc_warehouse_sk).

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize and describe markets for new call centers (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers associate each call center with a specific market (cc_mkt_id).

## 3. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers classify call centers based on their type or division (cc_class, cc_division).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with a product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Sales representatives create new pages for the catalog showcasing specific products (cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record customer returns with their respective dates and times (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse where returned items are stored (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Financial Obligations Settlement

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records financial obligations of members involved in mergers or acquisitions (cc_tax_percentage).

## 7. Committee Management

Performed By: TPC Board of Directors, Primary Representative

Activity:
- The TPC Board of Directors determines the membership and committee representation after a merger or acquisition (cc_mkt_id, cc_division).
- The Primary Representative designates a single committee member in case of conflicts (cr_reason_sk).
