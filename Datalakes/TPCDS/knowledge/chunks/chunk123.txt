 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse managers assign warehouses to specific districts (cc_division).
- Warehouse staff track the square footage of each warehouse (cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff record the hours of operation for each warehouse (cc_hours).
- Warehouse managers assign a manager to each warehouse (cc_manager).
- Warehouse staff maintain the address and location details of each warehouse (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country, cc_gmt_offset).

## 2. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific markets (cc_mkt_id).
- Call center managers categorize each call center based on its class (cc_class).
- Call center employees handle customer calls within the assigned call center (implied by cc_call_center_sk).
- Call center managers determine the tax percentage for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Market managers oversee multiple markets (cc_division).
- Sales representatives register new items in the catalog for their respective markets (implied by cp_catalog_page_sk).
- Market managers categorize each market based on its class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (implied by cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Sales representatives update the catalog with new or discontinued items as needed.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer orders by recording order details (implied by cr_order_number).
- Warehouse staff retrieve the necessary items from stock for each order (STOCK, ITEM).
- Warehouse staff update the inventory after an order is fulfilled.

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the inventory after a returned item is received.
- Warehouse staff ship the returned items back to the warehouse.
