 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives (CSRs), Supervisors

Activity:
- CSRs answer customer inquiries about products and services using call logs and customer information from the `call_center` table.
- CSRs update call logs with call duration, outcome, and resolution details after each interaction.
- Supervisors monitor CSR performance by reviewing call logs and quality assurance metrics from the `call_center` and `employee_performance` tables.
- Supervisors provide coaching and feedback to CSRs based on their performance evaluations.

## 2. Product Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Analysts

Activity:
- Product Managers define product specifications, pricing, and promotions using product information from the `product` table.
- Marketing Analysts analyze sales data from the `sales_fact` table to identify trends and customer preferences for each product.
- Based on analysis results, Product Managers adjust product offerings, pricing strategies, and marketing campaigns accordingly.

## 3. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Workers

Activity:
- Sales Representatives process orders from customers using order details from the `orders` table.
- Sales Representatives update order statuses in the `orders` table and notify customers about their orders.
- Warehouse Workers pick, pack, and ship ordered items based on order details and inventory levels from the `inventory` table.
- Warehouse Workers update inventory levels in the `inventory` table after each shipment.

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Inventory Managers, Purchasing Agents

Activity:
- Inventory Managers monitor and adjust inventory levels based on sales data from the `sales_fact` table and stock alerts from the `inventory` table.
- Purchasing Agents place orders for new inventory items when stock levels fall below minimum thresholds, using vendor information from the `vendor` table.
- Inventory Managers update inventory levels in the `inventory` table after receiving new shipments.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Accountants, Finance Analysts

Activity:
- Accountants process invoices and payments using transaction data from the `transactions` table.
- Finance Analysts analyze financial reports generated from the `financial_fact` table to track revenue, expenses, and profitability.
- Based on analysis results, Accountants make adjustments to accounting entries as needed.

## 6. Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Marketing Analysts

Activity:
- Sales Representatives use customer information from the `customer` table to identify potential leads and upselling opportunities.
- Marketing Analysts segment customers based on demographic and behavioral data from the `customer_demographics` and `web_logs` tables for targeted marketing campaigns.
- Sales Representatives update customer profiles with interaction history and contact information in the `customer` table.

## 7. Data Analysis and Reporting

Performed By: Business Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Business Analysts extract data from various tables (e.g., `call_center`, `orders`, `sales_fact`) to create reports and dashboards for different departments.
- Management reviews reports and dashboards to make informed decisions about business strategy and operations.
- Based on insights gained, Management sets goals and objectives for each department.
