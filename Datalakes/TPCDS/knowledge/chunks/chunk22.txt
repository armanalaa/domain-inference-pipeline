 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to warehouse staff based on available space (cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff allocate designated square footage for each call center (cc_sq_ft).
- Call center managers update the hours of operation for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific markets based on market needs (cc_mkt_id).
- HR managers recruit and hire employees for each call center according to the assigned market (cc_employees).

## 3. Market Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers classify markets based on their type and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Marketing managers update the market classification for each call center (cc_mkt_id).

## 4. Call Center Location Setup

Performed By: HR Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- HR managers provide the physical address details of each call center (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Warehouse staff set up the physical location for each call center according to its address (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).

## 5. Call Center Operations Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center operations and performance (cc_hours, cc_manager).
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and sales calls within the call centers (cc_call_center_id).

## 6. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing managers update the catalog with new products or changes to existing ones (cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 7. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns and refunds (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the inventory with returned items and prepare them for restocking (cr_warehouse_sk).
