 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the required skills and available positions (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_division, cc_company).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming customer calls during operational hours (cc_hours) and assist with inquiries or troubleshoot issues.
- Representatives record call details, including the customer's name, contact information, and issue description (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date, cc_closed_date_sk).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers define market strategies for each call center, including target customer demographics and marketing campaigns (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- They monitor the performance of each call center against these strategies and adjust as necessary (cc_market_manager).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- They update product details when necessary to ensure accuracy and relevance (cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 5. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive returned items from customers and record the return details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk).
- Customer service representatives process refunds for the returned items, updating customer records accordingly (cr_refunded_customer_sk).

## 6. Order Processing and Fulfillment

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process orders from customers, including selecting the appropriate shipping method (cr_ship_mode_sk).
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship the ordered items to the customer's specified address (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 7. Performance Monitoring and Reporting

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Analytics Team

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance metrics, such as average handling time and service level (cc_hours, cc_closed_date_sk).
- The analytics team generates reports based on these metrics to identify trends and areas for improvement (not specified in the database schema).
