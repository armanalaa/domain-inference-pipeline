 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by recording their personal details and job roles (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information in the database (cc_company_name, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives handle customer calls within specified hours (cc_hours).
- They record call details such as start and end dates, closed date, and manager information (cc_rec_start_date, cc_closed_date_sk, cc_manager).

## 3. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets (cc_mkt_id).
- Call center managers update the assigned market and its class in the database (cc_mkt_class, cc_market_manager).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, etc.).

## 5. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives log returns from customers (cr_returned_date_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location where the returned item is stored (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Interpretation Request Processing

Performed By: TAB Members, TAB Chairperson

Activity:
- The TAB chairperson receives interpretation requests and distributes them to members (3.3.4.1).
- TAB members discuss and provide a ruling on the request, which is posted on the Private Web Site (3.3.4.4).

## 7. Clarification or Conflict Resolution Requests

Performed By: TAB Members

Activity:
- TAB members review Pre-Publication Board requests for clarifications and conflict resolution (3.3.5.2, 3.3.5.4).
