 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the required hours and available positions (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information such as names, addresses, and employment details (cc_name, cc_street_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Facility managers determine the size and layout of call centers (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).
- IT support configures call center systems, including phone lines and software applications (not specified in schema summary).

## 3. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign each call center to a specific market division based on geographical location or market focus (cc_division).

## 4. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers categorize call centers according to their primary function or service level (cc_class).

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (not specified in schema summary).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold (not specified in schema summary).

## 6. Catalog Page Management

Performed By: Web Developers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Web developers create and maintain the structure of catalog pages (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number).
- Marketing managers update product descriptions, categories, and promotions on catalog pages (cp_description, cp_type).

## 7. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date, time, item SKU, and reason for return (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when processing customer returns (not specified in schema summary).
