 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- Market managers determine the market class and division for each call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_division).
- HR managers recruit new employees and update employee information in the call center database (cc_name, cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers and provide assistance (cc_call_center_id).
- Call center managers monitor call center performance and make adjustments as needed to ensure efficient operations.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Catalog Page Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Catalog page managers update and maintain the organization's online catalog pages.

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return date, time, item SKU, and reason (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update the inventory accordingly.

## 5. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department records all financial transactions, including filing fees for submitted results (cr_order_number).
- The finance department issues invoices for Filing Fees quarterly and processes payments from Test Sponsors.

## 6. Result Submission and Review

Performed By: Test Sponsor Representatives, TPC Committee Members

Activity:
- Test sponsor representatives submit results to the Administrator for review (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).
- TPC committee members review submitted results and assign them a status of Submitted for Review or Accepted.

## 7. Result Maintenance and Withdrawal

Performed By: TPC Committee Members

Activity:
- If fraud or serious violations are detected, TPC committee members withdraw the result from the TPC Results List (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).
- TPC committee members maintain a list of accepted results and ensure that they comply with pricing and availability sections of the Benchmark Standards.
