 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_division, cc_company).

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Facilities managers determine the size, layout, and location of call centers (cc_sq_ft, cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_city).
- IT staff configure call center systems, including hardware, software, and network infrastructure (cc_hours, cc_manager, cc_mkt_id, cc_division).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Agents, Supervisors

Activity:
- Call center agents handle customer inquiries, process orders, and manage returns (cc_call_center_sk, cr_returned_date_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Supervisors monitor agent performance, provide training, and address customer complaints (cc_open_date_sk, cc_closed_date_sk).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing managers create and update marketing descriptions for each department (cc_mkt_desc).

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders, confirm availability, and schedule delivery (cr_order_number, cr_ship_mode_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).
- Warehouse staff pick, pack, and ship ordered items to the customer's specified address (cr_returning_addr_sk).

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer returns, record return reasons, and issue refunds (cr_reason_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect for damage, and update inventory accordingly (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returning_addr_sk, cr_item_sk).

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record financial transactions, including sales, returns, and expenses (cc_tax_percentage).
- They generate reports on revenue, costs, and profitability for management review and decision making (not specific column/table references provided in the source materials).
