 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information such as names, addresses, and employment status (cc_name, cc_street_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls and assist customers with inquiries or issues (cc_call_center_id).
- Market managers oversee call center operations within their designated market segments (cc_mkt_id, cc_market_manager).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog with details like product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold (not specified in provided schema).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by selecting appropriate items from the catalog and determining shipping methods (cr_item_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk).
- Warehouse staff prepare orders for shipment and update inventory levels accordingly (not specified in provided schema).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return dates, times, reasons, and customer information (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_reason_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records tax percentages for each call center location (cc_tax_percentage).
- Finance department generates financial reports based on the data collected from various business processes (not specified in provided schema).
