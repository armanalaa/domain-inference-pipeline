 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers define the call center's structure by assigning roles, divisions, and responsibilities.
- HR managers recruit and hire new employees for various positions within the call center.
- Call center managers determine the number of employees needed based on the call center's size and workload.
- HR managers train new hires on call center policies, procedures, and systems.
- Call center managers assign employees to specific shifts based on their skills and availability.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Manager

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers during their assigned shifts.
- Call center employees assist customers with inquiries about products or services.
- Call center employees record customer interactions, including call duration, reason for call, and resolution provided.
- Call center managers monitor employee performance to ensure adherence to service level agreements.
- Call center employees escalate complex issues to their supervisors when necessary.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by providing product details such as name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers create and update catalog pages to showcase products and services.
- Product managers ensure that all product information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Marketing managers organize catalog pages into departments for easy navigation.
- Product managers collaborate with marketing teams to promote new or popular items.

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details and reasons.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items from customers and update their status in the system.
- Customer service representatives issue refunds to customers based on the return reason and item condition.
- Warehouse staff inspect returned items for damage or defects before processing the refund.
- Warehouse staff restock acceptable returns into inventory when applicable.
