 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Pre-Publication Board Appointment and Management

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, Pre-Publication Board Members

Activity:
- The Benchmark Subcommittee appoints Pre-Publication Board members for a six-month term (call_center).
- Pre-Publication Board members are selected based on their prior familiarity and extensive knowledge of the TPC benchmark.
- A substitute member is appointed if necessary, to serve temporarily or for the duration of the current term.
- Contact information for the Pre-Publication Board is posted on the TPC website (call_center).

## 2. Pre-Publication Board Certification Process

Performed By: Pre-Publiculation Board Members, Steering Committee (if needed)

Activity:
- The Pre-Publication Board reviews Results for compliance with the Benchmark Standard within ten business days.
- If a certification decision has not been reached within the allotted time, the chairperson requests involvement of the Steering Committee.
- The Steering Committee resolves the certification decision within five business days if needed.

## 3. Result Confidentiality Maintenance

Performed By: Pre-Publication Board Members

Activity:
- Results are confidential to the Pre-Publication Board until published.
- Pre-Publication Board members may not use knowledge gained during the review of a Result until it is public.

## 4. Conflict Resolution

Performed By: Pre-Publication Board, Test Sponsor, TAB (if needed)

Activity:
- A Test Sponsor cannot sit in review of their own publication and must recuse themselves from the Pre-Publication Board.
- Conflict resolution between the Pre-Publication Board and the Test Sponsor is handled by the TAB if necessary.

## 5. Result Publication Approval

Performed By: Pre-Publication Board Chairperson

Activity:
- The chairperson generates the approval report and handles communications upon successful certification of a Result.

## 6. Pre-Publication Board Member Qualifications Evaluation

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee

Activity:
- The Benchmark Subcommittee ensures an adequate number of board members are available to provide coverage in a timely manner.
- The Benchmark Subcommittee certifies those individuals who best meet the criteria for prior familiarity and extensive knowledge, as well as adequate certification coverage.

## 7. Result Submission Notification

Performed By: Pre-Publication Board Chairperson

Activity:
- Upon receiving a Result submission, the chairperson notifies the Test Sponsor and the Steering Committee of its status.
