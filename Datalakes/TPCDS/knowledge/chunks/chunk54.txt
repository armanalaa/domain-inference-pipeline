 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Web Content Management

Performed By: Webmaster, Content Creators

Activity:
- Webmasters ensure content is accessible in generic HTML formats for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge (call_center).
- Content creators develop and post documents on the TPC's public website in a preferred non-HTML format such as Adobe PDF (Portable Document Format) when necessary (call_center).

## 2. Privacy Policy Management

Performed By: Privacy Officer, Webmaster

Activity:
- Privacy Officer maintains and updates the TPC's privacy policy to address questions related to information collection, usage, sharing, unsubscribe, data removal, correction, and update (call_center).
- Webmasters publish the updated privacy policy on the TPC's website for public access (call_center).

## 3. User Data Collection

Performed By: Users, Privacy Officer

Activity:
- Users voluntarily provide personal information such as name, fax, telephone number, street address, and email address when interacting with the TPC's site for feedback or report requests (call_center).

## 4. User Data Processing and Use

Performed By: Privacy Officer, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Privacy Officer collects, processes, and uses personal information only for providing relevant services to users (call_center).
- Data analysts analyze the collected data for various purposes such as feedback analysis or report generation.

## 5. User Data Sharing

Performed By: Privacy Officer, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Privacy Officer shares user data with third parties only with the user's knowledge and approval (call_center).

## 6. User Data Access and Modification

Performed By: Users, Privacy Officer

Activity:
- Users can request to review, delete, correct, or update their personal information that they have previously provided (call_center).
- Privacy Officer assists users in accessing and modifying their personal data as requested.

## 7. User Data Removal

Performed By: Privacy Officer

Activity:
- Upon user request, Privacy Officer deletes or deactivates the user's name from the TPC's database (call_center).

## 8. User Support and Inquiry Handling

Performed By: Customer Support, Privacy Officer

Activity:
- Users can contact customer support for assistance with various issues related to the TPC's site or services (call_center).
- Privacy Officer addresses concerns regarding the collecting and/or use of personal information or privacy policy inquiries (call_center).
