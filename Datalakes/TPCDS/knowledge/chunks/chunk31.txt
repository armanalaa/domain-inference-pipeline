 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management
Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by specifying the required skills and qualifications.
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing necessary training and equipment.
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability.

## 2. Call Center Operations
Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers.
- Sales representatives assist customers with inquiries about products or services.
- Call center employees record customer interactions, including call duration and outcome.

## 3. Catalog Management
Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by providing product details such as name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers create and update catalog pages to display products in various categories.

## 4. Customer Returns
Performed By: Customers, Call Center Employees, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns by contacting the call center.
- Call center employees process return requests by recording customer information and reason for return.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update the inventory accordingly.

## 5. Policy Compliance and Violation Handling
Performed By: Test Sponsor, TAB (Technical Advisory Board), Council

Activity:
- The Test Sponsor conducts tests to ensure compliance with TPC policies.
- If the Test Sponsor fails to meet deadlines or if the TAB is dissatisfied with corrections, the TAB recommends a Level 3 policy violation.
- The Council votes on the TAB's recommendation regarding non-compliance and policy violation.

## 6. Immediate Withdrawal of Results
Performed By: TAB, SC (Steering Committee), Administrator

Activity:
- If a result is found to be non-compliant, the TAB may recommend immediate withdrawal of the result.
- The TAB's recommendation for immediate withdrawal is forwarded to the SC for approval.
- If both the TAB and SC approve the immediate withdrawal, the Administrator implements the withdrawal.

## 7. Public Relations Committee Operations
Performed By: PRC (Public Relations Committee) Members, TPC Administrator

Activity:
- The PRC promotes the TPC, its charter, and activities in the public arena.
- The PRC encourages use of TPC Benchmark Standards.
- The PRC works with the press for the advancement of the TPC and its public image.

## 8. Technical Subcommittee Operations
Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee Members, Administrator, Spokesperson

Activity:
- A Benchmark Subcommittee develops and maintains Benchmark Standards, Umbrella Specifications, or TPC Policies.
