 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and performance metrics (cc_name, cc_company_name, cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Call center managers determine the number of employees needed for each shift and adjust staffing levels accordingly (cc_employees, cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming customer calls and provide assistance with inquiries or issues (cc_call_center_id).
- Representatives record call details such as start and end dates, duration, and customer information (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date, cc_closed_date_sk, cc_open_date_sk, cr_returning_customer_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Representatives offer promotions or upsell products to customers based on their preferences and purchase history (cc_mkt_id, cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by entering product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing managers create promotional pages for specific departments or products in the catalog (cp_department, cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by selecting items from the catalog and entering order details (cr_order_number).
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory and prepare them for shipping (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer returns by recording return details such as returned date, time, item SKU, and reason (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for damage or defects, and update their status in the system (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records transactional data such as sales, returns, and expenses to generate financial reports (cc_tax_percentage).

## 7. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance by analyzing key metrics like average handling time, service level, and sales per representative (cc_hours, cc_tax_percentage).
- HR managers review employee performance data to identify training needs or potential issues (cc_employees).
