 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal information and qualifications (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers and provide assistance (cc_rec_start_date).
- Call center managers monitor employee performance and adjust schedules as needed (cc_hours).

## 3. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets based on geographical location or market segment (cc_mkt_id, cc_division).
- Call center managers update call center information related to the assigned market and division (cc_mkt_class, cc_division_name).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- IT staff maintain the catalog database to ensure data integrity and availability (not specified in provided schema).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk, etc.).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels and process returned items accordingly (not specified in provided schema).

## 6. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts extract and analyze data from the call center and catalog databases to generate reports on performance metrics (not specified in provided schema).
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about operations and strategy (not specified in provided schema).
