 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers define the call center's structure by specifying its name, class, employees, square footage, hours of operation, and manager.
- HR managers recruit new employees for the call center by recording their personal details such as street number, street name, city, county, state, zip code, country, and GMT offset.
- Call center managers assign market managers to specific markets based on market ID, market class, and market description.

## 2. Market Segmentation

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize customers into different divisions based on the division name and company name.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels by updating the quantity of each item in stock.

## 4. Catalog Page Management

Performed By: Web Administrators

Activity:
- Web administrators create and manage catalog pages by assigning a department, catalog number, and catalog page number to each page.

## 5. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in navigating the catalog pages and placing orders.
- Customers browse through the catalog pages, select items they wish to purchase, and place orders by providing their contact information.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Finance Department

Activity:
- Warehouse staff process incoming orders by picking the selected items from inventory and preparing them for shipping.
- Finance department records each order in the system by recording the order number, customer details, item details, and transaction date.

## 7. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer returns by recording the returned date and time, item SKU, refunded customer SKU, reason for return, and shipping mode.

## 8. Performance Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts

Activity:
- Data analysts analyze sales performance by accessing order details, customer details, item details, and transaction dates from the database.
