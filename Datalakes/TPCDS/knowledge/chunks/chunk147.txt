 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and the call center's needs (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and performance evaluations (cc_name, cc_company, cc_division, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls and assist customers with their inquiries or issues (cc_call_center_id).
- Market managers oversee call center operations, monitor performance metrics, and make decisions to optimize service quality (cc_hours, cc_tax_percentage).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by providing product details such as name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_description, etc.).
- Marketing managers update marketing information for each call center, including market class, description, and manager (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process new orders by entering customer details, item selections, and shipping information (cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk, etc.).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and fulfill orders by selecting appropriate warehouses for each order line (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return reasons, refunded customer details, and shipping modes (cr_reason_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk).

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Staff

Activity:
- Finance staff record payments by entering payment transaction details, customer selections, and remote warehouse numbers (cr_order_number, cr_returning_addr_sk, etc.).
