 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal information, qualifications, and performance evaluations (cc_name, cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers determine the market class and division for each call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_division, cc_division_name).
- Call center managers update the call center's market information accordingly (cc_mkt_id, cc_market_manager).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming calls from customers and provide assistance (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date).
- Customers place orders or inquire about products through the call centers (cr_item_sk, cr_order_number).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_page_id, cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new items are added or sold (cr_item_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns initiated by customers (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels and record returned items (cr_item_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records tax percentage for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).
- Finance department generates financial reports based on call center performance data (all columns in call_center table).
