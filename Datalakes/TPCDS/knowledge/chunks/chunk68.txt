 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific call centers based on capacity and demand (cc_employees).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center (cc_sq_ft).
- Call center managers determine the operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Allocation

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers collaborate with HR managers to distribute call centers across different markets (cc_mkt_id).
- HR managers assign employees to specific divisions within each market (cc_division).

## 3. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers and market managers categorize call centers based on their class and division (cc_class, cc_division_name).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 5. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive returned items from customers and record the return date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Call center representatives handle customer inquiries related to returns (cr_call_center_sk).

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process orders and record the order number (cr_order_number).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for each processed order (cr_warehouse_sk).
