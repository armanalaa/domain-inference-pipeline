 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. General Meeting Management

Performed By: Council Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Council members propose new motions for General Meetings.
- Council members vote on proposed motions according to voting rules.
- Council authorizes Mail Ballots for specific decisions requiring them.
- Administrator distributes Mail Ballots to eligible Directors within seven days.
- Administrator posts Mail Ballots on the Private Web Site.

## 2. Mail Ballot Voting

Performed By: TPC Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Eligible TPC members cast their votes on Mail Ballots via written form or email.
- Administrator collects and tallies votes to determine ballot outcome.
- Administrator notifies Council of the Mail Ballot results.

## 3. Bylaws Amendment Proposal

Performed By: Council Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Council members propose amendments to the Bylaws.
- Council votes on proposed Bylaws amendments according to voting rules.
- If approved, the Administrator implements the amended Bylaws.

## 4. Member Expulsion

Performed By: Council Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Council members propose expulsion of a member due to policy violations or non-compliance.
- Council votes on proposed expulsion according to voting rules.
- If approved, the Administrator implements the member's expulsion.

## 5. Standard Adoption and Revision

Performed By: Council Members, Administrator

Activity:
- Council members propose new TPC-Enterprise Benchmark Standards or revisions to existing ones.
- Council votes on proposed standards or revisions according to voting rules.
- If approved, the Administrator implements the new or revised standard.

## 6. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call center staff to specific markets and classes.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and ship products to customers as needed.
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and process orders.

## 7. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price.
- Sales representatives update catalog pages with current information about products.
