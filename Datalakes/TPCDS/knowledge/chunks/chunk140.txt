 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on the number of employees and sq ft available (call_center).
- HR managers update employee details in the call_center table for newly hired staff.

## 2. Market Division Allocation

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers determine market class and division for each call center (call_center).
- Call center managers update the division name and company name in the call_center table according to the assigned market division.

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Technicians

Activity:
- Warehouse staff set up physical space for new call centers by providing the required sq ft (call_center).
- IT technicians configure call center systems and equipment, updating the hours and gmt_offset in the call_center table.

## 4. Call Center Operations Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center operations to ensure adherence to hours (call_center).
- Sales representatives handle customer calls and update call records in the call_center table.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (catalog_page).
- IT staff update the catalog_page table with the newly added items.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording returned date, time, and reason (catalog_returns).
- Warehouse staff update warehouse stock levels based on returned items (catalog_returns).

## 7. Report Generation

Performed By: IT Staff, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- IT staff generate Full Disclosure reports according to the TPC specifications (not specified tables/columns).
- Call center managers review and verify the accuracy of the generated reports.
