 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Software Revision Management

Performed By: Subcommittee, Code Maintenance Team (if exists)

Activity:
- Propose changes to the TPC-Provided Software based on problem reports (Procedures § 1.1).
- Test proposed changes to ensure they address the associated problems and introduce no new ones (Procedures § 1.4).
- Gather feedback from at least one member company or community member in an end-to-end environment (Procedures § 1.4.5).
- Document testing details in subcommittee meeting minutes.
- Resolve any reported issues to the satisfaction of the subcommittee.
- Vote on the release of an official revision of the TPC-Provided Software (Policies § Error! Reference source not found.).

## 2. Open Source Development Management

Performed By: Subcommittee, Code Maintenance Team (if exists)

Activity:
- Collect approved code changes for inclusion in a revision (Procedures § 1.2).
- Optionally produce a "beta" revision with sufficient lead time for testing and integration.
- Test proposed changes to ensure they address associated problems and introduce no new ones (Procedures § 1.4).
- Gather feedback from at least one member company or community member in an end-to-end environment (Procedures § 1.4.5).
- Resolve any reported issues to the satisfaction of the sponsoring subcommittee and/or Code Maintenance Team.
- Vote on the release of an official revision of the TPC-Provided Software (Policies § Error! Reference source not found.).
