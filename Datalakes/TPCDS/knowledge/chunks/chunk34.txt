 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new staff by recording their details, including name, qualifications, and employment history.
- HR managers onboard new employees by providing them with training materials, work schedules, and company policies.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facility managers allocate space for call centers based on the number of employees and required square footage.
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary equipment such as phones, headsets, and computers.

## 3. Call Center Operation

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers, addressing their inquiries or complaints.
- Call center managers monitor employee performance, providing feedback and coaching to improve service quality.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers create and update catalog pages, including descriptions and promotional content.

## 5. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return dates, times, and reasons.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update inventory accordingly.

## 6. Voting Process

Performed By: Technical Subcommittee Members, Technical Subcommittee Chairperson

Activity:
- Technical subcommittee members present work for credit towards attendance time.
- The technical subcommittee chairperson credits the outside work hours and resolves any disagreements through a vote.

## 7. Voting Eligibility Determination

Performed By: Technical Subcommittee Chairperson

Activity:
- The technical subcommittee chairperson determines voting eligibility based on attendance, meeting frequency, and membership status.

## 8. Suspension of Voting Rights

Performed By: Technical Subcommittee Chairperson

Activity:
- The technical subcommittee chairperson suspends a member's voting rights if they are not in compliance with the policies.

## 9. Reinstatement of Voting Rights

Performed By: Technical Subcommittee Chairperson

Activity:
- The technical subcommittee chairperson reinstates a member’s voting rights when their attendance is in compliance with the policies.

## 10. Membership Termination

Performed By: Technical Subcommittee Chairperson, Council

Activity:
- If a member's attendance falls below 20%, the subcommittee chairperson notifies the Council, leading to involuntary termination of membership.
- A member can voluntarily terminate their membership by notifying the technical subcommittee chairperson and the TPC Administrator in writing.
