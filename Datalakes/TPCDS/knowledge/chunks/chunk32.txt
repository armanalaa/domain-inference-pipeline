 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Benchmark Standard Management

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee

Activity:
- Develop and maintain the TPC-Provided Software required by a Benchmark Standard.
- Create and update an auditor exam for each Benchmark Standard.
- Conduct work on behalf of proposed or completed Benchmark Standards until disbanded by the Council.
- Maintain minimum membership of at least six Members or Committee Members.
- Elect a chairperson by Simple Majority of the Council, with a run-off if necessary.

## 2. Working Group Creation and Management

Performed By: Council

Activity:
- Create technical subcommittees (working groups) to complete specific tasks.
- Set a specific time for working groups to report their findings to the Council.
- Disband working groups once they have completed their task.
- Ensure minimum membership of at least four Members or Committee Members on a working group.
- Elect a chairperson by Simple Majority of the Council for each working group.

## 3. Benchmark Standard Approval Process

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, TPC Council

Activity:
- Own and drive the Specification to become an approved TPC Benchmark Standard.
- Handle suggestions for clarifying the Benchmark Standard in the next revision.
- Request rulings on compliance issues from the TAB.
- Make recommendations to the Council to change the Benchmark Standard.
- Create a new version of a Specification and submit it to the Council for approval as a Benchmark Standard.

## 4. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Register new call centers with details such as name, location, size, hours, and market information.
- Assign call center managers to oversee operations.
- Track call center performance metrics like open dates, closed dates, and employee counts.
- Allocate call centers to divisions and markets based on their class and description.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price.
- Organize items into departments within the catalog.
- Maintain a record of catalog pages for each item.

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Initiate returns by customers due to various reasons (e.g., defective items).
- Record return details like returned date, time, and reason.
- Refund customers based on their customer demographic, home demographic, address, or order number.
- Track warehouse inventory levels after processing returns.
