 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Benchmark Standard Approval Process

Performed By: TPC Executive Committee (TPC EC)

Activity:
- TPC EC members review and discuss proposed TPC-Express Benchmark Standards or revisions.
- TPC EC votes on approval of a TPC-Express Benchmark Standard or Major Revision.
- TPC EC votes on approval of TPC-Express Benchmark Standard Minor Revision.
- TPC EC votes on approval of TPC-Express Benchmark Standard Third Tier Revision.
- TPC EC declares obsolescence of TPC-Express Benchmark Standards when necessary.

## 2. TPC-Provided Software Approval Process

Performed By: TPC EC, Benchmark Subcommittee

Activity:
- Software proposals are submitted to the TPC EC for review.
- Proposals are assigned to a Benchmark Subcommittee for evaluation.
- The status and direction of the software proposal are determined by the Benchmark Subcommittee.
- Authorization is granted for the public release of draft TPC-Provided Software.
- Formal review of the TPC-Provided Software takes place.
- Final approval of the TPC-Provided Software is given by the TPC EC.

## 3. Benchmark Submission Process

Performed By: Benchmark Participants

Activity:
- Benchmark participants submit their results for a TPC-Express benchmark.
- Full disclosure reports are prepared and submitted according to TPC-Express rules.
- Pre-publication certification is obtained before the results are published.
- Alert messages are sent to notify about the availability of new TPC-Express Results.

## 4. Benchmark Review Period Management

Performed By: TPC EC, Benchmark Subcommittee

Activity:
- The TPC EC and Benchmark Subcommittee oversee the review period for each TPC-Express benchmark.
- They ensure compliance with TPC-Express Benchmark Fair Use Rules during this period.

## 5. TPC-Provided Software Development Process

Performed By: TPC-DS Developers

Activity:
- General guidelines are followed for the development of TPC-Provided Software.
