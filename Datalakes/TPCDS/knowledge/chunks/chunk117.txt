 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific market managers based on the market's classification and description.
- HR managers recruit and hire warehouse staff for the assigned call centers, recording their details in the employee table.
- Call center managers determine the square footage required for each call center and update the corresponding column in the call_center table.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives answer customer calls and guide them through the product catalog, providing details such as product name, category, and list price.
- Call center employees track call duration and handle customer complaints or inquiries, updating the call_center table accordingly.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders for specific products from the catalog through call center representatives.

## 5. Delivery Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Delivery Personnel

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve the ordered items and prepare them for delivery.
- Delivery personnel transport the ordered items to the customer's specified address.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Customers return products that are unsatisfactory or no longer needed, and call center employees record these returns in the catalog_returns table.

## 7. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records all transactions, including sales, returns, and refunds, to maintain accurate financial data for the company.
