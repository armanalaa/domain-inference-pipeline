 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign specific divisions to each call center (cc_division).
- Market managers determine the market class and description for a call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- HR managers recruit and hire new employees for the call center (cc_employees).
- Call center managers set the operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).
- HR managers assign a manager to oversee each call center (cc_manager).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives answer incoming calls from customers (cc_rec_start_date).
- Sales representatives assist customers in navigating the catalog pages (cp_catalog_page_number).
- Call center employees record customer orders, including product details and shipping information (cr_item_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).
- Call center employees update the call center's open and closed dates (cc_open_date_sk, cc_closed_date_sk).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with their respective codes, names, categories, and list prices (cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing managers categorize each item based on market class (cc_mkt_id).

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the return date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for returned items (cr_warehouse_sk).
- Customer service representatives determine the reason for each return (cr_reason_sk).

## 5. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records the tax percentage for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).
