 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Customer Service Managers

Activity:
- Handle customer inquiries about order status by accessing the call history (cc_call_center_sk).
- Register new orders by recording customer details, order items, and payment information.
- Update order status when an order is shipped or cancelled, using the call center table.

## 2. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Verify stock availability for each order line item in the warehouse district (W_ID).
- If an item is out of stock, locate it in another warehouse and update its status accordingly.
- Pack and prepare orders for shipping based on the order details (order_number) and shipment mode (ship_mode_sk).

## 3. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Finance Department

Activity:
- Record customer payments and update their account balances (C_BALANCE).
- Issue credit to customers for returned items, updating the refunded customer's balance and the return details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).

## 4. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Inventory Analysts

Activity:
- Monitor stock levels for each item in the warehouse district (W_ID).
- Update stock quantities when items are received or shipped, using the stock table.
- Identify potential supply shortages and initiate reordering processes based on low inventory thresholds.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Maintain product information in the catalog (cp_catalog_page_sk), including product code, name, category, and list price.
- Update product descriptions and categorization as needed to ensure accurate representation of products.

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Process returns by recording the return reason (cr_reason_sk) and updating the returned item's status in the catalog.
- Replace returned items with new stock or update them as necessary based on their condition.

## 7. Market Analysis

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Analyze market data (cc_market_manager, cc_division_name) to identify trends and opportunities for growth.
- Adjust marketing strategies based on the analysis results, including targeting specific customer demographics or product categories.
