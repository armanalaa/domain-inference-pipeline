 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Result Submission and Review Process

Performed By: Test Sponsors, TAB (Technical Advisory Board)

Activity:
- Test Sponsors submit new Results for review to the Web Site upon completion (call_center).
- Upon issuance of a waiver, Problem Reports are opened to describe issues (catalog_page).
- During the Review Period, All Members can submit objections related to compliance with TPC Benchmark Standard (call_center).
- The TAB evaluates challenges following the process described in Policies § 3.3.3 (call_center).
- If no challenge is submitted within the Review Period, the Result is automatically given the status of Accepted (call_center).
- After the Review Period, new challenges must pertain to objections already filed or issues specified in Policies § 6.11.3 (call_center).

## 2. Challenge Evaluation and Resolution Process

Performed By: TAB (Technical Advisory Board)

Activity:
- The TAB meets to discuss challenges that may occur after the Review Period has expired (call_center).
- If a Test Sponsor submits documentation in response to a challenge, it is subject to the standard Review Period (call_center).
- Once all matters are heard by the Council, the Result passes into Accepted status under certain conditions (call_center).

## 3. Result Promotion and Trademark Usage Policy

Performed By: Test Sponsors

Activity:
- Test Sponsors promote Submitted for Review Results in the press (call_center).
- Test Sponsors are required to cite the TPC trademark in their public relations materials (call_center).
