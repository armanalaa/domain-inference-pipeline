 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific call centers based on workload (cc_employees).
- Warehouse staff update call center square footage and hours of operation (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers categorize each call center according to market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).

## 3. General Meeting Preparation

Performed By: TPC Administrator

Activity:
- The TPC Administrator prepares General Meeting materials for distribution within 24 hours of the meeting's conclusion (not specified).

## 4. Collateral Production

Performed By: TPC Administrator

Activity:
- TPC Administrator produces collateral for the PRC, including benchmark status reports and extended benchmark status reports (not specified).

## 5. Auditor Exam Question Development

Performed By: TPC Members

Activity:
- TPC members develop at least 50 questions and answers for auditor exams (not specified).

## 6. Pre-Publication Board Management

Performed By: TPC Members

Activity:
- TPC members form and manage a Pre-Publication Board as needed (Policies § Section 10).

## 7. General Meeting Minutes Recording

Performed By: TPC Administrator

Activity:
- TPC Administrator records and posts the minutes of the General Meetings, including materials presented by Members (not specified).

## 8. Technical Meeting Scheduling

Performed By: Standing Committee/Technical Subcommittee Chairpersons

Activity:
- The chairperson schedules meetings for their respective committees or subcommittees, specifying the meeting format (Policies § 4.1.2).
