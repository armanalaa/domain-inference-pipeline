 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Administrator, Directors

Activity:
- Administrators designate the place for call center meetings based on the current meeting type.
- Directors call meetings to discuss and make decisions about the corporation's operations.
- The Administrator notifies Directors of meetings according to the specified notice requirements.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming calls during designated hours.
- Customers place calls to the call centers for various inquiries or services.

## 3. Call Center Performance Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update call center records when a customer's order is ready for pickup or delivery.
- Call center managers track the performance of individual call centers based on metrics like hours and employee count.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details such as code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers update the catalog page information for each department.

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details like date, time, reason, and ship mode.
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse records when a returned item is received and processed.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- The finance team records tax percentage for each call center location in the database.
