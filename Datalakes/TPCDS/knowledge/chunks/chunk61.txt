 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Auditor Candidate Examination Management

Performed By: TPC Administrator, Benchmark Subcommittee

Activity:
- The TPC Administrator selects questions for the auditor candidate exam from the set maintained by the Benchmark Subcommittee (Policies § 4.1.8.1).
- The SC reviews the exam results and determines if a re-test is necessary (9.3.2.2).
- The TPC Administrator notifies candidates of their exam results and next steps (9.3.2.2, 9.3.3.2).

## 2. Apprenticeship Management

Performed By: SC, Auditors

Activity:
- The SC determines if an audit internship is necessary for the apprentice auditor (9.3.3.1).
- An Auditor provides hands-on experience to the apprentice auditor (9.3.3.1).
- The Auditor submits a written endorsement of the apprentice auditor to the SC (9.3.3.2).

## 3. Certification Process

Performed By: Auditor Certification Board, SC

Activity:
- The ACB reviews the candidate’s credentials and makes a recommendation to the SC (9.3.4.1).
- The ACB schedules an individual interview with each candidate (9.3.4.1).
- The ACB notifies the candidate and the SC of its recommendation within three days of the interview (9.3.4.1).

## 4. Call Center Management

Performed By: TPC Administrator, CC_market_manager, CC_division

Activity:
- The TPC Administrator designates a time and place for in-person exams (Policies § 9.3.2.2).
- Under extenuating circumstances, the TPC Administrator administers the exam remotely (Policies § 9.3.2.2).
- CC_market_manager and CC_division manage call centers for the benchmark (call_center table).

## 5. Exam Question Management

Performed By: Benchmark Subcommittee, TPC Administrator

Activity:
- The Benchmark Subcommittee maintains a set of questions for auditor candidate exams (Policies § 4.1.8.1).
- The TPC Administrator selects questions from the set for each exam (Policies § 4.1.8.1).

## 6. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product managers, catalog_page, catalog_returns

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog (catalog_page table).
- Customers return items and initiate refunds (catalog_returns table).

## 7. Exam Grading

Performed By: TPC Administrator

Activity:
- The TPC Administrator grades the auditor candidate exam based on the maximum score and passing threshold (Policies § 9.3.2.2).
