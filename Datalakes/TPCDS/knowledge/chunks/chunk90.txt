 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by recording their personal details and job roles.
- HR managers update employee records with their qualifications, experience, and training history.

## 2. Warehouse Operations

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive incoming shipments and record item details like SKU, quantity, and condition.
- They track inventory levels and update the database when stock is low or out of stock.

## 3. District Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Market managers assign sales districts to warehouse locations based on demand and geographical coverage.
- Sales representatives register new customers in their assigned districts by recording customer details and contact information.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by entering order details like item SKU, quantity, shipping address, and payment method.
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries about order status, returns, or refunds.

## 5. Inventory Control

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory records when items are shipped to customers or returned.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new stock based on current inventory levels and sales forecasts.

## 6. Financial Management

Performed By: Finance Department, Accounting Staff

Activity:
- Finance department calculates taxes based on the customer's location and records them in the database.
- Accounting staff records financial transactions like sales, returns, and expenses for reporting and analysis purposes.

## 7. Marketing and Promotion

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Web Developers

Activity:
- Marketing managers create promotional campaigns and update catalog pages with new products or offers.
- Web developers maintain the company's website by ensuring it is user-friendly and up-to-date with current promotions.
