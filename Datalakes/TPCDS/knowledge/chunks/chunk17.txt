 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by recording their details such as name, qualifications, and employment history.
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing training materials, work schedules, and company policies.

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific markets based on geographical location and market size.
- Market managers oversee the performance of assigned call centers, providing guidance and resources as needed.

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Technicians

Activity:
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary equipment like phones, headsets, and furniture.
- IT technicians configure and install the necessary software and hardware for efficient call center operations.

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers, addressing their queries or complaints.
- Call center managers monitor employee performance, providing feedback and coaching to improve service quality.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Technicians

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog by adding new items, editing existing ones, and removing discontinued products.
- IT technicians maintain the catalog database, ensuring data integrity and efficient access for call center employees.

## 6. Customer Service Incident Tracking

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Supervisors

Activity:
- Call center employees document customer service incidents, including details like incident type, resolution time, and customer feedback.
- Supervisors review incident reports to identify trends, address recurring issues, and improve overall service quality.

## 7. Performance Evaluation and Reward System

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers evaluate employee performance based on metrics like call handling time, customer satisfaction scores, and sales figures.
- HR managers implement a reward system to motivate employees, offering incentives for exceptional performance and service quality.

## 8. Financial Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Finance Managers, IT Analysts

Activity:
- Finance managers generate financial reports detailing call center revenue, expenses, and profitability.
- IT analysts analyze the data to identify trends, make recommendations for cost reduction, and optimize resource allocation.
