 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Call center managers assign specific call centers to market divisions (cc_mkt_id).
- IT support configures call center devices to handle the specified number of connections (cc_employees).
- Call center managers set operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls and direct them to appropriate departments (cc_call_center_id).
- Call center managers monitor call center performance and adjust staffing levels as needed (cc_employees).
- Call center employees record call details such as start date, end date, and closure details (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date, cc_closed_date_sk).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog with new items by providing product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- IT support maintains the catalog page structure and ensures it is accessible via terminals (cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return date, time, item SKU, and customer details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff retrieve the returned items from inventory and prepare them for shipment back to customers (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process new orders by selecting appropriate items from the catalog, determining shipping mode, and recording order details (cr_order_number, cr_ship_mode_sk).
- Warehouse staff pick and pack the ordered items for shipment to customers (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Support

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels by recording the quantity of each item received or shipped (cr_return_quan).
- IT support maintains the inventory database to ensure accurate tracking of all items in stock (not specified table/column).

## 7. Performance Monitoring and Reporting

Performed By: Call Center Managers, IT Analysts

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance metrics such as average handling time and service level (not specified column/table).
- IT analysts generate reports based on call center data for analysis and decision making (not specified table/column).
