 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Selection for Order Status Inquiry

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives select a customer based on their last name or customer number for order status inquiry.
- They use the non-uniform random value function to determine whether to select a customer by last name or customer number.

## 2. Customer Data Retrieval

Performed By: Database Query Execution

Activity:
- Upon selecting a customer, call center representatives retrieve the customer's balance, first name, middle name, and last name from the CUSTOMER table.

## 3. Order Identification

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives identify the customer's last order based on the retrieved customer details.

## 4. Item Status Checking

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- For each item on the identified order, call center representatives check the delivery date.

## 5. Order Status Inquiry

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Upon checking the status of all items on an order, call center representatives provide the customer with the order's status.

## 6. Customer Service Escalation (if necessary)

Performed By: Call Center Managers

Activity:
- If a customer inquiry cannot be resolved, call center managers escalate the issue to relevant departments for resolution.

## 7. Order Processing and Fulfillment (not detailed in provided source materials)

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Department

Activity:
- Upon receiving an order, warehouse staff pick and pack items, and shipping department prepares the shipment for delivery.

## 8. Inventory Management (not detailed in provided source materials)

Performed By: Inventory Managers

Activity:
- Inventory managers update inventory levels as orders are processed and shipped.

## 9. Financial Transactions Processing (not detailed in provided source materials)

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Upon order fulfillment, finance department processes the financial transactions related to the order.
