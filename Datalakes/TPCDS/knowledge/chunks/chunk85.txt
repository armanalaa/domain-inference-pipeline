 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the company's division and market (cc_division, cc_company).
- HR managers recruit and hire new employees for call center positions, recording their personal details (cc_employees).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives handle customer inquiries and sales calls during the call center's operating hours (cc_hours).
- They record call start and end dates, as well as the reason for the call (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date, cc_class).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers oversee multiple call centers within a specific market and class (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class).
- They assign market managers to each call center (cc_market_manager).

## 4. Call Center Expansion

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers, HR managers, and market managers collaborate to expand the call center by adding more square footage (cc_sq_ft) or hiring additional employees (cc_employees).

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog, providing details such as product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording the returned date and time, item SKU, and refunded customer details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).

## 7. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and ship modes for returned items (cr_ship_mode_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).
