 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and qualifications (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information in the system, including personal details, employment history, and performance records (cc_employees).

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Facilities managers allocate space for new call centers by providing square footage and hours of operation (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).
- IT support configures the call center's hardware and software, ensuring all necessary tools are available for employees (not specified in schema).

## 3. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign each call center to a specific market division based on geographical location or market segment (cc_division).
- Marketing managers define the marketing strategy for each division, including target audience and advertising methods (not specified in schema).

## 4. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Call center managers categorize call centers based on their function or service offered (cc_class).
- IT support ensures the classification is accurately reflected in the system (not specified in schema).

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold (not specified in schema).

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording the returned date and time, item SKU, and customer information (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when a return is processed (not specified in schema).

## 7. Testing and Quality Assurance

Performed By: QA Testers, Developers

Activity:
- QA testers perform platform testing of source code to validate the quality and compliance with coding best practices (not specified in schema).
- Developers conduct unit testing for specific functionality on a routine or method basis (1.4.3 Unit testing).
