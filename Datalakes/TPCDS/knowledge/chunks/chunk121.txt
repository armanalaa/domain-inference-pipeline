 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on staffing needs and skill sets (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information in the system, including personal details, qualifications, and performance records.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Facility managers allocate space for call centers, providing necessary square footage (cc_sq_ft) and equipment.
- IT support configures the call center's infrastructure, setting up systems, networks, and hardware.

## 3. Market Segmentation

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Marketing managers categorize markets based on demographic, geographical, or behavioral factors (cc_mkt_class).
- Data analysts analyze market trends and customer preferences to determine the best products for each segment.

## 4. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Call center managers classify call centers based on their specific functions or services (cc_class).
- IT support updates the system with the new classifications and associated details.

## 5. Market Description Update

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Marketing managers update market descriptions to reflect changes in market conditions or customer preferences (cc_mkt_desc).
- Data analysts verify the accuracy of the updated descriptions and make any necessary adjustments.

## 6. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the product catalog, providing details such as product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- IT support updates the system with the new product information.

## 7. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns, recording return details such as returned date and reason (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the inventory system to reflect the returned items (cr_warehouse_sk).
