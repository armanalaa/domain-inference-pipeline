 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Call center managers determine the number of employees needed for each shift and adjust schedules accordingly (cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming customer calls and provide assistance with inquiries or issues (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date, cc_closed_date_sk).
- Representatives record customer details, such as name, contact information, and concerns (cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- They guide customers through the return process by providing instructions on how to return items and request refunds (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers oversee multiple call centers within a specific market segment (cc_mkt_id, cc_market_manager).
- They monitor the performance of each call center and make adjustments to improve efficiency and productivity (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).
- Market managers also ensure that each call center adheres to company policies and guidelines (cc_division, cc_company_name).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog with new items by entering product details like code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- They monitor sales data to identify popular products and adjust inventory levels accordingly (cr_item_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording the returned item details and initiating refunds (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- They update customer records to reflect successful returns and issue refunds (cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Representatives may also coordinate with warehouse staff to arrange for the return of items (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and ensure that sufficient stock is available to meet customer demand (cr_item_sk).
- Purchasing teams order new inventory based on sales data and projected demand (cr_order_number).
- They work with suppliers to negotiate prices, delivery times, and other terms (cr_ship_mode_sk).

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions related to sales, returns, and inventory (cr_refunded_cdemo_sk, cr_refunded_hdemo_sk, cr_returning_customer_sk, cr_returning_cdemo_sk, cr_returning_hdemo_sk, cr_returning_addr_sk).
- They generate reports on revenue, expenses, and profitability for various time periods (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).
- Finance teams also manage tax calculations based on the location of each call center (cc_gmt_offset, cc_tax_percentage).
