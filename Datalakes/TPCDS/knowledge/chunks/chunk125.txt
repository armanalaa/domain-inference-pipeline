 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with unique identifiers and define their operational details such as opening and closing dates, square footage, hours of operation, and market classification.
- Warehouse staff update the call center's location details including street number, name, type, suite number, city, county, state, zip code, country, and GMT offset.

## 2. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Marketing managers create and manage catalog pages, assigning department, catalog number, catalog page number, description, and type.

## 3. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives handle customer calls at the call centers to process orders, provide information, or address inquiries.
- Customers place orders by specifying items of interest, quantities, and shipping preferences.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and fulfill customer orders by selecting appropriate items and updating their status to "shipped".
- Call center representatives update order details with the shipped date, ship mode, warehouse location, and reason for any returns or cancellations.

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record customer returns by noting the returned date and time, item details, and reasons for return.
- Warehouse staff process returns by updating the inventory levels and marking the items as "returned".

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records tax percentages for each call center based on their location.
- Finance department generates financial reports summarizing sales, returns, and profits for each warehouse and time period.
