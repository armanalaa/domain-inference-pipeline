 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal information and qualifications (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls and assist customers according to the call center's class (cc_class).
- Market managers oversee call center operations within their designated market (cc_mkt_id, cc_market_manager).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, etc.).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new items are added to the catalog.

## 4. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders for specific products through the call center (cr_item_sk).
- Sales representatives process customer orders and provide order details (cr_order_number).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service agents record returns from customers (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when processing customer returns (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Rebadging Results

Performed By: Test Sponsors, Reviewers

Activity:
- Test sponsors rebadge existing results by publishing new ones using the same performance tests (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).
- Reviewers ensure that the rebadged result complies with TPC policies and standards.

## 7. Multiple Currency Results

Performed By: Test Sponsors, Reviewers

Activity:
- Test sponsors publish results in multiple currencies by performing currency conversion (cr_ship_mode_sk).
- Reviewers ensure that the results comply with TPC policies and standards.
