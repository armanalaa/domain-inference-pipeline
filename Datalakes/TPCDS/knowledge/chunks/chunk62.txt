 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by specifying the required skills, qualifications, and job description.
- HR managers review applications, conduct interviews, and make hiring decisions based on the call center's needs and applicants' qualifications.
- Call center managers assign newly hired employees to specific call centers according to their skills and availability.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers, providing assistance or addressing inquiries.
- Call center managers monitor employee performance, ensuring adherence to service standards and quality control.
- Call center employees record customer interactions, including call duration, issue resolution, and feedback, for future reference and analysis.

## 3. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets based on geographical location and market needs.
- Call center managers implement strategies tailored to the assigned market, such as language support or local promotions.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by entering product details like name, category, list price, and product code.
- Marketing teams create and update catalog pages, organizing products into departments and categories for easy navigation.

## 5. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Employees, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns by contacting the call center to request a refund or exchange.
- Call center employees process return requests, providing customers with return instructions and tracking numbers.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update the database accordingly.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Finance teams track call center expenses, including salaries, utilities, and marketing costs.
- Call center managers review financial reports to ensure budget adherence and identify areas for cost optimization.
- Finance teams generate invoices for clients based on the call center's services provided and hours worked.
