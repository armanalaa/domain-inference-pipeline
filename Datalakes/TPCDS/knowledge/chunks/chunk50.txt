 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and qualifications (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, employment history, and performance evaluations (cc_name, cc_hours, cc_manager).

## 2. Market Division Allocation

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers determine the appropriate market division for each call center based on factors such as location and customer demographics (cc_mkt_id, cc_division).
- Marketing managers create marketing strategies tailored to specific market divisions, considering factors like target audience, product offerings, and competition (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Staff, IT Support

Activity:
- Facilities staff arrange for the setup of call centers, including acquiring necessary space, furniture, and equipment (cc_sq_ft, cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_suite_number).
- IT support configures call center systems, ensuring they are compatible with the company's infrastructure and equipped to handle call volume efficiently (not specified in provided schema).

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming calls from customers, addressing their queries or concerns regarding products, services, or orders (not specified in provided schema).
- Call center representatives record call details, including the customer's contact information and the nature of the call, for future reference (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date, cc_closed_date_sk).

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by providing product details such as code, name, category, and list price (not specified in provided schema).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new items are added or existing ones are sold, ensuring accurate records of available stock (not specified in provided schema).

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns initiated by customers, recording return details such as the reason for return and the returned item's SKU (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels to reflect the returned items and prepare them for restocking or disposal (not specified in provided schema).

## 7. Result Submission and Upgrading

Performed By: Test Sponsors, TPC Council

Activity:
- Test sponsors submit new results for benchmark tests, indicating the version of the Benchmark Standard with which the result complies (not specified in provided schema).
- The TPC Council reviews submitted results and determines whether they meet the requirements of the specified Benchmark Standard (not specified in provided schema).
