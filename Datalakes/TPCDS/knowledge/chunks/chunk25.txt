 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Standing Committee Elections

Performed By: Council Members, Nominees

Activity:
- Council members nominate candidates for Standing Committees (SC, TAB, PRC).
- Nominees campaign for election by submitting their profiles and statements.
- Council members cast votes in a closed ballot to elect representatives for each committee.
- The candidate with the most votes is chosen as the representative, ensuring a Simple Majority.
- In case of a run-off, the candidate with the fewest votes is eliminated and voting is repeated.

## 2. Standing Committee Chairperson Election

Performed By: Council Members, Nominees

Activity:
- Council members nominate candidates for Standing Committee chairpersons (SC, TAB, PRC).
- The candidate with the most votes in a closed ballot is chosen as the chairperson, ensuring a Simple Majority.
- In case of a run-off, the candidate with the fewest votes is eliminated and voting is repeated.
- If there is a single nominee for the chairperson position, they may be elected by acclamation.

## 3. Standing Committee Membership Management

Performed By: Council Members, Standing Committee Members

Activity:
- Council members determine membership on all Standing Committees based on individuals affiliated with Member companies.
- A Member company can only have one seat on a Standing Committee, with only one individual representing that company.
- An individual can belong to multiple Standing Committees concurrently.
- If an individual's affiliation with a Member terminates, their position on the committee becomes vacant and is filled through a new election at the next General Meeting.

## 4. Standing Committee Attendance Tracking

Performed By: Standing Committee Chairperson, Members

Activity:
- The Standing Committee chairperson maintains attendance records for meetings in ½ hour increments.
- To encourage participation, the chairperson may require a Member be present for the majority of the ½ hour to be credited for the time.
- Attendance is recorded for both face-to-face meetings and video conferences.

## 5. Standing Committee Vacancy Filling

Performed By: Council Members, New Nominees

Activity:
- In case of a vacancy on a Standing Committee due to attendance requirements or other reasons, the resulting vacancy is filled through a new election at the General Meeting.
- New nominees campaign for election by submitting their profiles and statements.
- Council members cast votes in a closed ballot to elect representatives for the vacant position.

## 6. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call center employees and allocate resources based on market needs.
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and process orders through the call center system.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and ship products to customers as orders are processed.

## 7. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details such as name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers create and manage catalog pages to display products for customers.
