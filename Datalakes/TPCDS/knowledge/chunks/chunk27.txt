 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Corporate Governance and Legal Compliance

Performed By: Legal Team, Administrator

Activity:
- Legal team reviews all legal agreements between members and the TPC. (call_center.cc_mkt_id)
- Legal team reviews all legal agreements between the TPC and other outside organizations. (N/A)
- The Administrator compiles accounting reviews and reports, including preparing and submitting tax returns. (N/A)
- The Administrator publishes financial reports and compiles financial statements of corporate income and expenses. (N/A)

## 2. Public Relations and Communications

Performed By: TPC Spokesperson, Administrator

Activity:
- The TPC Spokesperson serves as the primary contact with the press concerning TPC public relations. (N/A)
- The TPC Spokesperson coordinates with the Steering Committee and the PRC. (N/A)

## 3. Business Administration and Management

Performed By: Administrator, SC Members

Activity:
- The Administrator is the primary contact for business within the TPC. (N/A)
- The SC maintains a running list of issues for SC consideration. (N/A)
- Each SC meeting has minutes posted to the Private Web Site. (N/A)
- SC decisions and/or actions that require Council approval are posted to the Private Web Site at least ten working days prior to presentation at a General Meeting. (N/A)

## 4. Steering Committee Meetings Management

Performed By: SC Members, Administrator

Activity:
- All SC meetings are open to Members. (N/A)
- The agenda is posted to the Private Web Site. (N/A)
- The SC may declare a meeting, or portion of a meeting, to be a closed session when discussing sensitive topics. (N/A)
- Minutes capture actions taken and decisions reached in SC meetings. (N/A)

## 5. Issue Tracking and Resolution

Performed By: SC Members, Administrator

Activity:
- The SC maintains a running list of issues for SC consideration. (N/A)
- Some issues will require resolution within the two weeks between an announced agenda and the next General Meeting. (N/A)

## 6. Call Center Operations Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers oversee call center operations, including managing employees and facilities. (call_center.cc_manager)
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and process orders. (N/A)
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and fulfill orders. (N/A)

## 7. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details. (catalog_page.cp_catalog_number, catalog_page.cp_description, catalog_page.cp_type)
- Marketing managers categorize and describe products for easier navigation on the website. (catalog_page.cp_department, catalog_page.cp_catalog_page_number)

## 8. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns and refunds. (catalog_returns.cr_returned_date_sk, catalog_returns.cr_refunded_customer_sk)
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels after processing a return. (N/A)
