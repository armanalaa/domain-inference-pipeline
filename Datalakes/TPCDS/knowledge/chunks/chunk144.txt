 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and qualifications (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, employment history, and performance metrics (cc_employees).

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Facility managers allocate square footage for call centers and set up necessary infrastructure (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).
- IT support configures call center systems, including phone lines, software applications, and security measures.

## 3. Market Allocation

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Division Heads

Activity:
- Marketing managers determine market segments to target based on demographic, geographical, and customer behavior data (cc_mkt_id, cc_division).
- Division heads assign call centers to specific markets for optimal service coverage.

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Agents, Supervisors

Activity:
- Call center agents answer incoming calls from customers and assist with inquiries or issues (cc_call_center_id).
- Supervisors monitor agent performance, provide coaching, and escalate complex customer concerns.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Call Center Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center agents record new orders from customers, including item details, shipping information, and payment methods (cr_item_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk).
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items, pack them for shipment, and update order status accordingly.

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Call Center Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center agents process customer returns by recording return reasons and item details (cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for quality, and update inventory accordingly.

## 7. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: IT Administrators, Management

Activity:
- IT administrators monitor call center performance metrics, such as response times, throughput, and think time (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date).
- Management reviews these metrics to identify areas for improvement and adjust resource allocation.

## 8. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on call center performance, including transaction mix, order volume, and customer demographics (cp_catalog_page_sk).
- Management uses these reports to make informed decisions about staffing, marketing strategies, and operational efficiency.
