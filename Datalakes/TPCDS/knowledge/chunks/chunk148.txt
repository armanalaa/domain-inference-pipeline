 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the current staffing levels and skill requirements (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_division, cc_company).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming customer calls and assist with inquiries or troubleshoot issues (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date, cc_open_date_sk).
- They record call details such as the customer's name, contact information, and issue description (cc_name, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog with new items by providing product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by entering order details such as item selection and shipping information (O_ALL_LOCAL, S_YTD).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for order fulfillment (cr_item_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date and time, reason for return, and refund details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department records customer payments according to a non-uniform random distribution (C_PAYMENT_CNT).

## 7. Performance Monitoring and Reporting

Performed By: IT Administrators, Auditors

Activity:
- IT administrators monitor system performance and response times during benchmark runs (9.2.6.1, 9.2.6.2, 9.2.6.3).
- Auditors verify compliance with the TPC Benchmark™ C standards by querying various attributes (O_ALL_LOCAL, C_PAYMENT_CNT, S_YTD, S_ORDER_CNT, S_REMOTE_CNT).
