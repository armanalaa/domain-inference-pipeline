 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific markets based on the market's classification and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Market managers determine the appropriate division for each call center within their jurisdiction (cc_division).
- Call center managers set up new call centers by providing details such as name, square footage, operating hours, and manager information (cc_name, cc_sq_ft, cc_hours, cc_manager).

## 2. Market Division Allocation

Performed By: Market Managers

Activity:
- Market managers allocate call centers to specific divisions based on strategic considerations (cc_division).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Manager

Activity:
- Call center employees handle customer inquiries and sales calls during the specified operating hours (cc_hours).
- Call center manager monitors call center performance and makes adjustments as needed to ensure efficient operations.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 5. Catalog Page Maintenance

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers update or remove catalog pages as needed to reflect changes in product offerings (cp_start_date_sk, cp_end_date_sk).

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date and time, item SKU, refunded customer information, and reason for return (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels after processing a return (cr_quan).

## 7. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process orders by selecting the appropriate catalog page and shipping mode (cp_catalog_page_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk).
- Warehouse staff pick and pack items for shipment, update inventory levels, and generate shipping labels (cr_warehouse_sk).
