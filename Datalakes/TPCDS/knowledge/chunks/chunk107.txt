 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers based on market requirements (cc_market_manager).
- HR managers recruit and hire employees for the call centers (cc_employees).

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Management, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facilities management allocates square footage for new call centers (cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary resources (not specified in the schema).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming customer calls during operational hours (cc_hours).
- Sales representatives assist customers with product inquiries and process orders (not specified in the schema).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details like code, name, category, list price (catalog_page).
- Marketing managers categorize and describe each catalog page for easy navigation (catalog_page).

## 5. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives register customer returns by recording return details like date, time, item SKU, refunded customer information (catalog_returns).
- Warehouse staff track returned items and prepare them for restocking or shipping back to the vendor.
