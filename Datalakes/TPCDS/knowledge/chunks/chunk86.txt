 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information, including personal details and employment history (cc_name, cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Division Managers

Activity:
- Market managers define market class and description for each call center (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Division managers organize call centers into divisions according to geographical or operational criteria (cc_division, cc_division_name).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming customer calls and provide assistance (cc_call_center_id).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and handle product inquiries (cc_warehouse_sk).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with their codes, names, categories, and list prices (cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Marketing teams update product descriptions and categorization to reflect changes or promotions (cp_catalog_page_sk).

## 5. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives assist customers in navigating the catalog pages (cp_catalog_page_number).
- Customers place orders for specific items through the call center (cr_item_sk).

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns and record return reasons (cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels after processing a returned item (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 7. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Finance Teams, Management

Activity:
- Finance teams record financial transactions, including tax percentages, for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).
- Management reviews performance metrics to make informed business decisions (performance metrics defined in Clause 5).
