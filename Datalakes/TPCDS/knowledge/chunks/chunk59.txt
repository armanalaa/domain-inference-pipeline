 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by specifying the required skills and qualifications. (cc_employees)
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing training materials, company policies, and equipment. (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours)
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability. (cc_call_center_id)

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers determine the market division for each call center based on geographical location or customer demographics. (cc_mkt_id, cc_division)

## 3. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers classify call centers based on their size, specialization, or service level. (cc_class)

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price. (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type)

## 5. Catalog Page Maintenance

Performed By: Marketing Teams, Web Developers

Activity:
- Marketing teams update catalog pages with new products or promotions, while web developers ensure the website's layout and functionality remain optimal. (cp_catalog_page_sk)

## 6. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details and assigning a return reason. (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk)
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update the inventory accordingly. (cr_warehouse_sk)

## 7. Policy Enforcement

Performed By: TPC Council Members, Administrator

Activity:
- TPC Council members review policy violations and categorize them based on severity. (8.4.1.6 Violation Severity)
- The administrator takes appropriate response measures in accordance with the violation level. (8.4.3 Response Phase)
