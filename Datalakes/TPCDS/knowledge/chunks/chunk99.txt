 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the number of available positions and employee qualifications.
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, employment history, and performance evaluations.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facility managers determine the size, layout, and equipment needs for each call center based on the number of employees and operational requirements.
- Warehouse staff procure and install necessary furniture, technology, and communication equipment in the designated call centers.

## 3. Call Center Operations Planning

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers establish work schedules for sales representatives to ensure optimal coverage during peak business hours.
- Sales representatives prepare scripts and strategies for handling customer inquiries and sales calls.

## 4. Customer Service

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives answer incoming calls from customers, providing information about products and services, and assisting with orders or complaints.
- Warehouse staff verify product availability and process orders for shipping.

## 5. Call Center Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers review call center performance metrics such as average handling time, call volume, and customer satisfaction ratings.
- HR managers identify training needs and implement improvement strategies for sales representatives based on performance evaluations.

## 6. Marketing Strategy Development

Performed By: Market Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Market managers analyze market trends and customer demographics to develop targeted marketing campaigns.
- Sales representatives collaborate with market managers to implement marketing strategies and track their effectiveness.

## 7. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog with new items, including product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff ensure that newly added items are properly stocked and available for order fulfillment.

## 8. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return reasons, item details, and contact information.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for damage or defects, and update inventory levels accordingly.
