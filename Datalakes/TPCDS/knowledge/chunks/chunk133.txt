 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and the center's needs (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information, including personal details and employment history (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming calls and assist customers with their inquiries or issues (cc_call_center_id).
- Market managers oversee call center operations, ensuring adherence to quality standards and service level agreements (cc_market_manager).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers update the catalog with new items, providing details such as product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_description, etc.).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update them in the database when a new item is added or an existing one is sold (cr_item_sk).

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process customer orders by entering order details into the system (cr_order_number).
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment to customers (cr_ship_mode_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer returns by recording return details in the system (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff process returned items, updating inventory levels and preparing them for restocking or refunding (cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).

## 6. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: System Administrators

Activity:
- System administrators monitor system performance to ensure it meets the required throughput and response times (50%, 80%, and 100% of reported throughput rate) (see Clause 5.6.2, 5.6.3, 5.6.4).

## 7. Pricing and Availability Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team calculates the TPC-C Maximum Qualified Throughput (MQTh) rating, total 3-year pricing, and availability date for public reporting (see Clause 5.7.1).
