 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Test Sponsor Document Submission

Performed By: Test Sponsor

Activity:
- The Test Sponsor submits required documentation for a new Result according to Policies § 6.5 and § 6.6. (Submitted for Review column in TPC Results List)

## 2. Administrator Result Addition

Performed By: Administrator

Activity:
- The Administrator adds the submitted Result to the TPC Results List upon receiving the required documentation from the Test Sponsor. (TPC Results List)

## 3. Result Review

Performed By: Council, Steering Committee

Activity:
- The Council or Steering Committee reviews the accepted Result based on compliance with technical specifications of the benchmark. (Status column in TPC Results List)

## 4. Withdrawal of a Result

Performed By: Test Sponsor, Administrator, Council, Steering Committee

Activity:
- The Test Sponsor withdraws a Result or it is withdrawn by the Council, Steering Committee, or Administrator in accordance with TPC Policies. (Withdrawal Category column in TPC Results List)

## 5. Historical Result Designation

Performed By: Test Sponsor

Activity:
- The Test Sponsor designates an accepted Result as a Historical Result if it has been posted on the TPC Results List for at least 185 days past the submission date and at least 60 days past the availability date. (Status column in TPC Results List)

## 6. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- The call center manager assigns sales representatives to specific markets based on market class and description. (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc columns in call_center)

## 7. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price. (ProductID, Name, Category, List Price columns in catalog_page)

## 8. Return Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place returns and warehouse staff track the returned items, recording relevant details such as reason for return and ship mode. (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk columns in catalog_returns)
