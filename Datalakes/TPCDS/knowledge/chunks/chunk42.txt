 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on their skills and qualifications (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information in the system, including personal details, employment history, and performance records.

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers collaborate with market managers to assign each call center to a specific market division (cc_mkt_id).
- Market managers ensure that call centers are strategically placed within their respective divisions to optimize service coverage.

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Management, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facilities management teams set up the physical infrastructure of call centers, including office space, equipment, and furniture (cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff stock necessary supplies for call center operations, such as headsets, computers, and stationery.

## 4. Call Center Operations Scheduling

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers create schedules for employees, defining their working hours and shifts (cc_hours).
- Warehouse staff ensure that all necessary equipment is available during the scheduled operating hours.

## 5. Customer Service Provision

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives answer incoming calls from customers, providing information about products and services (cp_catalog_number).
- Customer service agents assist customers with queries or complaints, guiding them through the purchasing process or troubleshooting issues.

## 6. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Product managers update product information in the catalog, including product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_page_number).
- The marketing team creates and manages the overall structure of the catalog, organizing products into departments for easy navigation.

## 7. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service agents process product returns from customers, recording return reasons (cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, update their status in the system, and prepare them for restocking or disposal.
