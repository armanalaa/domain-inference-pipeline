 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Customer Selection for Order Status Query

Performed By: Terminal Operator

Activity:
- The terminal operator enters the customer last name to initiate a query.
- The terminal operator retrieves the customer ID from the sorted set of selected rows from the CUSTOMER table.
- The terminal operator selects the most recent order placed by that customer from the ORDER table.
- The terminal operator retrieves the order number, entry date, and carrier number from the selected row in the ORDER table.
- The terminal operator retrieves the details of each item in the order from the ORDER-LINE table.

## 2. Order Status Display

Performed By: Terminal Operator

Activity:
- The terminal operator displays the input/output screen with all fields cleared except for the Warehouse field.
- The terminal operator displays the customer name, balance, and order details on the Order-Status screen.
- The terminal operator displays the repeating group of fields (OL_SUPPLY_W_ID, OL_I_ID, OL_QUANTITY, OL_AMOUNT, and OL_DELIVERY_D) for each item in the order.

## 3. Customer Input for Order Status Query

Performed By: Terminal Operator

Activity:
- The terminal operator enters the required input data which is organized as the distinct fields: D_ID and either C_ID or C_LAST.

## 4. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, HR Manager

Activity:
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and qualifications.
- HR managers recruit and onboard new employees for the call center.

## 5. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Manager, Call Center Manager

Activity:
- Market managers define market divisions and assign call centers to these divisions.
- Call center managers manage call centers within their assigned division.

## 6. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Manager

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price in the catalog_page table.
