 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on the company's division and market (cc_division, cc_mkt_id).
- HR managers update employee count for each call center (cc_employees).

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facilities Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facilities managers allocate square footage to call centers (cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff stock call centers with necessary equipment and supplies.

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls during specified hours (cc_hours).
- Call center managers monitor call center performance and make adjustments as needed.

## 4. Customer Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers browse the catalog and select items to purchase (cp_catalog_page_sk).
- Sales representatives assist customers with their orders and confirm order details.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Warehouse staff retrieve ordered items from inventory (not specified in the provided schema).
- Call center employees update order status and provide estimated delivery time to customers.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Customers return items and request refunds (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_call_center_sk).
- Call center employees process returns, update order status, and issue refunds.

## 7. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance metrics such as response times (RT) and throughput rating.
