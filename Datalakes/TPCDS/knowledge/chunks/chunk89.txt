 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the required number of staff and available positions.
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Facility managers allocate square footage for call centers, ensuring adequate space for employees and equipment.
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary hardware such as phones, computers, and furniture.

## 3. Call Center Operations Planning

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers determine operating hours for each call center based on market demand and business needs.
- Sales representatives create schedules for employees to ensure optimal coverage during peak calling times.

## 4. Customer Service

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives answer customer inquiries regarding products, services, or orders.
- Warehouse staff provide information about product availability and shipping status to customers.

## 5. Call Center Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor employee performance metrics such as call handling time, sales conversions, and customer satisfaction ratings.
- HR managers evaluate employee performance based on these metrics and provide feedback or coaching as needed.

## 6. Market Segmentation

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Marketing managers categorize markets based on demographic, geographic, and psychographic factors.
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific market segments to optimize sales efforts.

## 7. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the product catalog with detailed information such as product code, name, category, and list price.
- Sales representatives update product descriptions and images in the catalog as needed.

## 8. Online Presence Management

Performed By: IT Staff, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- IT staff maintain the online presence of the call center, ensuring that the website is functional and up-to-date.
- Marketing managers create and manage online content to attract customers and promote products or services.

## 9. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording customer information, reason for return, and product details.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, inspect them for damage or defects, and update inventory accordingly.

## 10. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on call center performance, sales trends, and customer behavior using data from various tables in the database.
- Call center managers use these reports to make informed decisions about staffing, marketing strategies, and operational improvements.
