 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with details like name, class, employees, square footage, hours, and manager.
- Warehouse staff track the available space in each call center for future expansions.

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize and describe markets, then assign them to specific call centers.
- Call center managers update their division names based on the assigned market.

## 3. Call Center Operations Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center managers open and close call centers as needed, recording start and end dates, closed date keys, and open date keys.
- Sales representatives handle customer calls within the operational hours of their assigned call center.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.

## 5. Catalog Page Creation

Performed By: Content Creators

Activity:
- Content creators create and update catalog pages with department, catalog number, catalog page number, description, and type.

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record returned items along with the customer details, reason for return, and order number.
- Warehouse staff update stock levels upon receiving returned items.

## 7. Report Generation

Performed By: Finance Team, Auditors

Activity:
- Finance team generates required reports based on pricing methodology and full disclosure report requirements.
- Auditors check the generated reports against the auditor's checklist for accuracy.
