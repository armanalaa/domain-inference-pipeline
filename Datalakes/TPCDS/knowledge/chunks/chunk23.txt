 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Affiliate Management

Performed By: TPC Council, SC (Strategic Committee)

Activity:
- The TPC Council determines the eligibility of new affiliates based on their qualifications and alignment with TPC's mission.
- The SC reviews and approves affiliate applications.
- The SC recommends to the Council the termination, reduction, or suspension of an affiliate's status when necessary.

## 2. Affiliate Obligations

Performed By: TPC Affiliates

Activity:
- Affiliates maintain all TPC Confidential information as confidential.
- Affiliates do not have access to budgets, contracts, or other restricted TPC information without explicit permission.

## 3. Expulsion Procedure Initiation

Performed By: TPC Council

Activity:
- The Council votes to initiate the expulsion process for a member based on grounds specified in the bylaws.

## 4. Expulsion Procedure Study

Performed By: SC (Strategic Committee)

Activity:
- The SC studies the issue and makes a recommendation at the next General Meeting regarding the expulsion of a member.

## 5. Member Hearing

Performed By: Affected Member, SC (Strategic Committee), Council

Activity:
- The affected member is given an opportunity to be heard before the SC or in writing.

## 6. Expulsion Notification

Performed By: TPC Administrator

Activity:
- The administrator notifies all members of the proposed expulsion and reasons for proposed expulsion 15 days prior to the General Meeting.

## 7. Council Discussion and Vote on Expulsion

Performed By: SC (Strategic Committee), Council

Activity:
- The council discusses whether or not the member should be expelled, suspended, or sanctioned in some other way at the next General Meeting.

## 8. Mail Ballot for Expulsion

Performed By: TPC Council

Activity:
- If the council votes to send out a Mail Ballot, members have 30 days to respond regarding the expulsion of the affected member.

## 9. Merger, Acquisition, or Divestiture Management

Performed By: TPC Council

Activity:
- The council manages mergers, acquisitions, or divestitures of memberships according to the policies outlined in the TPC bylaws.
