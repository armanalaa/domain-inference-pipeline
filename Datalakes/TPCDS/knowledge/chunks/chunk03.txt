 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign specific areas to new hires based on their skills and expertise (cc_division).
- HR managers update employee count for each call center location (cc_employees).

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Call center managers assign market classes to each call center based on strategic considerations (cc_mkt_class).
- Marketing team updates the division name for each market class (cc_division_name).

## 3. Call Center Information Registration

Performed By: Administrators, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Administrators register new call centers with their details including location and square footage (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country, cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each call center (not specified in the schema).

## 4. Catalog Page Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (not specified in the schema).
- Marketing team updates the description and type of each catalog page (cp_description, cp_type).

## 5. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record returned items with their details such as returned date and time, item SKU, and customer information (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location for each returned item (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Call Center Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Finance Team

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance metrics such as hours of operation and GMT offset (cc_hours, cc_gmt_offset).
- Finance team records tax percentage for each call center location (cc_tax_percentage).
