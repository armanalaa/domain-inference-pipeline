 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the required skills and open positions.
- HR managers update employee information in the database, including personal details, employment history, and performance reviews.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming customer calls, providing assistance with product inquiries or processing orders.
- Representatives update call records with call duration, customer feedback, and resolution details.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers create and manage catalog pages, organizing products into departments and categories.

## 4. Customer Service and Returns

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer complaints or returns by recording the reason for return, processing refunds, and updating inventory levels.

## 5. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive shipments, update inventory levels, and prepare orders for shipping.
- Staff track warehouse inventory, including stock levels, location, and expiration dates.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Purchasing Teams, Finance Department

Activity:
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers based on customer orders and inventory levels.
- Finance department records transactions, including order details, payment terms, and invoices.

## 7. Performance Monitoring and Reporting

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Marketing Managers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance metrics, such as average handling time and customer satisfaction scores.
- Marketing managers analyze sales data to identify trends and opportunities for marketing campaigns.
- Finance department generates financial reports, including balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow statements.
