 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Technical Advisory Board (TAB) Operations

Performed By: TAB Members, Council

Activity:
- TAB members analyze and define interpretations or compliance questions related to Benchmark Standards (TAB Charter).
- TAB members hear arguments from both sides of an interpretation or compliance issue and make recommendations in the form of motions to the Council.
- The Council makes binding decisions based on TAB's recommendations.
- TAB responds to requests from Pre-Publication Boards to address conflicts within the Pre-Publication Board reviewing a Result.
- TAB provides the Council with a statement of issues brought to their attention, along with recommendation(s) for resolution and rationale for the recommendations.

## 2. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign employees and allocate square footage based on operational needs (cc_employees, cc_sq_ft).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for call centers (warehouse_sk).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (catalog_page).
- Sales representatives access catalog pages to find products for customers (cp_catalog_page_id).

## 4. Customer Returns Processing

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns by contacting call centers (cc_call_center_sk).
- Call center representatives record return details such as returned date and time, item SKU, and customer information (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Warehouse staff process returns by updating inventory levels and shipping the returned items back to the warehouse (warehouse_sk).

## 5. Market Analysis and Strategy Development

Performed By: Market Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Market managers analyze market data to identify trends and opportunities (cc_mkt_id, cc_market_manager).
- Sales representatives develop strategies based on market analysis to optimize sales performance.
