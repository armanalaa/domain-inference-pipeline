 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the current staffing needs and employee skills (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_class, ..., cc_zip).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer incoming customer calls during business hours (cc_hours) and handle inquiries or complaints related to products or services.
- Representatives record call details, such as the customer's name, contact information, and issue description (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date, ...).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers define marketing strategies for specific markets based on market analysis and customer demographics (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers implement the marketing strategies by adjusting call center operations or staffing levels accordingly.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, ...).
- IT staff update the catalog page with detailed product information, images, and descriptions (cp_description, cp_type).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details, such as the returned date, time, item SKU, and reason for return (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff receive the returned items, verify their condition, and update the inventory accordingly (cr_item_sk, ...).

## 6. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each product and notify purchasing teams when stock is low (cc_warehouse_sk).
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to suppliers for necessary items (not specified in the provided schema).

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records financial transactions, such as sales, returns, and expenses, and generates reports on revenue, costs, and profitability (not specified in the provided schema).
