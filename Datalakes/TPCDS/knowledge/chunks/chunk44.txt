 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Administrator, Market Manager

Activity:
- Assign call centers to specific markets based on market class and description (cc_mkt_id, cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Allocate square footage to each call center according to its employees count (cc_employees, cc_sq_ft).
- Determine the operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Oversee day-to-day operations of the call center (cc_manager).
- Answer incoming calls from customers and direct them to appropriate representatives.
- Register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (product managers).

## 3. Catalog Maintenance

Performed By: Product Managers, Catalog Administrator

Activity:
- Maintain the catalog structure by managing catalog pages and their associated details (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number).
- Update product information on specific catalog pages as needed.

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Record returned items with their relevant details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk).
- Track the location of returned items within the warehouse (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Result Management

Performed By: Administrator, Council

Activity:
- Mark accepted results as historical after three years unless requested otherwise by the Test Sponsor (cr_returned_date_sk).
- Remove historical results only by specific action of the Council.

## 6. Benchmark Standard Obsolescence

Performed By: Administrator

Activity:
- Change status of results based on an obsolete version of the Specification to Historical Result (cr_returned_date_sk).

## 7. Result Documentation Disclosure

Performed By: Test Sponsor, TPC Members

Activity:
- Prepare and submit Executive Summary, FDR, Supporting Files, and Result Log Files for each benchmark result (cp_catalog_page_sk).
