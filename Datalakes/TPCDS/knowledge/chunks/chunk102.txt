 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with unique identifiers (cc_call_center_sk).
- HR managers recruit and onboard employees for the call centers (cc_employees).
- Call center managers determine the size of each call center in square feet (cc_sq_ft).

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize and describe markets for call centers (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers assign specific markets to their call centers (cc_mkt_id).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff open and close calls during specified time periods (cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date, cc_closed_date_sk, cc_open_date_sk).
- They record the name of each call (cc_name) and classify it according to its nature (cc_class).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 5. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Customers place orders for specific items from the catalog (cr_item_sk).
- Call center staff process these orders by determining the supplying warehouse (cr_warehouse_sk) and quantity (cr_return_quan).

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff check if an order is home or remote based on the supplying warehouse (cr_OL_SUPPLY_W_ID).
- Warehouse staff track and ship orders to customers according to their locations (cr_refunded_addr_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk).

## 7. Tax Calculation

Performed By: Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Call center staff calculate the total tax percentage for an order based on warehouse and district tax rates (W_TAX, D_TAX).

## 8. Order Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records the details of each order in the ORDER table (O_CARRIER_ID, O_ALL_LOCAL).
