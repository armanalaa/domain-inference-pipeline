 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers hire new employees by posting job openings and reviewing applications (cc_employees).
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing training materials and setting up workstations (cc_sq_ft, cc_hours).

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Council Members

Activity:
- Call center managers assign call centers to specific markets based on capacity and demand (cc_mkt_id, cc_division).
- Council members approve market division assignments during regular meetings (cc_company).

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Call center staff answer incoming calls from customers seeking assistance or information (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date).
- Customers place orders, ask questions, or report issues during their interactions with call center staff.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details such as code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_description).
- Marketing managers update catalog pages to reflect changes in product offerings or promotions (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_type).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return details and assigning a return number (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_order_number).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update inventory levels accordingly (cr_item_sk, cr_warehouse_sk).

## 6. Technical Subcommittee Formation

Performed By: Council Members, Steering Committee

Activity:
- Council members propose new technical subcommittees based on emerging issues or needs (not specific to TPC-DS database).
- The Steering Committee reviews proposals and approves the formation of new technical subcommittees.

## 7. Technical Subcommittee Operations

Performed By: Subcommittee Members, Subcommittee Chairperson

Activity:
- Subcommittee members collaborate to address technical issues and make recommendations (not specific to TPC-DS database).
- The subcommittee chairperson facilitates meetings, maintains attendance records, and ensures quorum is met (cc_call_center_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
