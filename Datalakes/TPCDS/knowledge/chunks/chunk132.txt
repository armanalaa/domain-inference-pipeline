 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on their skills and the center's workload (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and employment history (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls and assist customers with their inquiries or issues (cc_rec_start_date).
- Market managers oversee the call center's operations, ensuring adherence to quality standards and service level agreements (cc_class, cc_market_manager).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold (not specified in provided schema).

## 4. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog and specifying quantities (cr_item_sk).
- Sales representatives process orders, confirming availability, calculating totals, and scheduling delivery (not specified in provided schema).

## 5. Order Fulfillment

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Delivery Personnel

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment (not specified in provided schema).
- Delivery personnel transport the orders to their destinations, updating status as they progress (cr_ship_mode_sk).

## 6. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customers, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns by contacting customer service and providing order details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_refunded_customer_sk).
- Customer service representatives process the return, updating inventory levels and issuing refunds (cr_reason_sk, cr_order_number).

## 7. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customers, Finance Department

Activity:
- Customers make payments for their orders using various methods (not specified in provided schema).
- The finance department records the transactions, updates customer accounts, and issues invoices if necessary (cr_refunded_customer_sk, cr_call_center_sk).

## 8. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: System Administrators, Data Analysts

Activity:
- System administrators ensure the system is running smoothly and performs checkpoints as required (checkpoint interval, checkpoint duration).
- Data analysts analyze performance data to identify trends, bottlenecks, and areas for improvement (response times, frequency distribution of response times).
