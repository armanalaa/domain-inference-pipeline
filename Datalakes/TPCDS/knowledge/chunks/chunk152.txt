 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers with specific market identifiers (cc_mkt_id).
- HR managers recruit and hire employees for the assigned call centers (cc_employees).
- Call center managers determine the square footage (cc_sq_ft) and operating hours (cc_hours) for each call center.
- HR managers set up the call center manager's details in the system (cc_manager).

## 2. District Management

Performed By: District Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- District managers define tax percentages (d_tax) for each district.
- Call center managers associate their call centers with specific districts (cc_d_id).

## 3. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse managers track inventory levels (stock) for each item in the warehouse.
- Purchasing teams order new items from suppliers when stock levels fall below a certain threshold.

## 4. Item Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with unique identifiers (cp_catalog_page_id), categories (cp_department), and list prices.
- Sales representatives update item details (i_name) and descriptions (i_data) for existing items in the catalog.

## 5. Order Placement

Performed By: Customers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Customers place orders by selecting items from the catalog (ol_i_id), specifying quantities (ol_quantity), and providing their contact information.
- Sales representatives confirm the order details with customers and process the transactions in the system.

## 6. Inventory Update

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update stock levels (s_quantity) when items are sold or returned.
- Sales representatives update order statuses (o_entry_d) and mark orders as shipped once inventory is available.

## 7. Return Processing

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns by contacting the call center (cr_call_center_sk).
- Call center staff record return reasons (cr_reason_sk) and update order statuses (o_entry_d) accordingly.

## 8. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Teams

Activity:
- Finance teams calculate total amounts for each order (total) and record them in the system (amt).
