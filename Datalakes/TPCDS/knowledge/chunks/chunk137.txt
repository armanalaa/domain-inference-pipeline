 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers recruit new employees by posting job openings and reviewing applications.
- HR managers onboard new hires by providing training materials, setting up workstations, and assigning roles.
- Call center managers allocate employees to specific call centers based on skills and availability.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Agents, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center agents answer incoming calls from customers and assist with inquiries or troubleshooting issues.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and process orders for shipped items based on customer requests.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details such as code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing managers create and update catalog pages to showcase products and provide descriptions.

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return details and updating the database.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, verify their condition, and update inventory levels accordingly.

## 5. Financial Record Keeping

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records financial transactions such as sales, returns, and expenses related to call center operations and catalog management.

## 6. Performance Analysis

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management

Activity:
- Data analysts collect and analyze performance data to identify trends and areas for improvement in call center operations and catalog management.
- Management reviews the analysis results and makes decisions based on the insights gained.
