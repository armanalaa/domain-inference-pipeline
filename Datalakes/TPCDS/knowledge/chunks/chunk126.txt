 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific districts based on D_ID from the DISTRICT table.
- Warehouse staff check STOCK quantities (S_QUANTITY) for items requested by customers.

## 2. District Management

Performed By: District Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- District managers oversee warehouse operations within their district, using D_ID as a reference.
- Warehouse staff update S_YTD and S_ORDER_CNT columns based on customer orders and stock levels.

## 3. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives identify potential customers using C_ID from the CUSTOMER table.
- Customer service agents assist customers with returns (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returning_customer_sk) and refunds.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Call Center Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives create new orders for customers based on their preferences and available stock (OL_QUANTITY).
- Warehouse staff prepare items for shipment once orders are confirmed.
- Call center agents update order statuses (cr_call_center_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk) as orders progress through the system.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff monitor stock levels (S_QUANTITY) and reorder items when necessary.
- Purchasing teams negotiate with suppliers to restock low inventory.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records financial transactions (cr_amount, H_AMOUNT) related to customer orders and returns.

## 7. Marketing Analysis

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Marketing managers analyze catalog page performance (cp_catalog_page_number, cp_type) to optimize product offerings.
- Data analysts use customer data (C_ID, C_DELIVERY_CNT) to identify trends and make recommendations for future marketing strategies.
