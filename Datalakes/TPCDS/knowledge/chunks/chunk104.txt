 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific districts and set their associated details like name, class, size, hours of operation, and location.
- HR managers recruit and hire employees for the call centers, recording their personal and professional information.

## 2. District Management

Performed By: District Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- District managers oversee multiple call centers within a district, ensuring efficient operations and effective customer service.
- Call center managers report to district managers regarding call center performance metrics such as employee count, square footage, and operational hours.

## 3. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives initiate new orders with customers by displaying the input/output screen for order placement.
- Customers enter their required data, including district ID, customer ID, and item details, as prompted by the sales representative.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives process new orders by determining the order ID, calculating the total amount, and displaying the execution status message.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and fulfill orders by shipping items to customers based on the provided order details.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update stock levels for each item in the warehouse, ensuring accurate records of available inventory.
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders to replenish depleted inventory, considering factors such as supplier, item ID, quantity, and price.

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer returns by recording the returned date and time, item SKU, refunded customer details, and reason for return.
- Warehouse staff process returns by updating inventory levels, marking items as returned, and preparing them for restocking or disposal.

## 7. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Managers, District Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance metrics such as average handling time, service level, and abandonment rate to identify areas for improvement.
- District managers analyze the performance of multiple call centers within their district and implement strategies to optimize overall efficiency.
