 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers to specific districts based on market and division information.
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center as needed.

## 2. Customer Service Operations

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries about products and services.
- Customer service agents assist customers with product orders, returns, and refunds.

## 3. Product Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording their codes, names, categories, and list prices.
- Marketing managers update the product descriptions and categorization for better visibility on the catalog pages.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives take orders from customers over the phone or online.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and prepare items for shipping based on order details.
- Customer service agents confirm order status and delivery dates with customers.

## 5. Inventory Management

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Purchasing Teams

Activity:
- Warehouse staff update the stock levels of each product as items are received or shipped out.
- Purchasing teams place orders for new inventory based on current stock levels and sales forecasts.

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Team, Management

Activity:
- Finance team records financial transactions such as payments, refunds, and taxes.
- Management reviews the financial reports to monitor revenue, expenses, and profitability.

## 7. Performance Monitoring and Analysis

Performed By: Management, IT Support

Activity:
- Management monitors call center performance metrics like average handling time and customer satisfaction scores.
- IT support ensures database consistency and transaction isolation during peak usage periods.
