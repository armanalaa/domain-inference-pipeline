 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers based on market needs (cc_market_manager).
- Warehouse staff allocate square footage to newly assigned call centers (cc_sq_ft).
- Call center managers determine the number of employees required for each call center (cc_employees).
- Warehouse staff set up call centers in designated spaces according to the allocated square footage (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Call center managers establish operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Classification

Performed By: Call Center Manager

Activity:
- Call center managers categorize call centers based on their class and division (cc_class, cc_division).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels after receiving returned items (not specified in provided schema).
- Customer service representatives determine the reason for each return (cr_reason_sk).

## 5. Call Center Performance Monitoring

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Finance Department

Activity:
- Call center managers monitor call center performance by tracking open and closed dates (cc_open_date_sk, cc_closed_date_sk).
- Finance department records the tax percentage for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).
