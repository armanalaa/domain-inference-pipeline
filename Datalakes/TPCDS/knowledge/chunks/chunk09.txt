 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on the available space (cc_sq_ft).
- HR managers update employee information in the system, including name, position, and start date (cc_rec_start_date).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Call center employees handle customer calls during operational hours (cc_hours).
- Call center managers monitor call center performance and adjust staffing levels as needed.

## 3. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets based on market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- HR managers update employee information for employees working in a particular market.

## 4. Call Center Expansion

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Facility Management

Activity:
- Call center managers request additional space from facility management when the current call center is at capacity (cc_sq_ft).

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, IT Staff

Activity:
- Product managers update product information in the catalog, including product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- IT staff maintain the integrity of the catalog data and ensure its accessibility to call center employees.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns and record the returned date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when a return is processed.

## 7. Inventory Tracking

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update them in the system (cr_warehouse_sk).
- IT staff ensure accurate inventory data and handle any discrepancies that arise.
