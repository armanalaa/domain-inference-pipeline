 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Assign call centers to specific districts based on market class and division.
- Determine the number of employees required for each call center.
- Allocate square footage for each call center according to its employee count.
- Set operating hours for each call center.
- Appoint a manager for each call center.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Staff, Customers

Activity:
- Answer incoming customer calls during operational hours.
- Route calls to appropriate departments based on customer inquiries or issues.
- Record call start and end dates, as well as any relevant details about the call.
- Close calls when they are no longer active or require further follow-up.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Assign each item to a specific department within the catalog.
- Create unique catalog pages for each item.

## 4. Catalog Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Record returned items from customers and their corresponding reasons.
- Assign returned items to appropriate warehouses for processing.
- Update the catalog with information about the returned item.
- Refund customers upon successful processing of their returns.

## 5. Delivery Transaction Processing

Performed By: Delivery Drivers, Call Center Staff

Activity:
- Schedule deliveries for orders placed by customers.
- Assign delivery drivers to specific routes and orders.
- Track the status of deliveries in real-time.
- Update order status when deliveries are completed or encounter issues.
