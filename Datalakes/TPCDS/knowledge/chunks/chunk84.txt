 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers register new call centers by recording the call center ID, name, class, employees, square footage, hours, manager, market ID, division, and company.
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each call center by updating the sq_ft column.

## 2. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets based on market ID.
- Call center managers update the market class and market description for each call center.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Sales representatives update the catalog page number for each item.

## 4. Catalog Page Maintenance

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives update the description and type of a catalog page.
- Warehouse staff adjust the inventory levels associated with a specific catalog page.

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives record returned items by specifying the returned date and time, item SKU, refunded customer details, reason for return, order number, and ship mode.
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse SKU and adjust the inventory levels for each returned item.

## 6. Access Control Management

Performed By: TPC Administrators

Activity:
- TPC administrators grant read-write access to specific users with a signed CLA for private code repositories.
- TPC administrators grant read-only access to all members for non-code repositories (except for Standing Committees).
- TPC administrators grant read-write access to Standing Committee members only for non-code repositories related to Standing Committees.
- TPC administrators grant read-write access to specific users with a signed CLA for public code repositories.

## 7. Repository Creation

Performed By: TPC Administrators, Technical Subcommittee Members

Activity:
- TPC administrators create new repositories when necessary for a new benchmark specification, software package or kit, major revision of a benchmark or software package, or license changes to a software package or kit.
