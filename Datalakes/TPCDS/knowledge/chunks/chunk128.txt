 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on the number of employees and square footage available (cc_employees, cc_sq_ft).
- HR managers update employee information in the system when a new employee joins or leaves (cc_name, cc_manager).

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers determine which market division each call center belongs to based on the market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Market managers assign specific markets to each call center for handling customer inquiries and transactions (cc_market_manager).

## 3. Call Center Operations Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers schedule operating hours for each call center (cc_hours).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse location associated with a call center when there is a change in stock levels or delivery status (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 4. Transaction Mix Regulation

Performed By: Test Sponsors, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Test sponsors set the weights for each transaction type to ensure the required minimum percentage of mix over the measurement interval (not applicable columns/tables provided).
- Call center managers adjust the weight associated with each transaction type during the measurement interval to maintain the required transaction mix within 5% of their respective initial value.

## 5. Transaction Selection and Processing

Performed By: Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Call center representatives select a new transaction uniformly at random from a weighted distribution or deck of cards, ensuring the required transaction mix (not applicable columns/tables provided).
- Call center representatives process each selected transaction according to its type (New-Order, Payment, Order-Status, Delivery, Stock-Level) within the specified response time constraints.

## 6. Wait Times and Response Time Monitoring

Performed By: System Administrators

Activity:
- System administrators monitor the Menu RT and Transaction RT for each transaction type to ensure they meet the required response time constraints (not applicable columns/tables provided).
