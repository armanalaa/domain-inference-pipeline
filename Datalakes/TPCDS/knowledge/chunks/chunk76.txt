 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific call centers based on capacity and demand (cc_division).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center (cc_sq_ft).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming customer calls and provide assistance (cc_call_center_id).
- Customers place orders over the phone, which are recorded by call center employees (cr_order_number).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_catalog_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- HR managers hire additional call center employees to handle increased call volume (cc_employees).

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the inventory levels after processing a return (cr_warehouse_sk).

## 5. Market Analysis

Performed By: Market Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Market managers analyze market trends and classify call centers based on their performance (cc_mkt_class).
- HR managers adjust staffing levels in response to market analysis findings (cc_employees).

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department records the tax percentage for each call center (cc_tax_percentage).
- The finance department generates financial reports based on call center data (not specified tables/columns).
