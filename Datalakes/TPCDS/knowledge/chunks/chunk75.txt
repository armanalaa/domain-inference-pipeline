 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Test Submission Process Management

Performed By: Test Sponsor Team, Administrator

Activity:
- Test Sponsor team prepares the Executive Summary and Result Log Files according to the Benchmark Standard (Policies § 12.4.1).
- Test Sponsor team submits the Executive Summary, Result Log Files, and Alert Message to the Administrator (Policies § 12.4.3).
- Administrator reviews the submitted documents for compliance with the Benchmark Standard and Policies (Policies § 12.4.2).
- If necessary, the Pre-Publication Board or TPC certified Auditor certifies the Result for publication (Policies § 10.2, § 9.3).

## 2. Result Certification

Performed By: Pre-Publication Board, TPC certified Auditor

Activity:
- Pre-Publication Board or TPC certified Auditor reviews the submitted documents for compliance with the Benchmark Standard and Policies (Policies § 12.4.2).
- If the entire Result is certified by a TPC Auditor, the Test Sponsor can publish the Result using TPC-Enterprise mechanisms (Policies § 12.4.2.1).

## 3. Alert Message Creation

Performed By: Test Sponsor Team

Activity:
- Test Sponsor team creates an Alert Message containing the required information (Policies § 12.4.3).

## 4. Result Publication

Performed By: Administrator, Test Sponsor Team

Activity:
- Administrator posts the certified Result after receiving the Alert Message from the Test Sponsor (Policies § 12.4.3).

## 5. Review Period

Performed By: TPC Community

Activity:
- The TPC community reviews the published Result during the 60-calendar day Review Period (Policies § 12.5.1).

## 6. Historical Result Update

Performed By: Test Sponsor Team

Activity:
- If components of the SUT become Generally Available after a Result is published, the Test Sponsor can update the Result and start a new Review Period (Policies § 12.4.4.3).

## 7. Fair Use Rule Definition

Performed By: Benchmark Standard Committee

Activity:
- The Benchmark Standard Committee defines additional benchmark Fair Use Rules that do not conflict with Policies § 8.2 (Policies § 12.6).
