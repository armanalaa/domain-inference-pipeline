 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers define the call center's structure by assigning roles, divisions, and responsibilities.
- HR managers recruit and hire new employees for various positions within the call center.
- Call center managers allocate appropriate workspace (sq ft) to each employee based on their role.
- HR managers establish working hours for each employee according to the call center's schedule.

## 2. Market Division Assignment

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Market Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers determine market segments and classes for the call center operations.
- Market managers are assigned specific markets based on their expertise and the call center's strategy.

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Technicians

Activity:
- Warehouse staff equip call centers with necessary equipment such as phones and headsets.
- IT technicians configure and set up the call center's technology infrastructure.

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Teams

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Marketing teams create and manage catalog pages to showcase products and services.

## 5. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Sales representatives take customer orders over the phone or through the call center.
- Warehouse staff prepare and ship ordered items to customers based on their location.

## 6. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives handle customer returns by recording return details and reasons.
- Warehouse staff process returned items and update inventory accordingly.

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance department records all financial transactions, including tax percentages, to maintain accurate financial reports.
