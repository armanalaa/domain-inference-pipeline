 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new call centers based on market requirements (cc_market_manager).
- Warehouse staff allocate space for the newly established call centers (cc_sq_ft).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Sales Representatives

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming customer calls during operational hours (cc_hours).
- Sales representatives assist customers with product inquiries and place orders (cr_order_number).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with their respective details (cp_catalog_page_id, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when a new item is added to the catalog.

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns and record return details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels and handle the returned items.

## 5. Market Classification

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Marketing Team

Activity:
- Call center managers categorize call centers based on their market class (cc_mkt_class).
- The marketing team assigns appropriate marketing descriptions to each category (cc_mkt_desc).

## 6. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- Finance records the tax percentage for each call center location (cc_tax_percentage).

## 7. Membership Management

Performed By: TPC Administration Team

Activity:
- The administration team manages membership applications, fees, and dues (2.4 Application Fee, 2.5 Dues).
