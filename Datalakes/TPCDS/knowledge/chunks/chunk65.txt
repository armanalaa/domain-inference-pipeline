 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Test Sponsor Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Warehouse Staff, HR Manager

Activity:
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center (cc_sq_ft).
- HR managers manage employee schedules, recording hours worked by each employee (cc_hours).

## 2. Auditor Onboarding

Performed By: Test Sponsor, Auditor

Activity:
- Test sponsors negotiate audit rates with auditors (not specified in provided database schema).
- Test sponsors release auditors from non-disclosure agreements regarding compliance issues during the course of an audit (not specified in provided database schema).

## 3. Audit Execution

Performed By: Auditor

Activity:
- Auditors interpret specifications conservatively when ambiguity arises (not specified in provided database schema).
- Auditors decide to waive requirements if certain conditions are met, and report these waivers in their attestation letters (not specified in provided database schema).

## 4. Audit Reporting

Performed By: Auditor

Activity:
- Auditors share new decisions made during the course of an audit with other auditors to increase consistency (not specified in provided database schema).

## 5. FDR Submission

Performed By: Test Sponsor, Auditor

Activity:
- Test sponsors submit FDRs (Fairness Documentation Reports) to the TPC after an audit is completed (not specified in provided database schema).

## 6. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Manager, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels for each item (not specified in provided database schema).

## 7. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer, Warehouse Staff, Call Center Representative

Activity:
- Customers place returns by contacting the call center (cr_call_center_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when items are returned (not specified in provided database schema).
- Call center representatives record return details such as returned date and time, item SKU, and reason for return (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk, cr_item_sk, cr_reason_sk).
