 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new hires to specific call centers based on staffing needs (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal and professional details (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Market Division Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers define market classifications for each call center (cc_mkt_class).
- Call center managers update their division and company information accordingly (cc_division, cc_company).

## 3. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, IT Technicians

Activity:
- Warehouse staff measure and record the square footage of call centers (cc_sq_ft).
- IT technicians configure call center systems to operate within specified hours (cc_hours).

## 4. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Call center representatives select a transaction type from the menu based on weighted distribution (not applicable columns/tables).
- Customers place orders or inquire about order status through call centers.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price (not applicable columns/tables).
- Warehouse staff update stock levels as orders are processed (not applicable columns/tables).

## 6. Return Processing

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Representatives

Activity:
- Customers initiate returns of purchased items and provide reason for return (cr_reason_sk).
- Call center representatives record the returned item details in the database (cr_item_sk, cr_call_center_sk, etc.).

## 7. Reporting and Analysis

Performed By: Finance Teams, Analysts

Activity:
- Finance teams record transactional data for tax calculation purposes (cc_tax_percentage).
- Analysts generate performance metrics reports based on completed transactions and response times (not applicable columns/tables).
