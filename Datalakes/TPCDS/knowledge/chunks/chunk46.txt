 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the required skills and open positions (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee information in the database, including personal details, employment history, and performance records.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer customer queries, handle complaints, and provide product information (cc_call_center_id).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update them in the database when products are shipped or received.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers add new items to the catalog by recording product details such as name, category, list price, and product code (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number).
- Marketing managers update catalog pages with promotional information or seasonal offers.

## 4. Customer Interaction

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Customers

Activity:
- Customers place orders over the phone by providing their details and selecting products from the catalog (cc_call_center_id).
- Call center representatives process orders, update order statuses, and handle any issues that arise during the transaction.

## 5. Return Management

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return reasons, customer details, and product information (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_item_sk).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when returned products are received.

## 6. Result Submission and Review

Performed By: Test Sponsors, TPC Council

Activity:
- Test sponsors submit new benchmark results for review (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).
- The TPC Council reviews the submitted results, approves or rejects them based on the policies, and updates the TPC Results List accordingly.

## 7. Disclosure of Implementation

Performed By: Test Sponsors, TPC Technical Subcommittees

Activity:
- Test sponsors disclose their implementation details in the FDR (cr_ship_mode_sk).
- TPC technical subcommittees review and copy the disclosed implementations for benchmarking purposes.
