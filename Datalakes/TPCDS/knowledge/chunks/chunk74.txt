 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Benchmark Standard Approval and Obsolescence Management

Performed By: Council, Steering Committee, Primary Representatives, Affiliates

Activity:
- Council members review proposed new or revised TPC-Express Benchmark Standards (call_center).
- Council votes on approval of a new TPC-Express Benchmark Standard or Major Revision (not specified).
- Council or Steering Committee votes on approval of a Third Tier Revision.
- Council sets obsolescence date for older versions of a TPC-Express Benchmark Standard.
- Council evaluates obsolescence requirements and passes a motion to extend the life of a TPC-Express Benchmark Standard.
- Steering Committee notifies Primary Representatives and Affiliates about the obsolescence of a TPC-Express Benchmark Standard.

## 2. TPC-Express Benchmark Submission

Performed By: Benchmark submitters, Council

Activity:
- Benchmark submitters conduct tests using TPC-Express TPC-Provided Software.
- Benchmark submitters prepare and submit results for a TPC-Express Benchmark Standard.
- Council reviews submitted results for a TPC-Express Benchmark Standard.
- Council approves or rejects the type of revision level for changes to TPC-Express TPC-Provided Software.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product managers, Warehouse staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog (catalog_page).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels and update product information accordingly.

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Customer service representatives, Warehouse staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns (catalog_returns).
- Warehouse staff receive returned items and update inventory levels.

## 5. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call center employees, Market managers

Activity:
- Call center employees handle incoming calls from customers or affiliates (call_center).
- Market managers oversee call center operations within their market division.
