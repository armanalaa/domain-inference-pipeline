 TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Item Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff track item availability by updating the quantity on hand for each item in the warehouse.

## 2. Warehouse Management

Performed By: Warehouse Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Warehouse managers assign warehouses to districts based on geographical location and capacity needs.
- Warehouse staff receive incoming shipments and update the quantity on hand for each item in the warehouse.

## 3. Customer Management

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives identify potential customers and add their contact information to the customer database.
- Customer service representatives handle customer inquiries and update customer records with any relevant information.

## 4. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Sales representatives process orders by selecting items from the catalog, determining shipping methods, and calculating total costs.
- Warehouse staff pick and pack ordered items for shipment.
- Customer service representatives confirm order details with customers and update order statuses in the system.

## 5. Invoice Generation

Performed By: Finance Team

Activity:
- Finance team generates invoices based on completed orders, including item details, quantities, prices, taxes, and total amounts due.

## 6. Payment Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Finance Team

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer payments by recording payment methods, amounts, and dates.
- Finance team updates customer accounts with paid invoices and reconciles payments against outstanding balances.

## 7. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process returns by recording return reasons, item details, and customer information.
- Warehouse staff receive returned items, update the quantity on hand, and prepare them for restocking or disposal.

## 8. Analytics and Reporting

Performed By: Data Analysts, Management Team

Activity:
- Data analysts generate reports on sales trends, customer behavior, inventory levels, and warehouse performance using data from various tables in the TPC-DS database.
- Management team reviews reports to make informed decisions about marketing strategies, inventory management, and operational efficiency.
