 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the number of available positions and employee qualifications.
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, contact information, and employment history.

## 2. Call Center Setup

Performed By: Facility Managers, IT Support

Activity:
- Facility managers allocate square footage for call centers, ensuring adequate space for employees and equipment.
- IT support configures call center systems, including phones, computers, and software, to meet specific operational needs.

## 3. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Supervisors

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls, assist customers with inquiries, and process transactions as needed.
- Supervisors monitor call center performance, addressing any issues that arise during operations.

## 4. Market Segmentation

Performed By: Marketing Managers, Market Researchers

Activity:
- Marketing managers divide the market into segments based on demographic, geographic, and psychographic factors.
- Market researchers gather data on customer preferences, behavior, and trends to inform segmentation decisions.

## 5. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price.
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold.

## 6. Order Processing

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Customer Service Agents

Activity:
- Sales representatives take customer orders over the phone, entering order details into the system.
- Customer service agents confirm order details with customers and process returns when necessary.

## 7. Delivery Coordination

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Shipping Clerks

Activity:
- Warehouse staff pick and pack orders based on customer requests and shipping requirements.
- Shipping clerks label packages, arrange for delivery, and update order status in the system.

## 8. Performance Monitoring

Performed By: IT Administrators, Management

Activity:
- IT administrators monitor call center performance metrics, such as tpmC (transactions per minute), to ensure steady state conditions are met.
- Management reviews these metrics and makes decisions about system upgrades or capacity adjustments based on the results.
