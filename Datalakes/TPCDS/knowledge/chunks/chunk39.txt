 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, HR Manager

Activity:
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers update employee records with personal details, qualifications, and performance evaluations (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country).
- Call center managers determine the number of employees required for each shift and adjust staffing levels accordingly (cc_hours).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center representatives answer customer inquiries, handle complaints, and provide product information (cc_call_center_id, cc_rec_start_date, cc_rec_end_date).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels, process orders, and coordinate shipments to customers (cr_item_sk, cr_warehouse_sk, cr_ship_mode_sk).

## 3. Voting Process

Performed By: Primary Representatives, Administrator

Activity:
- Primary representatives cast their votes in written form, either by hard copy, fax, or email (cp_catalog_page_sk, cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number).
- The Administrator receives and acknowledges each vote within the next business day (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk).

## 4. Confidentiality Maintenance

Performed By: Administrator

Activity:
- The Administrator maintains secrecy about who has voted, how they have voted, and the current tally until the ballot closes (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).

## 5. Vote Certification

Performed By: Administrator, SC

Activity:
- The Administrator announces the results, including how each Director voted, after the vote closes (cr_reason_sk, cr_order_number).
- The SC certifies the Mail Ballot results if no protests are filed within seven days of the announcement (cr_refunded_customer_sk, cr_refunded_cdemo_sk, cr_refunded_hdemo_sk, cr_refunded_addr_sk).

## 6. Calendar Management

Performed By: TPC Secretary

Activity:
- The TPC Secretary schedules and announces the time and location of each General Meeting (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).

## 7. Financial Reporting

Performed By: Finance Department

Activity:
- The finance department prepares and distributes the annual report, statement of transactions or indemnification, and financial reports (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk).
