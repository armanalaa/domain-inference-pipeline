 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign new employees to specific call centers based on the number of available positions (cc_employees).
- HR managers recruit and hire new employees for open call center positions.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center representatives handle customer inquiries and process orders by recording customer details (cc_name, cc_class) and order information (order number).
- Warehouse staff track inventory levels to ensure sufficient stock for order fulfillment.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product details (product code, name, category, list price).
- Marketing managers create and update catalog pages to display products and their information (cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description).

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording return details (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk) and updating the inventory accordingly.
- Warehouse staff receive and inspect returned items before restocking or disposal.

## 5. Market Analysis

Performed By: Market Managers, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Market managers analyze market trends and customer preferences to determine product demand (cc_division).
- Data analysts use this information to optimize inventory levels and marketing strategies.
