 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Manager, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific call centers based on capacity needs (cc_employees).
- Warehouse staff track the square footage (cc_sq_ft) and hours of operation (cc_hours) for each call center.
- Call center managers update the contact information (cc_street_number, cc_street_name, cc_street_type, cc_suite_number, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_zip, cc_country, cc_gmt_offset) for each call center.
- Warehouse staff record the tax percentage (cc_tax_percentage) for each call center.

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers and assist with inquiries or issues related to products or services.
- Customers place orders for products by providing necessary details (catalog_number, list price) to call center employees.

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording the product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).

## 4. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customer Service Representatives, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Customer service representatives process customer returns by recording the returned date and time (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_returned_time_sk), item SKU (cr_item_sk), and reason for return (cr_reason_sk).
- Warehouse staff update the warehouse inventory (not specified in provided schema) based on customer returns.

## 5. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Manager

Activity:
- Market managers assign call centers to specific markets based on market class and description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers update the market manager for each call center (cc_market_manager).
