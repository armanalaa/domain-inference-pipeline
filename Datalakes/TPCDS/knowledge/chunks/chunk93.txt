 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, HR Managers

Activity:
- Call center managers assign employees to specific call centers based on their skills and availability (cc_employees).
- HR managers recruit new staff members and update their details in the system (cc_name, cc_company, cc_street_number, etc.).

## 2. Call Center Operations

Performed By: Call Center Employees, Customers

Activity:
- Call center employees answer incoming calls from customers (cc_rec_start_date).
- Customers place orders over the phone, providing necessary details like product code and quantity (cr_item_sk, cr_order_number).

## 3. Market Management

Performed By: Market Managers, Call Center Managers

Activity:
- Market managers categorize call centers based on their class and market description (cc_mkt_class, cc_mkt_desc).
- Call center managers update the operational details of their centers, such as hours and square footage (cc_hours, cc_sq_ft).

## 4. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog with product code, name, category, and list price (cp_catalog_number, cp_catalog_page_number, cp_description, cp_type).
- Warehouse staff update inventory levels when new products are added or existing ones are sold (not specified in the database schema).

## 5. Returns Processing

Performed By: Customers, Call Center Employees

Activity:
- Customers return items and request refunds, providing relevant details like returned date and reason (cr_returned_date_sk, cr_reason_sk).
- Call center employees process returns by updating the database with the necessary information (cr_call_center_sk, cr_catalog_page_sk, etc.).

## 6. Reporting and Analytics

Performed By: Finance Teams, Data Analysts

Activity:
- Finance teams record tax percentages for each call center location (cc_tax_percentage).
- Data analysts generate reports based on the collected data for analysis and decision making (not specified in the database schema).
