 # TPC-DS — Business Workflows

## 1. Call Center Management

Performed By: Call Center Managers, Warehouse Staff

Activity:
- Call center managers assign warehouse staff to specific call centers based on capacity and demand (cc_division).
- Warehouse staff update the square footage of each call center (cc_sq_ft).

## 2. Call Center Operation

Performed By: Sales Representatives, Purchasing Teams, Customers

Activity:
- Sales representatives handle customer inquiries and process orders over the phone or through remote presence (cc_call_center_id).
- Purchasing teams issue purchase orders for new call center equipment or supplies (not specified in provided materials).
- Customers place orders for call center services, which are recorded in the call center database (not specified in provided materials).

## 3. Catalog Management

Performed By: Product Managers, Marketing Managers

Activity:
- Product managers register new items in the catalog by recording product code, name, category, and list price (not specified in provided columns).
- Marketing managers update the description and type of each catalog page (cp_description, cp_type).

## 4. Return Processing

Performed By: Warehouse Staff, Customer Service Representatives

Activity:
- Warehouse staff receive returned items from customers and record them in the returns database (cr_item_sk).
- Customer service representatives process customer refunds based on return reasons (cr_reason_sk).

## 5. Meeting Organization

Performed By: TPC Staff, Council Members

Activity:
- TPC staff schedule meetings and determine their location (cc_open_date_sk, cc_city, cc_county, cc_state, cc_country).
- Council members vote on changes to meeting locations or forms when necessary (not specified in provided materials).

## 6. Meeting Conduct

Performed By: Council Members, TPC Staff

Activity:
- Council members and TPC staff follow documented bylaws and policies during meetings (not specified in provided materials).
- In case of uncovered situations, Robert's Rules of Order are applied (not specified in provided materials).
